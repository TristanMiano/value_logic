## 1. Signature, semantics, and symbol audit

### 1.1 Compact and elaborated requests

The compact request is consistently `(s,e,q,P)`: finite state, evaluated plan,
context, and profile. The elaborated context supplies domain, task criterion,
aggregation, tolerance, fallback, resources, frame, evidence mode, evaluated
set, and versioned provenance. Elaboration fixes those fields before atom
evaluation; it does not identify the learned input vector with the logical
request.

The layers remain distinct:

- an object computation or prediction;
- the task criterion `L_q` and risk `J_q(e)`;
- a learned estimator of a statistic or interval relevant to that criterion;
- the optimization loss used to fit the estimator;
- a proposed calibration expansion;
- an accepted, version-bound certificate;
- an exact atom diagnostic and `K_3` value; and
- a profile assessment followed, when granted, by masked selection.

This separation blocks two recurrent invalid inferences: cross-entropy fit does
not create evidence, and a positive estimated margin does not create a complete
license.

### 1.2 Well-formedness, atom values, and profiles

`WF` is evaluated before meaningful atom states. A well-formed atom is
Supported, Open, or Refuted; the public request outcome is then derived as
Undefined, Granted, Withheld, or Refused. Inclusive smaller-is-better adequacy
uses support at `U <= epsilon` and refutation at `L > epsilon`. Equality is
therefore supported, while its positive-surplus ReLU channel is zero.

Profiles select and meet atom requirements; they do not change an already
instantiated atom's value. The profile-refinement result is sound under the
typed refinement side conditions and relatively complete only inside one fixed
instantiation fiber for the independently realizable unary fragment. The
paper does not generalize this to arbitrary conjunctive semantics or automatic
well-formedness transfer.

The diagnostic cardinality result is stated over the realizable query quotient.
It yields `ceil(log2 |V|)` bits for a fixed-length discrete code. It is not a
lower bound on real-valued neural width. The familiar `3^n` count requires
independent realization of every ternary vector.

### 1.4 Certified plan composition and self-evaluation

The finite plan construction preserves three typed outputs: computational
payload, quantitative grade, and certificate/provenance. Each constructor has
an ordinary payload map, a grade transformer, and a locally checked certificate
rule. Structural induction constructs the annotated root; proof erasure
recovers the ordinary computation. A separately checked root certificate can
then lift through the actual license kernel. Meeting component grants alone
cannot grant the composite: two same-sign errors of `.06` pass local `.10`
bounds yet add to a root error `.12`.

Grounded support in the finite DAG reaches a typed zero-premise evidence base.
Strict evaluator ranks make a total deterministic system assessment unique and
order independent. A system's same-rank self-endorsement is insufficient.
Cycles are outside the core. The paper names Kripke--Kleene/Fitting semantics
as neighboring fixed-point machinery and explicitly says that an ordered
space, operator, fixed-point selection rule, and evidence bridge would be new
obligations.

The path-sensitivity recurrence

`e_v <= delta_v + sum_(u in pred(v)) L_(u,v) e_u`

unrolls to the sum over directed path products. The empty path at the root has
coefficient one; absent paths have coefficient zero. Bounds must hold on the
reachable perturbation tube. Resource grades are not silently forced into the
same sum: latency, peak memory, and energy retain their declared aggregators.

### 1.5 Routing, factorization, and ReLU scope

The exact routed-risk identity partitions authorized expert use, misrouting,
and fallback. Whole-cell average risk does not imply selected-subset risk after
rescaling by the selected mass; fallback frequency without severity also does
not bound fallback contribution.

The canonical factorization theorem is consumer-relative. An encoder supports
the declared query family exactly iff every query factors through it; the
observation partition supplies the coarsest exact quotient. Task 17's
state-plus-surplus code is minimal only for a consumer family containing every
state and surplus coordinate projection. It is a coordinate-completeness
result, not information-theoretic optimality for arbitrary consumers.

For a registered positive scale `sigma_i`, normalized surplus
`ReLU(m_i / sigma_i)` is covariant under positive unit changes when the margin
and scale transform together. Exact state remains beside it because supported
equality, open, refuted, missing, and invalid cases can collide at zero. A
general downstream payload is separate unless the joint code is sufficient
for that declared consumer. Exact masks, rather than ReLU's zero branch,
authorize selection.

The CPWL theorem is scoped to finite ordinary feed-forward ReLU networks and
global finite continuous piecewise-affine targets under the cited architecture
and depth convention. Restricted-domain targets require an explicit global
CPWL extension. The paper makes no universal-approximation, SGD-recovery,
minimal-width, architecture-superiority, scientific-cell-alignment, or
mechanistic-interpretability claim. The current depth-lower-bound frontier was
not added: it would not sharpen the retained finite existence/depth upper bound
and would invite an efficiency claim the paper does not test.

## 2. Policy/value reconstruction rederivation

Let `pi(x)` be the intended action, `W(x,a)` its ideal score code,
`W_hat(x,a)` the approximation, and

`e(x) = max_a |W_hat(x,a)-W(x,a)|`.

For at least two legal actions, define the intended gap

`g(x) = W(x,pi(x)) - max_(a != pi(x)) W(x,a)`.

If `e(x) <= rho`, every competitor `a` satisfies

`W_hat(x,pi(x)) - W_hat(x,a) >= g(x)-2 rho`.

Thus `g(x)>2 rho` forces strict recovery under the compatible tie rule. Taking
the contrapositive gives the pointwise event inclusion

`{pi_hat != pi} subset {e>rho} union {g<=2 rho}`,

and therefore

`D_mu <= mu(e>rho) + mu(g<=2 rho)`.

The coefficient two is exact for coordinatewise error: at gap `2 rho`,
moving the intended action down by `rho` and a competitor up by `rho` creates a
tie; just below `2 rho` it creates a flip. A source-policy tie has gap zero and
is deliberately charged to the small-gap event even when a fixed tie rule
selects `pi`. At a singleton legal-action state the decoder is forced and the
gap is defined as positive infinity.

An accepted certificate may replace the oracle event masses with validated
upper bounds and combine them under its stated joint coverage rule. An
independent IID holdout estimate supplies a separate Hoeffding disagreement
bound only for the frozen policy/decoder pair and holdout population. Neither
route is a training-set or off-support guarantee.

Coordinatewise radius `rho` induces pairwise gap-error radius `2 rho`. A
conservative decoder that requires the estimated gap's accepted lower bound to
be strictly positive has lower bound at least `g-4 rho`; hence
`g>4 rho` is a generic sufficient condition for both correct recovery and
non-abstention. Equality can still withhold. A directly accepted gap-error
certificate may have a smaller radius, so `4 rho` is not asserted to be
necessary.

The semantic variants remain separate:

- an exact finite encoder and decoder are inverse only on the encoder image;
- greedy decoding of standard `Q^pi` returns the source policy only on the
  self-greedy subset with compatible conventions;
- scalar `V` reconstruction requires a transparent harness containing legal
  actions, dynamics, reward timing, discount/horizon, perspective, terminal
  convention, and tie rule;
- a hidden policy lookup makes scalar sufficiency vacuous;
- modal agreement for a stochastic policy does not establish fidelity to its
  action distribution; and
- trajectory agreement needs a coupling and first-divergence/occupancy
  analysis, not an IID state-error bound.

Consequently the paper retains finite representational existence and a
conditional behavioral bound. It makes no claim about whether arbitrary
policies have true utilities or whether any surrogate recovers one.

## 3. Proper-score information check

The report symbol was changed from `J` to `R` so `J` remains task risk.
With `p_N=P(Y|N)`, `p_(R,N)=P(Y|R,N)`, and issued predictor `q_(R,N)`,

`L_N-L_q = I(R;Y|N) - E KL(p_(R,N) || q_(R,N))`.

Therefore a population improvement of `delta` nats over the true
nuisance-conditioned Bayes predictor implies

`I(R;Y|N) = delta + E KL(...) >= delta`.

Against an arbitrary nuisance comparator, its Bayes regret must be subtracted.
If raw latent tasks `Z` are quotiented by equality of `P(Y|Z,N)` and mediation
`R independent Y | (Z,N)` holds, the same mediation holds through the quotient
`bar Z`; data processing yields

`I(R;bar Z|N) >= I(R;Y|N)`.

This identifies only outcome-visible task distinctions. The misspecified
baseline, direct leakage, duplicated-label, and recursive-copy countermodels
remain adjacent. No factual-ontology or general interpretability conclusion is
drawn.
