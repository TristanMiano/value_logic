## 2. Reconstruction of the load-bearing implications

**CT05-1/2 and CT05-6/8.** Finiteness plus a checked current feasible incumbent
establishes a nonempty minimum family. Every minimizer is in the incumbent's
sublevel. The exhaustive binary tree covers that whole sublevel, not merely
visited candidates or one winning branch. At a reuse leaf, current premises
imply every mapped old premise and the old rank cutoff. The old inequality
then holds at the mapped point; positive scaling and the checked loss correction
supply the common receiving bound. A strict above-cutoff exclusion cannot remove
a tie. Taking a minimum over valid proofs is valid because they all prove the
same action comparison; it does not grant hidden-state action choice.

**CT05-7.** The actual current frame and cell generate nonpositive numerical
rows. Equality contributes both directions. With nonnegative multipliers,
subtracting the row combination before enclosing the residual gives a valid
upper bound on the target after the combination is added back. Boolean OR
implies that its leaf-sum is at least one, but does not imply every disjunct.
No feature independence is used when identical nonlinear terms cancel.
The producer's incomplete elimination heuristic and basis memoization are not
trusted by the receiver. At singleton Boolean cells, actual expression values
are exact; that proves the finite relative-completeness argument within the
stated size limits, not universal cheap proof search.

**CT05-9/10/11.** Exact future outputs induce the usual continuation congruence.
Closure supplies sufficiency; distinguishing words establish separation of
every distinct class. Some acceptable action needs a nonempty *whole*
intersection, not only pairwise compatibility. A relational encoding uses a
closed cover rather than a canonical partition. Its history dependence is
stored in the current finite code; no unread hidden state or free transcript
is supplied at choice time. The three-record two-versus-three example survives
both update directions. The executable's optional edge service preserves the
entire outgoing observation menu. A code count excludes the available update
and output tables and graph-construction cost; it is not a claim that the entire
agent fits in two or three bits.

**CT05-12–14/18.** Compilation is structural induction over a validated finite
pure-program DAG and complete Boolean tables. Live function IDs, copies and
predictor IDs are separately supplied. The receiver reconstructs the paired
loss expression from both programs and the fixed coefficient table. In the
counterpossible front end, support of quoted negation is the negative-support
flag, not Boolean complement of the positive flag. Recompilation from the exact
quoted antecedent, exceptions and frame bridges the inherited P3-04 semantics
to the numerical receiver. Ordinary impossibility of p-and-not-p is retained;
there is no claim of discovering the uniquely correct relevance policy.

**CT05-15/16.** All winners are in G exactly when the minimum outside G is
strictly above the minimum inside G. The exact safe region is a union over
covered incumbents, not necessarily the region of one common incumbent. For
compact W the minimum of the continuous gap is attained, so robust coverage
is equivalent to a positive minimum gap. Exchanging two minima and writing
minus a finite minimum as a maximum yields the stated epigraph LPs. Feasible
primal/dual witnesses with matching objective establish optimality by direct
multiplication of inequalities. This neither trusts solver output nor checks
only vertices of W. Zero-gap outside ties are unsafe. The actual soft-clause
adapter holds source, clauses and loss fixed and requires strictly positive
box lower endpoints; the abstract linear-profile theorem is more general.

**CT05-17/19.** Ordered admissions can use only previously admitted handles.
Thus each new stored domain has a separately verified conditional theorem;
there is no circular endorsement. Separate scalar correction maxima can lose
cancellation even though the chained bound remains valid. For a supplied finite
future request/map family, pointwise portfolio eligibility gives an ordinary
set-cover retention problem. Whole-task completion is not a submodular proxy:
two individually useless supports can jointly complete a task. This is a scoped
obstruction to immediate marginal-gain retention, not a general metareasoning
lower bound or a P3-07 policy.

## 3. Difficulties that changed the work

The initial stronger symbolic premise search timed out in its full regression.
A profile isolated repeated basis construction; version v3 memoizes producer
hints within a cell. The receiver still reconstructs every submitted witness.
The interrupted run, partial diagnostic, old source and current complete runs
remain separate. This is a real computation-cost difficulty, not an unexplained
successful retry. New harnesses save completed units incrementally.

Two wrappers initially imported the same receiver under different module names.
They worked separately but could not share nominal cache/proof types. A saved
integration diagnosis records that rejection. New version-two wrappers use one
shared module identity; class/function AST comparison records that numerical
behavior was not edited. Their full regressions and a cross-front-end example
pass. The first joint harness used a string input reference where the declared
program grammar requires an integer index; that failure and its one-token input
repair are preserved separately.

The rank-profile adapter initially gave the zero-bit assignment an empty state
name, rejected by its own validation. The new version gives the sole assignment
an explicit identity and tests it. The mathematical domain was nonempty; the
failure was an adapter boundary, not an infeasibility proof. The primal/dual
arithmetic and coverage theorem did not change.

Most importantly, an optimized ordinary comparison changes the significance.
Warm memoized algebraic decision diagrams were faster than retained-portfolio
construction/checking in **all six declared resource families** in the saved
local run. In the seven-bit parity family the approximate totals were 69 ms
for reuse, 722 ms for fresh certificate search, and 8 ms for warm ADD reasoning.
Thus the observed savings against the fresh-proof ablation do not establish
an advantage against the stronger ordinary method. Timing is one local
observation per unit, not a statistical or universal performance theorem.
The ADD result and independently checkable certificate are different delivery
services; an ordinary method is nevertheless allowed to supply both.

## 4. Remaining limits are explicit, not postponed defects in a claimed theorem

The frontend uses finite pure Boolean programs and named tables. It does not
handle arbitrary programs, cyclic self-reference, a learned causal graph or a
complete arithmetic model. The hypothesis/support rules, task losses and rank
weights are provided. The exact unit/source bridge is external rational
arithmetic, not a new native phase-two proof compiler. The optional rank-region
adapter enumerates its small source; this cost is explicit, not waived.

Capped search can fail before finding a valid proof. A bad point in a loose
incumbent sublevel need not be a bad actual optimum. A failure of old-domain
coverage alone need not refute the action comparison. An old certificate is a
conditional mathematical statement and remains historical when its current use
becomes invalid. These distinctions appear in the request/status interfaces.

No new anticipation, calibration, expert regret, Logical Induction criterion,
model-plurality benefit, endogenous self-trust, or optimal reasoning-purchase
policy is claimed. BRIA is a named comparison for the later learning tasks,
not a theorem that this static receiver inherits. P3-N01 stays NOT YET SUPPORTED.
The candidate contribution is the concrete finite integration and its exact
selection/information boundaries, assessed against named prior methods—not
worldwide priority and not an ordinary agent denied our finished algorithm.
