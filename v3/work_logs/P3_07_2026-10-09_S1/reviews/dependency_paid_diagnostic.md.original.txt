# P3-07 paid dependency construction and repair diagnostic

Contributor: ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro), implementation subagent, 2026-10-09 UTC.
Classification: separate deterministic development illustration. Agent time is
unmeasured, with zero principal Research90 credit. This is not part of the
modular experiment's statistical implementation coverage, and it is not a
performance win for one decision calculus over an ordinary comparator.

## Outcome

The new `v3/checks/07_dependency_paid_probe.py` executes actual existing P3-05
program compilation and actual P3-04 best-repair search on one supplied
four-bit model. Search distinguishes three materially different outputs:

| Actual frontier pops | Feasibility | Rank established? | Repair identities complete? | Difference report |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0, 1, or 3 | Unknown | No | No | Outer interval `[-1,1]`; no exact image |
| 8 | Nonempty | Yes: rank 3 | No | Witness `1011`, still only outer interval `[-1,1]` |
| 13 | Nonempty | Yes: rank 3 | Yes | Best repairs `1011` and `1100`; exact image `{0,1}`, hull `[0,1]` |

The last call allowed up to 31 total pops; the inherited search stopped after
13 because no frontier work remained. An independent interpreter then checked
all 16 input assignments and verified the source-bound reports. Its results
were not supplied to the search. A source-scope mutation on a separate search
object was rejected with `Changed request requires a fresh search.`

Under the predeclared base tariffs, charging only checking and retention gives
a cost of **0.61339** cash units. Including observed model construction and
repair search gives **1.79639**. For a posted service fee of **1**, incomplete
accounting therefore says pay, while complete accounting of the selected
counters says do not pay. The fee is the buyer's known payment for delivery
of an actually checked complete repair report. It is not a supplied law of
future task benefit or a value-of-information oracle.

## Supplied finite model

Four Boolean inputs are named `a,b,c,d`. Both programs define
`left = a AND b` and `right = c AND d`. Their output calls a supplied two-input
table `fuse(left,right)`. The old table `(0,0,1,1)` returns its first input; the
new table `(0,1,1,1)` is disjunction. Thus:

\[
\operatorname{old}=a\land b,
\qquad
\operatorname{new}=(a\land b)\lor(c\land d).
\]

The hypothetical source requires `a=1` and `new=1`. Soft defaults preserve the
baseline `0000` with positive weights `(1,2,1,1)`, so the scalar repair rank is
`a + 2b + c + d`. Every admissible repair pays the forced cost of `a=1`.
Choosing `b=1` costs two more; choosing both `c=1,d=1` also costs two more.
Their minimum total rank is three, giving precisely `1100` and `1011`.

The requested difference is `new-old`. It is zero at `1100` and one at `1011`.
An exact rank does not identify a unique repair or a unique loss. At eight
pops the search has enough information to establish the best rank, while
remaining work is needed to identify all best repairs and the exact loss
image. This is the concrete reason a partial-search result cannot be sold as
the complete service.

`P3-05 compile_program(new)` constructs the new output expression used by the
hard source. `P3-05 compile_comparison` then constructs the old/new difference
and binds the supplied whole programs, soft defaults, weights, hard source,
scope, and units in its comparison record. This pipeline recompiles the new
program as part of `compile_comparison`; that is the executed API path, not a
claim that such repeated work is unavoidable or optimal. The relevant compiled
support is the supplied `left` and `right` definitions and `fuse` table. The
model and its semantics are supplied input, not automatically discovered
dependencies.

counts are:

The script supplies a named rational cash tariff for **every included
counter**, rather than silently treating these heterogeneous measurements as
identical work. At the base tariff, construction counters and search counters
cost `1/1000` cash units each. Independent checker assignment, raw node, and
table-lookup counters cost `1/1000`; its listed auxiliary counters cost
`1/10000`, except report-binding characters at `1/100000`. Retained record
bytes cost `1/100000`. These are declared illustrative service prices, not
measured hardware prices or an assertion of equal execution times.

Before execution the probe fixed posted fees `{1/4,1/2,1,2,4}` and tariff
multipliers `{1/10,1,10}`. All **15** rows are saved. Exactly one reverses the
pay/no-pay decision when construction and search are included: multiplier
one, fee one. The incompletely accounted net is `+0.38661`; the complete
counted net is `-0.79639`. The other 14 rows are retained, including rows where
both accounting conventions agree. There is no selection of only favorable
prices after inspecting execution.

The checking-only number is an illustration of missing charges, not a
complete ordinary algorithm offered a fair head-to-head comparison. An
ordinary method with the same supplied inputs can exploit the source's simple
Boolean structure, cache compilation, or derive repairs in another way. This
diagnostic does not compare those alternatives or claim an algorithmic lower
bound.

The pay/no-pay labels are retrospective accounting readouts of the measured
deterministic execution at the posted prices. They do not supply a policy
with its future realized computation cost before execution. An executable
ex ante selection rule would need its own justified, paid cost assessment.

## What is and is not bounded

There are four Boolean variables and at most 31 nodes in their full binary
assignment tree. This bounds frontier pops for this fixed source. Each pop can
perform different amounts of expression evaluation, and `report()` performs
additional work over the frontier and witnesses. Therefore the 31-pop bound
is **not** a total CPU, memory, or response-resource bound for inherited code.
The source files themselves expressly disclaim such a bound.

The recorded costs also omit some real work. The finite semantic model and
table contents are supplied; acquisition or design of those semantics is not
measured. Inherited counters do not include every repeated validation,
canonicalization, request-record reconstruction, Fraction operation, Python
control step, allocation, or arithmetic-bit cost. The direct checker records
the explicit operations shown in its vector, not every interpreter overhead.
Retained bytes are the chosen protocol records, not physical RAM or Python
heap size. The full omission list is saved in both the prospective plan and
results. These limits must accompany any economic use of the numbers.

The modeled complete cost is complete only for the **selected priced
counters**. If omitted operations also carry nonnegative charges, adding them
can increase that cost. It cannot rescue the negative counted net in the
reported fee-one case. No claim is made that the service is profitable at
prices where these incomplete physical measurements show a positive net.
