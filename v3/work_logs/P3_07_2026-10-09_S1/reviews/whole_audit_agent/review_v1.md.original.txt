## Whole-profile scope limitation

`07_whole_procedure_audit.py` constructs the profile scope by taking `D.actual_scope(source_hashes)` and then replacing its law with the public law name and only the whole-wrapper source hash. The replacement removes the driver hash that `actual_scope()` had included. The driver defines the population, query conversion, row evaluation and price vector used by the wrapper.

The saved probe changes only the driver-hash entry of the source registry, leaving all files untouched. The driver's own scope changes; the whole-profile scope remains equal. A profile validated solely under that scope cannot distinguish the changed driver.

The v1 global closure records all four hashes and each profile acquisition checks the live registry against them. All four recorded source hashes matched their retained copies. Thus the saved v1 result remains identifiable under its global closure. A joint generator digest of both driver and wrapper is a suitable repair for future reusable profile scopes.
