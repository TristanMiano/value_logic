# Acquisition-loss scope addendum to manuscript integration review v1

The root raised this accounting interpretation while the final integration report was being saved. This addendum preserves the earlier report and numeric output and records the additional scope check against the same two frozen manuscript snapshots. It is a same-model, targeted, nonblind development check with zero principal Research90 credit.

## Interpretation supported by the evidence

Profile and audit requests in the retained arithmetic experiments are **offline mathematical evaluations**. Their recorded false-positive, false-negative and fallback features describe what a candidate policy would do on deployment. They do not enact additional externally staked decisions while the profile or audit is being acquired. The computation, checking, evidence acquisition, numerical construction, assessment and retention needed to obtain those records are paid under the declared resource tariff. Accordingly, charging those hypothetical task-feature values again as additional acquisition losses would change the declared experiment rather than repair its current arithmetic.

The frozen main §3 asks the reader to run every retained complete policy on a sampled request, and §6.3 calls the rebuilt-episode costs all-in. Neither frozen manuscript explicitly spells out the offline interpretation above. I found no explicit claim that these evaluations are live externally consequential acquisitions, but the distinction is material enough to state directly near the acquisition protocol or tariff definition.

## Recommended scope sentence

> Profile and audit evaluations here are offline: their FP/FN/fallback features estimate deployment loss, while their computation, checking and retention resources are charged acquisition expenses. If acquiring an observation itself incurs real task loss, that loss must also enter setup or the whole-episode account.

For the general root comparison, such actual acquisition losses belong in the incurred setup cost \(S\), or in the corresponding complete acquisition-and-deployment episode outcome. The existing abstraction already permits this, but the empirical scope should be explicit. No live-acquisition guarantee follows from the current offline evidence.

This is an accounting-scope clarification, not an additional numerical defect under the declared offline interpretation. It requires no numerical rerun. The two correction findings remain those in [integration_review_v1.md](integration_review_v1.md): the separately reported multi-action setup fee, and the validity/evaluation requirements of the general credal-set extension.
