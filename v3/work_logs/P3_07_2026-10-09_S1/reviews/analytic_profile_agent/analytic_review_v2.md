## Independent class-label proof

For each admitted odd prime `p`, put `n=(p-1)/2`. Multiplication by a nonzero residue `a` permutes the `p-1` nonzero residues. Multiplying this permutation identity and cancelling their nonzero product gives `a^(p-1)=1` in the field. Therefore `(a^n)^2=1`, so `a^n` is either `1` or `-1`.

The two nonzero degree-`n` polynomials `T^n-1` and `T^n+1` have at most `n` roots each, by the factor theorem and induction on degree. Their root sets are disjoint because the field has odd characteristic. Together they contain all `2n` nonzero residues, so each has exactly `n` roots. Thus exactly half of the nonzero residues have the positive query label.

The residue `a=1` is always positive. The residue `a=p-1` is positive exactly when `n` is even. Removing them gives the ordinary-class formula used by the comparator:

```math
N_+=n-1-\mathbf 1[n\text{ even}],\qquad
N_-=n-1+\mathbf 1[n\text{ even}].
```

The delegated reviewer independently proved the same count using the two-to-one square map and the degree bound, providing a different route to the same conclusion.
