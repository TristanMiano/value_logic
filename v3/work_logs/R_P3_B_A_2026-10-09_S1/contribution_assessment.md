its particular assumptions. The supplied constructive repair and metered
implementation answer the selected question at a useful restricted scope.

The recurrence establishes no priority for those methods or a superior
regret rate. An ordinary program may implement this entire service unchanged.

## Why the negative results do not disappear from the judgment

At the recorded `T=3968,B=8` comparison, the uniform v1.1 exact-state learner
spent 2,935,898 units and its 16-bit-state counterpart spent 1,039,110, a reduction
of about 64.6%. Their maximum stored weights used 1,342 and 18 bits respectively.
This is a representation improvement **within this learner**, not a claim that
all intermediates fit 18 bits or that the learner is the best service.
The v1.2 registry-only transfer adds 26 units; it is not a rerun.
[Complete comparison and provenance](development/service_comparison/analysis.md).

The exact table spent 171,880 units with zero terminal errors on that horizon.
The normalized uniform learner made 1,702 errors, and the adaptive learner made
1,754 errors while spending 1,382,703 units. The source argument is stronger
than a seed comparison: for the current uniform closure, its successful cold
resource bill exceeds the table's by at least

```math
4908+11T+R_{\rm purchases}.
```

The adaptive closure has 8,809 in place of 4,908. The table is funded throughout
the admitted horizon. For a common nonnegative primitive price and terminal
error price, this is a deployment obstruction for the particular admitted
terminal-answer service. Different category prices and independent certificate
delivery are separate comparison contracts. Setup-free thresholds retain the
[two explicitly different registry scopes](development/service_comparison/setup_scope_erratum_2026-10-09.json).

The [analytic null diagnostic](development/allocation_comparison/analysis.md)
also shows that all six adaptive/matching-uniform issued Brier scores exceed
the exact `T/4` score of `q=1/2`. A lower adaptive score relative to uniform
therefore does not show good forecasting. Correcting bought actions can
improve terminal decisions while an issued forecast remains poor. The
observable certificate makes such performance questions available to a
learner without granting it all answers; it does not reverse the unfavorable
ordinary comparison.

The negative result alone would not establish the contribution. It matters
here because the new finite guarantee and implementation identify exactly
which economic inference fails, and provide a stronger alternative instead
of withholding the easy mathematical shortcut. Conversely, equal outputs
from an ordinary implementation do not disqualify the explicit synthesis
under the author's accepted criterion.
