## 2. What is inherited, and what the recurrence actually adds

Product weights and its first/second-order potential argument are established:
Cesa-Bianchi, Mansour and Stoltz, *Improved second-order bounds for prediction
with expert advice* (2007), [§3, Lemmas 1–2, pp. 327–328](https://cesa-bianchi.di.unimi.it/Pubblicazioni/J28.pdf).
The core's inequality is an explicit specialization of that method. Neither
rational weights nor a familiar potential inequality supports method priority.

Purchasing the current best action and improving regret under a query budget
are also established: Russo et al., *Online Learning with Sublinear Best-Action
Queries* (NeurIPS 2024), [§1.1 and §2.2](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/47795c4ae2f7d07ea2fb0d11fa2c3c90-Paper-Conference.pdf).
The uniform core's large-budget rate has the same ordinary scale. Its exact
one-per-block construction is a checked finite adaptation, not evidence that
the capability or rate first appears in this project. The restricted loss-table
mapping and lower-bound non-transfer are detailed in
[source_scope_supplement.md](source_scope_supplement.md).

Disagreement-guided selective sampling and propensity weighting are ordinary
as well: Castro, Hellström and van Erven, *Adaptive Selective Sampling for
Online Prediction with Experts* (NeurIPS 2023),
[§§2, 4–5](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/00b67df24009747e8bbed4c2c6f9c825-Paper-Conference.pdf).
Its per-round Bernoulli acquisition and post-prediction loss contract differ
from the exact-quota, purchase-corrected target here. That distinction requires
the local derivation; it does not establish novelty of adaptive sampling.

The recurrence's useful addition is the complete restricted bridge. The
[main derivation, §§2–6](../../../../derivations/07_selective_feedback.md)
identifies what is scored, when labels become available, why the terminal
sampling factor is `B−1`, why the all-issued forecast factor remains `B`,
and which rounding and funding terms survive. The
[adaptive review](adaptive_quota_review.md) independently checks
`X_i=(1/pi_J−1)ell[J,i]`, the probability-floor condition and the finite update.
Simply copying an expected-query theorem, clipping its query count, using its
bound for a purchased correction, or dropping the new propensity factor would
not establish this contract.

The exact failure witness makes that interface consequential. With 64
four-position blocks, the naive update has expected regret above 64.261798,
although the asserted allowance is below 34.3; the correct update has negative
regret on the same tape. The target was specified before its exact calculation.
This is evidence that a concrete tempting interface is false, with a proved
repair. It is not a profitable service benchmark. See the
[target](adaptive_propensity_witness_target_v1.json) and
[exact result](adaptive_propensity_witness_result_v1.json).

This is why I recommend narrow support instead of treating the recurrence as
only a list of standard components. Its implementation boundary changes and a
previously missing guarantee becomes available. The economic obstruction then
determines where that guarantee should not be mistaken for useful deployment.
The negative comparison alone would not establish a contribution; nor would
the ability of an ordinary program to reproduce the same useful integration
disqualify it under the author's criterion.

## 3. What the ordinary comparisons actually establish

The [service comparison](../../development/service_comparison/analysis.md)
reports 20 prospectively specified learner arms and eight ordinary control
runs. The mathematical tape and seeds are development cases, not IID
replications, confidence intervals or a final-policy selection procedure.
The equivalent ordinary blocked learner was identified as an equivalence,
not rerun under another name as supposedly independent evidence.

### 3.1 Representation cost improved; mathematical forecasting did not win

At `T=3968,B=8`, the observed v1.1 exact and 16-bit-state resource bills were
2,935,898 and 1,039,110 units, a reduction of about 64.6%. The retained weight
maximum changed from 1,342 to 18 bits. These are implemented representation
measurements, not Python heap measurements or a claim that every transient
fits in 18 bits. The sampler has its separately declared allowance.
The theorem gives an expert-relative rounding allowance; it does not promise
pointwise proximity to the exact-state trajectory. At `B=4`, for example, the
normalized trace made 29 more terminal errors. These facts and their version
scope are recorded in the [comparison's resource section](../../development/service_comparison/analysis.md).

### 3.2 The strongest exact table dominates this learner under the declared tariff

Every executed exact control made zero errors. At `T=3968`, the ordinary
quadratic-residue table cost 171,880 units including its source registry,
construction and lookups. The `B=8,s=16` learner made 1,702 terminal errors.
The [current-registry transfer](../../development/service_comparison/current_registry_reprice_v1.json)
adds exactly 26 source-procurement units to the learner's observed successful
v1.1 path, giving 1,039,136 units for the current v1.2 closure. It does not
relabel the run as a v1.2 measurement or infer a new physical runtime.

At task price 1 and primitive-unit price `1/1000`, that current learner's
realized all-in total is **2741.136**, versus **171.880** for the table.
At higher nonnegative task prices the table's lack of errors remains decisive
for this comparison. These figures are not a positive break-even example for
selective learning.

More importantly, the reported source argument extends beyond the chosen
seeds. Expert evaluation plus the learner's close operation costs 11 units
more per request than table lookup, before all other learner work and
purchases. For the observed closure the cold difference is bounded below by
`4882+11T+R_purchases`. **Applying only the verified 26-unit registry transfer**
gives the current-closure version

```math
R_{\rm learner}-R_{\rm table}\ge4908+11T+R_{\rm purchases}.
```

The ongoing `11T+R_purchases` difference remains when learner setup is granted
for free. At every legal equal-block horizon at least 148, `11T` alone pays
the table's 1,611-unit construction bill. This is a consequential rejection
of this particular learning deployment, not just an unfavorable seed.
It relies on the admitted service, successful executions, source-bound
implementations and common primitive-unit tariff. It is not a lower bound
on every selective learner, every category-specific price vector, every
certificate-delivery service or physical hardware. The
[comparison](../../development/service_comparison/analysis.md) states those
boundaries explicitly.

### 3.3 Paid correction must be separated from learning value

On the saved normalized `B=8` selector path, substituting the fixed lower-half
expert's unbought actions gives 1,625 errors, compared with the learner's 1,702,
while retaining the full executed bill. This is a counterfactual action
substitution, not an independently deployed optimized baseline and not a
retrospective right to select an expert. It shows why beating an uncorrected
expert's full-tape loss would not isolate value added by learning.
The [same analysis](../../development/service_comparison/analysis.md) also
separates realized terminal loss, its conditional action expectation and the
immutable Brier score. None is interchangeable with the others.

The nonempty abstract sufficient gain region in the main derivation remains
mathematically useful, but the tested Euler service does not occupy a supported
positive region against its strongest exact control. The adaptive selector
currently has a proof and exact algebraic witness; these data do not establish
its service-level economic performance.

### 8.1 The two setup scenarios have different sufficient horizons

```math
R_{\rm learner,ongoing}-R_{\rm table,lookups}
\ge 11T+R_{\rm purchases}.
```

It must be translated to a complete bill using the specified setup treatment:

| Setup treatment | Table cost still to cover | Horizon sufficient using `11T` alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both source registries are excluded, or source procurement is treated as shared/sunk; learner setup is waived | Construction: 1,611 units | `ceil(1611/11)=147`; first possible legal equal-block horizon 148 |

### 8.2 A half-probability forecast is a stronger analytic score comparison
