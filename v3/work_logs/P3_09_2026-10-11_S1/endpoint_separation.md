# What a positive endpoint would and would not show

Contributor: **ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro)**. PREPARATION review, with no final
population construction or execution. These explicit finite witnesses test the
interpretation rules rather than add a new confirmatory endpoint.

## 1. Better Brier loss can coexist with worse decisions

Take two binary outcomes `(1,0)` and equal error costs. Method A issues forecasts
`(3/5,2/5)` and takes the deterministic minimum-expected-error actions `(1,0)`.
Its mean Brier loss is `4/25`, and its mean realized action loss is zero.
Method B issues `(1,51/100)` and takes actions `(1,1)`. Its mean Brier loss is
`2601/20000`, while its mean action loss is `1/2`.

Thus B improves the mean Brier endpoint by `599/20000 = 0.02995`, exceeding the
prospective 0.01 learning cutoff, while worsening decisions. There is no
contradiction: proper scoring concerns the forecast loss, and a finite realized
cohort does not give uniform action-cost error control. This is why the frozen
analysis reports A2 and A3 separately. The example is a deterministic decision
readout; it does not purport to reproduce the broker's randomized action rule.

The elementary decision bridge requires a different premise. If every action's
estimated cost differs from its true cost by at most epsilon in common units,
then the chosen estimated minimizer has true excess cost at most `2 epsilon`:
true cost at the chosen action is at most its estimated cost plus epsilon, that
estimate is at most the estimate at a true minimizer, and the latter is at most
the true minimum plus epsilon. A mean Brier difference is not that uniform
premise and supplies neither the same bound nor a learning rate.

## 2. Marginal refinement does not supply joint coherence

Consider abstract Boolean propositions A and B with an actually represented
constraint `A implies B`. Marginals `p(A)=3/4` and `p(B)=1/4` cannot belong to a
probability law supported on the admissible assignments: the event A is a subset
of B, so every such law has `p(A)<=p(B)`. A scalar predictor and a cache that harden
only individually checked coordinates need not enforce that joint constraint.

This is a witness about a claimed joint-probability service, not an assertion
that the final broker produces these particular values. The challenge therefore
retains U03's represented-fragment semantics and U04's current-coordinate
interface separately. Exact receipt reuse, correct answers on selected CNFs,
and lower finite Brier loss do not establish an unimplemented global projection
or logical-induction guarantee. The separate certificate service can prove a
current bound inside its supplied structural sublevel without becoming such a
probability state.

## 3. Equal service and total cost are separate coordinates

For fixed request universe `{r1,r2}`, method A may deliver only `{r1}` at cost90
and B only `{r2}` at cost100. Equal success counts and a ten-percent smaller bill
do not give A coverage dominance: neither delivered set contains the other.
If both deliver the same set, all failed attempts still belong in their bills.
If an outer interrupted prefix has unknown cost, even a cheaper sealed
replacement cannot establish the full attempted-run saving. The frozen analysis
keeps the completed-path calculation while withholding that end-to-end claim.

Likewise, a total-tariff win is not a win at every primitive resource price.
Vectors `(2,0)` and `(0,3)` have totals2 and 3 under unit prices, but prices `(2,1)`
reverse the order to4 and 3. Componentwise dominance would survive every
nonnegative reweighting; total dominance at one chosen tariff does not. Output,
storage and source diagnostics also overlap the producer/consumer partition,
so adding those five diagnostic columns would double-count work. The accepted
analyzer keeps the original total and a separate disjoint primitive vector.

## 4. A report's width is not a value-of-computation theorem

A paid report can be valid under its stated assumptions yet add no narrowing to
its own deterministic observable envelope. The current-source preparation
records have seven successful reports and all 21 V/F/Z width reductions are zero.
Their fees remain paid. This is a DEVELOPMENT result and no basis, grid, horizon,
threshold, seed or final method was selected in response.

Even a narrower interval would not by itself establish that procuring it was
worth its fee for an unspecified downstream decision. That requires the relevant
decision loss and what changes after receiving the interval. The frozen A5
endpoint is the explicitly stated report service: signed normalized endpoint
changes and an independent fee. The terminal-action objective does not silently
charge or benefit from an optional report it did not request for that action.

The inherited fixed-end confidence statements also keep the fair-bit and
all-path-funding premises. An interface eligibility flag or one successful seeded
execution is not a coverage experiment. Different methods and episodes do not
receive simultaneous confidence from a per-episode statement without a new
allocation, and unsuccessful or conflicting reports do not disappear from the
configuration denominator.

## 5. Decisions can be identified while the underlying law is not

For a binary state with probability p of state1, the exact expected zero-one
losses of actions0 and 1 are `(p,1-p)`. In known units this pair identifies p.
With a common unknown additive offset b, its difference still identifies p:
`p=(L0-L1+1)/2`. With a common unknown positive scale a and no offset, normalization
identifies p: `p=L0/(L0+L1)`. The two nuisance conventions therefore do not lose
the same information as their combination.

With a common unknown positive affine transformation, take p=`3/4`, a=1, b=0.
The observed pair is `(3/4,1/4)`. The different law p=`2/3`, a=`3/2`, b=`-1/4`
produces exactly the same observed pair. Both scales are positive and both
probabilities belong to the simplex. The observed losses cannot select between
the two laws when the common offset is unrestricted. An independently supplied
offset restriction would be a different observation contract and must not be
silently added to this witness.

Nevertheless, action1 is the unique minimum in both examples. Positive common
affine transformations preserve every action ordering because the difference
is multiplied by a positive constant. Thus exact decision recovery and exact
probability recovery are different services even for the complete two-action
loss vector. This is why Q5's calibration conventions, requested target and
collision/recovery certificates stay explicit; a non-identification certificate
can be a successful conformance result rather than an algorithmic failure.

These are expected losses under supplied coherent laws. A single realized loss,
or an arbitrary learned estimate, is not that observation type. Nor does the
binary example supply a decoder for arbitrary action-loss matrices, restricted
source sets, unknown non-shared scales, or an unrepresented law. No information
audit endpoint or final case constructor is called to make this witness.

## 6. An identical ordinary kernel bounds any superiority claim

Fix a complete candidate configuration c and a normalized nonnegative resource
price lambda. Let its objective be `Jc = Wc + lambda*Uc/T`. Its identical ordinary
implementation has the same forecasts, actions, feedback and invoice, hence
exactly the same Jc. Consequently, the minimum over an ordinary catalogue that
includes this implementation is at most Jc at every price. Its ordinary-minus-
candidate difference cannot be positive, much less exceed the 0.01 practical
cutoff. This follows pathwise and survives the prescribed configuration means;
it is not an empirical prediction about a chosen seed.

The distinct ordinary catalogue remains useful for comparing particular paid
designs. The complete ordinary catalogue, including identical kernels, answers
a broader question and retains the exact alias. The analyzer publishes both
catalogues rather than converting the distinct-catalogue comparison into an
exclusive advantage over ordinary probability, decision theory and computation.
If one required configuration lacks complete service or a necessary bill,
the corresponding complete-catalogue minimum remains unavailable; a success-only
minimum is not substituted.

This is compatible with the author's accepted modest-synthesis criterion.
Implementation identity excludes a particular kind of superiority claim; it
does not erase a separately justified interface, formal adaptation, composition
result, or application. Such a contribution still needs its own named delta and
closest comparison. Conversely, an elapsed research floor or a negative
superiority result alone cannot establish that contribution. P3-09 preserves
the prior contribution assessment and leaves the final interpretation and
adversarial reconstruction to their selected later tasks.

These witnesses strengthen the interpretation boundary without changing any
frozen code, recipe, numerical criterion, or contribution status.
