## Narrow pre-freeze amendment

The original adapter snapshot, its seven successful development checks and the
source hash `e39d14770b8af6bd615c4479d84510d0e1285de43eeae013cf124c06f56e4a3e`
remain under [conformance_v1](../conformance_v1/). Review identified a disposition
gap: a semantically invalid delivered certificate would raise out of the scorer
and resemble an orchestration exception, even though the proper scientific
outcome is a failed validation.

The principal authorized a narrow amendment. Named information semantic
rejections and `InvalidCertificate`, and the legacy complete-reply checker's
declared `ValueError`, now produce a completed score with `status='FAIL'`,
`validity_pass=False`, its diagnostic and `retry_eligible=False`. The original
public record and, for structural work, its complete invoice are retained.
Received but invalid payloads do not count as valid delivered warrants.

Outer unit/result/input hash mismatches still raise before the semantic catch.
Unexpected runtime exceptions are not swallowed as scientific negatives. Thus
an adverse mathematical check is not an unchanged-retry trigger; a genuine
execution or integrity problem remains distinguishable.
