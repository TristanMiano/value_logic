## 1. Recommendation

Freeze **three separate service tracks**: paid numerical forecasts and terminal
actions; current checked structural bounds; and exact probability-information
certificates. Within the second track, distinguish the supplied-witness bound
service from complete repair/optimal-action semantics. Answer the five author
questions through these services, inherited theorem checks and explicit open
obligations. Do not require five performance victories.

The central economic comparison must retain the strongest ordinary controls,
including the identical ordinary kernel. Exact equivalence with that kernel is
a positive interface result and prevents an exclusive advantage claim over all
ordinary implementations. Improvements over a named ablation can still be useful.
They must not be promoted to a notation-level or strongest-ordinary advantage.
The prior outcome is adverse enough that ordinary wins, exact ties, costly reuse
and failed delivery should be planned outcomes of the challenge, not reasons to
change its population. [R1–R4]

I recommend **exact finite-panel descriptive primary analysis**, without
significance claims about an unspecified population. The challenge will have a
prospectively chosen finite target, not a sample-size calculation secretly based
on the development wins. Fixed seeded runs do not make deterministic mathematical
answers stochastic; repeated methods or coupled variants are not independent
trials. Algorithmic expectation and confidence theorems keep their own random-bit
and feedback assumptions. [R2, R4]

The proposed practical cutoffs below are prospective operational conventions:
one hundredth of normalized loss per request, and five percent of the complete
declared resource bill at equal service. They are useful magnitudes to freeze,
not theorem constants, power claims or application-derived utility estimates.
The principal can adopt different numbers **before any final exposure**, with
the reason recorded. All exact effect sizes should still be reported.

Keep the terminal-answer service distinct from the optional confidence-report
service. Every method receives the same public input and access to the same

ordinary roster should include both no-computation endpoints, complete

the ordinary combined controller, and the identical-kernel interpretation.

**Pairing is by public episode, configuration and initial random-source slot.**

### 3.2 Exact endpoints and proposed hypotheses

immutable forecast issued before the current purchase, the terminal action at

Let `S>0` be the declared upper bound on raw task loss. It is fixed before the
decision and common to the compared methods. Normalize raw loss and its resource
price by the same `S`; never rescale task loss alone after running a policy.

```math
F_e=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(q_t-y_t)^2,\qquad
W_e=\frac{1}{TS}\sum_{t=1}^{T}\ell_t(a_t,y_t),\qquad
J_e(\lambda)=W_e+\frac{\lambda R_e}{T}.
```

Here `lambda` is already expressed in normalized-loss units and `R_e` includes
the billed work for the specified service. Use exact rational arithmetic for
scores, comparisons and ties. Display both local and installed-source-inclusive
bills; use the latter for the declared fresh owned-episode comparison. If
retention spans several requests, charge setup once at that owned episode's

### 3.3 Missing forecasts, terminal failures and adverse prices

Use **all prespecified requests** as the action denominator. If the contract
already specifies and funds a fallback, score that actual fallback. Otherwise
record no terminal answer and assign the prespecified maximum normalized task
loss of one for the decision-service endpoint, while retaining the separate
failure flag. This is an evaluation penalty for unavailable service, not an
assertion that an unresolved mathematical answer is false. Retain all paid work
before failure. A stopped episode leaves its remaining request IDs explicitly
unserved; deleting the suffix is not an acceptable denominator change.

A missing forecast has no Brier score. Do not fill it with truth, zero or a
retrospective half forecast. Record the complete-cohort Brier total's sharp
bounded-data range by assigning each missing Brier contribution the range
`[0,1]`; primary point-valued forecast comparison is incomplete. A claimed gain
is robust only if it survives the corresponding worst-case comparison. Report
delivered-only descriptive scores with their denominators as supplementary
records, not replacements for the planned cohort.

For native-price comparisons, each price-sensitive policy must execute at the
declared price. A later exact line `loss + lambda * bill` describes a **frozen
path**. It does not reveal what the same controller would have acquired at that
different price. Positive/zero/extreme-price fixed-action controls should all be
retained. Do not assume that raising price lowers bills or that more logical
cache hits imply lower total cost. [R4 §4]

## 5. Track C: exact information recovery, ambiguity and repair

The existing P3-02 audit and independent verifier provide a narrow ready-made
service: rational statewise loss rows over the full simplex, one supplied law,
and one of four shared nuisance conventions—known units, common unknown offset,
common positive scale, or common positive affine transformation. Each target is
certified by a row identity/normalizer or refuted as exactly recoverable by an
explicit pair of admissible laws with the same observed values and different
targets. The returned repair count concerns freely chosen fixed linear queries
under that same semantic convention. [R6 §§2–4, R7]

The exact proposed endpoints are:

1. **C0 — positive certificate:** zero rational residual in every claimed row
   identity; valid common positive denominator where used; reconstructed target
   agrees with every admitted observation fiber under the theorem's scope.
2. **C1 — obstruction:** both witness laws are normalized and admissible, their
   actual observations coincide exactly, and the requested target differs.
3. **C2 — repaired service:** returned additional rows have the claimed count;
   independent certificate checks establish target recovery and the lower-bound
   pairing. A full-law request and a smaller task-target request remain distinct.
4. **C3 — boundary classification:** invalid input, incompatible observations,
   missing nuisance premises, realized-score misuse and unknown unrelated stakes
   are not silently interpreted as exact expectations under the supported model.

These are exact conformance and separation endpoints. The practical threshold is
exact correctness, not a percentage win. Ordinary probability methods may use
the same linear algebra and certificates. There is no paid economic comparison
unless a common explicit resource service is newly prepared and frozen.
Likewise, the current audit does not implement restricted-source polytopes,
arbitrary noisy estimates, acquisition, conditional-event denominators or
decision-only minimal repair; those broader derivation results should remain
theorem-scope statements unless separately implemented. [R6–R7]

One concrete preparation issue needs an explicit disposition. The inherited
audit emits an inner proof object with `stage='development'`, and its verifier
requires exactly that format. A final conformance harness should preserve that
unchanged inner format and wrap it in a separately versioned final exposure
record, with a statement that the algorithm is inherited but these inputs are
final. Alternatively version both proof-format endpoints prospectively. Do not
rewrite an inner stage after execution or label an old fixture replay a new
held-out challenge. [R7: `audit`, `verify`]

of favorable examples appears. Use equal episode weights within each declared
stratum and report each stratum separately before any predeclared pooled mean.
This review deliberately does not select, inspect or disclose final seeds.

These are reproducibility, invariance or reuse controls. |
| Checked structural delivery | Threshold/factorization identities or disjoint-selector multiplexer compositions instead of the exposed parity-fold and constant families; different allowed rank/edit compositions; fixed alternative orders included for every method. | A larger or renamed parity instance alone is size or metamorphic testing; a hand-favorable order selected after observing cost is tuning. |
| Information recovery | Newly specified block/composite measurement layouts and target subspaces under the same supported nuisance contracts, including both identifiable targets and constructive collisions. | Permuting states or rescaling already known exact fixtures is a semantic invariance test. Generic row-rank conformance is not evidence of probability learning or transfer to unknown causal structure. |

The word *transfer* must name what changes and what remains assumed. Final cases
can be unseen while remaining in the same generator family; call that held-out
within-family evaluation. An operation outside an accepted theorem's premises
may be an exploratory stress test, but cannot be counted as a transfer of that
theorem. In particular, endogenous acquisition delays do not inherit the frozen
exogenous selective-feedback guarantee. [R2 §§5,8; R3–R4]

Reserve explicit null/negative controls with fixed denominators:

- **Cheap exact and no-reuse CNF:** give ordinary shortcuts and cases where cache
  overhead need not pay. Keep both fixed answers and a constant-half/frozen
  readout. Do not withhold syntactic shortcuts from ordinary methods.
- **Exact coupling and unit scaling:** a renamed request with the same complete
  key may reuse a checked answer; a source change may not. Known positive common
  rescaling of losses and resource price must preserve the declared decisions.
- **Current-evidence rejection:** stale versions, mismatched query/provider IDs,
  conflicts, denied resources and rejected receipts must produce their typed
  statuses. Do not insert false hard labels and call resulting forecasts a valid
  soundness experiment.
- **Structural nulls:** a constant/easy bound, a single sufficient old domain,
  and ties/empty-domain controls distinguish useful reuse from unnecessary proof
  machinery. A negative requested bound must not pass through a vacuous or
  incumbent-only check.
- **Information nulls:** constant targets need no informative measurement;
  degenerate equal stakes carry no event-probability information. Same-summary
  witnesses with different targets test non-identification. Shared positive
  nuisance assumptions must not be replaced by independent per-row ones.

These are prospective recipe categories, not generated final examples. Every
null is reported whether it favors the candidate or ordinary method.

## 7. Pairing, ties, denominators and multiplicity

### Fixed configurations and complete cohorts

For a stratum with `E` planned episodes, average a fixed configuration's exact
per-episode endpoint over all `E` episodes using the frozen weights. A retrospective
best fixed ordinary catalogue reference is then the minimum of those complete
configuration means. It is **not** the mean of per-episode or per-seed minima.
Retain every tied minimizer. This oracle catalogue summary is not a deployable
policy; compare the explicit ordinary combined controller separately. [R2 §8,
R4 §4 and balanced-mean analysis]

Deterministic controls run once per distinct public input/price/service
configuration. Their exact record may be reused when pairing against random
replicas, but such reuse does not increase the number of independent observations
or create multiple executions. Identical-kernel aliases and exact coupled
mathematical paths receive their own invoice/implementation fields without being
counted as additional independent forecast evidence.

An episode or retained stream is the natural comparison unit for adaptive
policies. Its requests affect later purchases and caches, so a requestwise table
is a useful decomposition, not an IID sampling analysis. State whether the
primary mean weights episodes equally or weights requests equally; fixed common
horizons make these coincide. Keep every planned stratum visible even if a
pooled mean reverses one of its conclusions.

### Exact ties and unavailable results

Use rational equality for action thresholds, equal ranks, equal bounds and cost
crossings. Freeze action tie policies before execution. In structural semantics,
an output must preserve all minimizing consequences unless the receiving service
explicitly requests an announced policy-selected one. Arbitrary tie breaking
must not be disguised as unique identification. [R2 §§6–7]

Report denominators as counts of planned requests, executed attempts, delivered
records, valid current warrants, selected logical receipts, successful physical
provider calls, distinct semantic keys and completed episodes. They answer
different questions. Confidence-report counts are not numbers of independent
experiments. If the private evaluator cannot resolve an admitted final case,
mark that scoring panel incomplete rather than silently dropping its methods.

### Multiplicity and claims

The primary recommendation is an exact descriptive finite challenge, so no
unjustified null-hypothesis test, standard error or population confidence level
is attached to its win counts. Predeclare the primary columns and publish every
comparison, including unfavorable strata and named endpoints. Secondary
ablations, fixed-record envelopes and mechanism decompositions remain explicitly
secondary; finding a favorable one cannot rescue a failed primary endpoint.

The reporting theorem's within-episode joint event is not a simultaneous
ninety-five-percent claim across every method, episode, basis and transfer arm.
If the principal wants that latter claim, its error allocation and executable
reporter must be prepared before final exposure. The existing predeclared rate
grid addresses only the selections its theorem actually budgets. Do not apply
an unproved global confidence label to the finite results table. [R4 §3]

If a population-level inferential claim is later desired, specify the random
episode-generating law, independent replication unit, bounded endpoint,
sampling/analysis rule and multiplicity before generation. That would be an
additional design choice, not a consequence of supplying integer seed labels.
It is unnecessary for the scoped final finite conformance and economic results
proposed here.

## 8. Preparation, exposure and retry mechanics

The accepted protocol requires every tuned method and selected representation,
including transfer and secondary arms, to be prepared, saved, hashed and
validated before **any** final population is generated or revealed. Existing
development sources may be reused, but their actual dependencies, imports,
method-specific advice, tariff, source charge, environment and evaluator access
must be explicit. A generator which constructs inputs during module import is
itself an exposure hazard; preparation validation must not import it in a way
that creates final cases. [R1 §5]

Use a complete episode/retained stream as the scientific unit, with a paid,
versioned persistent-state boundary. Per-request completion records can support
resumption only when the complete corresponding state/RNG history has a frozen
restoration contract. Specify the expected unit-ID list and counts independently
of results. A final integrity readback should verify every expected completed
row and referenced blob against hashes and counts; a summary claiming thirty
rows does not supply a missing thirtieth record.

The prospective stage machine should distinguish:

1. **PREPARATION:** source/configuration/environment validation and explicitly
   separate development-only probes; no final inputs.
2. **FROZEN, UNEXPOSED:** all methods, representations, schedules, counts,
   hypotheses, prices, output locations and analysis are hash-bound. P3-09 stops
   here. The freeze must not automatically launch P3-10.
3. **EXPOSED PUBLIC EXECUTION:** only after later authorization; append an
   exposure record before generation, then produce the final public policies'
   records without evaluator truth access. Paid providers may compute answers
   through the declared method API.
4. **SEALED SCORING:** verify complete public records and source identities
   before the separate truth evaluator and fixed analyzer read them.
5. **INCOMPLETE, RETRY or AMENDED:** explicit preserved state, never an erased
   run with a new seed or favorable stopping point.

Each frozen stage permits at most **one unchanged retry for an unexplained
failure**. Intentional resource exhaustion, refusal of an invalid request, a
mathematically adverse result and an uncovered probabilistic interval are
scientific outcomes, not retry triggers. A deterministic code defect requires a
versioned amendment and an explicit account of which inputs/results were already
exposed. [R1 §5]

The frozen output root, unit IDs, population, seeds, counts, sources and settings
must remain unchanged for a retry. Preserve complete units and failed prefixes;
the implementation needs append-only attempt records or a predeclared immutable
attempt-history mechanism **inside the same frozen stage location**, rather
than changing `--out` to get another attempt. If the current worker rejects any
existing output root, a preparation-stage resume wrapper is needed. Do not
relax this rule after final data appear.

Where a whole owned episode must restart because no valid state checkpoint
exists, label that attempt and preserve all prior observations and bills. Freeze
how the single resumed/retried scientific record is selected and how integrity
is checked against completed deterministic prefixes. Never choose the better
of attempts. A second unexplained failure exhausts the retry allowance; it does
not license another folder or altered seed. An amendment after final exposure
does not retroactively restore an unexposed confirmatory status.

## 9. Five-question disposition

| Question | What this design can answer | Claim it must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: logical uncertainty | Executable bounded forecasts, explicitly unresolved status, paid checked answers, immutable pre-resolution scoring and current-scope hard correction. | A coherent law of all arithmetic, unreceived deductive closure or eventual affordable resolution of every query. |
| Q2: logical counterfactuals | Exact supplied operation/repair distinctions and current bound delivery; separately typed classical infeasibility and supplied exceptional counterpossible rules. | Identification of missing structure or exception policy, or full-optimum semantics from a supplied incumbent certificate. |
| Q3: multiple useful fallible models | A scoped equivalence/limitation against the strongest ordinary catalogue; mechanism-specific learning, retention and economic results. | A necessary exclusive advantage when an identical ordinary kernel is admitted, or general proof-system hardness from one tree leaf language. |
| Q4: improving usefulness with costly thinking | Fixed-expert/theorem conformance, pre-feedback empirical scores, actual actions, paid reasoning, genuine budget failures and public-report informativeness. | Calibration, general Logical Induction non-exploitation, optimal value of computation, or a stochastic theorem contradicted by any single adverse path. |
| Q5: probability information in value | Exact target recovery, nuisance-stakes limits, same-record collisions and a stated repair service. | That a fallible forecast, realized task score or scalar resource bill determines an underlying joint truth law. |
