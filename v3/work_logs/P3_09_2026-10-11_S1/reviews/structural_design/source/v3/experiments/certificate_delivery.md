## 1. Finding and exact service

An ordinary algebraic decision diagram (ADD) producer can deliver independently
checked evidence for the same finite current loss-bound service as the native
portfolio. The full bridge checks shared node, operation and input-linkage facts;
it does not trust a producer's reported zero root. On the primary comparison,
all 288 deliveries succeeded and all 24 current outputs agreed across methods
and recipient conditions. Direct ordinary checking was cheapest among all six
methods in every complete six-request cell.

These results resolve an interface difference in the earlier P3-05 resource
comparison. They do not establish that the portfolio is cheaper than the
strongest ordinary alternative. Reuse does beat fresh native proof construction
on the nonconstant families and eventually beats one cold ordinary comparator
on two resident streams, but the full ordinary catalogue remains decisive.

For a current frame $`F`$, feasible supplied incumbent $`w`$, weighted rank
$`r_F`$, loss difference $`D_F`$ and supplied bound $`B`$, the receiving service is

```math
Q_F(w)=\{x\in\{0,1\}^n:H_F(x)=1,\ r_F(x)\le r_F(w)\},
\qquad Q_F(w)\ne\varnothing,
\qquad \forall x\in Q_F(w),\ D_F(x)\le B.
```

It covers the **entire incumbent sublevel, including all minimizing ties**.
It can include nonoptimal points. The caller supplies the incumbent and bound
to every method; finding either is outside this experiment. Successful methods
return the same `CURRENT_BOUND_CERTIFIED` record. A paid unsuccessful attempt
returns `NO_CURRENT_CERTIFICATE`, which does not assert that the bound is false.

The [derivation](../derivations/05_equal_certificate_delivery.md) gives the
soundness argument, the exact inherited-tree obstruction, complementary-domain
witnesses, arithmetic-fragment limits and trust assumptions. The
[primary-source comparison](../literature/05_certificate_delivery_sources.md)
separates the established BDD/ADD and proof-checking antecedents from this
finite implementation and service adaptation.

### 3.3 Robustness to common prices on the fixed invoices

The additional [exact price analysis][prices] pools every recorded category
through each request prefix. Direct ordinary checking uses no more of any
category than P-REUSE on all 48 such prefixes. It follows by a nonnegative
weighted sum that no fixed common nonnegative prices on these pooled categories
can make P-REUSE strictly cheaper on any of those fixed records. Strictness
requires positive price on a strictly smaller coordinate; zero-price choices
can tie.

This componentwise statement is not true on every individual request: three
resident parity requests have 307, 7 and 486 extra native C-call events for
direct checking. Its total bill is nevertheless lower on all 48 individual
pairs. Pooling categories does not allow the same category to have a different
price at each stage or time. Changing prices can also change which execution
paths a funded policy completes, so this analysis explicitly reprices fixed
invoices rather than rerunning a budget policy.

For a simpler nonconsumer/consumer tradeoff, define
$`C_\rho=(\mathrm{total}-\mathrm{consumer})+\rho\,\mathrm{consumer}`$
for $`\rho\ge0`$. The exact lower envelope across all six methods is direct
checking in seven cells. In the k5 resident cell the two relevant lines are

```math
C_{\mathrm{ENUM}}(\rho)=345252+4672413\rho,
\qquad C_{\mathrm{ADD\ warm}}(\rho)=2901347+3436019\rho.
```

They tie at $`\rho=2556095/1236394`$, approximately 2.067. Direct checking wins
below this value and resident warm ADD above it. P-REUSE is never on the lower
envelope, even at a tie: direct checking strictly dominates its two coordinates
in seven cells, and warm ADD does in the remaining cell, with difference
$`3058900+506982\rho`$. This is an exact finite resource tradeoff, not an
estimate of real hardware or organizational prices.

## 5. Exact proof-format and arithmetic scope

### 5.1 What the parity obstruction establishes

On the no-hard/no-soft full-cube parity family, every proper Boolean box has
loss enclosure upper bound one under the inherited rules. The requested bound
is zero. Fresh `BandProof` and empty-choice `PortfolioProof` therefore require
exactly $`2^n`$ leaves and $`2^{n+1}-1`$ visited tree nodes, for every allowed
split order and all valid current-row multipliers. An already admitted useful
lemma or richer algebraic language is outside this lower bound.

For the same cap-relaxed family, the ordinary full shared trace has a constructive
$`O(n^2)`$ fact bound and $`O(n^2\log n)`$ complete encoded-size bound, including
intermediate operation facts and written expression keys. The independent
receiver checks local dependencies without unfolding a Shannon proof tree.
The statement is specific to this family, ordering, grammar and metadata
convention, not a general polynomial ADD compiler.

The [finite reconstruction][finite] checks dimensions 2 through 8, every proper
box and singleton there, all three actual native tree counts, and the actual
checked DAGs. It passes 21,170 checks: 9,329 proper boxes and 508 singletons are
among the enumerated obligations. In these small dimensions, complete DAG
packets are **larger** than canonical legacy tree records: 3,031–11,604 bytes
versus 701–7,637 bytes. Those two encodings are a representation diagnostic,
not matched paid deliveries. No dimensions 9 or 10 were added after exposure
to search for a byte crossover.

All 12 complementary edits have explicit exclusive incumbent-sublevel witnesses
and accepted five-node reuse covers. Replacing the pair by either one old
warrant alone fails all 24 prescribed substitutions. This establishes joint
necessity for the declared reuse cover, not a need for multiple learned models
in every reasoning algorithm. The ordinary hybrid uses that same cover.

### 5.2 The deployed arithmetic domains go in both directions

The new exact ADD format caps terminal components at 4,096 bits. A valid
one-bit loss formed from 64 input fractions with 128-bit components has an exact
negative value whose reduced denominator has 7,914 bits. Both points satisfy
its zero upper bound.
An exact ADD root for that constant requires a terminal outside the format,
while a sign-based native direct leaf can certify it.

There is also a converse witness. Two distinct soft rows for the same bit have
weights $`1/(2^{127}-1)`$ and $`1/(2^{127}-3)`$. The zero incumbent's computed
rank has a reduced 254-bit denominator. The native preparation interface feeds
that computed cutoff back through a 128-bit input-literal checker and rejects
it. The unchanged ADD receiver and direct ordinary checker accept the true
zero-loss bound. The ADD-to-native adapter also crosses that native cutoff
boundary, so its accepted input domain is not the entire ADD receiver domain.

The [common-service arithmetic diagnostic][rational] fixes five inputs in
sequence for all six original methods, with fresh recipients and unchanged
worker source. Its separately recorded unchanged integrity retry completes
30 attempts and passes 302 assertions:

| Input | Delivered methods | Expected failures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negative large exact constant, true bound | Native reuse, native fresh, direct ordinary, ordinary hybrid | Both exact ADD methods: `EvidenceLimit` |
| Sign-reversed positive constant, false bound | None | All six deliver no current certificate; an ADD format failure alone is not a disproof |
| Oversized computed cutoff, zero loss | ADD cold, ADD warm, direct ordinary | Native reuse/fresh and ordinary hybrid: cutoff `ValueError` |
| Small zero loss, total budget exactly the 1,024-unit reserve | None | All six: `ResourceExhausted`, 113 charged units each |
| Small zero loss, normal budget and new IDs | All six | None; receipt and cache recovery checks pass |

The original run's summary declared 30 units, but final source-bound review
found only 29 unit rows with a different digest. Both observations were

All 29 preserved original outputs, statuses, errors, complete invoices and

There are 13 successful deliveries and 17 expected paid failures in the

witnesses show that the deployed proof formats are not nested and that more
