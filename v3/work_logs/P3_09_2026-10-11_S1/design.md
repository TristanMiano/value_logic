## 1. What one experiment can distinguish

A forecast, a chosen action, a minimizing repair, and a checked current-bound
certificate are different services. A single scalar score that mixes their
tariffs would assign an unprovided exchange rate and could reward a method for
omitting an obligation. We therefore retain separate paid forecast/action and
certificate-delivery comparisons. Previously exposed structural examples and
exact information-recovery identities form a conformance panel. They do not
become unseen transfer by receiving a new filename or seed.

The experiment can distinguish finite execution, exact semantic agreement,
learning/readout effects on a fixed cohort, and economic tradeoffs under the
specified tariffs. It cannot establish arbitrary logical induction, a general
counterpossible decision rule, physical-computation optimality, or sampling
coverage from a handful of pseudorandom seeds. An identical ordinary kernel is
a required control: demanding that its candidate alias beat it would make the
success criterion incoherent. Distinct ordinary catalogue comparisons and
ordinary identity must both be visible.

## 2. Finite pairing and useful effects

For a fixed configuration c, population recipe r, policy seed s, and horizon T,
the paid run yields immutable forecasts p_t, actions a_t, and charged units U.
Private evaluation subsequently supplies y_t. Brier loss is
`sum((p_t-y_t)^2)/T`; action loss is divided by the declared maximum known
per-round loss before averaging. A 0.01 absolute reduction is the predeclared
practical learning/readout effect. It is a design threshold, not an estimate
from final observations. We also report exact deltas and ties below it.

First average each whole fixed configuration over its fixed paired population
and seed slots. Only then compare ordinary catalogue means or form a catalogue
minimum. Selecting a different ordinary method after observing each seed would
describe an unimplemented oracle. Deterministic controls run once per population;
their exact value may fill the common seed slots algebraically, while executed
run counts and analytic slot counts remain distinct. No duplicated control is
an independent observation. A catalogue minimum is a descriptive comparator,
not a deployable selector chosen before seeing an episode.

Identical initial seeds do not imply identical acquired feedback. The existing
broker consumes selector and action bits, while ordinary probability methods
consume their own stream differently. Exact path-ablation claims are reserved
for the implementations that actually share a recorded path (base/live,
selected-receipt provider, and ordinary mirror). Other comparisons pair public
episodes and declared initial streams only. The gate-CNF transfer suffix follows
its ordinary-CNF prefix within a continuing episode; both segment endpoints and
the whole episode are retained.

Economic repricing of a fixed run uses its fixed losses and U. Running a
price-sensitive acquisition policy at another price is a different frozen
configuration and executes its own history. Both are useful; they are not
interchangeable. Cold common source procurement is included in the corresponding
paid method comparison. Optional public reporting has a separate bill and
conditional theorem; it is not quietly added to a terminal-answer obligation.

The primary final summaries are exact descriptive finite-cohort quantities.
There are no p-values, confidence claims about the seed cohort, multiplicity-
selected discoveries, or assumed superpopulation. Positive interpretation
requires the full prospectively scheduled denominator. Missing or invalid runs
remain visible; they cannot disappear through a complete-case ranking.

## 3. Exact certificate coverage and cost

For each fixed family/recipient/budget condition, let the request universe I be
all scheduled edit slots. For method m, C_m is the set of requests for which
the independently checked current receipt proves the complete, nonempty,
supplied-incumbent sublevel with its full current source/request scope. This
includes all minimizing ties and every nonoptimal assignment below the cutoff.
W_m is the sum of every measured producer/receiver attempt, construction,
admission, retention, edit, failed delivery and output invoice in that history.

A success-only price would discard the very cost that a capped history is
intended to reveal. We instead report `(C_m, W_m)` and exact pairwise coverage
inclusion. A materially cheaper dominance claim requires nonempty C_a,
`C_a >= C_b` as sets, and `W_a <= 0.95 W_b`, with no unknown attempt cost or
validity failure. Counts alone are insufficient: two methods may cover different
requests. The main full-service comparison requires complete I for both.
All non-dominated ordinary and candidate configurations are retained, including
ties. Fresh and resident recipients have separate universes and eligible rosters;
the fresh-only pruned receiver is not fabricated into a resident method.

Changing a consumer budget can change eviction/admission and later bills.
Each cap is therefore a new actual sequence with its own freshly initialized
method state, not a static threshold applied to an earlier full-budget trace.
The old proofs supplied to a reuse method are constructed and paid within that
sequence. Incumbent and bound acquisition are supplied task inputs and remain
outside this delivery service; no full action-selection advantage follows.

The new families compare two equivalent Boolean constructions embedded in a
nonconstant signed loss, with changes to hard/rank constraints, a nonoptimal
incumbent, and a known loss shift. Public formulas and ordinary simplification
are available to every method. Renamed parity examples remain regression or
metamorphic cases, and fixed arithmetic-format failures remain contract checks.

## 4. A preparation barrier is a causal boundary

All method code, initial state rules, representations, source closure, prices,
seeds, counts, generators, score/analysis code and exception policy must be
durably saved and hashed before the first final input is generated. Generation
itself is exposure, even if a private truth evaluator has not yet been called.
Pure lists of opaque recipe/configuration IDs are scheduling metadata, not
generated cases. Workload-dependent certificate construction happens only after
exposure and is charged execution, not hidden pretraining.

The frozen driver has four ordered stages: public-input generation, paid public
execution, private evaluation, and exact analysis. It checks the saved preparation
receipt, every frozen source/configuration hash and the required interpreter
before recording exposure. Private evaluation begins only after every expected
public unit has a valid completion seal. Only P3-10 can invoke these final
stages; this task exercises the generic lifecycle on disjoint development toys.

One completed unit is a method's entire stateful episode/sequence, or one public
input recipe. Its immutable seal binds exact output bytes, inputs, configuration,
freeze, attempt and unit identity. Atomic result and seal writes are separate:
an orphan result is not completion. A valid seal may reconstruct a missing
derived index, but missing/corrupt sealed bytes are an integrity failure, not
permission to recompute a flattering replacement. Analysis derives its count
from the expected unit schedule and verified seals, never a summary's own claim.

Each stage has one initial attempt and at most one unchanged retry after an
unexplained infrastructure failure. An interrupted attempt is preserved with its
start and partial/orphan bytes. Already sealed units are verified and reused;
only remaining units use the next predeclared attempt directory. A resource
failure returned by a paid method is a completed scored outcome, not an
infrastructure retry. A deterministic defect needs a versioned amendment and
explicit exposure disposition. Changed code, seeds, counts, paths or methods
cannot pass as an unchanged retry. The stage writer holds an exclusive lock.

## 5. Failed alternatives retained in the design

* Treating all broker methods as exactly feedback-coupled fails because random
  bit consumption differs. Pair episodes and reserve exact equality for actual
  matched interfaces.
* Reusing the old nine-fixture structural paid executor on arbitrary new formulas
  exceeds its admitted tariff/completion scope. Its published fixtures remain
  conformance; the general finite Option B service supplies the new transfer.
* Repricing a full certificate trace with smaller consumer caps can misstate
  later cache history. Execute each cap independently.
* Making each of the five questions require an empirical win confuses theorem
  obligations with economic evidence. Exact information-recovery and repair
  witnesses have exact conformance endpoints and their original premises.
* A seeded Monte Carlo run is not a fair-bit coverage experiment. Retain the
  implementation's DEVELOPMENT_MT provenance inside a prospective finite-cohort
  outer stage; do not relabel the bit source as a theorem premise.
* A successful summary row cannot repair a missing raw completion record. The
  freeze establishes the authority of immutable unit seals prospectively.

These choices constrain later interpretation regardless of whether the final
ordinary methods, candidate methods, both, or neither attain practical effects.
