## 5. Source and strongest-comparator audit

The source contracts remain fit for the present claims. In particular:

- S01's exact LI interfaces retain the distinction between bounded threshold
  expectations and a finite coherent joint law. S16's pinned correction
  declarations and the independently reconstructed finite-coordinate argument
  control the finite-perturbation example; neither an unrestricted finite-day
  theorem nor an independently executed Lean verification is claimed.
- S10/S14 do not supply arbitrary logical prediction or paid-discovery
  guarantees. Their scoring/output, full-feedback or delay hypotheses must be
  re-established for a future learner. The contract already says so.
- S08/S15 permit several useful models without assuming one is the exact
  generator. S03/S20 supply computation-selection/profile interfaces, S17
  supplies incremental checking, and S21/S22 supply numerical/logical
  uncertainty comparisons. O-COMB can combine these on the same access terms.
- S04–S07 distinguish intervention, rank/repair and counterpossible semantics.
  Neither ordinary positive-probability conditioning nor nonempty-event
  imprecise conditioning supplies contradiction-conditioning by itself.
- P02-S1–S4 preserve coherent functional versus restricted query, report
  versus risk, and direct task-property versus full-law distinctions. Finite
  strict-propriety span claims retain finite score vectors and an interior
  report/law domain; infinite boundary log probes are excluded from the finite
  matrix argument. No sample or convergence guarantee follows from these
  algebraic statements.
- P02-S6/S7 allow low-dimensional task expectation or nonlinear optimized
  reports. A fixed linear-query dimension is not convex-surrogate dimension,
  bit count, sample complexity or a universal representation lower bound.
- P02-S5/S8 provide ordinary LP and fractional-programming comparisons under
  their domain conditions. P02-S9/S10 provide information-radius and scalar
  optimal-recovery ancestry; the sharp noise example is already attainable by
  a strong ordinary affine decoder. P02-I1 attributes phase-two retention,
  repair and compatible recovery rather than relabeling them as new results.

The strongest credible comparator is therefore the declared O-COMB family
augmented by these exact finite services. It can retain a joint law, use a
credal source, retain only necessary losses or contrasts, elicit a smaller
property, exploit the same calibration anchors, run the same finite audit,
choose the same useful model and reuse the same valid certificate. It is not
forced to reconstruct the full law or use naive inversion. A fair policy
comparison gives equal initial access and budgets while permitting different
purchased histories; a fixed-transcript comparison instead uses identical
evidence and a common historical-cost convention. An oracle choosing the best
method after seeing hidden answers is not the comparator.

No concrete source-derived overclaim was found in the reviewed current
