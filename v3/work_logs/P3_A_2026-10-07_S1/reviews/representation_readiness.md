## 2. Exact provisional candidate

**R0: finite versioned rational loss/constraint profile, with an optional finite expectation witness.**

For one episode, retain:

1. **Scope and evidence identity:** theory, interpretation, program, checker, source, objective and unit versions; active query IDs; received checked constraints; proof dependencies; pending or failed computations; immutable dated forecast/report records.
2. **A declared finite assessment domain:** either supplied finite cases or cases constructed from a finite selected Boolean fragment using paid computation. Record whether the case set is complete for that fragment, a certified outer approximation, or merely partially searched. These are assessment cases for represented information, not all models of arithmetic.
3. **Finite rational semantic data:** the known statewise loss rows, requested service, source inequalities, and permitted directed unit conversions. Start with known calibrated units. Costs may be signed rational quantities in their declared units; no universal normalization of all costs to \([0,1]\) is selected.
4. **A set-valued numerical carrier:** retain the joint rational constraints on relevant loss coordinates. When coherent expectation semantics is explicitly supplied, a normalized nonnegative vector over the finite cases may be used as a latent witness, and \(y=Lp\) relates it to the loss profile. A full probability vector need not be the exposed report or the minimum retained summary.
5. **Separate report types:** stipulated exact expectation, fallible point estimate, deterministic conditional bound, statistical interval with its coverage assumptions, realized loss, or unresolved result. Their numerical shapes do not collapse their meanings.

The executable core can begin with the full simplex on a finite supplied case list and known rational \(L,C\), which is exactly the P3-02 design-auditor domain. Richer rational source constraints have the separately declared native certificate route; they are not accepted by pretending that the full-simplex auditor implements arbitrary restricted sources.

The representation uses finite rational descriptions of constraints and certificates even when their mathematical source coordinates range over real numbers. It does not store an arbitrary exact real in a finite machine word. A later stored point predictor needs its own precision and computation contract.

**No default point weights are selected.** In particular, a uniform distribution over retained cases is not licensed as a calibrated forecast merely because it is easy to write down. The provisional carrier can retain uncertainty as a set or interval while P3-03 supplies a bounded information/update procedure and later tasks analyze forecast refinement.

## 4. Boundaries that must travel with the candidate

- **Oracle and construction boundary:** visible program text does not supply its answer, full arithmetic closure, all satisfying models, an exact value-of-computation law, or free expectation evaluations. A loss table or assessment catalogue must be supplied with its status or built and charged. Finite source compatibility is not adequacy for the actual application.
- **Payoff and evidence boundary:** \(y=Lp\) is justified only for known payoff semantics and the declared supplied law. Learned estimates and realized columns of \(L\) are different inputs. Error bars require their own deterministic or statistical warrant, including shared errors and selection. Proper scoring does not create that warrant.
- **Unit boundary:** start with known rational calibration and explicit directed unit paths. A numerical inverse is not a native inverse conversion. Unknown-scale ratio results remain external semantic adapters unless a permitted native threshold formulation and its positive-denominator premises are supplied. An open \(s>0\) domain is not silently encoded by finitely many closed positive-margin cases.
- **Operation boundary:** no exact arbitrary product of jointly uncertain stakes and probabilities, no native variable division, and no arbitrary exact-real literal. Fix known coefficients per request, retain finite cases, use a proved enclosure, or explicitly change the kernel before claiming more.
- **Information and dependence boundary:** preserve the joint constraints needed by the requested loss or action service. Marginals may be enough for one sum and insufficient for a joint decision. A later price/objective change can require a new retention audit or repair. Observational probability recovery does not identify counterfactual dependencies.
- **Cost boundary:** charge case/table construction, parsing, proof discovery and checking, variable-size rational arithmetic, storage/access, optimization, acquisition and repair where they differ between methods. Track the hard resource vector separately from any priced loss total. Coordinate count and free-row repair count are not bit count, runtime, sample complexity or affordable purchases.
- **Revision boundary:** retain proof and source dependencies; mark changed-scope warrants stale until rechecked or transported. Empty/conflicted sources, unfinished enumeration and unavailable unit routes have explicit failure or unresolved statuses. They do not authorize vacuous useful-action claims.

These are usable scope restrictions for R0, not a checklist of already demonstrated future performance guarantees.
