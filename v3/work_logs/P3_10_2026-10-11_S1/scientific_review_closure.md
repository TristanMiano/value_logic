## 3. Substantive review corrections and their scientific consequences

**Forecast rounding and signs.** The final dyadic forecast projection starts
from already integer-rounded fixed-mass weights. Its bound does not cover
accumulated weight-update rounding or an unrounded learner. Binary Brier and
the affine randomized-error functional have matching pointwise change signs,
but average signs can differ because their magnitudes differ. The latter
functional becomes an expected lottery loss only under its matching random-bit
premise. `p(1-p)` is Bernoulli-parameter variance, distinct from variance of p
across positions. The main report and additive mechanism note use those scopes.

**Report widths and target geometry.** The quoted width differences are
already divided by the horizon and are not divided twice. F and V retain an
exact affine relation using the public paid transcript, while Z adds a
different realized target. A polygon is the image of a binary full-key
relaxation: it preserves repeated-key identity but need not encode every
logical relation among different public CNFs. Its area is not probability,
confidence level or empirical coverage. All public geometry was saved before
that observer parsed private targets, but its design remains post-outcome and
the investigator was already unblinded. It is not a paid report improvement.

**Gate representation fit.** Suffix uniqueness alone would not exclude a
prefix receipt. The clarified premise requires unique suffix keys disjoint

and no-prior-hard fields. The pooled signature fit is a retrospective relaxed
