   separately. Merely substituting the current live mask into the old proof fails.

   outcomes; the finite two-sign union allowance licenses the minimum radius.
   Basis/radius selection across constructions requires its own error allowance.
   All statements remain fixed-end and per episode, with the specified fair-bit
   probability premises distinct from deterministic seed checks.
4. **Every-path funding versus selected success.** Exact finite enumeration of
   65,536 selector paths gives the retained 17-path completion counterexample.
   A correctly derived unconditional bound can have zero coverage after a cheap
   budget selects only adverse completed paths. Completed development records
   do not by themselves establish an every-path guarantee.
5. **Logical receipts versus physical solver calls.** The paid selected-receipt
   cache changes physical computation under a coupling, without skipping a
   logical receipt, changing its propensity, reallocating quota or updating early.
   Fresh provider state is required for a fresh epoch. Reuse has its own cap.
6. **Original fixed experts versus upgraded experts.** For sound hard corrections,
   `F^H-F_i^H=(F-F_i)+(Delta_i-Delta)`. No sign controls the last term. The
   inherited upper bound retains the original expert comparator. Matched-history
   benchmarks do not establish regret against a hard-corrected hindsight best
   expert; `min_i E` and `E min_i` must also remain distinct. The independent
   [U08 qualification](reviews/forecast_benchmarks_independent/u08_comparator_qualification.md)
   supplies a causal algebraic separator. It is not a failing deployed Prod run.
