## Independent reconstruction of the coupling

Let the admitted public tape be fixed. Compare a cold service with a fresh empty
cached-service instance, using the same broker contract, experts, hard-state
configuration, and complete selector/action bit string. A key is the complete
CNF interpretation version, source version, variable count, and canonical tuple
of clauses. The request identifier is intentionally excluded from the mathematical
key, but each returned local receipt must bind the current request identifier.

The initial numerical and hard states agree. Before a selected receipt, both
brokers have the same expert predictions, product weights, selected position,
propensity, and retained base forecast. A paid cache miss invokes the same sound
CNF provider. A hit originates from an earlier successful, checked, full-key
receipt from that provider. It must therefore return the same deterministic
truth bit as a new successful cold call at that key. Current request rebinding
is charged and verified; a source-version change is a different key.

The facade retains the exact CNF expert and cost-proxy aliases. The broker does
not use the facade's cache state, audit data, or realized fees to choose its
selector, action bits, or product update. Thus each selected position yields the
same checked bit and update. At an unselected position, equal retained states
and equal action bits yield equal emitted forecasts and terminal actions. The
induction preserves base and emitted forecasts, selected positions, actual
propensities, labels, numerical updates, action-bit consumption, live hard masks,
block records, and terminal actions. Eviction changes future hit status and
bills, but not the bit returned by any successful checked call.

This argument is about the entire successful path for fixed tape and bits. It
does not identify the cold and cached provider names, invoice operations,
remaining account balances, or partial failure paths. The facade declares
`owned_cached_cnf_receipt` and its own source version; broker and reporter must
bind the actual service instance. Focused checks accepted the correct facade
and rejected reporting with the wrong direct-CNF provider binding.

The statistical transfer retains the accepted fair-bit, deterministic-tape,
soundness, predictable expert, and all-path funding premises. A selected cache
hit is one checked logical service receipt, not a new independent mathematical
observation or a new physical cold computation. Neither a convenient seed nor
completion with an account below the advertised cap creates the unconditional
expectation/coverage contract. The earlier focused cap-minus-one examples
completed while correctly reporting `all_path_funded=False`.

**Ownership condition:** the provider is created inside each episode's job loop.
Its cache has no epoch field of its own. Fresh-instance ownership and disposal on
withdrawal/restart are part of the contract; the API does not independently
prevent a caller from reusing an instance across epochs. Such reuse is outside
this accepted transfer. Audit serialization remains external measurement and
must not become additional policy input.

## Conservative all-path cap

Write $`Q`$ for a bound on every queried/retained key's words and $`K\leq64`$ for
cache entries. Exact admitted keys have $`Q\leq343`$; use 512 conservatively. A
complete CNF admission costs at most $`A(Q)=Q+1377`$. The paid digest and bucket
helper costs at most $`H(Q)=4Q+16`$. These bounds include at most 16 request-ID
words, 256 literals, 64 clauses, and complete validation/encoding work.

Including initialization and both lookup and insertion at their worst collision
paths, the cache-only miss bounds reconstructed from source are

```math
L(Q,K)=32+2A(Q)+K(2Q+4)+2Q,
```

```math
G(Q,K)=271+2A(Q)+2H(Q)+2K(Q+13)+2Q.
```

These are conservative even though first initialization and an already full
cache cannot coincide. Complete digest collisions still require exact-key
comparison. FIFO replacement is funded before mutation, and hits/capacity-zero
paths fit below these full-miss bounds.

The v1 facade's remaining non-child work was $`200+3Q`$. Replacing its $`64+Q`$
output bundle with the fixed 576 bundle adds $`512-Q`$, giving v1.1 non-child
facade work $`712+2Q`$. At $`K=64`$, total v1.1 bounds are therefore

```math
L_{1.1}(Q)=134Q+3754,\qquad G_{1.1}(Q)=142Q+5433.
```

classify the returned direct local receipt itself as free external denial.
The broker separately prepays a terminal failure status/state footer; that
payment does not retrospectively purchase the facade's denied direct output
bundle. Consequently the initial finite successful comparisons remain valid,

The ordinary invoice JSON remains external measurement.
