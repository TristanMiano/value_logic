## 2. Why the contribution remains unestablished

The ordinary comparison is intentionally capable: it can combine explicit
bounded belief, finite probability or credal models, forecast learning,
computation selection and incremental checking. It may reproduce the candidate's
successful algorithm. The prior LI discussion already sketches a resource-limited
controller consulting an inductor; metareasoning, uncertain numerical computation
and incremental certificates also have inspected antecedents. Merely connecting
these names does not identify a research delta.

A defensible narrower ambition would identify one concrete service where the
interfaces matter: for example, a restricted retained representation and
current-use warrant that remain sufficient after a specified change, with a
proved information or resource consequence. This is an example of the **kind
of evidence needed**, not a selected representation, new result or additional
task executed here. Equivalence to an ordinary implementation can coexist with
a useful formal adaptation or application, but that usefulness still needs
a concrete object, magnitude and comparison.

Consequently **P3-N01 remains NOT YET SUPPORTED**. The elementary witnesses
displace loose inferences such as “cost notation supplies logical induction”
or “several useful models imply superiority to classical methods.” They do not
displace every specialized integration. No worldwide priority or broad
superiority claim is warranted, and the amount of work is not evidence for it.
