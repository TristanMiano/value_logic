## Native versus companion boundary

The companion's verifier establishes exact rational row identities,
normalized-law collisions and repair certificates for the problem declared in
its artifact under the full-simplex/common-calibration contract. It neither
authenticates the original payoff table or observation semantics nor
independently binds external input bytes or execution metadata. Its rational
matrices also carry no phase-two native unit labels or conversion permissions.
To obtain a native conclusion, provide the actual typed source constraints,
both equality orientations when needed, nonnegative inequality combinations,
and licensed conversion paths. Loss-unit bounds on `4p` do not license inverse
probability-unit inference when only `P -> U` is declared. Variable ratio
decoding under an unknown positive scale remains an external calibrated
interpretation unless native rules justify it; a fixed threshold can be
cleared into an affine inequality only with the necessary positivity premise.
Freely selected repair-row counts also require separately justified query
availability, cost and common law/calibration. Section 13 and
`reviews/native_probability_bridge.md` supply the relevant native distinction.
