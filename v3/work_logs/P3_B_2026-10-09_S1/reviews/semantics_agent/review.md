## 3. Hard source, exceptional semantics and proof reuse

### 3.1 A finite source warrants truth only through its active premises

For a request with deterministic answer vector $`y`$, let $`H`$ be received
constraints, $`F(H)`$ their Boolean assessment set, and $`A(C)`$ the cover's
denotation. Reconstructing U03-1 requires only that splits cover their parent
and prunes exclude no assignment satisfying $`H`$. Thus

```math
F(H)\subseteq A(C).
```

To conclude an actual loss bound, one additionally needs
$`h(y)=1`$ for every active $`h\in H`$ and an enclosure valid over the cover.
These imply $`y\in F(H)\subseteq A(C)`$. A consistent theory can still fail
the intended-interpretation bridge. A checked derivation establishes its
theory-relative conclusion; its mere checked status does not establish that
the theory's axioms are true of the intended program or mathematical structure.
The proof-stream extension explicitly distinguishes consistency from soundness
for this reason. A nonempty cover also does not prove the received source is
nonempty while filtering remains unfinished. These are not missing assumptions
in the current statements: they are stated in
[P3-03 §§2, 6](../../../../derivations/03_logical_uncertainty.md) and
[U03-8](../../../../derivations/03_refinement_extensions.md#2-a-constructive-proof-stream-adapter-for-a-fixed-logical-fragment).

The monotone-addition result is equally scoped. Adding sound constraints
narrows $`F(H)`$, so an old outer cover remains safe. Withdrawal can enlarge
it, making that cover stale. Mere determinacy of the query neither discovers
the withdrawn branches nor licenses retaining the old narrow warrant. Fair
scheduling, retained progress, sufficient capacity and an admissible proof or
decision computation are separate premises of eventual fixed-query resolution;
they do not imply better initial forecasts on a growing sequence of queries.

### 3.2 Outer source quantification must precede inner repair minimization

Suppose unresolved received information admits sources $`u\in U`$. In source
$`u`$, let $`F_u`$ be the finite hypothetical repair family and define

```math
S_u=\mathop{\mathrm{argmin}}_{x\in F_u}\rho(u,x),\qquad
S^{\mathrm{source}}=\bigcup_{u\in U}\{u\}\times S_u.
```

An all-source comparison is

```math
\forall u\in U\ \forall x\in S_u:\quad d(u,x)\le B.
```

It is not obtained by minimizing $`\rho(u,x)`$ jointly over $`u`$ and $`x`$.
Writing $`r^*(u)=\min_{x\in F_u}\rho(u,x)`$, joint optimization keeps only
sources attaining the smallest $`r^*(u)`$. If every $`F_u`$ is nonempty,
the tagged families agree exactly when $`r^*(u)`$ is constant. This follows
by taking the minimum in two stages; no theorem about probability is needed.

The independent two-source check has one repair in each source, with
source/rank/loss rows $`(0,0,0)`$ and $`(1,1,10)`$. Sourcewise analysis
retains losses $`\{0,10\}`$; joint minimization returns only zero. A fallback
costing four is not uniformly dominated over the sourcewise family. Repair
preference has supplied no evidence that the second deterministic source
assessment is false. This reproduces the separating logic of
[CE04-6](../../../../derivations/04_selection_extensions.md#ce04-6--exact-scope-of-the-joint-optimization-shortcut)
independently.

Branch definedness is part of this contract. If some admitted source has no
feasible hypothetical, report that branch's undefined/infeasible status or
include a specified fallback there. A feasible repair in another source is
not evidence of definedness in this one. Likewise,
$`\forall u\,\exists x\in F_u`$ is not a policy able to observe $`u`$.
A deployable action must be common to the unobserved cases, or its branching
must depend on a permitted observation that has actually been acquired.

### 3.3 The counterpossible bridge does not revise ordinary truth

For the fixed quoted antecedent $`p\land\neg p`$, ordinary Boolean
satisfiability remains empty. In the exceptional adapter, its positive
support is $`p^+\land p^-`$ because quoted negation swaps the support
channels. The support flags themselves are ordinary Boolean coordinates.
Ordinary arithmetic on those coordinates therefore does not replace the
antecedent with a classically satisfiable proposition.

I reconstructed the normal-fragment induction: normal atoms have support
$`(v,1-v)`$; swapping channels and the prescribed min/max connective rules
preserve complementary ordinary evaluation. If feasible all-normal cases
exist and positive first-tier penalties apply only to abnormality, every
selected case is normal. The normality-first hypothesis is substantive;
unrelated first-tier penalties or hard frames excluding every normal case
would invalidate that conclusion. The current C04-2 states it correctly.

For the genuine counterpossible, allow only $`p`$ to be abnormal, keep
$`q`$ normal, and initially frame $`q=0`$. The unique selected support is
$`(1,1,0,1)`$. After withdrawing that frame, both
$`(1,1,0,1)`$ and $`(1,1,1,0)`$ are selected. For
$`d=4q^+-1`$, the exact new comparison image is $`\{-1,3\}`$.
The map to the old framed domain fixes the last two coordinates to
$`(0,1)`$; its loss correction is $`4q^+`$. Thus the old bound minus one
transports to three, not minus one. The second support is an explicit
counterexample to stale uncorrected reuse. Forbidding the exception on
$`p`$ makes the original hypothetical infeasible, which supplies no useful
action certificate. The check enumerates only the sixteen support assignments.
See [C04-2 and GC01](../../../../derivations/04_counterfactual_semantics.md#5-a-genuine-counterpossible-adapter)
and [CT05-18](../../../../derivations/05_counterpossible_bridge.md).

### 3.4 All-optimum coverage and quantity transport are separate obligations

Let a checked current incumbent $`z\in F_1`$ exist, and let a map
$`\pi:F_1\to F_0`$ satisfy
$`\rho_0(\pi y)\le\rho_1(y)+\eta`$. An old certificate on the band
$`G_h=\{x\in F_0:\rho_0(x)\le h\}`$ applies to every current optimum
whenever $`\rho_1(z)+\eta\le h`$, because

```math
\rho_0(\pi y)\le\rho_1(y)+\eta
\le\rho_1(z)+\eta\le h\qquad(y\in S_1).
```

This proves domain coverage, not the receiving numerical bound by itself.
The second obligation is a valid quantity relation such as
$`d_1(y)\le\alpha d_0(\pi y)+\delta(y)`$, with $`\alpha>0`$ and the
correct loss units. An old inequality and a checked upper correction then
give the new one. In a portfolio the witnessing proof may vary with the
case, while the receiving action pair remains fixed. The quantifier
$`\forall y\,\exists\text{ valid proof}`$ grants no ability to act on
unobserved $`y`$.

I independently checked the turnover example with rank $`p+2q`$ and
difference $`4q-1`$. The old winner is $`00`$; the cutoff-one band is
$`\{00,10\}`$ and certifies minus one. Adding $`p=1`$ removes every old
winner but leaves current winner $`10`$ inside the band. Adding $`q=1`$
instead gives current winner $`01`$, rank two and difference three: the
cutoff test correctly fails. An all-optimum search must keep rank equality;
two rank-zero cases with losses zero and four refute a singleton report based
on the first witness. See [CT05-1/2](../../../../derivations/05_counterfactual_transport.md#2-finite-selection-and-domain-first-transport),
[CT05-6/8](../../../../derivations/05_portfolio_transport.md), and
[C04-5](../../../../derivations/04_counterfactual_semantics.md#c04-5--optimal-family-outer-coverage).

## 4. Forecasting and acquired policy value compose under additional contracts

### 4.1 A checked forecast theorem is not a checked mathematical answer

I reconstructed the P3-06 potential identity directly. For a binary admitted
label, $`e=y-p`$ satisfies $`e^2=p(1-p)+(1-2p)e`$, so

```math
\|R+e\Phi\|^2-\|R\|^2-p(1-p)\|\Phi\|^2
=2e\left(\langle R,\Phi\rangle+
\frac{1-2p}{2}\|\Phi\|^2\right).
```

Maximizing its right side over the two possible labels gives the recorded
allowance. Summing yields the stated potential bound. The expert-regret and
finite-test calibration bounds are coordinate consequences, under their
actual issue-time weights and settled feedback. This is pathwise algebra;
neither independent mathematical truths nor an accurate subjective law are
required. Correct interpretation of the admitted labels is still a separate
premise.

The finite witness starts with $`R=0`$, $`\Phi=1`$ and issued $`p=1/2`$.
The root score and allowance are zero. Either outcome has residual square
$`1/4`$, equal to the potential budget. Yet the estimated loss $`4p=2`$
is not an upper bound on the actual loss four when $`y=1`$. The theorem
can be exactly satisfied while an individual forecast is wrong. The check
is a one-step algebraic witness, not an asserted development trajectory of
the full production feature library.

Known repeated queries must route through their admitted exact-answer channel;
their old pre-answer forecasts remain fixed for scoring. Postprocessing a
report, changing weights, changing the objective, or rerunning a stateful
learner each needs the relevant transport or replay rule. A marginal scalar
for each query does not establish a coherent joint source. These restrictions
are explicit in [P3-06 §§1–6](../../../../derivations/06_cost_forecast_refinement.md)
and [forecast transport §§2–3](../../../../derivations/06_forecast_transport.md).

### 4.2 Paid feedback cannot be changed without changing the guarantee

The delayed-copy construction proves bounds on each copy's settled prefix
and then on their aggregate. Pending labels receive no score. It does not
estimate the unresolved population. In the P3-07 multi-action integration,
exact feedback is acquired on every query, even when the selected role did
not purchase the exact-answer action. Its recorded unfavorable cost comparison
therefore has a structural explanation: the always-buy comparator pays the
same mandatory feedback fee, avoids prediction errors, and avoids forecasting
overhead.

**Non-import required by this gate:** changing the schedule to reveal only
purchased answers does not inherit an all-issued calibration, fixed-role
regret or full-information profile theorem. A correct residual identity on
the purchased subset can survive while the advertised population changes.
An appropriate sampling/selection argument, a different target restricted to
settled cases, or a new selective-feedback method is necessary. The current
manuscripts acknowledge this rather than asserting the missing theorem.
See [P3-06 §5](../../../../derivations/06_cost_forecast_refinement.md#5-delayed-feedback-and-unresolved-answers)
and [P3-07 §6.6](../../../../derivations/07_paid_reasoning.md#66-forecast-conditioned-actions-and-the-full-feedback-limitation).

### 4.3 Complete-policy expectation requires a request-law bridge

For a fixed bounded policy, retain the paired feature saving
$`d_{\pi,b}(X)=z_b(X)-z_\pi(X)`$. Suppose a paid construction supplies
simultaneous valid bounds $`l_j\le E_\mu[d_j]\le u_j`$. The signed
linear lower bound follows term by term:

```math
E_\mu[\lambda\cdot d]\ge
\sum_{\lambda_j\ge0}\lambda_jl_j+
\sum_{\lambda_j<0}\lambda_ju_j.
```

Choosing a policy or a constant price vector after seeing the simultaneous
event is safe. Choosing a new complete action map, changing state resets,
switching the request population, or taking query-dependent prices outside
the retained feature span is not covered by that same event. In particular,
a P3-06 learner whose state evolves across requests cannot silently inherit
a P3-07 per-query profile that reset that state. A valid composition can make
one whole bounded adaptive episode the fixed policy and sampling unit, with
fresh independent episodes and the specified initial state.

IID request sampling randomizes which deterministic question is drawn; it
does not randomize that question's mathematical truth. A subjective joint
law over unresolved cases is another allowed but explicitly supplied/admitted
model. A uniform measure on tied support identifiers is not supplied by
P3-04: duplicating one zero-loss identifier changes the uniform average of
losses zero and four from two to $`4/3`$, while their semantic image is
unchanged. An acquired request law and a repair tie policy must therefore have
different identities and evidence.

The feature event also needs the actual observation model: fixed policy maps,
bounded paired ranges, correct paid labels and resource measurements,
independent sampled requests or a justified replacement theorem, and valid
checkpoint allocation. Inside one paired row, policy outcomes may be
dependent. No independence across coordinates is needed. A nonempty outer
joint-law family can support conservative expected gains, but one feasible
law cannot certify the infimum over that family.

Finally, a nonnegative continuation bound is conditional on already paid
assessment. The initial-root lower gain remains
$`H L-t-S-h`$. A branch that buys a profile and then falls back loses
$`S+h`$ relative to never buying it. The current
[P3-07 §§2–4](../../../../derivations/07_paid_reasoning.md) and
[ordinary acquisition model](../../../../derivations/07_acquisition_planning.md)
retain this distinction correctly. A positive post-profile branch does not
prove profitable prior acquisition or unrestricted self-trust.

## 5. A compact positive composition with a separating branch

Here is a finite service example combining the preceding types. It is a
stipulated mathematical/tariff example, not a new empirical claim about the
published adapters' resource usage or a hardness assertion.

There are two versioned deterministic query types, with true answers
$`q=0`$ and $`q=1`$. The declared future request law gives each mass one-half.
Before an admitted answer, the source contains both assessments. In source
$`u`$, evaluate the original quoted counterpossible $`p\land\neg p`$,
allow only $`p`$ abnormal and frame the normal hypothetical $`q`$ to $`u`$.
The inner selected set in each source is the singleton
$`\{(1,1,u,1-u)\}`$. The outer source family contains both singletons.
Continue has stipulated hypothetical task loss $`4u`$; fallback costs one.
These are hypothetical costs by the declared service, not inferred physical
consequences of an actual contradiction.

An unobserving controller cannot treat the source-zero certificate as a
uniform reason to continue. Source one gives difference three. The complete
paid policy instead acquires and checks the actual query's answer for fee
$`1/5`$, then continues at zero or falls back at one. Its branch is now
licensed by an acquired observation. The hard resource cap admits that
evaluation and reserves terminal/fallback emission. The finite tariff includes
all stated response work; it is not a claim of equal wall-clock time.

| Deterministic query type | Fallback cost | Complete paid-policy cost | Saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Answer zero | $`1`$ | $`1/5`$ | $`4/5`$ |
| Answer one | $`1`$ | $`6/5`$ | $`-1/5`$ |

For this existence example, stipulate a paid exact evaluator of both finite
query types and checked table arithmetic under the declared one-half law,
with a complete setup and assessment tariff of two loss units. These are
premises of the example, not newly measured acquisition records. The resulting
exact fresh-request mean saving is $`3/10`$, and a specified future horizon
of ten has all-in expected gain $`10(3/10)-2=1`$. The same rule loses $`1/5`$ on
the answer-one query. Thus positive population value and a negative individual
branch coexist without inconsistency. Returning to fallback after already
paying the evaluation correctly retains that cost.

This positive composition needs an actual law/support bridge: its request law
is declared, its deterministic answer table is acquired, its policy is fixed,
and its target loss is explicitly the stipulated hypothetical service. A
profile using a different mixture of query types or a new outcome meaning
would not inherit this value. No distribution over counterpossible ties was
inferred. The hypothesis that every source branch has a valid hypothetical
also holds explicitly in this example.

An ordinary constraint solver, exact evaluator and complete-policy calculator
with the same information and tariff produce the same selected sets, table,
policy and gain. Ordinary symbolic shortcuts are permitted and may improve
their cost; the example establishes a coherent nonvacuous composition, not
an efficiency or contribution advantage. The separately checked source/rank
example in §3.2 explains why the outer and inner quantifiers remain essential
even though their local optimum ranks happen to agree in this positive case.

## 6. Accepted composition contract and reopening conditions

The gate can accept the following restricted contract.

1. **Versioned meaning and evidence:** canonical query, theory/program,
   interpretation, checker, receipt, source, objective and resource meanings
   match. Hard premises have their explicit intended-answer bridge; unsupported
   estimates never prune the hard source.
2. **Sourcewise hypothetical selection:** unresolved source cases remain
   outer-quantified. Each admitted branch has a feasible hypothetical or a
   specified undefined/fallback outcome. The exact quoted antecedent,
   exceptional rules, routing, ranking and tie service are supplied.
3. **Complete relevant coverage:** bounded covers retain unvisited possibilities
   and every potentially optimal tie. A receiving proof binds its actual
   mapped domain and one fixed loss comparison, with any rank/loss correction
   and unit transport checked. Proof choice is not hidden-state action choice.
4. **Honest forecasting and receipt chronology:** the issued scalar, known
   affine rows, expert inputs, issue weights, feature library and feedback
   events are bound. Historical reports remain immutable. Guarantees identify
   their settled population and actual numerical allowances.
5. **A justified expectation bridge:** a law over requests/complete episodes,
   or an admitted nonempty joint-law family, covers the policy's feature
   distribution. State resets, available observations, shared dependencies,
   fixed complete policy maps and any permitted price family match that law.
6. **An actual cost and feasibility account:** acquisition, source/model
   construction, search, forecasting, retention, validation, selection and
   failed work are charged at their comparison root. Hard caps and a reserved
   fallback hold per execution; a mean resource estimate does not prove them.

Under these hypotheses, a uniform statewise bound may be integrated against
any justified law supported on its valid cases. Conversely, an expected gain
does not produce a statewise certificate. A hypothetical loss bound becomes
a claim about physical deployment only with a justified outcome interpretation;
agreement of numbers is insufficient. These are conditional composable
services, not a proof that one current controller implements the full chain.

**No BLOCKED item is raised by this review.** A downstream claim that omits
any of the following would reopen the earliest affected dependency: replacing
sourcewise selection by global rank minimization; using a nonempty cover as
a feasibility certificate; averaging ties without a declared law; reusing a
bound after changing its mapped premises or quantity; scoring unresolved
labels; importing a full-feedback theorem under selected settlement; or using
a frozen per-query profile for a changed stateful policy. In each case the
narrow repair is the missing bridge or a correctly scoped output, rather
than redoing an unrelated completed task.

challenge target. The decision to authorize or schedule it remains with the
principal/user; this review has not started it. P3-N01 remains
