| Object | Its actual meaning | What a receiving module may use |

| Deterministic answer `y(q)` | Truth of the specified bounded mathematical/VM query in its declared interpretation | A checked, version-matched result through the implemented production/replay contract; failure to produce one remains unresolved |

The reference is still the [P3-A finite versioned interface](../../checkpoints/A_1.md).
P3-03–07 provide bounded adapters and substantially stronger local results.
They do not automatically supply a single implemented record manager or an

## 2. Reconstructing the hard-information bridge

P3-03 §2 maintains an outer cover of the finite source satisfying all received
constraints. Splitting preserves its denotation. A justified exclusion removes
no satisfying assignment. Intersecting with admitted direct literals also
preserves coverage. Thus a sound source-wide upper bound on a fixed action's
regret remains a conditional certificate after any committed refinement step.

The certificate is pointwise: it concerns every represented assignment under
the active premises. It reaches interpreted truth only when those premises
hold of the actual answer. This independently reconstructs the central use of
U03-1 and U03-5, rather than treating receipt type or a hash as semantic evidence.
The implementation's bounded VM gives a narrower operational bridge; arbitrary
first-order arithmetic soundness is not implemented by that VM.

The retained P3-03 §10.2 example makes this useful without inventing a point
probability. With losses `10x` and `10x + 1`, the first action's regret is zero
even when the answer and both cost levels remain unresolved. Changing the
second loss to `11 - 10x` preserves both marginal ranges and destroys that
certificate. The shared assignment is essential. Ordinary symbolic
cancellation is an allowed, possibly cheaper comparator.

The fairness result has a different quantifier. For each fixed admissibly
decidable query, retained finite production/checking work eventually finishes
if the scheduler supplies it and keeps the result. That does not give a uniform
decision time, anticipation before doing the work, or a complete arithmetic
truth procedure. A scheduler spending unbounded total work can still starve
one query. The scope in P3-03 §9 states these conditions explicitly.

## 3. Source uncertainty must remain outside repair selection

P3-04's support pairs are hypothetical labels, not contradictory outputs of
the ordinary proof checker. Normality on every atom restores ordinary Boolean
evaluation by structural induction. A contradiction can be positively supported
only through the expressly permitted exception; it remains classically
impossible. The hard frame and rank preferences are semantic input, not facts
learned from the contradiction itself.

For an unresolved source `u`, form the best admissible repairs within that
source and then retain all admitted sources. Jointly minimizing over `u` as
well would turn a repair preference into evidence about an unknown fact.
CE04-6's two-source case makes the error exact: one source has rank 0/loss 0,
the other rank 1/loss 10. Both remain possible in sourcewise semantics;
joint optimization reports only loss 0. It consequently miscompares a fallback
costing 4. The relevant quantifier order is a downstream contract, not a
particular solver preference.

Undefined source branches also remain visible. Existence of some feasible
source/repair pair does not prove that every admitted source has a repair,
and a source-specific feasible replacement is not necessarily an executable
common replacement when the actor cannot observe the source. A total consumer
needs verified coverage or an explicit fallback on undefined branches.

P3-05 CT05-1 respects this order. For each current optimum, use only a permitted
old proof in its actual domain; transport its numerical bound with positive
scale and justified correction; take the best valid bound there; finally
take the worst bound over the nonempty current optimal family. These operations
establish a bound for the fixed action pair. The choice of proof per case is
a mathematical covering argument and need not be an acting policy's observable
choice. Covering one incumbent or preserving an old optimizer is insufficient.

## 4. Statewise warrants and population value are different guarantees

Let a request `q` have its deterministic answer `y(q)`. For a complete bounded
policy, let `g(q)` be its gain against the named fallback, including that
policy's own charged execution resources. A pointwise bound `g(q) >= b` for
every request/answer admitted by the source implies expected gain at least `b`
under any probability law supported by that source. This implication follows
by averaging the same inequality; it does not supply or identify such a law.

The converse fails. As a compact gate reconstruction, stipulate two equally
likely requests with deterministic answers 0 and 1. Fallback costs 1. A fixed
guess-0 policy costs 0.1 in execution and loses 1.5 on answer 1. Its two gains
are 0.9 and -0.6, giving expected gain 0.15. The positive expectation is not a
guarantee for the second request. This is exact stipulated arithmetic, not a
new sampled result, learned distribution or cheap computation benchmark.

If a separate evidence/assessment bill is 0.9, the same continuation comparison
still favors that fixed policy under the stipulated law. Total expected gain
over a fixed independent future horizon is `0.15H - 0.9`: negative at H=4 and
positive at H=10. A positive continuation decision and a negative total bill
are consistent. The earlier paid profile and procedure audits implement much
richer instances of this distinction with their own actual bills.

P3-07's simultaneous event permits data-dependent choice among its fixed
complete-policy catalogue and admitted price coordinates. It does not permit
an arbitrary new policy discovered after profiling, a chosen deterministic
future query, or an outcome-selected stopping horizon. In particular a fixed
seeded development trace is not a demonstration that an IID premise holds.
The current driver, scope object, manuscript and whole-audit closure explicitly
retain that distinction. This was cross-checked with the implementation reviewer.

## 5. Feedback and objective changes cannot be imported silently

The P3-06 potential and admitted-label guarantees are valuable local results.
Their full-feedback form is not a theorem about all issued requests when only
selected purchased answers are observed. P3-07's selected-feedback witness and
the actual full-feedback cost reversal make this a material boundary. An
importance-weighted, exploration-based or other selective-feedback extension
would need its own selection assumptions, variance/bias allowance, resource
account and comparator. Merely dropping the unbought labels is not that proof.

Similarly, constant repricing of retained coordinates differs from changing
their meaning, introducing a new context rule or changing the deployed policy.
P3-07's represented context and proved-shift extensions state their additional
premises. P3-06's replay distinguishes rescoring old decisions, making new
decisions from old estimates and recomputing the learner trajectory. The older
certificate is not interchangeable with any of those new procedures.

## 6. Consequence for the gate

These reconstructions reveal substantive restrictions, rather than a current
contradiction between the modules. A defensible P3-B contract should carry the
restrictions forward as required fields and rejected imports. It can certify
readiness for bounded matched development while leaving universal logical
induction, economical selective feedback, model-discovery advantage and
contribution magnitude open.

The strongest question-level opportunity is where useful learning changes
which labels are bought and repays the actual information bill. That is an
optional scientific recurrence candidate, not a reason to reclassify the
already bounded theorem claims as false. Whether to pursue it immediately or
later should depend on a concrete acceptance target and its overlap with
P3-08. The separate recurrence assessment will make that choice explicit.
