## The contribution being assessed

The unchanged [V19-R/v1 event](discovery/s19/claim.md) asks for a useful,
moderately novel synthesis with an exact mathematical contract. Independently
bounded rational source vectors generate several interval observations of
minimum affine readouts. The vectors are shared across observations. Given
a new minimum-affine readout, recover its entire attainable scalar set,
including gaps, or establish joint inconsistency. Supply complete checkable
local evidence and reconstruct a compatible rational source for any attainable
rational query value.

The result needs at most2*n*2^k local LP solves, O(n*3^k+n*2^k*log(n*2^k))
endpoint comparisons, and O(n*2^k) scalar entries plus inputs, backpointers,
certificates and exact-number costs. The output has at most n*2^k closed
intervals. Here k is the number of retained receipts and n the number of
source blocks. These are mathematical bounds, not measured CPU or fee savings.

The explicit delta is a complete, witness-producing range service whose
combinatorial dependence is parameterized by retained receipts, rather than
an n^k expansion of all possible receipt-winner assignments. The scalar
image can matter even when both extreme endpoints were already sharp:
the fixed noisy example gives[21,21.2] union[21.9,22.2], excludes the later
band[21.4,21.6], and certifies the sharp bound10/3 for |1/(V-21.5)|. Its hull
[21,22.2] cannot supply either conclusion by itself.

This is distinct from the earlier P3-N01 formal-adaptation and implementation
support. It supplies a new specified inference guarantee, complete local
certificate interface and positive conditional consequence. It does not
reclassify earlier negative total-cost comparisons as positive results.

## Reconstruction and evidence

The source proof chooses an upper witness for each receipt and decomposes the
conditioned product into local polytope products. Every nonempty local affine
image is an attained interval. Any attained global minimum has an anchor
block; all other blocks need only attain values at least that anchor value.
Maximizing their bottleneck upper endpoint yields one clipped interval per
anchor/local mask. This proves both inclusions and supplies a constructive
source witness. Finite prefix/suffix subset recurrences compute the caps and
retain a maximizing partition. Empty states, ties, no receipts and one block
have explicit conventions. See [projection](confirmation/v19a/projection.md),
[resources](confirmation/v19a/resources.md) and [ordinary reconstruction](confirmation/v19a/marginal_control.md).

Matching nonnegative primal/dual data prove local attained extrema; a strict
Farkas identity proves a local slice empty. Every indexed slice is required.
The checker rebuilds constraints from the original supplied problem, verifies
the identities exactly, and recomputes the complete image. Its input digest
binds an object, not empirical truth. The source-query service copies that
object and recovers all source coordinates, paying their output cost. See the
[implementation audit](confirmation/v19a/implementation.md).

The [formal evidence map](confirmation/v19a/formal_map.md) identifies the
supplemental Lean obligations actually checked: finite source assignments,
local certificate algebra and interpolation, global anchor equality, capped
outside maxima, layered optimality, exact receipt-partition coverage, and
finite enumeration counts. It also names the unformalized bridges. The
Python service and numerical certificate producer are not end-to-end machine
verified; the producer may fail to return evidence on a valid input.

Finite development checks support the source/interface argument. They cover
mixed dimensions, degenerate cells, huge exact constants, inconsistency,
source reconstruction, band queries and altered/missing certificates. They
are exposed same-model development evidence, not a held-out empirical study
or independent replication. Supplemental proof failures and deterministic
repairs are retained. No scientific-candidate code or theorem repair has
been needed during A1; the frozen event remains unchanged.

## Strong conventional comparisons

The strongest reconstructed ordinary method ties exactly. A layered subset
DAG has at most n*3^k transitions; ordinary bottleneck max-marginals determine
the largest attainable upper cap for every edge. Aggregating edges with the
same local label yields the same n*2^k intervals. LP duality, disjunctive
programming, subset dynamic programming and inside-outside inference are
credited machinery. This is not a new algebra or an exclusive Value Logic
method. See [the marginal control](confirmation/v19a/marginal_control.md).

The close comparison includes inverse optimal-value parameter sets,
bounded-error piecewise-affine identification, max-plus conditional reach
sets, convex risk-function ambiguity, interval/absolute-value optimization,
and bottleneck marginal computation. The [development comparison](discovery/s19/comparison.md),
[A1 addendum](confirmation/v19a/comparison_addendum.md),
[max-plus analysis](confirmation/v19a/maxplus_prior.md) and
[final contract comparison](confirmation/v19a/late_comparison.md) distinguish
read theorems/proofs from abstracts and inaccessible texts.

None of the read results supplies the same combined retained-receipt input,
complete scalar output, receipt-parameter resource guarantee and rational
witness/certificate contract. That supports a moderate-synthesis judgment;
it does not prove worldwide priority. The generic derivation also shows why
a specialist could judge the adaptation too routine. That remains a real
uncertainty, not something formal checks can settle.

Several stronger controls narrow usefulness. Known winning pieces can turn
the source into one convex cell. A single known band can be checked directly
without compiling a complete range. Worst-case convex scalar losses need
only the two extreme endpoints. Keeping the full source and observations
lets an ordinary solver recover the same answers. A larger convex class of
risk functions can legitimately fill the gap, because it changes the modeling
premises. The result's benefit therefore requires warranted source restrictions
and a duty sensitive to gaps. See [query duty](confirmation/v19a/query_duty.md)
and [premise boundary](confirmation/v19a/premise_boundary.md).

## Relation to the original goal

The author's goal is to help a bounded reasoner use unresolved mathematics,
fallible models, reasoning costs or counterfactual changes more effectively.
V19 advances the fallible-model and retained-information parts of that goal:
it preserves unresolved source alternatives, derives exact conditional
consequences from shared observations, exposes reasoning/storage costs, and
distinguishes a changed query from a changed source premise. Its finite
probability-envelope interpretation is inherited from s19 and reconstructed
in [the five-question map](confirmation/v19a/question_fit.md).

It does not learn arbitrary mathematical truths, solve logical counterpossibles,
learn a universally economical reasoning policy, or prove that the supplied
model class contains reality. Those broader questions retain the scoped
answers and limitations of earlier phase-three work. A useful contribution
need not answer all of them affirmatively. Conventional methods can embody
the same task-relative concern with usefulness, reliance and revision; there
is no claim that their ordinary formulation excludes the spirit of Value Logic.

## Judgment and residual uncertainty

The reconstructed mathematics, certificate interface and noisy consequence
support the fixed claim. My scientific judgment is that the combined contract
is a useful moderately novel synthesis under the author's stated standard.
The principal uncertainty is expert assessment of that synthesis and the
possibility of an exact antecedent in unread literature. Cerny--Hladik2016's
full text and Automatica2025's full proof remain access limitations; no
negative claim about their complete contents is made.

The prospective80--90%, central85%, forecast was for this entire bounded
contribution event. The current evidence supports retaining that subjective
range rather than moving it toward certainty: proof/interface risk has fallen,
while the complete ordinary max-marginal derivation makes the routine-adaptation
risk clearer. This is not a statistical confidence interval, calibrated
frequency, truth probability for the premises, or probability of outperforming
an identical algorithm. It is a forecast that a competent further review would
uphold the specified useful-contribution judgment. No numerical credit comes
from elapsed time, proof volume, or separate successful extensions.

A matching prior theorem, a source/implementation defect, or a persuasive
argument that the specialization supplies no meaningful additional guarantee
would reopen the assessment. A real application requires its own premise
validation and economic comparison. The new component-count, certificate-
transport and constant-noise hardness branches remain separate development.
N16, O18, sparse-structure inference and the wider portfolio remain available;
accepting one bounded contribution would not erase them or settle their claims.
