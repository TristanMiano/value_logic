# Stress test of the contribution judgment

Codex (GPT-6), October29,2026 UTC. Same-model P3-11-A1 synthesis.

The fixed event is not fulfilled merely because the algorithm is correct.
It also asks for a useful, moderately novel guarantee or synthesis. The
following objections are therefore scientific, not administrative caveats.

**“The strongest ordinary solution already ties.”** Correct. The subset-DAG
max-marginal derivation ties outputs and resources. If the event were strict
superiority over that method, its probability would be zero. The actual
prospective event explicitly allows an ordinary equivalent and concerns the
receipt-conditioned whole-image guarantee. The remaining delta is described
in late_comparison.md; a new name for the same primitive is not the delta.

**“A domain expert could derive it quickly.”** Also plausible. The proof is
short once the right local summaries and anchor cap are identified. Moderate
novelty is not measured by proof difficulty. The positive judgment rests on
the explicit new contract and its consequential output/resource guarantee;
it remains vulnerable to a specialist's assessment that this is too routine.
That uncertainty is not cured by formalization or elapsed research time.

**“The application is constructed.”** Yes. It establishes a mathematical
capability: conditional band rejection and a pole-distance guarantee with
full-dimensional, positive-cost, nonzero-tolerance premises. It does not
establish a deployment benefit or justify those premises empirically. The
fixed event never included empirical transfer or measured economic gain.

**“An interval hull plus the original source could recover the answer.”**
Yes, by retaining and using that extra information. The loss comparison is
with the hull alone; the complete-information control is the tying ordinary
solver. The new range service supplies one bounded reuse strategy, and its
costs are explicit. No unconditional break-even count is claimed.

**“The proof record is not independent.”** Correct. The same model developed
and reconstructed the candidate, while Lean checked specified mathematical
steps. The scientific judgment is not an independently authored review.
P3-11 permits mathematical reconstruction rather than manufactured held-out
data; this meets that method with the familiarity limitation disclosed.

**“Unread close papers might already contain the result.”** This remains
the strongest factual literature limitation. Full text was not obtained for
two named close sources. Their available statements and earlier related
papers inform the comparison, but do not license a universal absence claim.
A matching result would displace the novelty component and reopen the review.

These objections constrain the claim substantially, but do not presently
invalidate the bounded positive judgment under the author's clarified
standard. Retain80--90%, central85%, for a competent further review supporting
the whole specified contribution. This estimate is deliberately unchanged
after formalization: reduced proof risk and clearer routine-specialization
risk pull in opposite directions. It is a subjective judgment, not a
calibration result, product of independent probabilities, or threshold-driven
score. Separate new extensions contribute zero to this estimate.

The contribution can be supported while the discovery program still awaits
the author's disposition and phase-three report/final gate remain unfinished.
Those are distinct events. Recommending an author gate is not recording its
acceptance, and reaching a time floor cannot decide any scientific objection.
