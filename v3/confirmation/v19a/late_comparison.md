## The strongest ordinary derivation is an equivalent

The [interval-path reconstruction](marginal_control.md) is a complete ordinary
algorithm, not a deliberately restricted baseline. Its subset DAG, bottleneck
max-marginals, local LP extrema and interval aggregation reproduce V19's
answers and resource bounds. The O(n*3^k) work is ordinary finite-state
dynamic programming. The comparison cannot justify calling the algebra,
the max-marginal computation or the existence of a disjunctive formulation new.

The proposed mathematical contribution is instead the specialization that
connects these pieces under the stated input contract. There are k retained
scalar reports, each of which conceals an upper-witness identity among n
blocks. Naively expanding all identities gives n^k assignments. Product
source uncertainty makes each block's relevant information indexable by one
receipt subset. The whole future scalar image can then be recovered from
only the local lower/upper extrema, with all gaps, complete local certificates,
and recoverable sources. The claimed parameter dependence is exponential in
k but only linear in n apart from sorting and the supplied local LP costs.

This is more specific than the generic observation that one can enumerate
polyhedra, optimize endpoints, or run an inside-outside algorithm. It identifies
which local numerical summaries suffice for the entire image, proves the
n*2^k output bound, charges all witnesses and exact arithmetic, and gives a
conditional use where sharp endpoints alone lose an answer. Those explicit
guarantees are the proposed useful synthesis. Their proofs are elementary
once the reduction is exposed; difficulty of proof is not itself the criterion
for the author's moderate-contribution standard.

This distinction is also the main vulnerability. A specialist may regard the
specialization as routine application rather than a moderately novel guarantee.
Finding no exact combined statement in the inspected sources does not settle
that judgment, establish priority, or create an empirical advantage.

## Additional current primary-source screen

[Hladik, arXiv2510.04604v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04604), dated October2025
and listed by the author as an October2026 journal article, studies best/worst
optimal values for interval absolute-value LPs. Read its definitions and
Section2 through Proposition3 and Example2, including the displayed proofs.
Proposition1 decomposes by orthants into ordinary LPs; the worst-case value
can be unattained in Example2. This is a strong reminder that nonconvex
piecewise-linear range analysis and LP decomposition are established. Its
coefficient-interval/optimal-value contract differs from V19's compact source,
shared retained observations and continuous scalar readout. The read text
does not supply the receipt-parameter whole-image guarantee. No claim is made
about its later basis-stability results or about solving its harder problem.

The author's bibliography still directs Cerny--Hladik2016 to the same shared
publisher page; this screen found no new accessible full-text copy. The
Automatica2025 author PDF appeared in search with its introduction, but direct
retrieval again failed. Search excerpts are not a full proof review. The
[earlier max-plus comparison](maxplus_prior.md) and its access limitations
therefore remain in force. Neither inaccessible paper is declared cleared.

## Evidence that should and should not change the judgment

The Lean units reduce the risk of a quantifier, witness, clipping or recurrence
mistake in the mathematical bridge. Exact rational probes and the source-level
audit reduce the risk of an interface or sign mistake. They do not answer the
novelty question. The source premise remains conditional and the numerical
producer remains incomplete. The new component-count, certificate-transport
and constant-noise hardness branches are separate development; no part of
their success is borrowed to pass the fixed event.

The existing noisy consequence supports usefulness for a band/pole-sensitive
duty. It does not show that compiling the range is profitable for one query,
that a real measurement system meets the premises, or that arbitrary convex
losses need gap information. The [duty review](query_duty.md) and
[premise review](premise_boundary.md) retain these stronger controls.

The remaining judgment is consequently bounded and substantive: whether this
explicit, checked, parameterized inference interface is a useful moderately
novel synthesis. It is not the probability of beating an identical ordinary
implementation. A matching prior theorem or a convincing specialist argument
that the adaptation adds no meaningful guarantee would revise the assessment
downward even if every formal and finite check continued to pass.
