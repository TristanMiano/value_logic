## CF-S5 — paired support is an established semantic table

Stefan Wintein and Reinhard Muskens,
[A Gentzen Calculus for Nothing but the Truth](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10992-015-9385-3),
online 2015, JPL 45 (2016), 451–465. Inspected §1's four-valued setup and
Definition 1. Negation interchanges positive and negative support; conjunction
uses positive intersection and negative union, with disjunction dual. The
four possibilities include a gap and a conflict. The paper identifies the
Belnap–Dunn ancestry; those originals were not newly read in this repair.

**Import:** the FDE paired-support tables only. FDE designates true and both;
the paper's ETL designates exactly true, and its ETL proof-system results are
not assigned to our selector. We do not claim that two-bit encoding, nonexplosion
or the support table is new. CF-S7 independently supplies a signed-Boolean
presentation of the same relevant semantic operations.
