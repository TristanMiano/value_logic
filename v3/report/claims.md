## N16

The paper's N16 theorem is the unchanged [s17 claim](../discovery/s17/claim.md).
Its [direct proof](../discovery/s17/proof.md) reconstructs all interior, cap,
and cancellation branches. The [Gram SDP](../discovery/s17/gram_sdp.md) is a
same-information conventional reduction using operator interpolation, not an
external paper falsely attributed as stating N16 verbatim. The
[s18 reduction](../discovery/s18/n16_reduction.md) derives the analytic envelopes
from ordinary primal/dual reuse and a hidden second-moment minimax argument.
It also gives a smaller witness with k inputs and at most k+1 outputs; that
separate consequence is not needed for the original 2k bound.

The [cancellation example](../discovery/s17/cancellation_decision.md) and
[exact result](../discovery/s17/cancellation_result.json) support the displayed
threshold and old-vector counterexample. [Retention controls](../discovery/s17/retention_tradeoff.md)
show why three block-acquisition queries are sufficient rather than necessary
for this particular decision. [Checker v2](../discovery/s17/block_check_v2.py)
repairs a deterministic formatting failure while preserving the original
version and its failure history; [repair evidence](../discovery/s17/format_repair_result.json)
is separate. Its soundness only requires the pointwise bounds, not theorem
sharpness or successful scalar optimization.

The last [assessment](../discovery/s18/n16_assessment.md) is 55--80%, central
65%, for a useful moderately novel verified contribution under the unchanged
event. It does not quantify the paper's every mathematical statement. The
earlier 23.4% interval-width headline shrank to about 1.80% against the stronger
old-solution comparison. That adverse comparison remains relevant even though
the more informative retained block can still settle useful thresholds.
