sound partial evidence, retained whole ranges, counterfactual semantics and
transport, N16, structured correction, finite forecasting, stopping and
observation bounds, and probability fibers. Together they explain decisions
under uncertainty about answers, adequacy, and further work. Selection does
not mean that every selected theorem is new or that excluded routes failed.

## Negative results worth keeping visible

The frozen original experiment did not deliver a strongest-ordinary economic
advantage. Later local feedback learning was beaten on fees by its bandit0
control. N16's old 23.4% width headline became about 1.80% against a stronger
old-vector control. Some proposed model-scope envelopes were weaker than known
scenario bounds. Private validation counts lost force against interpolation.
An identical conventional implementation necessarily tied the same controller,
range algorithm, or correction identity.

These comparisons sharpen what remains positive: information-dependent
capabilities, exact theorem scopes, explicit algorithmic resource bounds,
and paid-service tradeoffs. They prevent a chronological list of improvements
against changing baselines from masquerading as one steadily improving
benchmark. Historical fee/work gains remain dated component measurements.

## Prioritized follow-up, without authorizing new work

1. Define one natural repeated-decision application with real source access,
   bounded receipt acquisition, and changing readouts; compare full bills
   against direct band queries, MIP/disjunction, structural shortcuts, and
   complete-source retention. A failed access-economic case should redirect
   the application, not trigger indefinite microbenchmarking.
2. Obtain a narrow specialist judgment on N16's explicit analytic solution
   after crediting the SDP and moment reduction. Preserve a useful corollary
   even if its novelty must be reduced; do not force a promotion percentage.
3. If the application warrants it, implement the versioned transport step
   of the worked episode and a separately justified statistical receipt layer.
   Validate source identity, all affected slices, policy class, and simultaneous
   coverage before claiming an integrated adaptive reasoner.
