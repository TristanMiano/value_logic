# Broader discovery goal and worthwhile contribution

Date: October 14, 2026, America/Los_Angeles.
Author direction: **Tristan Miano**. Recorded by **Codex (GPT-6)**.
Scope: prospective clarification of the continuing discovery program.
This documentation change adds **zero research credit**.

## Author clarification

The author regards continued concrete gains as a reason to continue the
program and would be satisfied with a positive contribution that respects the
existing literature while making a moderately novel contribution. Final
answers to the deepest metaphysical questions are not required. An initially
minor obstacle can justify sustained inquiry without making earlier completed
work incomplete or becoming the sole measure of the program's worth.

The organizing goal remains to find and develop mechanisms through which a
bounded reasoner can make materially better use of unresolved mathematics,
fallible models, reasoning costs or counterfactual changes. Search across the
five author questions and their connections. The intended output is a promising
positive construction with a credible verification path and a useful, explicit
delta.

## Contribution and discovery standard

A **useful, moderately novel contribution** is a worthwhile outcome and can
satisfy P3-POS01 when its supported delta is explicit. It may be a mechanism,
guarantee, representation, formal adaptation, useful synthesis, or demonstrated
capability/resource improvement. Explain what it enables and distinguish
established machinery, reproduced results, adaptations and new consequences
against the closest literature and implementations. Mere relabelling, a
contrived weak comparison, or restating the accepted P3-N01 result is insufficient.
A general learner of mathematical uncertainty, metaphysical resolution, and
universal superiority over conventional reasoning are not completion conditions.

Keep the discovery-first allocation: approximately **75% generation and
constructive development, 20% cheap screening, and 5% synthesis/readiness**.
Generate materially different candidates across the questions and reject or
redirect weak ones using short arguments and small probes. Deepen a branch as
its useful delta becomes credible; reserve expensive confirmation for a mature
claim. Labels, variant counts, test volume and elapsed time do not establish
breadth or progress. Periodically assess cumulative gains across the portfolio,
including connections and limitations that materially improve the next search.

Value Logic and conventional methods overlap. An ordinary implementation can
embody the same task-relative reasoning about usefulness, reliance and revision.
An exact equivalent tying a candidate limits a superiority claim; it does not
alone disqualify a useful synthesis. A performance claim must still face strong
relevant controls with fair information and complete relevant costs.

## Forecasts, history and advancement

F01/S28-B1/V1 is one fixed cost-advantage event, not the program's goal or a
forecast of the whole portfolio. Its historical numbers and failed or unresolved
comparisons remain intact. Any reassessment retains that event's specification;
a different contribution gets its own claim, comparison and forecast when
appropriate. A narrow candidate's probability or speculative time-to-readiness
estimate is not a program-wide probability, deadline or effort commitment.

The 80% intended readiness threshold, with 90% preferred, continues to govern
promotion of a specified promising claim to rigorous confirmation. Apply it to
the useful contribution actually proposed, rather than silently enlarging that
claim into a general solution. Continued concrete gains can justify further
discovery before any candidate is ready for promotion. The author retains the
advancement/completion decision.
