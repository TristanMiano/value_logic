# Five questions after phase three

October 29, 2026. **Codex (GPT-6)**. Requested by **Tristan Miano** after
accepting P3-D. This is an advisory reassessment of existing results, not a
phase-four plan, selected recurrence, new confirmation, or new research-time
claim. It creates no protected floor or TODO. The scientific evidence and
historical forecasts remain those in the linked records.

## What the completed phase changes

Phase three establishes that a reasoner need not finish learning a source,
computing a value, or resolving every mathematical answer before it can justify
an action. Its positive contribution is an exact retained-range construction,
supported by a wider collection of conditional decision, correction and reuse
mechanisms. These are substantive restricted answers, not merely a failed
attempt at a universal learner. The [paper](../paper_v3.md) and
[claim map](report/claims.md) give their different evidence levels.

The main unfinished transition is **from useful conditional components to a
reasoner that acquires, maintains and economically uses their conditions**.
Three gaps dominate my assessment. Structural warrants are often supplied or
obtained under a narrow declared model; learning when those warrants deserve
reliance remains difficult. The components do not yet form a complete adaptive
loop with compatible uncertainty, revision and feedback contracts. Finally,
the complete service has not demonstrated an advantage over the strongest
distinct conventional alternatives in a natural repeated task. The negative
cost comparisons make the last gap concrete, not hypothetical.

The project's [broader motivation](../README.md) adds a further obligation:
models, representations and criteria of usefulness can all change. A future
phase should investigate that dependence rather than presume that one fixed
loss matrix, hypothesis class or counterfactual semantics settles it. General
arithmetic learning, unrestricted counterpossibles and recovery of a uniquely
true utility remain much larger ambitions; useful intermediate results can
still be moderately novel and worth publishing.

## 1. Can a bounded reasoner learn decision-useful mathematical structure instead of receiving it as a premise?

**Why this remains open.** Sound finite covers preserve possible answers;
finite forecasting learns from supplied features, experts and checked labels.
Neither by itself discovers the useful structural relation connecting a new
mathematical task to previous work. The polynomial correction construction
does acquire a scope under a declared degree bound, showing a restricted route,
but it does not learn an unrestricted residual class.

**A useful next answer.** In a specified family of proof or computation tasks,
learn a reusable relation or abstraction that reduces the evidence needed for
later decisions. Separate conjectured regularities, statistical prediction and
checked implications. Price discovering and checking the relation, including
delayed or selectively purchased answers. The target can be one transferable
finite mechanism; it need not approximate a language-wide Logical Inductor.

**Discriminating comparison.** Use the same answer access and budget for fixed
and learned abstractions, conventional proof/solver reuse, and ordinary online
prediction. An identical implementation under another name is a translation,
not an independent competitor. A useful new guarantee, relation-discovery
method or measured transfer capability could each be a contribution.

**Foundation:** [Q1](../paper_v3.md#sec-q1),
[forecasting](../paper_v3.md#sec-forecast),
[structured correction](../paper_v3.md#sec-q3). This question develops original
Q1 and Q4, with Q3 providing a concrete restricted bridge.

## 2. How should a reasoner choose between acting, computing, observing and revising when information can be reused?

**Why this remains open.** The sharp stopping gap bounds refinement of a fixed
decision, and the observation bound changes the admissible policy class.
Their single-use assumptions deliberately exclude future reuse and other
benefits. A zero current computation gap therefore does not settle whether
learning something for tomorrow is worthwhile. A positive upper bound also
does not show that an available computation can deliver the benefit.

**A useful next answer.** Give a finite repeated-decision policy that chooses
among at least two genuinely different information operations and immediate
action. Include the cost of selecting the computation itself. Establish a
sound stopping or regret guarantee under explicit feedback assumptions, or
demonstrate a useful full-cost frontier on a natural task. Avoid hiding an
intractable exact metaplanner behind an allegedly bounded agent.

**Discriminating comparison.** Price acquisition, checking, storage, updating
and policy selection. Compare myopic value-of-information rules, conventional
metalevel planning, direct queries and task-specific shortcuts; use exact
dynamic programming as an oracle only where its own cost is reported. Retain
loss, cost and coverage separately when no common scalarization is warranted.

**Foundation:** [stopping and observations](../paper_v3.md#sec-stopping),
[the worked episode](../paper_v3.md#sec-episode), and the
[adverse full-cost evidence](../paper_v3.md#sec-evidence). This connects original
Q1, Q3 and Q4. It is the most direct test of whether the components help an
actual bounded reasoner across time.

## 3. What should a reasoner retain now to remain useful after questions, prices or counterfactual assumptions change?

**Why this remains open.** V19 and N16 show that retained numerical information
can support more than its original query. They characterize particular
information states; they do not generally choose what to acquire, store or
discard before future requests are known. Exact retention can also cost more
than a direct future query.

**A useful next answer.** Characterize a memory-versus-future-decision frontier
for an explicit family of edits and readouts, with approximate as well as exact
answers. Determine when a summary suffices, when a small repair is possible,
and when reacquisition is unavoidable. Include the encoder and maintenance
costs, not only the number of retained scalars. If the edit family is unknown,
state the distributional or adversarial restriction that makes a guarantee
possible.

**Discriminating comparison.** Compare sufficient information for the actual
queries, conventional compression/sketching, direct reacquisition, full-source
retention and warm-start optimization. N16's same-information SDP is a strong
reference, while its unresolved analytic novelty comparison remains a separate
bounded question. Neither a new application nor V19's acceptance resolves it.

**Foundation:** [V19](../paper_v3.md#sec-range),
[N16](../paper_v3.md#sec-n16),
[phase-two retention](../paper_v2.md), and
[the preserved portfolio](report/portfolio.md). This develops original Q2 and
Q5 and connects them to the project's long-standing revision problem.

## 4. When can several fallible models justify an action, and what evidence should trigger correction, replacement or withdrawal of reliance?

**Why this remains open.** Task-relative correction can be exact within a
warranted residual space. Typed counterfactuals identify which operation an
answer refers to. But a real reasoner must also confront misspecification,
shared model failures and ambiguity about which counterfactual interpretation
is relevant. Agreement among models is not a warrant that their common premise
is right, and changing a model can invalidate earlier coverage.

**A useful next answer.** In a bounded model family with declared possible
misspecification, track a task-specific reliance claim through an observed
failure and a revision. Show what survives, what becomes stale and what new
evidence repairs it. Where plausible counterfactual interpretations disagree,
preserve the ambiguity or justify an interpretation using additional premises;
do not choose whichever semantics produces a favorable answer. Include cases
where every full model is wrong but a particular decision remains warranted.

**Discriminating comparison.** Compare robust model sets, model averaging,
selection, residual correction and adaptive validation under the same evidence.
A useful contribution might be a compositional revision guarantee or a cheaper
reliance repair, without claiming that multiple models are inherently superior.
The admissible shifts and feedback assumptions must remain explicit; arbitrary
unobserved change cannot be made harmless by a version label.

**Foundation:** [counterfactual typing](../paper_v3.md#sec-q2),
[model revision](../paper_v3.md#sec-model-revision), and the
[first report](../paper.md). This develops original Q2 and Q3 and most directly
returns to scoped reliance on models without assuming a final theory.

## 5. What can be inferred and preserved when the criteria of value are themselves uncertain or revisable?

**Why this remains open.** Phase three identifies probability information in
expected values when the payoff map is known, and sometimes identifies a good
action without identifying either absolute values or the probability law.
That leaves a deeper ambiguity when probabilities, stakes or the interpretation
of a reported value can change together. A scalar does not tell us which of
those changed. Nor does a convenient scalarization establish that it preserves
the intended comparisons.

**A useful next answer.** Start with finite declared families of probability
laws and payoff maps, or a specified partial ordering of requirements. Determine
which recommendations are invariant across the jointly compatible pairs and
what extra observation, preference clarification or retained structure would
settle a disputed action. Separate identifying a decision from recovering a
unique probability/utility decomposition. Explicitly account for any boundedness
or unit assumptions; the project's general notion of value need not fit one
fixed bounded score.

**Discriminating comparison.** Use conventional robust and multi-criterion
decision analysis and the probability-identification baseline, crediting their
existing machinery. The contribution would need a useful revision rule,
representation or information requirement beyond a renamed robust optimizer.
This is an agenda for comparison, not a claim that the relevant literature is
exhausted or that a new calculus is already necessary.

**Foundation:** [probability information](../paper_v3.md#sec-q5),
[the project's value semantics](../README.md#why-begin-with-value), and
[phase-two loss comparisons](../paper_v2.md). This extends original Q5 while
testing the broader project's commitment to revisable criteria of usefulness.

## How I would prioritize these questions

Question 2 offers the best first integration target: one natural repeated task
can expose whether acquiring and retaining evidence actually pays. Questions 1
and 4 contain the deepest missing capability: earning and revising the premises
on which the decision mechanisms rely. Question 3 is the closest continuation
of the strongest mathematical results and a promising source of tractable new
theorems. Question 5 is the most foundational and should start with a small
identification problem rather than an unrestricted theory of value.

These are a portfolio, not five sequential hurdles or five variants of V19.
An application can screen more than one at once, but failure should redirect
the mechanism or application before an extended confirmation campaign. Maintain
the accepted standard of a useful, moderately novel result. A new theorem,
better representation or demonstrated capability may suffice even if its
implementation can be expressed entirely in ordinary mathematics. Conversely,
an unchanged algorithm with a new label does not establish such a delta.

No phase-four task, budget, candidate promotion or research commitment is selected.
