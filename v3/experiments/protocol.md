## 3. Hypotheses, endpoints and decision conventions

The comparison unit is a complete stateful episode/history. Its individual
requests may affect later feedback, weights or caches and are not treated as IID
trials. The exact fixed configuration is averaged over its own prescribed seed
slots within each public population, then equally over its prescribed public
populations in that family. Deterministic controls execute once per population;
their single exact result supplies the corresponding analytic weight without
creating more executions or independent evidence. Sizes/recipient/cap strata are
reported separately; there is no post hoc pooled winner that hides an adverse
stratum.

For a complete CNF episode of horizon `T`, define immutable pre-purchase Brier
loss `F=sum((p_t-y_t)**2)/T`. Normalize realized action loss by the declared maximum
known per-request loss `S=max(false_positive,false_negative)`, giving
`W=sum(loss_t)/(T*S)`. The priced endpoint is
`J(lambda)=W+lambda*cold_units/T`, where lambda is in **normalized** loss units.
A raw acquisition-policy price must also be divided by S when identifying its
native normalized objective. Repricing a completed path at another lambda does
not rerun that acquisition controller.

The common descriptive repricing grid is
`0,1/100000,1/10000,1/1000,1/100`; actual native normalized prices are also retained.
The native-price catalogue admits a price-sensitive combined controller only at
its own native price. The fixed-record repricing catalogue retains every frozen
path with its original price/configuration clearly identified. In either case,
average each **whole fixed configuration first**, then take the ordinary catalogue
minimum. Per-seed or per-query hindsight switching is not a deployed comparator.
Every exact tied minimizer is retained.

For an asymmetric-stakes pairing, re-score both recorded action paths using the
current common false-positive/false-negative costs and the same maximum S before
subtracting. Their originally reported means use different objectives and cannot
be directly subtracted. Pair any acquisition-price comparison at that same common
normalized price. This also distinguishes an action/readout effect from a change
in the numerical units used to report it.

bill gain under the declared tariff need not be componentwise dominance or a gain
under every new resource price. No exchange rate combines CNF word units with
structural opcode/native-call/byte units.
The certificate's producer and consumer coordinates partition its bill; output,
storage and source coordinates are overlapping diagnostics. They are never summed
as a second total. The analysis separately compares the disjoint primitive tariff
categories, treating an absent primitive category as zero; a gain in the five
named diagnostics alone is not dominance at every primitive price.

### Missing outcomes and semantic failures

Every expected unit must acquire either a valid completed result, including typed
service failure, or a preserved unresolved infrastructure-failure disposition.
No unfavorable unit is dropped. A partial forecast episode has no point-valued
full-horizon Brier/action mean in the primary table; its closed-prefix values,
missing IDs, horizon and measured costs remain explicit. The primary positive
ranking is withheld when its complete fixed denominator or a required comparator's
bill is unavailable. The aggregate also carries the complete outer attempt audit;
an unknown interrupted public prefix suppresses an end-to-end positive cost
claim, while its successfully sealed replacement path retains a separately
labelled descriptive threshold result. An implemented funded fallback is scored as the actual
action, not replaced by an imagined better answer. Unresolved mathematical truth
is never scored as zero.

A false or malformed delivered semantic warrant is a completed **FAIL** private
score with its paid public output and diagnostic retained. It is not an
infrastructure retry. Outer source/unit/input/result hash mismatches are integrity
failures. Unexpected programming exceptions remain preserved failures requiring
investigation, not automatic negative-result conversion. If an investigator
finds a deterministic defect, the amendment rule below applies.

There are no p-values, IID standard errors or post hoc significance thresholds.
The result is an exact descriptive finite-panel comparison, with prospective
practical cutoffs and every effect size. There is no multiple-testing search for
an unannounced winner. A new exploratory analysis must carry that label and cannot
replace these endpoints.

## 5. Preparation, source closure and environment

The selected methods have no learned checkpoint or neural alignment artifact.
Their tuned objects are executable code, fixed expert/initial-state rules,
representations, variable order, configuration catalogue and selected parameters.
Preparation saves and hashes **all** of them, including secondary/transfer arms,
before generating any final input. It validates every opaque schedule/configuration,
the exact source/import origins, and arm-specific development operations. The
receipt distinguishes newly exercised toys from source-bound inherited method
evidence; it does not claim to have executed the final fixtures for validation.

The executable entry is [p309_freeze.py](p309_freeze.py), with the immutable
[lifecycle](p309_lifecycle.py) and frozen [analysis](p309_analyze.py). Preparation
copies the complete executable local source closure and listed contract sources,
preserving relative paths and the immutable baseline commit for historical context,
then checks that copied bundle in a fresh interpreter. A failed preparation
attempt is retained. A successful attempt becomes `frozen_v1` without rewriting
its code or receipt. Every selfcheck must return explicit PASS and exit normally.
During those checks, a preparation guard also rejects an accepted final-generation
token before any adapter generator can run. Paid development failure records are
saved with failed preparation evidence, including a worker's attached partial
record when supplied.

The exact required runtime is Linux, little-endian CPython 3.12.14. The runtime
record hashes the interpreter, all supplied standard-library source/extension
bytes and required native loader closure. The supplied unused Tk extension lacks
two optional Tcl 9 libraries; that absence is explicitly inventoried, and no frozen
worker imports GUI code. Any other missing required native dependency blocks
preparation. Later execution must reproduce the bound content/ABI/machine record;
boot ID and UTC need not match. No external data download or package resolution
occurs during a final stage.
Official preparation, verification and final commands require `-E -S -B` and
`-X pycache_prefix=/dev/null/value_logic_p309`. These disable Python environment
overrides and site startup, prevent bytecode writes, and make derived bytecode
cache reads fail beneath the Linux null device. Source modules are compiled from
the bound bytes; built-in and frozen modules are bound by the interpreter.
Loaded source-less bytecode, site-packages, or modules outside the declared local
and supplied standard-library closure block preparation. Normal startup had
loaded an unbound setuptools helper during development; the preparation runtime
contract removes that dependency rather than silently omitting its bytes.

The paid service tariffs are deliberately distinct from physical runtime.
The driver separately records wall nanoseconds, process CPU nanoseconds and the
process's nonadditive peak resident-set observation per completed unit. Supplier
generation, experimental orchestration and private scoring are outside a deployed
method's abstract tariff and are identified as such. In the two economic arms,
the whole newly supplied adapter source is included in the common applicable
source fee; helper packaging does not hide its installed bytes. The conformance
adapter is experimental machinery outside the inherited structural worker's
original invoice and supplies no new economic comparison. Optional report
procurement is separate.

## 6. Final stage, unit and retry rules

The only final output root is **`v3/experiments/final_v1`**. Neither seeds, counts,
methods nor output roots are command-line tuning options. Final commands require
the separately selected P3-10 task, its repository pointer, and a unique P3-10
chunk marked in progress. A next-task pointer alone does not authorize exposure.
Every stage first
verifies the freeze, preparation checks, actual source bytes and runtime.

1. **inputs:** one unit per public group, twelve groups total. Under the exclusive
   writer lock, save/fsync the irreversible exposure marker **before** calling
   any final generator. Generating a public input already counts as exposure,
   regardless of whether private truth has been computed.
2. **public:** 500 units total: 336 broker episodes, 104 certificate histories and 60
   conformance executions. Each uses its group's sealed public input. Paid method
   logic may compute answers through its billed service. It receives no private
   evaluator array. All public units must be complete before private evaluation.
3. **evaluation:**500 units, one independent private score per corresponding
   public method unit. Bind the public result and input hashes in each score.
   Preserve valid obstructions, unavailable services and invalid delivered warrants.
4. **analysis:**one unit consumes every expected private-score seal and evaluates
   the frozen exact endpoints. The expected schedule, not a summary's own count,
   determines its denominator.

A completed unit consists of a start receipt, exact result bytes and an immutable
completion seal binding the unit, attempt, schedule, freeze and output hashes.
Result publication and seal publication are separately durable atomic operations.
Unsealed orphan files are not completion. A missing derived stage index may be
reconstructed from all valid unit seals without rerunning a method. Missing or
altered sealed bytes are an integrity failure; a successful summary is not a
replacement for them. A single writer lock prevents concurrent stage mutation.

Each stage permits **one initial attempt and at most one unchanged retry** for
an unexplained infrastructure failure. Before retry, record the investigator's
reason and classification. Verify and reuse every completed unit byte-for-byte;
only unsealed units execute in the predeclared second attempt directory. Preserve
the prior start, failure/interruption, partial outputs and all measured invoices.
An unmeasured interrupted attempt's resource cost is unknown and cannot support
an end-to-end economic saving. Service budget exhaustion is a completed outcome,
not retry permission. A completed stage cannot be replayed as another attempt.

A deterministic defect requires an explicit versioned amendment, with the old
freeze, failed outputs and exposure history retained. A change before any exposure
can be frozen prospectively as a new version after full preparation. A change
after exposure makes affected data ineligible for the original confirmatory label;
state precisely which data, methods or analysis were affected. A fresh seed,
different count/path, deleted marker or changed method cannot masquerade as an
unchanged retry. An amended or exploratory run cannot replace the original record.
