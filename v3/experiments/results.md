A post-outcome moment decomposition explains the recorded uniform failure
more precisely. Within each path/slice, exact expansion gives

```math
\overline{(p-y)^2}
=\bar y(1-\bar y)+(\bar p-\bar y)^2
 +\mathrm{Var}(p)-2\mathrm{Cov}(p,y).
```

The equal-minus-base suffix Brier difference of −0.0947277652 separates into
−0.0689171173 from squared mean bias, −0.0037935725 from forecast variance and
−0.0220170754 from the negative-twice-covariance term. The uniform mean forecast

The decomposition is computed within each slice and then uses the frozen
seed/population hierarchy. Squaring the global mean forecast minus the global

### 3.3 A retrospective lower bound for the restricted advice representation

The two gate advice signatures are `(0,1,1,1,0,0)` and `(0,1,1,1,0,1)`.
Inside each four-position acquisition block, the six nonnegative expert weights
are fixed. The base forecast is therefore constant within each signature and
the second signature's forecast cannot be lower than the first. The saved
suffix keys are pairwise distinct and disjoint from the prefix, and the
reconstruction checks that no matching prior hard receipt alters this fact.

An explicitly retrospective calculation relaxes the integer weights, update
dynamics and dyadic output to arbitrary real forecasts `0 <= p0 <= p1 <= 1`,
chosen separately for every block with full knowledge of that block's labels.
For a signature group with size n and empirical true fraction mu, its squared
loss decomposes as `n*mu*(1-mu) + n*(p-mu)^2`. The first term is the unavoidable
mixed-label residual for a forecast that treats this group identically. If the
two empirical means violate the forecast order, pooling them adds the exact
order penalty `n0*n1/(n0+n1)*(mu0-mu1)^2`. An absent signature adds no penalty.

### 3.4 Quantization, hard evidence and action are different mechanisms

For binary truth, `(p-y)^2 = p+(1-2p)y-p(1-p)`. This separates a Brier comparison
into a binary-error-functional comparison and a Bernoulli-parameter variance
term `p(1-p)`, distinct from the within-slice temporal variance in Section 3.2.
The two loss changes have the same sign pointwise, but their averages can have
opposite signs. Neither the affine functional nor its Brier counterpart is the
realized terminal action path. Interpreting the affine expression as an expected
Bernoulli-action loss requires its matching bit hypothesis; reconstructing the
fixed pseudorandom trace does not establish that hypothesis.

The final projection from the already integer-rounded fixed-mass weights to a
dyadic forecast has absolute Brier contribution at most **5/196608**, about
0.0000254313, at every position. Thus an A2 effect of at least 0.01 cannot come
from this final projection alone: the corresponding pre-projection mixture
advantage is at least **49027/4915200**. This bound does not control accumulated
weight-update rounding or compare against an unrounded real-weight learner.

A prior sound matching receipt replaces an issued base forecast by its truth,
so its Brier gain is exactly the old squared error and is nonnegative. A new
receipt obtained at the current position arrives after forecast issuance and
can correct only the current terminal action. Under the preserved coupled draw,
the terminal gain from prior hardening is exactly the indicator that an
unselected, previously resolved claim would otherwise receive the wrong base
action. The reconstruction finds **526 prior-receipt forecast corrections**,
**162 changed prospective actions**, and **98 terminal corrections**. Selected
positions can have better hard forecasts without an additional terminal gain,
because both terminal paths already use the purchased truth.

These identities explain why a learning score, receipt hardening and a terminal
decision effect cannot be exchanged as evidence for one another. They preserve
the historical issuance record rather than rewriting earlier forecasts using
later labels.

### 4.4 Forecast quality is an additional objective, not part of W/C dominance

examines the saved symmetric-stakes paths under

```math
K_{\lambda,\mu}=W+\lambda C+\mu B,\qquad \lambda,\mu\ge0,
```

where C is the existing complete cold terminal bill per scheduled request and
B is the already issued forecast's Brier mean. The new mu gives a separate
value to forecast quality. No policy is rerun, the four asymmetric rows are
excluded with their identities recorded, and the 19 distinct ordinary

### 7.3 A decision value need not be an expected-loss observation

In the tied structural fixture, `take_p`, `take_r` and fallback have loss rows

never a best expected-loss action for any law on those two retained states.

The robust values themselves also fall outside the Q5 expected-loss map for

is exactly s, which is positive. The robust vector `(4,4,3)` gives −1 instead.
This is an algebraic separation between the two declared services; it is not
a claim that arbitrary other value encodings cannot contain probability data.

A hypothetical independent fair lottery over the two specialized actions has
worst-over-states expected loss 2, but worst-over-states-and-realized-draw loss
4. It does not give uniform realized non-deterioration against fallback. The
frozen structural menu has no paid lottery implementation, so this calculation
adds no ordinary competitor or empirical saving. It identifies the quantifier
and action-menu limits of the existing optimality claim.

Likewise adding the same state-dependent term to every action preserves each
same-state loss difference and fixed-law expected-loss ordering, but can change
absolute minimax choice. Adding `(100,0)` to the displayed rows gives maxima
