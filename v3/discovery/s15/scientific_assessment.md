# Contribution assessment after the s15 search

Codex (GPT-6), October 23, 2026. DEVELOPMENT and same-model assessment.
This assesses scientific scope; the final clock and publication are separate.

The session has made concrete progress toward useful constructions. It has
not established a new general theory of mathematical uncertainty, nor does
that remain the required standard. The strongest prospects are a sharp
small-information numerical contract, an economical all-input model-correction
service, and a precise characterization of information available through
restricted computation counts. Useful moderate novelty in any one can be a
worthwhile contribution. They should not all be bundled into a single gate.

## Where the new work changes what a bounded reasoner can do

**Numerical reuse is substantially broader.** A retained regularized optimum
now gives dimension-unrestricted sharp envelopes for arbitrary finite real
normed spaces and 1<p<infinity, simultaneous model/target changes, and a
relative scale change. The proof uses a dual direction whose relevant
information reduces to one scalar; two-dimensional limiting examples match
the envelopes. Endpoint norms and rank-one edit paths are additional adapters.
The result excludes new values without reconstructing the old matrix or
optimizer, after the old receipt and change warrant have been acquired.
This is a formal capability gain. No new matrix-call or CPU benchmark establishes
a complete-service saving. The exact information-conditioned envelope, rather
than norm duality or regularization itself, is the plausible mathematical delta.

**Model errors can be useful structure rather than just error rates.** A
certified polynomial error image can protect many decision contrasts and can
repair every output from a small set of exact anchors. Approximate membership
requires a conditioning factor; gappy POD directly precedes that extension.
Proved model-edit images preserve the old common event. Fresh coefficient-space
certification can recompress an overlarge correction space using anchor outputs
instead of reacquiring every output. These are different duties from merely
estimating how often a model is correct. A model that is wrong on most inputs
can still be useful if its error is cheaply correctable.

The forecasting example supplies a bounded delta: at degree4, grid257,
true residual rank4 in a larger ambient space and error budget .001, a
fallible completion forecast reduces the expected full-vector probe count
from a lower bound6.995 for the declared fixed-theta control to an upper
bound6.060819, at least13.35499%, while retaining cap7. These are analytic
resource bounds. Halt Fast! already supplies the general learned-mixture plus
safe-component idea. A known rank upper bound, direct symbolic factoring,
candidate-plus-private-PIT validation and other mixture choices can be stronger
controls. The proposed contribution is the algebraic/partial-evidence correction
service, not generic forecast-assisted testing.

**Unfinished proofs can support continuing selection.** The anytime program
and conditional-release constructions keep a latent complete cohort and use
sound pessimistic refinements. They permit new program enumeration, posterior
selection, prefix selection and late proofs on one declared confidence event.
The conditional version handles unknown release decisions as well as unknown
correctness, and keeps release-weighted risk distinct from an average of model
risks. The cost-constrained two-policy example changes the certified mean cost
16.1652 to1.0706 after five additional correct receipts, but it assumes those
receipts and costs are available. It is a constructed certification frontier,
not an observed economic gain. PAC-Bayes, test martingales and convex selection
already do much of this work; the explicit partial-execution/proof adapter is
the remaining synthesis question.

**The computation-observation interface is now better specified.** Signed
polynomial degree characterizes any information and approximate identification;
nonnegative literal degree with zero-separation characterizes exact identification
with random termination. The seven-bit Hamming summary reaches cap6 under
fresh random coins, with a constructive almost-sure exact decoder. This changes
the earlier per-tape purity duty and spends more episodes, so it is not a new
fee improvement over s14. The balanced-weight family separates approximate
identification at cap3 from zero-error identification at cap(n+1). Approximate
per-episode purity removes the cap-two impossibility, but imposes a resource
price growing as the tolerance shrinks and does not protect the full transcript.
Known randomized-query, encoding and information-theoretic methods are the
appropriate comparisons; an interface distinction alone is not enough novelty.

## Relation to the original five questions

1. **Unresolved mathematics:** partial proofs, identities, lower-dimensional
   error relations and bounded program outcomes can be used before all cases
   are resolved. None supplies a probability learner for arbitrary fixed
   mathematical truths. Sampling guarantees concern a declared algorithm or
   task population, not repeated independent truth draws of one theorem.
2. **Counterfactual changes:** model, target, regularization and proved edit
   images have explicit transfer rules. The order of choosing an action and
   changing the problem matters; a reoptimized value is not a certificate for
   a single precommitted action.
3. **Several fallible models:** common-event scopes and posterior program
   mixtures can remain useful when individual models are poor. Ordinary
   correction, robust optimization and statistical selection express the same
   mechanisms. Their being conventional does not put them outside the spirit
   of value logic.
4. **Refining usefulness relative to cost:** truthful evidence refinement
   improves a certificate or enlarges the feasible policy set; predicted proof
   yield can guide spending without being a validity premise. Actual proof,
   model, representation and bookkeeping costs still determine whether it pays.
5. **Probability information inside costs/values:** the query-count channels
   address this directly. The selective-information frontier states the base
   rate and information-per-query premises needed before information becomes
   a computation lower bound. No unexplained conversion from nats to fees is used.

## What deserves further work

The numerical envelope has the cleanest standalone mathematical target.
The correction service has the clearest path to an end-to-end paid example.
The query characterization has a crisp finite formal contract, but its
practical relevance and closest formulation need more scrutiny before further
engineering. The remaining constructions form a reserve of useful components
and counterexamples; their volume does not measure novelty.

The next inexpensive screens should be (i) an exact prior-art comparison for
the numerical information contract; (ii) one realistic oracle/access regime
where correction acquisition amortizes against strong ordinary controls; and
(iii) a closest-query-theory comparison and concrete reason to require the
count-only interface. Continue searching outside these routes if those screens
fail. Do not turn a promising candidate into the entire recurrence program.
