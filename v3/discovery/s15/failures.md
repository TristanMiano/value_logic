# Negative results, failed shortcuts and unresolved duties

Codex (GPT-6), October 23, 2026. DEVELOPMENT; scientific dispositions.

The useful constructions survive with narrower claims than several attractive
shortcuts would suggest. These limits are evidence, not reasons to abandon
the broader search.

* **Generic methods are often already known.** Norm duality, gappy POD,
  polynomial identity testing, low-rank interpolation, PAC-Bayes and safe
  betting mixtures are conventional. Halt Fast! directly precedes the generic
  learned-mixture plus fallback construction. The possible delta lies in a
  precise new envelope or useful evidence/access composition. Merely renaming
  those methods would not constitute a contribution.
* **A better bound is not measured performance.** Conditional margins tighten
  a worked risk upper13% versus27.66%; the forecast improves a declared probe
  expectation by at least13.35499%; partial program proofs change a constructed
  feasible-cost frontier. None is a new audit-fee or population-error measurement.
  The full implementation/representation/proof bill remains unresolved.
* **Changed duties cannot be compared as one benchmark.** Cap6 randomized
  Hamming readout, cap7 per-tape purity, approximate identification and exact
  random-time identification have different requirements. Approximate purity
  permits eventual lower-cap recovery but loses global transcript protection.
  Persistent hidden state can defeat fresh-reset lower bounds.
* **Positive algebra matters for zero error.** Signed low-degree functions
  can separate cells statistically without a zero-probability event needed
  for exact finite-mean elimination. A Fourier-degree argument alone is
  insufficient. The early claim that no finite exact cap6 decoder existed
  was too broad; its later addendum limits it to the constructed channel.
* **Rationalization can fail.** The first zero-channel LP rounding and the
  weight-channel rounding failed exact primal checks. Their original raw
  results remain. The zero-channel repair reconstructs an exact supported
  solution; the stronger weight channel has an explicit separate symmetric
  construction. Numerical solver output alone is not the certificate.
* **Partial evidence cannot be deleted or relabelled.** Ignoring unresolved
  outcomes, treating a later larger span as the frozen earlier span, or
  selecting a successful betting start after seeing its future invalidates
  the corresponding guarantee. The finite negative examples retain these
  failures. Unknown evidence must use the declared pessimistic bound.
* **Secret validation has a specific lifetime.** Continue after the first
  rejection with the same hidden PIT sample and a sequence of false
  identities can eventually pass with certainty. Retire it, charge fresh
  epochs, and keep residuals/points/private sketches out of the proposer’s
  transcript. Unconditional failure probability is not a posterior error
  probability conditional on a particular release.
* **Exact and approximate structure differ.** Noisy subspace membership needs
  an independently warranted residual and conditioning factor. A few noisy
  samples do not inherit the exact polynomial range theorem. Five exact
  values identify a rank-one rational path; five approximate values can
  conceal a narrow unit-height peak without a direction/conditioning bound.
* **A model edit is not an evidence transfer rule.** Image/degree bounds must
  be proved, not inferred from a version name. Algebraic transport can retain
  an old common event; a new statistical recompression needs a new valid
  error allocation or common process. Counterfactual target changes need
  their own semantics and warrant.
* **Good proposals and known rank are different information.** A fallible
  rank forecast can guide betting. A proved rank upper bound can instead
  permit an earlier exact stop and must be given to ordinary controls too.
  Guided probes of the same price need not reduce total range-acquisition
  count. An aggregate oracle is not cheaply priced merely because its
  answer is one scalar.
* **Accumulated knowledge strengthens the control.** After enough input
  features have been learned, two-sided interpolation removes future anchor
  queries. Charging an ordinary correction method forever would inflate
  the proposed benefit. Direct symbolic factoring and shared vector solves
  may also dominate a coordinate-query construction.
* **Conditional risk has a denominator.** A policy mixture's error among
  released answers is weighted by release frequency, not by the policy
  posterior alone. The finite example gives98.02% actual conditional error
  where the naive mean is49.5%. A low conditional error still needs a
  separately reported release-rate guarantee.
* **Information is not correctness or CPU time.** The base error rate and
  target are essential in the sharp information/coverage frontier. One
  inspected source's pairwise-KL/MI inequality fails a binary counterexample
  and is not imported. A mutual-information ceiling becomes a query bound
  only with a justified conditional information-per-query contract.

The private-scope, feature-repair and numerical path probes are exact only
for their enumerated finite cases. Floating root, norm and capacity probes
are DEVELOPMENT screens. All derivations/checks came from the same model;
none constitutes independent confirmation. No deterministic program failure
in this recurrence is attributed to hardware merely because the host can
be unstable. Recovery/unread/mixed-clock intervals are excluded explicitly.

The unresolved positive task is to select a useful moderate contribution,
compare its closest formulation fairly and reconstruct its load-bearing
proof independently when ready. P3-POS01 remains OUTSTANDING, without
converting the negative screens into a claim that the program made no progress.
