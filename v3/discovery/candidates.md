## First constructive update

- **S01/S02:** exact threshold-certificate prototype, an explicit two-error
  failure of a Hamming-based cost bound, full-cost dovetail requirements and
  redundant certificate proposals. The ordinary SBB rule is a required strong
  comparison; the first fixtures show no material complete-cost advantage.
  [Development](s01/development.md), [residual derivation](s01/residual.md).
- **S03/S04:** exact primal/dual regret certificates and an ordinary Bellman
  query planner reconstruct decision-region and complementary-query examples.
  Joint ambiguity matters even when all pairs share an acceptable action.
  Known DRD/HEC methods cover the basic target; this is a developing
  specialization with an unresolved usefulness/cost obligation.
  [Decision regions](s01/regions.md).
- **S05:** paired noisy values can cancel shared nuisance error and support
  regret-directed refinement without full calibration. The static recovery
  facts largely reuse P3-02; the constructive target is paid refinement and
  source-sensitive scheduling. Direct contrast evaluation is a mandatory
  conventional control. [Value queries](s01/value_queries.md).
- **S06, preferred next constructive branch:** exact coupled cost ranges for
  two fixed-order k-of-n procedures in O(n^2) unit arithmetic, conditional on
  partial truth evidence. An explicit stopping-pair construction and endpoint
  witnesses matched 5,286 completions in 320 development fixtures. The
  outcome-dependent extension matched 420 retrospective/live comparisons
  over 5,546 completions. Next find a nontrivial application
  and compare specialized ordinary analytic methods. Preserve the distinction
  between retrospective costs and remaining costs after receipt reuse.
  [Pair-policy comparison](s01/comparison.md).

## s02 constructive revision

- **S06 narrowed:** the pair kernel is unnecessary for exact post-certificate
  feedback on static thresholds; two O(n) replays suffice. For positive prices
  and equal setup fees, different live orders are equivalent or incomparable
  on the unrestricted residual source. Explicit witnesses and finite checks
  are in [comparison limits](s02/comparison_limits.md). Retain the range kernel
  for tolerance/overhead questions, but do not prioritize robust elimination.
- **S07 added, linked to S01/S04:** explicit clause edits permit local SAT
  witness and UNSAT-support maintenance. A lazy, bounded witness library can
  preserve temporarily invalid models until a later edit makes them useful.
  [Derivation](s02/revision.md), [developmental results](s02/revision_results.md).
  Ordinary incremental SAT and the identical kernel are direct comparators.
  Warm small-stream savings merit development; cold economics, proof-carrying
  UNSAT/upper bounds and historical memory remain open.
- **S04/S07 preferred constructive next step:** exact rational action refinement
  using MaxSAT counterexamples over a compact CNF source. Tiny source revisions
  matched enumeration; warm pools saved oracle calls but slightly increased
  time. Develop newly admitted-region separation and cheap action-change
  transport before scaling or confirmation. [Construction](s02/constraint_generation.md).

## s02 second constructive revision

- **S04/S07 tolerance refinement narrowed:** exact delta separation works,
  but much of the initial speedup is an unchanged-source shortcut. Prepared
  ordinary SAT reverses the first guard comparison; compiled guards are a
  near tie. Preserve the threshold-encoding failure and its separate repair.
  [Results](s02/tolerance_results.md). Deprioritize tuning these tiny fixtures.
- **S08 added, S05/S06 lineage:** a priced finite-population value service
  resolves deterministic mathematical queries through randomized sampling.
  A fallible model supplies groups; checked samples determine their values.
  Grouping quality differs from prediction accuracy: a globally inverted
  partition can still have low variance. Exact confidence mixtures retain
  coverage with arbitrary fixed errors, but noisy groups can be more expensive
  than direct ordinary decision tests. [Construction](s02/value_sampling.md).
  Finite-population PPI, stratification and without-replacement confidence
  sequences are mandatory conventional antecedents, not claimed discoveries.

  clause edits, using common paid receipts, monotonicity and lazy witness
  checks. Measure the complete evaluator/controller bill. This may connect
  Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4/Q5 at an executable scope; it does not settle general logical
