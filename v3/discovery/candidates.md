## First constructive update

- **S01/S02:** exact threshold-certificate prototype, an explicit two-error
  failure of a Hamming-based cost bound, full-cost dovetail requirements and
  redundant certificate proposals. The ordinary SBB rule is a required strong
  comparison; the first fixtures show no material complete-cost advantage.
  [Development](s01/development.md), [residual derivation](s01/residual.md).
- **S03/S04:** exact primal/dual regret certificates and an ordinary Bellman
  query planner reconstruct decision-region and complementary-query examples.
  Joint ambiguity matters even when all pairs share an acceptable action.
  Known DRD/HEC methods cover the basic target; this is a developing
  specialization with an unresolved usefulness/cost obligation.
  [Decision regions](s01/regions.md).
- **S05:** paired noisy values can cancel shared nuisance error and support
  regret-directed refinement without full calibration. The static recovery
  facts largely reuse P3-02; the constructive target is paid refinement and
  source-sensitive scheduling. Direct contrast evaluation is a mandatory
  conventional control. [Value queries](s01/value_queries.md).
- **S06, preferred next constructive branch:** exact coupled cost ranges for
  two fixed-order k-of-n procedures in O(n^2) unit arithmetic, conditional on
  partial truth evidence. An explicit stopping-pair construction and endpoint
  witnesses matched 5,286 completions in 320 development fixtures. The
  outcome-dependent extension matched 420 retrospective/live comparisons
  over 5,546 completions. Next find a nontrivial application
  and compare specialized ordinary analytic methods. Preserve the distinction
  between retrospective costs and remaining costs after receipt reuse.
  [Pair-policy comparison](s01/comparison.md).
