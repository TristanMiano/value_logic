## Strongest positive construction: observations that enable revisions

The chain model now supplies a complete proposed representation rather than
only a fixed-policy oracle: private history flow, conditional globally supported
mask relations, a pathwise-cap realization network and a common-original-world
regret separator. The [seven-case LP screen](repair_results.md) includes exact
small comparisons and 41-variable cases, with rational upper/lower enclosures.
The implication example has exact optimum 65/64 instead of 7/4 for the no-query
class. The [branching example](star_example.md) has exact primal/dual witnesses
89/48 instead of 33/16, checked over all fourteen pure policies in st01.

On six exposed CNF sources, [conditional menu selection](cnf_results.md)
reduces regret by at least 46.0078% and 56.5029% relative to a class that can
query but must retain its initial edit menu. Both use the same full operational
comparator. This isolates the ability to certify new edits from merely predicting
cost. One other source gains from predictive information only; three already
have zero regret. More admitted masks need not help. Fees for queries and edits
are included, but source admission, selection, optimization and checking prices
are not. These are not complete-cost improvements or wins over ordinary methods.

[Closest-prior analysis](repair_prior.md) limits the candidate claim to an
exact structured adapter with an explicit information/certification contract.
[Bounded-width extension](structured_repair.md) shows a plausible route beyond
chains using standard CSP extended formulations, still with exponential query-
set and width dependence. [Fallible-source analysis](fallible_repair.md) proves
only a coverage-conditioned safety consequence; it does not make the regret
numbers robust to false source premises. A useful moderate synthesis is a
reasonable target. Its novelty, practical value and complete proof still need
separate scrutiny.

## Learning: capability progress, adverse economic comparisons

Local full-information feedback buys 4999 additional fee units instead of
112193 on the four exposed streams: 95.54% less for that same feedback duty.
Including execution, its fee falls from 1047247 to 940053 versus the full-feedback
implementation. Yet bandit0 spends 937656. The free sparse learner spends
938744, and the new ordinary path learner 944395. Thus none of these new
pooled learners beats that stronger fee control. Matrix-product reductions
do not imply total-computation reductions when other work changes.

The [model-selection derivation](model_selection.md) identifies an observable
variance score and a conditional prediction-sensitive guarantee using established
optimistic-learning ideas. Its three-case screen saves no fees, even when a
correct predictor is in the menu. The [ordinary path construction](path_feedback.md)
also gives a square-root expected regret bound without extra feedback purchases,
so the earlier two-thirds bound is not an interface lower bound. The [off-path
bias witness](path_feedback.md) prevents an unjustified free-feedback improvement.

Retain the exact geometry and sparse representation as useful capabilities.
Deprioritize horizon/tuning sweeps until a new mechanism predicts a meaningful
gain against the already successful controls. Avoid converting a theorem's
conditional model-error term into evidence that a cheap useful model exists.

## Information: a separate positive direction with a sharp boundary

[Invoice information](invoice_information.md) characterizes the full-cube
measurement span at depth H as Boolean characters of degree at most H-1.
Its affine-source specialization relates identifiability to dual-code covering
radius. This is a precise adapter of decision-tree/Fourier and coding facts,
not a new general probability-recovery principle.

[Support sensitivity](support_sensitivity.md) supplies explicit indistinguishable
probability laws: very small support leakage can coexist with a large full-law
error. In the constructed 41-bit case, leakage about 1.85e-6 permits total
variation 1/4 at cap 21. The same invoices can nevertheless identify a permitted
task cost exactly. [Sampling](invoice_estimation.md) and [representation design](invoice_design.md)
turn that distinction into a finite bill and a task-error allowance, with exact
optimality only for their declared allocation surrogate and measurement menu.

This is relevant progress toward using partial value information without
demanding a general mathematical-belief learner. It needs a convincing
measurement setting and a closer priority comparison before promotion; the
constructed examples alone do not establish application impact.

The [repeated-instance composition](invoice_composition.md) adds an explicit
operation-change result: k reset evaluators revisiting the same hidden instance
with caps H_j have transcript span of degree at most min(n,sum_j(H_j-1)), and
that span is attained. Repeated purchases and marker fees are charged. Fresh
independent instances do not combine their characters this way. This makes
the sampling contract part of the useful result rather than an implicit
assumption about what a reasoner can observe.

## Ordinary methods and the contribution standard

All three directions use ordinary probability, optimization, logic or learning.
Those methods are compatible with the project's spirit: provisional models,
paid reasoning, explicit changes and usefulness relative to a task. Their
origin does not make them less relevant. The proposed contribution is a
specified mechanism, representation or consequence built with those tools.
An identical ordinary realization ties because it is the same procedure under
another description. It cannot be a superiority target. Restricted ablations
such as no querying or a static menu answer what the added capability does;
they do not substitute for the closest ordinary formulation.
