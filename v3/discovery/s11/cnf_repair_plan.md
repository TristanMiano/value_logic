# A small general-CNF repair screen

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT. The chain/cardinality constructions are promising
only at their stated structural scope. Before another large optimizer, check
whether paid evidence actually enables small valid revisions on six existing
logical sources whose clauses do not have the chain form.

Use the already exposed s10 sources uf20-01, uf20-02, uf20-03, uf50-01,
uf50-02 and uf50-03, in that order. Reuse their saved unit-propagated source
diagrams as historical input, recording hashes and inherited admission cost;
this is not a fresh free source purchase or new admission credit. Queryable
variables are 1 and ceil(n/2), selected before inspecting menus. Consider all
n-bit XOR masks of weight <=2 and all nine partial histories. A total cap
four permits every tested combination of two queries and two flips.

Target equals the original CNF F. At history h, a mask b is valid iff
F AND h entails every transformed clause of F(x xor b). Clauses not touched
by b already follow from F and need no entailment query. For each affected
clause, ask whether F AND h AND its transformed negation is satisfiable.
Any satisfying assignment is a directly checkable invalidity witness.
An unsatisfiable assumption core can reduce the subset of h needed for that
clause; the union over affected clauses supports a more general valid mask.
Core results are native solver advice, not independent formal proof objects.

Compare a direct incremental-SAT arm with a second arm that also reuses valid
history cores and compatible invalidity witnesses. Both use the same native
solver and candidate order. Reconstruct all models of the inherited checked
diagram, verify each against original clauses, and compare every menu outcome
against explicit universal mask checking on that model set. The exhaustive
comparison inherits the prior completeness of the admitted source diagram;
do not claim that mere model enumeration establishes completeness.

Record enabled masks by history, native calls/statistics, witness/core reuse,
explicit-model work and elapsed times, preserving all failures. At most two
unchanged process retries for unexplained failures. Use no output-dependent
source or query-coordinate selection. If evidence rarely enlarges the menu,
retain that negative result and return to the wider portfolio. This six-source
screen is not a full service, ordinary-planner victory, priced-economic
comparison or confirmation of a general novelty claim.

The mechanism is ordinary SAT entailment, assumption-core generalization and
counterexample reuse. The question is whether those ingredients provide a
useful concrete interface for evidence-enabled logical revisions here.
