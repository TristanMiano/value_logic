# Candidate-specific verification targets, not a new gate attempt

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT. These targets make the next useful scrutiny
concrete. They are neither a confirmation freeze nor a request to spend the
next recurrence rigorously testing only one method. Broad generation and early
discard remain the default. No readiness probability is assigned here.

## R11-M: paid information enabling certified repairs

Object: the chain-source/target randomized optimizer with fixed query set,
nonnegative rational fees and a joint pathwise operation cap, as specified in
safe_revision.md and repair_core.py/repair_lp.py. A useful positive confirmation
would establish all of the following for that exact contract:

1. Every admissible finite randomized procedure has a source-independent
   private-flow representation, and every feasible representation has an
   implementable procedure. Merging query orders must preserve all relevant
   private information; perfect recall cannot simply be cited for that merge.
2. Globally supported projections produce exactly the universally valid masks;
   terminal realization preserves both marginal costs and each realized cap.
3. The point-source comparator pays for certificates under the same operation
   rules, and one common original assignment is used by every separator term.
4. The compact epigraph and rational primal/dual checking support the stated
   optimum enclosure. Bit complexity and source preparation are separated
   from the LP's combinatorial dimensions.
5. A closest-method comparison identifies a useful explicit adapter or
   consequence beyond restating standard contingent planning or CSP flows.
   If an existing formulation is exactly equivalent, say so and assess whether
   the synthesis/implementation still provides a substantive useful delta.

The toy and exposed-source examples already show a capability gain relative
to restricted menus. They do not supply an application or complete-cost win.
A motivated configuration/repair family would strengthen usefulness, but its
physics, source trust and access fees must be defined before benchmarking.
Matching the identical ordinary optimizer is expected. Beating that identical
procedure is not part of R11-M. The bounded-width adapter is a possible later
extension, with its join/count proof checked separately.

## I11-M: the information carried by predictable invoices

Object: the exact invoice-depth span, affine-source corollary, approximate-
support obstruction and task-specific sampling rule. Useful confirmation
would reconstruct the highest-degree cancellation, attainable marker basis,
restricted-source rank calculation and nonnegative indistinguishable-law
witnesses. It would distinguish full-cube impossibility, support-conditioned
identification, numerical stability and paid finite-sample estimation.

The repeated-instance result has a separate operation contract: the same
hidden instance, private reset, public invoice memory, repeated purchases
charged, and specified per-episode caps. Its transcript-product argument must
not be used for independent fresh worlds. Neither theorem licenses observing
answers, outcome-dependent fees or private retained answers for free.

The closest comparison must cover ordinary decision-tree polynomials,
measurement-channel identification, coding/character algebra and task-relative
experimental design. A credible application could make this modest adapter
worthwhile even though its conventional implementation is identical. No
claim of economical full-law recovery is required or currently supported.

## L11: retain a useful structure, defer a superiority attempt

The local-feedback geometry and sparse receipt estimators are useful exact
capabilities. Their observed fee comparisons do not currently motivate a
larger superiority study. A new attempt should first specify why a different
source, predictor or resource tradeoff is likely to change the comparison
against bandit0 and ordinary semi-bandit methods. Repeating the same streams
with more tuning would not be independent confirmation.

F01/S28-B1/V1 remains a different historical complete-cost event. Its dated
20--45%, central 30% assessment is carried unchanged, not transferred to any
of these targets. The author's useful-moderate-contribution standard permits
these mathematical or synthesis targets without demanding universal superiority,
general logical-uncertainty learning or answers to all metaphysical questions.
