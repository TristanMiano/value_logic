# Initial portfolio search and cheap screens

Codex (GPT-6), October 15, 2026 local date. DEVELOPMENT, in progress.
These are hypotheses and dispositions, not separate numbered families or
completed confirmation tasks. The four-hour session clock is active.

## Decision-relative use of fallible models

Hypothesis: retain models that disagree globally but agree on the computations
needed for action values. This is relevant to Q1/Q3/Q4, but the generic principle
is already established in value equivalence and value-directed compression.
Existing S11L also develops linear continuation spaces. Do not benchmark a new
name for that principle. The promising narrower question is whether there is a
canonical cheapest potential baseline within a specified linear representation
class, including informative observations and explicit computation charges.
See [the candidate derivation](potential.md). This extends S11L; no new family
count is earned by revisiting it.

## Learning model reliability from disagreement alone

Hypothesis: cheaply update model weights from agreement on unresolved facts,
without buying an outcome or a justified model-reliability premise. Screen:
two models constantly predict 0 while a third predicts 1. Worlds with truth 0
and truth 1 give the learner identical disagreement histories. They require
opposite reliability rankings. Any conclusion needs additional evidence,
structural restrictions or an explicit prior. Shared bias is not removed by
adding models. Deprioritize this unrestricted claim; paid checked feedback and
context-specific calibration remain valid existing S06/S15/S16 directions.

## Recovering probabilities from changing unknown stakes

Hypothesis: repeated value reports disentangle changing stakes without separate
calibration. Screen: observations v_t=s_t p allow any fixed p in (0,1] by taking
s_t=v_t/p when each positive s_t is unconstrained. Additional dates alone do not
identify p. Shared within-query stakes or anchors can repair this, but P3-02
already treats their basic algebra. Deprioritize the unrestricted claim; only
a new restricted query interface or substantive acquisition consequence merits
further development. This avoids creating an artificial probability benchmark.

## Counterfactuals from decision agreement

Hypothesis: observationally equivalent models agreeing on current optimal action
must support the same action after an intervention. Screen: two binary models
have Y=X naturally. One uses the structural rule Y=X; the other shares an
unobserved cause U with X=U and Y=U. Natural observations agree. Under do(X=1)
with U=0, Y differs, so a task choosing the predicted Y has different optima.
Current decision equivalence is insufficient. A changed operation needs an
explicit transport identity on the decision-relevant space. Develop such
identities as a restricted counterfactual interface, not learned semantics from
natural agreement. This connects the potential candidate to Q2 and S19.

## Resource-aware reuse and remaining alternatives

Unrestricted reuse of an interrupted solver's internal state by another solver
has no guarantee: intermediate bytes need not mean the same thing. Shared
checked certificates can be reused under their declared source dependencies,
but S07/S17/S34 already establish that route. A new hypothesis must say what
additional source change or representation it handles. Avoid another large
SAT sweep solely to produce a headline percentage.

The s10 sharp marginal bound has a different open route: heterogeneous marginals
lose information when replaced by the weakest common law. A small coupling
derivation could test whether that loss admits a cheap repair. This remains an
alternative, not the default destination of every research question.

## Current selection

Develop canonical potential-relative compression far enough to decide whether
it produces a useful new guarantee beyond its known ingredients. Keep source
acquisition, computational complexity and ordinary equivalence explicit.
Also seek a distinct useful continuation in partial identification or robust
dependence, rather than spend the entire session rigorously checking one lemma.

## Broader second pass: avoid repackaging completed finite facts

Cross-context value calibration with unknown positive scales/offsets is tempting:
two distinct overlapping anchors align affine units; cycles can test consistency.
But the essential identification, calibration and restricted-measurement issues
already appear in P3-02 and ordinary measurement/sensor-calibration methods.
No new acquisition consequence has yet been specified. Park the generic network
relabeling rather than write another extensive exact-rank test.

Another candidate pools several fallible confidence regions under a promise
that at most s models fail. The justified source is the union of intersections
of all retained m-s subsets. Intersecting every model's region is unsound;
duplicating a biased predictor does not earn a stronger premise. Scalar trimmed
intervals and robust aggregation are conventional. A useful extension would
need a paid way to warrant the failure budget or exploit genuinely new logical
structure. Park the generic consensus claim.

The paid-query/flip branch now has an exact fixed-policy interval-regret
construction, and the dependence branch has a single-coupling invoice flow.
Their new obligations differ: acquire/validate conditional values for the first;
learn or calibrate marginal/dual information economically for the second.
Both have conventional equivalents. Neither answers general mathematical
uncertainty, and neither needs to do so to merit continued development.

The next broader question is *transport under a change in the allowed operations*:
which old value measurements survive, and how much genuinely new information
must be acquired? This is more relevant than retesting unchanged small tableaux.
Investigate a task-relative increment with an explicit lower bound and a paid
query menu; generic incremental linear algebra alone will not be a contribution.

## Third pass: inexpensive coherence screen

Checked logical relations can improve a fallible model's proper-score reports
without knowing individual truth values. The projection/dominance result is
already established, and a two-bit example shows that its improvement need not
improve the user's actual action. A paid partial-source dominance checker is a
possible adapter but currently no new acquisition consequence is established.
[Derivation and disposition](coherence_screen.md). Park the generic claim.

The query/flip branch now has a direct compact minimax LP with rational upper
and lower checks. Its finite support is stronger than the earlier iterative
prototype, but remains an S11M continuation. The next constructive search will
seek an actual counterfactual *comparison* or information-acquisition gain,
and will not spend the remainder of the recurrence on variations of that LP.

## Fourth pass: local feedback and a return to the broader claim

Local order changes now admit exact counterfactual feedback without buying
every hidden value: adjacent swaps, fixed blocks and bounded displacements
have separate acquisition bounds. The latter has matching worst-case examples
and a compact policy automaton. Potential-relative losses further make its
updates sparse. These strengthen S06/S01/S02, not separate families for each
neighborhood. Ordinary partial-monitoring path learners are relevant controls
that may avoid extra acquisition entirely. Full-information reconstruction is
a useful capability claim; its fee savings alone do not establish total-cost
learning superiority.

Before a larger learning benchmark, return to the query/flip branch's exact
delta against minimax-regret and reward-uncertain MDP literature, and search
for a different connection to model use or changing operations. Keep the
portfolio wider than a single stream learner.

## Fifth pass: what should a useful improvement preserve?

The grouped-mean oracle preserves checked aliases while permitting individual
output revisions. It tightens one large fixed-controller regret bound without
independent coordinate scenarios, but its source premises are supplied. This
extends S11/S01; it does not create a new family for each grouping.

Two broad screens redirect tempting generic claims. [Dynamic replanning](replanning.md)
can invalidate an ex-ante robust guarantee; its menu and likelihood issues have
established treatments. [Uniform baseline improvement](baseline_tradeoff.md)
is impossible, except ties, for an affine-cost baseline optimal at an interior
source. Neither obstacle means that lower regret, cheaper information or a
useful synthesis is worthless. They prevent silently imposing an impossible
all-sources-superiority goal on the research.

## Sixth pass: evidence changes the permitted action

[Conditional repair](safe_revision.md) adds operational certificates to the
query/revision model. Chain constraints admit exact conditional mask networks;
the [executed LP](repair_results.md) and [CNF adaptation](cnf_results.md) distinguish
newly admissible actions from mere predictive information. [Geometry](repair_geometry.md)
rejects a generic claim that every logical source yields this benefit: affine
parity supports do not gain new masks from conditioning, and extra masks need
not improve the objective. The [abduction screen](certificate_selection.md)
parks a generic new hitting-set selector claim. [Prior comparison](repair_prior.md)
and [bounded-width extension](structured_repair.md) identify a narrower useful
synthesis and its proof obligations. [Fallibility](fallible_repair.md) separates
globally warranted model coverage from adaptive selection of convenient models.

## Seventh pass: local feedback versus ordinary learning

The [paid local service](local_service_results.md) saves most of the fee for
exact feedback but loses to the ordinary bandit fee control. The [free estimator](free_feedback.md)
removes extra purchases, yet its four-stream service still loses slightly.
[Predictable baselines](model_usefulness.md) and [model selection](model_selection.md)
give a conditional error-sensitive construction; its three-case probe gains
no fees. [Ordinary path feedback](path_feedback.md) provides a square-root rate
without a model and retains adverse finite results. The new off-path geometry
does not authorize biased use of the old observation propensities. Deprioritize
larger tuning sweeps until a new mechanism predicts a useful comparative gain.

## Eighth pass: information from costs and changing interfaces

[Invoice information](invoice_information.md) derives the exact degree/depth
span and checked-support consequences. [Source sensitivity](support_sensitivity.md)
shows a sharp approximate-support obstruction to full-law recovery, while
[task estimation](invoice_estimation.md) and [representation design](invoice_design.md)
retain a cheaper value-specific route with an explicit error allowance.
[Repeated-instance composition](invoice_composition.md) shows how the limit
changes when the learner can revisit the same world; fresh samples do not
provide that operation. These strengthen the probability-information and
counterfactual-interface questions without requiring a universal learner.

The closing [scientific assessment](scientific_assessment.md) retains three
distinct packages and their ordinary antecedents. New source geometry,
interface capabilities and adverse comparisons are scientific progress;
proof novelty, application utility and confirmation readiness remain separate.
