# What the conditional-repair candidate can contribute

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT; October 16 UTC. This is a comparison of specified
contracts, not a priority claim based on an exhaustive literature search.

The proposed object is an exact optimizer and checkable realization for a
bounded reasoner that pays to inspect an original Boolean source before
releasing a constrained revision. An observation can certify a revision that
was not previously admissible. The source law remains unspecified; the same
operational certification rules constrain the comparator. Query and edit
fees enter regret, while preparation and execution computation still need
pricing. [Construction](safe_revision.md), [executed chain evidence](repair_results.md)
and [CNF extension](cnf_results.md) keep these distinctions explicit.

## Closest connections read during the closing search

- [Juba (2016)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume17/13-584/13-584.pdf),
  Sections 1.1--1.2, Theorems 8 and 10, Algorithm 1 and the soundness proof on
  pages 22--23: integrated learning and logical planning is already established.
  The theorem uses weighted traces from an exploration policy, typical actions,
  bounded Strahler proofs/policies and approximately testable knowledge. This
  is stronger than our work on learning dynamics. Our supplied finite source,
  costed acquisition, atomic repair menu and exact minimax objective are a
  different restricted contract. We cannot claim to originate useful reasoning
  with fallible logical models, nor to remove its exploration premise.
- [Rigter, Lacerda and Hawes (2021)](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~nickh/papers/rigter2021aaai.pdf),
  Definition 5, Propositions 1--3 and the appendix proof of Proposition 2:
  regret dynamic programming and the distinction between rectangular and
  dependent model uncertainty are existing work. Their fully observed SSP
  formulation and option approximation do not directly establish our partially
  inspected source contract. Our separator keeps one original assignment
  through both the controller and comparator; this is a restricted structural
  reduction, not a discovery that stagewise adversarial changes can be too
  conservative. The separate JAIR 2017 URL failed to open during this pass;
  its search description is only a lead, not an additional imported theorem.
- [Zong, Lehtonen and Rintanen (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.24672),
  Section 3 Definitions 1--3 and Section 5.2 Theorem 3: exact decision diagrams
  for paid Boolean evaluation already optimize acquisition under a known
  joint distribution. Our unspecified-law regret and admissible output
  transformations differ. This does not establish a faster evaluator.
- [Abduction comparison](certificate_selection.md), contingent planning in
  [literature.md](literature.md), ordinary network-flow realization and the
  earlier minimax-regret sources supply further established ingredients.

## A defensible candidate claim, still requiring confirmation

For checked chain source and target constraints, a fixed set of r inspectable
bits, nonnegative rational inspection/edit fees and a joint pathwise cap H,
the private-history flow plus conditional mask networks and common-world
regret epigraph represents the full feasible randomized controller class.
It optimizes worst-law regret without enumerating all original assignments
or all complete query trees. Its dependence on r remains exponential. A
point-source benchmark still pays for an operational certificate; secretly
giving it all facts would change this theorem.

The incremental contribution could be this precise synthesis, its complexity
and realization proof, and a useful example where information changes the
permitted action menu. It is not the generic idea of information value or
constraint-preserving planning. The two CNF cases with 46.0% and 56.5% lower
regret than an equally querying static-menu class show why the menu distinction
matters in the exposed examples; they do not establish broad application value.

The remaining verification path is narrow enough to be credible: reconstruct
private-flow completeness with merged histories; prove the comparator and
separator use the same original assignment and operation rules; check terminal
mask realization and rational certificates independently; then compare the
result with the closest planning/robust optimization formulation. No new
confirmation attempt is started here. An equally informed ordinary formulation
ties by design. Moderate novelty of the adapter and usefulness of the restricted
contract remain open, rather than being inferred from a percentage.
