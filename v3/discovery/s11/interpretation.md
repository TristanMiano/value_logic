# What the finite objects mean for unresolved mathematics

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT interpretation review. The original goal concerns
bounded reasoning, not merely efficient Boolean arrays. The following bridges
are necessary before any application claim.

An uninspected bit can represent a bounded program predicate whose outcome has
not yet been computed. A checked source constrains the possible answer vectors
without deciding every coordinate. The task may depend only on an affine
contrast or a low-degree character. The positive constructions specify how
less information can suffice for that task, conditional on the source and
observation interface. They do not manufacture probabilities for arbitrary
undecided arithmetic, equate a timeout with falsity, or solve unbounded halting.

The repair construction changes a released configuration or explicitly chosen
output vector. It does not make a false arithmetic sentence true. To interpret
a revised bit as a change to a program, axiom or physical mechanism, supply a
versioned semantics relating the output transformation to that changed object.
Preservation of a declared Boolean target is a useful finite guarantee, but
it is not causal identification or a semantics for all counterpossibles.

A model may propose a cheap representation, mask or useful predictor. A checked
logical certificate warrants its stated consequence under its premises. A
statistical coverage statement warrants a different, explicitly probabilistic
consequence under a specified population/sampling contract. Neither can stand
in for the other. The new task-error terms and source-coverage adapter expose
that distinction while still permitting useful bounded decisions.

There is an elementary concrete application bridge for aliases: if two costly
program predicates are h(z) and h(z) xor p(z), their parity is the cheaper
predicate p(z), provided the program relation is actually checked. This avoids
resolving h(z) for that contrast. Ordinary symbolic simplification already
does this; the example alone is not a new contribution. The research question
is which richer operation/source interfaces admit a useful general guarantee,
affordable acquisition or reusable checked representation beyond that simple
cancellation. The invoice and repair constructions now give precise candidates
for that question, with their limits and ordinary counterparts explicit.

This is why progress can be worthwhile without settling the broadest learning
or metaphysical questions. A moderately novel, useful restricted construction
would meet the author's contribution standard. Its value still requires an
honest delta and credible verification, rather than a Value Logic label alone.

The CNF query selector uses model counts to split a finite feasible set.
That is an explicit heuristic on a representation, not an assertion that
possible mathematical answers are uniformly distributed. Its minimax objective
still ranges over all source laws. Confusing those two roles would turn a
combinatorial search heuristic into an unsupported probabilistic belief.
