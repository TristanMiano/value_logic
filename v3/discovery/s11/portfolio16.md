## Most promising contribution packages now

**Local feedback geometry** is the cleanest new capability claim. It combines
an exact information budget, worst-case necessity, sparse relative losses and
a tractable representation. Its next question is whether the specific bound
and construction are a worthwhile contribution relative to sequential testing,
post-certificate replay, cascade feedback and path learning. Ordinary
semi-bandit learners can avoid extra observations and remain strong economic
controls. The capability theorem does not assert they are worse learners.

**Compact query/revision regret** is a second candidate package. The finite
response lifting clarifies its relation to Mastin--Jaillet--Chin and sequence
form; checked aliases give a more expressive source interface. A useful
verification event could concern the exact reduction and its complexity,
with separate evidence for practical costs. Avoid demanding a general
reward-uncertain-MDP solution or promoting a midpoint comparison alone.

**Dependence-robust invoice learning** remains useful but currently less ready:
the exact bound is constructive, while the paid calibration route often has
unattractive sufficient sample counts. Seek a genuinely cheap information
source or a different use for the bound before another broad learner sweep.
