# Constructive assessment after the acquisition developments

Codex (GPT-6), October 12, 2026 UTC. DEVELOPMENT.

Work performed: sparse batch and adaptive acquisition derivations, exact
prototypes, primary comparisons, and a corrected sequential certificate.
This is scientific development rather than independent adversarial confirmation.

Findings by the author's questions:

- **Q1, logical uncertainty:** checked partial evaluations, compact contrast
  representations and explicit conditional models now connect to paid query
  plans. Truth receipts and fallible forecasts retain different roles. No
  evidence supports treating the independent-bit prior as a universal account.
- **Q2, counterfactuals:** pure observations permit martingale baseline removal;
  mutable probes require the earlier state-transition correction. The matching
  example additionally separates valid individual information cells from an
  implementable sequential observation strategy.
- **Q3, multiple useful models:** fixed or prospectively coded policy families
  permit evidence-dependent selection without silently adding free hypotheses.
  Conventional PAC-Bayes, symbolic evaluation, maximum closure and bounded DP
  are the relevant strong comparisons. No incremental portfolio economy has
  been demonstrated; closure loses the first small complete-cost comparison.
- **Q4, refinement:** delayed receipts, prefix-code selection costs, an explicitly
  budgeted lambda grid and monotone Bellman frontiers provide concrete update
  mechanisms. These import conventional principles; they do not establish the
  broader duties of Logical Induction or computational no-exploitation results.
- **Q5, probability information in values:** the useful carrier depends on
  the proposed policy menu. High-order parity may have rank one; informative
  probes can require correlations that current marginal values omit. A compact
  value representation neither determines an unrestricted probability model
  nor makes its acquisition or justification free.

Constructive progress is strongest in the explicit boundary between a
certificate-improvement scheduler, a real decision gain, and a sequentially
realizable query policy. The common-baseline closure certificate is a plausible
component, with a sharp small example, and the partition obstruction identifies
when it must be strengthened. The finite repair is conventional bounded DP;
its cost means it has not yet become a materially useful service.

P3-POS01 remains OUTSTANDING. P3-11 stays deferred. Readiness is unassessed:
there is no specified externally useful claim with evidence sufficient for an
80% verification forecast. Next development should emphasize cheaper verified
flow advice, realistic acquisition costs, and robustness to fallible forecast
models, while retaining alternatives rather than tuning the exposed small
frontier experiment toward a favorable comparison.
