# Broad early screens beyond the current harnesses

Codex (GPT-6), October26,2026. DEVELOPMENT; qualitative mechanism screens.
These are not new candidate families or literature priority claims.

## Collective evidence about unresolved answers

A checked statement that at most b of n model predictions are wrong can be
useful without identifying which are wrong. For a linear decision contrast
with nonnegative error impacts w_i, its worst extra loss is the sum of the b
largest impacts (with the obvious integral-budget convention). Intersecting
overlapping collective constraints gives ordinary robust optimization over
an error set. Such a certificate can survive repricing and can be cheaper
than resolving every answer, but that acquisition advantage needs an actual
proof family and cost comparison. The optimization itself is the familiar
budgeted-uncertainty mechanism; renaming its error variables mathematical
uncertainty supplies no new learning or proof-discovery guarantee. PARK the
bare mechanism. Keep aggregate proofs as potentially useful future inputs.

## Robustness of a proof bank to assumption withdrawal

If each valid proof has a dependency set, an edited theory can reuse it
exactly when all those assumptions remain admitted under the same versions.
The fewest assumptions whose withdrawal destroys every available proof is
a hitting-set quantity. Disjoint dependency paths give familiar packing or
cut arguments in restricted proof graphs; general AND/OR proof structure
does not inherit a network min-cut algorithm for free. This is ordinary
provenance and repair. PARK a stand-alone resilience score. A useful new
contribution would need paid certificate acquisition or a new structural
algorithm with a fair dependency-aware comparator.

## Robust decisions across changing information

Keeping one common source across time can improve a policy bound over
independently maximizing each future branch. But reoptimizing worst-case
choices after observing evidence can be dynamically inconsistent if the
uncertainty is coupled. Full source-dependent policy optimization and the
corresponding rectangular uncertainty comparison already express the basic
issue. No new theorem is claimed. A bounded implementation would need to
charge policy/source representation and preserve which counterfactual
observations the same source can actually produce; a sequence of pointwise
minima is not such a policy.

These screens reconnect mathematical uncertainty, fallible model scope,
reasoning expense and counterfactual reuse without pretending that familiar
methods are outside the spirit of value logic. They redirect attention to
specific resource or representation deltas, not general relabellings.

## What capped runtime observations identify

For a random completion time R in the nonnegative integers or infinity,
the expected capped work W(t)=E[min(R,t)] satisfies
W(t+1)-W(t)=Pr(R>t). The complete curve identifies the runtime law and its
noncompletion mass in the limit. Any finite prefix leaves arbitrary tail
behavior compatible. If R is the runtime of a sound but incomplete proof
searcher, noncompletion is not a negative truth label. The ordinary survival
identity is useful input to scheduling, but PARK it as a new probability
reconstruction principle. Acquiring exact expectations is itself a strong
observation contract; sampled capped work has ordinary estimation costs.

## Mid-computation switching versus proof progress

Recent ordinary algorithm-selection work proves gains by switching between
search operators at different optimization stages. A proof service whose
only reliable event is a completed checked certificate does not inherit
the intermediate fitness signal used in such an argument. Node counts,
plausible partial proofs and a long unsuccessful run do not establish
distance to a proof. Fallible progress models may guide paid work, but the
fallback-inclusive guarantee must survive misleading signals. Our shared
proof adapter has that narrow robust accounting; the current integration
panel does not show an aggregate gain over fallback. Keep stronger dynamic
ordinary portfolios as comparisons before proposing new experiments.

## Several fallible models with a collective validity promise

If at least r of m set-valued models contain the actual source, a sound
combined source is the set of points belonging to at least r model sets.
Intersecting all models silently strengthens the promise and can be wrong.
For intervals this coverage-depth region follows from a sorted endpoint
sweep; higher-dimensional computation may be much harder. This is ordinary
robust set aggregation. PARK a generic quorum-model claim unless a new
representation or paid-acquisition benefit is demonstrated. The validity
promise must be warranted externally, not inferred from agreement alone.
