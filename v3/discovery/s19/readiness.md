## Why this is a promising positive construction

AR1 has a short two-direction witness proof. Its local convexity and
independence premises are explicit; every returned rational value has a
constructive source witness. It recovers gaps that endpoints erase, and
replaces witness-assignment enumeration by a parameterized full-range
algorithm. The exponential-output example explains a real representation
cost instead of hiding it. The noisy, positive-coordinate example makes
the gap consequence survive beyond an exact-equality toy.

Development evidence includes180 rational polygon cases against two
separate composition routes and472 source-witness checks. The exact
certificate panel contains44 accepted bundles,854 bounded and336 empty
local slices, and273 rejected targeted corruptions. The verifier imports
no optimizer. Its generic row arithmetic is broader than the producer's
mostly two-dimensional examples; two manual three-dimensional cases do
not constitute a generic high-dimensional implementation audit.

As an elicitation aid, the mathematical/algorithmic core looks about90--95%
likely to survive careful reconstruction with ordinary repairs, while a
sufficient nonredundant and useful contribution conditional on a sound core
looks about65--75%. These are dependent judgments, not independently
measured probabilities. Their scale motivates roughly65% overall; multiplying
test pass rates would not. Implementation/interface uncertainty is included
in that overall assessment rather than declared settled by finite probes.

## What still blocks promotion

1. **Closest-result comparison.** Ahmed--Guan already study inverse optimal
   values; max-affine fitting and disjunctive LP already encode the source.
   Subset-cover and min-max convolution are established. The remaining
   question is whether the exact whole-range parameter/output statement
   is a useful nonroutine specialization, or already follows explicitly
   from an existing treatment of anonymous optimum observations. Broad
   searches have not located an identical statement; that is weak evidence,
   not proof of novelty. A compact assumption-by-assumption comparison is
   more valuable next than another random panel.
2. **A justified retained-information duty.** The quote and risk-envelope
   examples are constructed interfaces. Full source access, cheap winner
   labels, homogeneous generators and a single direct band query can remove
   much of the motivation. Convex scalar losses need only sharp endpoints.
   Identify a realistic repeated-query or restricted-disclosure duty and
   count its acquisition, retained descriptions, checking and query work.
   A useful theorem can qualify without a deployed benchmark, but its
   intended use must withstand those ordinary controls.
3. **A focused reconstruction.** Recheck AR1's completeness, empty-slice
   treatment, rational source recovery and the certificate's entire input
   binding contract in a fresh pass. The current evidence is generated and
   interpreted by the same model, with shared geometric helpers. A later
   confirmation phase must follow the repository protocol; this development
   screen is not that phase.

Evidence that could raise the estimate is a clear delta from the nearest
parameterized/disjunctive result, a credible duty that needs the output,
and a fresh reconstruction without a substantive counterexample. More
derivatives, more same-model test cases or an arbitrary percentage gain
against an endpoint-only baseline would not by themselves justify80%.
An exact prior theorem with the same contract would lower the estimate and
may park this event; the result would remain useful implementation knowledge.
