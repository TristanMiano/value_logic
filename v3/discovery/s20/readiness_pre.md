any P3-11 confirmation attempt. The event remains [s19/claim.md](../s19/claim.md).

**Readiness: 80--90%, central 85%. Disposition: ready to propose and prepare
rigorous confirmation of the specified bounded contribution.** This is a
subjective forecast of the whole event, including useful moderate novelty.
It is not a calibrated frequency, a statistical confidence interval, the
probability that the model premises are true, or a forecast of universal

## Evidence that changes the assessment from s19

The earlier 50--80%, central 65% forecast had three concrete blockers.
Their present dispositions are:

1. **Proof and checker reconstruction:** the two directions of AR1, all
   empty/outside conventions, the subset recurrence, rational witness
   recovery and exact certificate identities have been reconstructed from
   the original input. The new audit includes arbitrary-dimensional and
   mixed-dimensional slices, degeneracy, huge exact constants and global
   inconsistency. The separate verified query service now constructs a
   compatible source for a requested value and binds it to a copied input.
   Finite probes support these interfaces; the argument remains essential.
2. **A useful output duty:** the max-affine measurement example uses
   scalar-only aggregate observations and independently bounded piece
   parameters. Its full-dimensional, noisy source has two prediction
   intervals. A fixed-readout consistency service uses the complete set
   for later adaptive bands and source requests. Costs include local LPs,
   compilation, retained certificates, verification, comparisons and full
   witness output. This is a constructed conditional use, not deployment
   evidence or a measured saving. Winner labels, one-band feasibility,
   convex losses, known slopes and an added model piece have explicit
   controls; several remove the gap or make a cheaper method preferable.
3. **Closest-result comparison:** the new comparison goes beyond generic
   keyword searches. It checks inverse parameter sets, bounded-error PWA
   fitting, active-piece MIP, compatible-subset covering, outcome ranges,
   generic semiring outside computation and compiled solution sets. It
   credits the substantial overlap. In particular, the outside caps are
   an ordinary inside-outside specialization, and the scalar clipping
   identity explains how to derive AR1 from set-valued circuit semantics.
   The surviving delta is the explicit whole-image specialization with
   its receipt-parameter, output, witness and certificate bounds. None of
   the read results states that same combined input/output guarantee.

The judgment is that this surviving formal adaptation is likely enough to
count as a useful, moderately novel contribution under the author's stated
standard. It is not a newly discovered inference algebra. The estimate
would be much lower for that different event, or for an empirical speedup
claim. Those were never requirements of V19-R/v1 and are not being dropped
after an adverse result.

The increase is not based on the number of tests, the new multinomial
component theorem, time spent, or a desire to complete the recurrence.
The component, forest/cycle and faster-convolution extensions remain
separate. A contribution using one of them requires its own assessment.

## Principal residual uncertainties and failure conditions

- A domain expert may reasonably judge the specialization too routine,
  despite its explicit resource/output consequence. This is the largest
  remaining risk and the principal reason for stopping well below near
  certainty. Confirmation must reconstruct the strongest ordinary route
  and state exactly what the synthesis adds.
- The Cerny--Hladik 2016 full text remains inaccessible; only its abstract
  and stated scope were read. The author-shared browser page requires a
  CAPTCHA. No universal absence-of-prior-work claim is made. An earlier
  theorem with the same conditioned product-source contract and complete
  range/resource result would displace the novelty component.
- The implementation is checked development code, not a formally verified
  runtime or a hardened hostile-input service. Confirmation must resolve
  source/certificate/interface mismatches rather than assume finite passes
  prove universal soundness. Hash binding is to canonical serialization of
  the supplied problem object, not authentication or raw-file provenance.
- A real application can violate fixed parameters, product uncertainty,
  piece count, error bounds or affine structure. The contribution is
  conditional on those premises. Its worked consequence is mathematical;
  no empirical transfer or economic advantage is included in this event.

There is no relevant calibrated forecasting track record that converts
these judgments into frequencies. Same-model familiarity can make this
estimate optimistic. A substantive defect or a genuinely matching prior
result must lower the assessment or return the event to development;
successful completion of a time floor cannot repair either problem.
