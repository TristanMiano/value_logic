## Established ideas that account for the new interfaces

The [partial-range derivation](partial_ranges.md) credits abstract interpretation,
anytime inference, bound propagation, indexed unsatisfiable cores and rational
certificates. Its contribution to this project is their explicit attachment
to every indexed receipt slice, with monotone global interval brackets and
original-source witnesses for positive answers. The opposite reuse directions
matter: a feasible point supplies subsets of its compatible receipt mask,
whereas a dual bound or Farkas support supplies supersets of its required mask.
Neither sparse certificates nor interruption-safe bounds are new generic ideas.

The exact new source-reading record is in partial_ranges.md:

- [Gokavarapu](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.24339), v2: rational piecewise-linear
  verification, sections1--2, Theorems4.6--4.9, sparsity4.12, and Lemma4.22
  with their specified proofs. Its certificate interface is a close antecedent;
  the read results do not supply the retained-receipt whole-image contract.
- [Maua and de Campos](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.6424): Theorems1/4 and their
  displayed proofs, and Algorithm3. Anytime factor-set bounds are established;
  their rates and probabilistic contract are not imported.
- [Bottesch, Haslbeck and Thiemann](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-29007-8_13):
  sections1,3.1 and4, covering incremental indexed cores and Farkas translation.
- [Cousot and Cousot](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cousot/COUSOTpapers/POPL77.shtml):
  author introduction/summary only. No uninspected theorem is credited as read.

For the decision interfaces, partial_decisions.md records the precise reading

## Structural controls that narrow when the general method is useful

[Dlask2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-024-05980-z),
section1.1, Remark2/equation1, Theorem4 and Proposition1's proof, already gives
the greatest-element/max-closure machinery used in greatest.md. The adaptation
finds forced receipt witnesses, an exact interval and rational segment sources
using2n LPs in a restricted class. This is a reason to dispatch away from the
general exponential method. It is not independent evidence of V19 novelty.

The [2021 inverse-image paper](https://perso-laris.univ-angers.fr/~hardouin/RenatoIEEETAC2020.pdf)
was read at its introduction, sectionIII equations25--34, Lemma1 and
Proposition1 proofs, and sectionIII-C equations53--58/Algorithm1 complexity.
It computes a full inverse set using disjoint hyperrectangles. The middle
partition construction was not fully reconstructed. The proposed bound
O(n*p^(n+1)) belongs to that paper's notation and model; no literal comparison
to V19's n,k is made without translating dimensions and inputs.

The [WODES2024 paper](https://perso-laris.univ-angers.fr/~hardouin/Wodes2024_WinckGuilherme.pdf)
was read at the introduction, section2.1 two-sided/residuation argument,
section3.2 equations5--6, sections4.1--4.2 and conclusion. Its recursive
state estimate and fixed-point assumptions should not be confused with the
2025 Automatica exact-set result. A general polynomial guarantee is not
inferred from the finite fixed-point discussion.

The [ECC2022 predecessor](https://perso-laris.univ-angers.fr/~hardouin/ECC22_Guilherme.pdf)
was already read during A1; this later pass revisited the author-hosted copy
and its explicit worst-case generator-count caveat. tropical_boundary.md
illustrates the same closure obstruction already proved in A1 with the new
sum-strip example. It is not a second contribution or an independent novelty
clearance. The [Automatica2025 full proof](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109824003935)
and [2025 SMT/fixed-point full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10626-025-00413-w)
remain unread beyond the stated abstract/snippet scope. Author PDFs timed out;
HAL returned an access block; the author-linked thesis was blocked too. None
was bypassed. Cerny--Hladik2016's full text remains unavailable as recorded
in the sealed review. Access failures are uncertainty, not novelty evidence.

The max-closed semilinear CSP lead [Bodirsky--Mamino](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.04184)
was screened only through its abstract and author material. No general

## Computational refinement versus new observations

refinement_gap.md sharpens the earlier bound by preserving dependence among
the actions' worst losses. Its exact result is over all supports permitted
by a partial cell signature. This is an abstraction of the original source;
a maximizing signature witness need not be geometrically attainable. The
extension can establish robust optimality before the full range is known.
It cannot certify actual-state safety or make new evidence worthless.

observation_value.md uses the proper signal-contingent policy class for
buying a new observation, and bounds its value under the same partial support.
The conventional ancestor is particularly strong:
[Paradiso et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.04115), section2/equations1a--1b,
section3.1.1 and Lemma1/EC.1 proof already express robust information
discovery and its information monotonicity. The finite threshold-cover DP
is an adapter using ordinary subset covering. Neither that paper's general
algorithms nor the full published revision were audited here. The distinction
between information types also appears in the search lead *Deciding under
Partial Ignorance*, but its full PDF timed out; no result is imported from it.
