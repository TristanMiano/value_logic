# S30: a common learned cost law can drive a checked repair

**DEVELOPMENT — ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro), October 12, 2026 UTC.**

The new [executed service](population_service.py) connects the earlier
common-CDF source to an actual logical repair certificate. It buys a raw
negative proof and partial cost observations, then returns one checked repair
whose expected-cost regret is at most the requested tolerance. The targeted
policy closes after **29 physical audits** while identifying **none** of the
audited latent modes exactly. Full latent identification uses **58 audits**.
The strong ordinary direct-contrast policy also uses **29**, with the same
named bill. A deterministic finite-cohort calculation closes on the same
29-observation prefix. This is an implemented composition and a useful
acquisition example, with no demonstrated advantage over those stronger
ordinary alternatives.

The source, schedule, raw proof, every observation, confidence state, support
certificate and fee component are in [the v2 results](population_v2_results.json).
The [independent reconstruction](population_check_results.json) checks both
preserved versions, six arms, 180 gate stages and 896 CDF support certificates
without new acquisitions. The [focused review](population_review.md) provides
the detailed statistical and logical argument.

## What is newly connected, and what was already established

The [phase-three probability-information derivation](../../derivations/02_probability_information.md)
already distinguishes full-law recovery, absolute values, contrasts,
preferences and one warranted decision. In particular, its Section 5 gives
a changing optimal decision with unidentified common absolute costs.
[S27 value acquisition](../s05/value_acquisition.md) already derives partial
payoff acquisition, its censoring/sampling width decomposition, complete
range-target bundles, fresh-row duties and the finite-cohort alternative.
[Payoff routing](../s05/value_routing.md) already contracts contiguous payoff
plateaus and compares the pointwise CDF source with a stronger retained-row
source. Those principles are inherited, not new S30 discoveries.

The new step here is their executable composition with the independently
checked original-source repair cover. The CDF support oracle is called on
the actual action-versus-cover contrasts, including signed contrasts, and
returns a checked attaining mean-cost vector. The same common source event
warrants the adaptively selected repair and every intermediate evaluation.
No solver status is used as a probability label or lower proof, and no
empirical mode frequencies are supplied before acquisition.

This synthesis also exposes a useful distinction between two remaining gaps:
insufficient information about the cost law, and insufficient logical lower
evidence at the resulting mean costs. Resolving one need not resolve the other.

## The fixed development contract

There are 64 known physical identities, each with an unknown latent value
H in 0,1,2,3. Before inspection, the source fixes 128 iid uniform identity
draw occurrences with replacement. The recorded realization contains 59
distinct identities. Multiplicities remain in every empirical calculation;
one physical observation updates all occurrences of that identity.

The public cost map is:

| H | Cost of repair 1 | Cost of repair 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 or 1 | 0 | 4 |
| 2 or 3 | 4 | 0 |

The original hard formula is `x1 OR x2`; the two soft violations are the
truth values of x1 and x2. Both single-true assignments are independently
checked feasible actions. The replacement price is 6 and tolerance is 2.
The target is **one fixed action at the mean costs of the uniform 64-row
population**, with its uncertainty warranted by the stated sample event.

Each arm buys its own complete query at objective (1,1), budget zero. Its
checked negative tree covers every feasible repair and supplies the lower
`min(w1,w2)` at every nonnegative mean-cost vector w. This is enough to make
the contrast support calculation an exact logical comparison for this small
source. The CDF box may still be an outer approximation to the physical source.

The observation menu is the existing truthful cut `H <= j`. The relevant
policy queries only cut 1, in fixed first-appearance identity order. A direct
ordinary contrast calculation chooses that same cut. The full-latent policy
also asks cut 0 or 2 to identify each inspected H before reevaluating its gate.
No new direct-payoff sensor is assumed.

The fixed-n outward CDF radius is 1/8 and failure allowance is 1/20. The code
checks the exponential tail conservatively with exact arithmetic. The imported
statistical input is the DKW-Massart bound; the primary statement and its
one-sided strengthening are in
[Reeve, Theorem 1 and Corollary 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.16651v1).
The fixed-sample event contains the unobserved full empirical CDF; truthful
interval refinements enclose it pathwise. It consequently covers all later
refinements and action choices on this fixed sample. The development seed is
a reproducible realization, not a calibration study or a proof of a deployed
sampler's randomness.

## More accurate probabilities can leave a logical gap

Write the best fixed-action cost as:

```math
V(w)=\min\{p,C^*(w)\}.
```

It is concave because it is the minimum of the affine costs of all feasible
repairs and the constant replacement action. For a known distribution of H,
let $`\mu=\mathbb E[m(H)]`$. The target used here compares a selected fixed
action with $`V(\mu)`$. A policy allowed to observe H before selecting its
action instead has comparator $`\mathbb E[V(m(H))]`$. Their difference is:

```math
J=V(\mu)-\mathbb E[V(m(H))]\geq0.
```

Suppose a cheaper evaluator retains only modal clipped lower certificates
$`\ell_h\leq V(m_h)`$ and bounds a fixed action a by its mean cost minus
$`\mathbb E[\ell_H]`$. Even if the distribution is known exactly, that
bound decomposes as:

```math
\mathrm{cost}(a;\mu)-\mathbb E[\ell_H]
=\underbrace{\mathrm{cost}(a;\mu)-V(\mu)}_{\text{fixed-action regret}}
+J+\mathbb E[V(m(H))-\ell_H].
```

Thus it can contain a Jensen gap in addition to the genuine logical proof
gap. Further probability precision alone cannot generally remove either.
In the two-repair family, every mode has optimum zero. Under a known balanced
law, the fixed optimum costs 2, so perfect modal lowers remain zero while a
mean-objective checked cover proves the correct fixed optimum 2. The executed
service uses that cover; it does not identify the two comparator duties.

The saved realized mean is `(17/8,15/8)`. The returned second repair has
zero regret against the best fixed action, but expected regret 15/8 against
the mode-informed comparator, whose optimum is zero. This latter equality
with the reported certificate bound is incidental, not a general identity.

## How source geometry can reduce what must be learned

[S29](s29_affine.md) supplies a separately checked feasible-source relation
$`Az=b`$. For any two feasible repairs u,z, their difference satisfies
$`A(u-z)=0`$. A cost component in the row space of A therefore cancels in
the payoff contrast $`m(H)^\top(u-z)`$. This applies before computing the
payoff's oscillation, variation, CDF support or range-target audit plan.

The older S27 variation bound already controls every represented contrast
on one common CDF event. A source proof can now reduce the family of contrasts
that needs evaluating and expose a simpler one. This is an integration of
the existing bound with newly admitted logical structure, not a new
concentration theorem. If every feasible repair shares an H-dependent mandatory
cost, that cost cancels in repair comparisons. It remains in a comparison
with a fixed replacement price. A valid dominance certificate can sometimes
remove replacement from the current decision, but it cannot be silently dropped.

The [quotient derivation](quotient_audits.md) gives the corresponding exact
linear representation and action-safe audit cells. The
[ambiguity lower certificate](ambiguity_theory.md) goes in the other direction:
it can prove that the remaining cost uncertainty is too large for **any** fixed
action to close, even after additional logical work. Its mixture coefficients
are adversarial lower-bound weights, not learned probabilities. These two
directions suggest a concrete next controller: determine which current gap
can be reduced by a logical proof, a witness, or a cost observation, and pay
only for a bounded useful plan. The quotation and proof costs must be measured.

## Disposition

S30 is a new DEVELOPMENT population-source integration, separate from
F01's finite-row S28-B1 validation event. It supports a correct implemented
connection between partial cost learning and logical repair. It establishes
neither an economic advantage over direct contrast/finite-cohort reasoning nor
a positive confirmation result. The next useful comparison must retain those
ordinary controls and charge the acquisition of any helpful logical geometry.
