### Complete cost and symbolic price region

Keep resources separate before pricing. For each arm retain: initial historical
acquisition charged once for the declared reuse horizon; current raw admission;
quotation, compilation and objective updates; all successful and unresolved
native/proof work; independent checking; observation handling and actual external
audits; serialization and retained bytes; CPU/wall measurements and peak memory.
Include all components incurred by the ordinary controls too.

V1 uses a declared billing convention with no duplicated charges. Let `U` be
named billed service units, `A` external audits, `T` additional host CPU not
already charged in U, and `B` retained byte-time not already charged in U.
Then `C = U + a*A + c*T + s*B` in named-unit-equivalent currency. Any purchased
native service fee not represented in those terms is an additional explicit
coefficient; it is not zero by omission. Common sunk acquisition may cancel in
an incremental contrast, but the full-horizon result must also be reported.

Before generating confirmation streams, use development invoices and external
contracts to freeze positive reference rates `a0,c0,s0` and the rectangular
region `a/a0 in [1/4,4]`, `c/c0 in [1/2,2]`, `s/s0 in [1/2,2]`. Fix any
other coefficients the same way. The claimed twofold audit-price interval must
work throughout the host/storage ranges. A missing price can remain symbolic
with an explicit bound; an unbounded omitted component prevents a full-cost
pass. The exact rate values, source generator, seeds, caps and implementations
must be committed before confirmation exposure. This is a conditional symbolic
cost claim unless those rates also have an external economic warrant.

### Verification plan V1

1. Reconstruct the uniform certificate and full-outcome quotations under their
   exact nonnegative-source assumptions, including source conflict and
   unresolved branches. Re-admit the load-bearing raw evidence.
2. Freeze the source generator, candidate and complete comparator dependency
   closures, rates, caps, seed-generation procedure and paired analysis before
   producing the 24 fresh streams. Permit no selection using realized optima.
3. Execute actual acquisitions for every arm with separate retained state and
   invoices. Randomize timing order and use three whole-run timing repeats;
   semantic and purchase results must reproduce. Repeats measure timing, not
   72 independent source samples.
4. Independently check every returned gate. Calculate paired full-cost savings
   against the best comparator envelope, retaining all streams and failed
   requests. Require the one-sided 95% lower paired-bootstrap bound on pooled
   savings to exceed 10%, with resampling stratified by the six design cells;
   report sensitivity to timing and symbolic-rate variation. This supports the
   declared generator domain, not arbitrary real-world deployment.
5. Assess whether that resource consequence is material to P3-POS01 and how it
   relates to the strongest ordinary construction. A sound implementation
   without the positive cost/contribution result does not resolve F01 true.

The plan is deliberately still prospective. Its preparation requirements and
the risk that the proposed controller is merely an expensive ordinary planner
are included in the forecast. Final freeze occurs only on promotion; exposed
development runs remain development.

## 2. Why 25–50%, and what should move it

The existing [joint service](../s06/joint_findings.md) executes the joined cost
and logical evidence path, returns all sixteen checked outputs under several
policies, and can stop before every cost is known. The
[anchor construction](../s06/source_anchor.md) gives a reconstructable cheap
transport mechanism, complete audit/logical quotations and concrete opposite
acquisition regimes. The [native](../s06/native_findings.md) and
[proof](../s06/rup_findings.md) bridges make a serious warm comparison callable.
Those observations support taking S28-B1 seriously.

The key obstacle is the material cost consequence. The executed broad
shared-deficit rule costs 43,366,220 named arm units versus 380,207 for
audit-first, before remaining cost components; the later scalar/hull evidence
is replay and has not demonstrated new acquisition savings. The strongest
ordinary controls can use the same bounds. Small lookahead may exploit mixed
audit/proof opportunities, but its quotation cost, complete proof bill and
transfer to fresh cases could consume the advantage. Cost support and small
finite scopes also limit how much the result would establish. These facts
support an early, wide numerical estimate well below promotion.

Raise the forecast when actual bundle executions preserve all-outcome validity,
show material complete-cost savings against the strongest matched controls,
and repeat across prospectively varied development sources with a robust price
region. Lower it when quotation/proof overhead dominates, the advantage vanishes
under equal native reuse, or the required source assumptions make the positive
claim insubstantial. Passing routine tests or adding files earns no fixed
probability increment. A same-model review is reasoning input, not independent
calibration.

At baseline the central gap is **45 percentage points to 80%**, or **55 points
to 90%**. The lower endpoint is **55/65 points** below those gates. A range
extending below 80% does not qualify for promotion. Each subsequent recurrence
must give an updated numerical range and reason, including an unchanged range
when new evidence is immaterial. New unfavorable information may reduce it.

## 3. A dashboard that follows the work

Preserve the current historical measurement rows and exact JSON. Move retired
272-edit and 80-request duties into an explicitly historical view if the main
table becomes unwieldy. Keep a compact current S28-B1 table with one row per
new hourly recurrence and at least these metrics:

| Metric | Definition and useful display |
|---|---|
| Checked output coverage | Checked tolerance-valid outputs / 16 for the fixed s06 development stream. Record unresolved requests. This is a reliability guard and may remain 100%. |
| External observations | Actual new audits used to close that same stream, including observations whose purchase did not lead to closure. Lower is favorable only at the same output duty and with the other resources shown. |
| Logical purchases | Actual native/complete-proof purchases on that same stream, broken down in the source record. Never compare unlike purchase units as though equal. |
| Complete-cost margin | `100*(C_best_control-C_candidate)/C_best_control` at a frozen reference tariff, plus the symbolic audit-price interval meeting the 10% threshold. An empty region is **empty / 0 qualifying price intervals**, not N/A. If a component remains unpriced, show the exact inequality and missing coefficient bound rather than a fictitious total. |
| Quote overhead | Paid quotation/selection units as a fraction of the whole measured service bill, with whole bill retained. This directly tracks the bottleneck exposed by the previous broad priority rule. |
| Promotion forecast | F01 probability range, central estimate, same-event percentage-point change and gap to 80%/90%. |

Use the actual new acquisition stream for these numbers. A new source or request
order creates a new scope ID; retain the old fixed scope as carried evidence
and report the new developmental scope separately. Scope IDs bind source,
request order, tolerance, caps, initial admission and price contract. The
principal should add exact selectors/hashes from the actual s07 outputs rather
than guessing values from this proposal.

If a metric is not remeasured during a later constructive round, display the
latest value as **carried from s07; no new measurement**, including its age and
last actual measurement in JSON. Do not call carry-forward a rerun or assign
`delta = 0` as though a new measurement occurred. Actual comparable measurements
get their change since the last actual measurement, including unfavorable or
zero changes. This lets the table remain informative while the text explains
what the new research contributes. Revisit the forecast every round even when
the numerical experimental columns are carried forward.

The JSON should distinguish `measurement_status`, `observed_in_row`,
`scope_id`, `variant_id`, `value`, `previous_actual_row`, `delta`, and
`sources`; forecast records additionally need `event_id`, assessment UTC,
