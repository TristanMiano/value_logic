the actual purchase order, and **every entry of each local named-operation

existing archive. Reconstruction then checks all 36 exported robust-proposal

complete UNSAT proof trees and their weighted-source bindings, and all 28 actual
physical audit receipts in order. Hidden modes are read only by this diagnostic
verifier to check receipt truth; they are not supplied to either reconstructed
decision policy. New witnesses and proofs are retained only at their actual
event positions, and the resulting complete banks match the saved banks.

The separate schedule reconstruction verifies the first informative audit,
one proposal per actual row/price/mode-set/lower state, persistent engine reuse,
actual enabled-row bound updates, the singleton-only complete fallback and its

intermediate recorded evaluator output, including its invoice, also matches.

Fresh finite enumeration of the original eight-variable logical sources gives
the exact feasible violation masks. It confirms that each of the 36 proposals
attains the minimum of the **scalar-anchor surrogate** at its actual cost state;
this is a newly computed diagnostic, not a conclusion drawn from native status.
All 32 final outputs have exact finite-source regret **zero**, while their
reported conservative certificate bounds range from 0 through 4. The two
schedules return identical final action, model, mask, method and bound on every
request. These facts concern the constructed source only.

The whole-arm ordering is opposite to the native-only ordering. In this harness,
each progress save serializes the full result, including previously completed
arms. The second arm therefore repeatedly writes the first arm's records too.
These whole-arm timings are actual observations of this execution but do not
establish comparative algorithm speed. Process time and wall time remain
separate measurements; neither is converted into named units.

The valid economic conclusion is the named-unit and purchase-count comparison

default. A full monetary or deadline advantage has therefore not been shown.

## Limits and next useful comparison

No source-admission, action-feasibility, audit-order, gate or named-meter defect
was found in these saved executions. Native primal column arrays were not
retained, so the checker cannot reconstruct the solver's original floating
integrality test or exact native execution. It instead checks every exported
Boolean model and violation mask against the original source and confirms the
scalar optimum separately on this small finite domain. Native objectives, dual
bounds, optimal statuses and timing remain recorded guidance; they never supply
a lower proof. Persistent object identifiers and call counters verify the saved
reuse chronology, not unobserved retention of a solver's branch tree.

The strongest demonstrated control here is ordinary audit-before-witness with
the same expanded menu. A further useful controller would avoid repeatedly
requesting the same scalar-optimal witness unless a changed source/lower state
can alter the useful action, while retaining a checked proof fallback. That is
a new implementation choice requiring its own prospective execution; the saved
result does not show that such a policy is already optimal or that its benefit
transfers to new sources. The mature scope remains scheduling and certificate
composition under this menu, not superiority over an older menu or promotion
of the broader F01 claim.
