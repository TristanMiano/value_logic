## Small records can support new counterfactual decisions

The numerical route has the clearest focused mathematical contribution
target. A scalar response and a warranted operator edit now have sharp
finite-distance envelopes with explicit extremizers. Retaining a second
scalar, the squared norm of an old solve vector, gives stronger optimal
envelopes. The upper endpoint reduces to one convex scalar problem;
the lower endpoint distinguishes finite attainment from an infimum.
A separately paid operator-norm cap strengthens the remaining cases.

This changes what a bounded reasoner can decide before a fresh full solve.
With the two partial receipts in receipt_decision.md, exact outward
arithmetic proves the revised response is below0.24. The smaller receipt
source admits a counterexample at0.256. The certificate function uses no
new matrix operator call. This is an information/capability separation,
not a claim that original receipt acquisition or edit certification was free.
The cap example separately certifies a lower threshold0.045 that neither
old-value information nor a norm cap alone can certify.

Other useful consequences are evidence retention and receipt placement.
Pairwise optimal scalar distances do not form a metric, so replacing an
old anchor by a newer scalar interval can lose a valid constraint. Four
redesigned rational receipts give a certified band about49.68% narrower
than the earlier four-receipt placement under the same precision allowance.
These are exact contract improvements, not measured runtime gains.

Robust least squares, graph-subspace geometry, rational interpolation and
operator-interpolation SDPs are strong conventional ancestors. The new
target is an explicit optimal small-record contract and its partial-
arithmetic decision service. A close match in that literature could absorb
much of the claimed mathematical delta. A focused source comparison is
more valuable next than another grid of random matrices.

## Incomplete evidence can protect changing model scopes jointly

The model route connects three previously separate questions: which
consequences a limited reasoner may use, how incomplete cases can train
that permission, and how paid refinement can widen it. Exact graph/code
arguments compile the equations needed by a target and receipt budget.
Rado's theorem gives a hidden-rank certificate for separate feasible clouds;
ordinary block completion handles some joint receipts without discarding
cross-case dependence. A standard maximal inequality supplies a sequential
fixed-risk stopping rule. Once accepted, one latent protected set supports
later truthful refinements and model revisions that retain the cohort.

The worked graph example expands maximal authorized integer budget1 to5.
In a separate joint-receipt example, preserving dependence changes the
rank upper bound20 to1 while retaining980 equations; the same established
scenario bound changes from0.172065 to0.033690. The gain is a stronger
certificate from retained information. It is not a measured sample-cost
or population-error reduction. The affine counterfactual adapter shows
how a specific changed parity can be justified even when the entire
feature model cannot be transported, provided intervention semantics are
independently supplied.

The literature screen materially narrowed this route's novelty claim.
Garatti/Campi's scenario envelope already slightly beats our new elementary
binomial envelope on its initial headline example. Partial-label conformal
prediction already uses truthful domination of latent evidence. Therefore
neither a new generic incomplete-data validity principle nor the original
6.24%-to-3.38% comparison is the contribution. The plausible synthesis is
the precise incomplete-evidence certificate, changing scope and retained
common event, with a useful acquisition duty still to be established.

The guarantee is frequentist and population-specific. It is not a95%
posterior that a particular observed model is right. Selective forgetting,
unwarranted population shifts and unverified intervention semantics break
the intended inference. confidence_scope.md gives explicit counterexamples
and separates paid shift warrants from the original guarantee.

## Nonlinear acquisition buys a scoped fee improvement

The acquisition work moved beyond exact scalar parity to vector and
approximate duties. A classical seven-bit Hamming quantizer, adapted to
the private-read/count interface, has12.5% mean bit error and at most one
error per block. At reset fee100 its modeled fee is at least6.0834% below
the lower bound for EVERY affine-terminal-cell protocol at the same error;
the exact rational separation holds for reset fee>55.5. This comparison
prices queries and resets. It does not price all encoder/decoder work.

The nonlinear representation matters: its posterior one-error ball has
the whole cube as its affine hull, so that outer representation discards
its useful coordinate predictions. This is a concrete example of a mostly
false complete model whose individual uses remain reliable. The coding
principle is classical, and a conventional nonlinear realization ties.

A stronger pure-summary duty gives an exact one-episode cap/mean-query
optimum. A small all-functions screen closes the seven-bit multi-episode
minimum cap at7, with a23-query mean construction versus30 for the earlier
cap8 construction. It does not establish the optimal mean bill or an
all-size theorem. Purity conditioned on each coin tape is stronger than
distributional privacy after averaging hidden coins. Keep those scopes
visible instead of converting a restrictive result into a practical claim.

The interface remains this route's main usefulness risk. If an application
can directly transmit a computed summary, the artificial invoice restriction
may disappear. The next useful question is where the joint acquisition and
delivery restriction is justified, or whether its exact theorem is already
contained in cost/causal coding. Repeated finite policy comparisons alone
would not resolve that question.
