# The general Boolean extension is classical abductive machinery

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT, October 22, 2026. A short generative screen;
no new generic inference method or large prototype is selected.

For a nonempty Boolean model C, allowed targets T, nonnegative receipt
prices and budget B, keep the same conditional-release rule as
target_warrants.md. The largest protective world set is the set satisfying
all prime implicates K of C with a literal on some target j in T for
which the other coordinates of K cost at most B to observe.

Necessity: if x violates such K, observe the falsifying assignment on
K's other literals. This assignment is consistent with C: otherwise
K without its target literal would already be an implicate, contradicting
primality. The target literal is then implied and wrong at x.

Sufficiency: a legal implication from observed S to a target literal
corresponds to an implicate containing that target literal and the
negated observation literals. A prime sub-implicate must contain the
target literal, since otherwise the observations would be inconsistent
with C. Its other coordinates are a subset of S and remain affordable.
If x satisfies all such clauses, no permitted prediction is wrong.

This specializes to the affine-circuit characterization. It differs from
requiring every target-plus-observation projection to match C: it preserves
the correctness of actual consistent-fiber predictions and allows an
unrelated model contradiction to cause abstention. It also differs from
s13's fixed-action permission closure, which need not preserve future
conditional permissions.

The central correspondence is directly anticipated by classical abduction
through prime implicates. Nordh and Zanuttini's *What makes propositional
abduction tractable*, Section 6.2, Definition 36 and Lemma 37 (printed p29),
attributes the explanation characterization to Reiter and de Kleer.
Dechter and Pearl's *Structure identification in relational data* (1992),
Section 3.2, treats strongest restricted-scope CNF approximations via
projections. These are close ordinary antecedents, not mere neighboring
keywords. Target/price restrictions and the adequacy interpretation are
an adapter, not evidence of a new general logic.

Computing all required prime implicates can be expensive. The budget caps
evidence used in the conclusion, not the work to find the conclusion.
The graph parity specialization has a concrete polynomial compilation
route using rooted cycle bases; general propositional abduction does not
inherit that tractability. No generic prime-implicate harness is warranted
merely to re-demonstrate this established correspondence.
