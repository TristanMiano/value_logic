# Useful contribution targets after s12

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT. These are candidate-specific assessment targets,
not a freeze, promotion, confirmation attempt or requirement to spend the next
recurrence on one candidate. All remain **UNASSESSED** at the full contribution
event. Broad discovery and inexpensive rejection remain the default.

## R12-S: a task-scoped permission and evidence-reuse service

Object: [scoped_service.md](scoped_service.md), with exact binary models,
fixed permitted observation origins/target scopes, branch-aware model unions,
one-origin constant/copy/complement lineage, exact observations and supplied
source-adequacy warrant. A positive verification event would establish:

1. The general local-projection compiler characterizes the full stated
   coordinatewise action menu, and its source contradiction rule is explicit.
2. Fixed binary sources admit exact projection updates under every consistent
   unit receipt; the initial preparation and per-stream work bounds include
   repeated input records, current-register initialization and new pair scopes.
3. The task envelope is the largest source preserving the declared conditional
   projections, including conflict detection, and its weaker adequacy event
   suffices through adaptive observations and exact lineage updates.
4. Alternative models are kept separate until after conditioning. One initial
   coverage event warrants the whole adaptive stream, with unconditional
   rather than automatic conditional safety. The stronger late target uses
   the [envelope of the model union](union_adequacy.md), which can be larger
   than the union of individual envelopes. Per-target marginal error warrants
   must also be compared against ordinary per-task model selection.
5. A close comparison with forgetting, logical filtering/regression, abstract
   interpretation and source-aware configuration shows a useful explicit delta
   in the combined contract, bound or implementation. The result may be a
   moderately novel formal adaptation or synthesis; it need not beat an
   identical ordinary implementation or learn arbitrary mathematical truth.

Items 1–4 have reconstructable development arguments and component screens.
Item 5 remains a substantive uncertainty, and the full service has no complete
end-to-end execution panel. A small motivated duty should reveal whether source
preparation, model adequacy and operation prices leave useful room. Do not
replace those duties with more random CNFs just because that generator exists.

The [ordered interval adapter](ordered_sources.md) and
[temporal specialization](temporal_receipts.md) are possible extensions, not
silently part of the same proof. Their median-closure, interval convexity,
prepared projection/distance costs and unchanged reference must be checked.
The temporal query bound is an exact algorithmic consequence; priority over
existing temporal-network implementations is unresolved.

## I12-M: invoice information with exact acquisition prices

Object: the restricted noiseless/quantized invoice contract, cap-two reduction,
reachable transcript sources, exact hard parity prices and source-aware
specializations. Verification must bind the private/public information,
no-repeat rule, actual billed prices, resets, random choices and readout map.
In particular, final private answers do not become public merely because the
private computation saw them. Positive prices and exact readout are hypotheses
where used. The fixed-source cache must not treat new binary invoice clauses
as unit receipts.

An explicit mathematical delta could be the observation-source/price adapter
with its inference boundary, after the closest coding, value-dependent query
cost and side-channel literature comparison. The expected prefix construction
is an upper bound with finite optimality support through five bits at the
specified prices. Its all-size optimality is NOT part of a supported claim.
Neither failed potential becomes evidence for it. Quantized hard prices and
noisy population identification have different hypotheses and proofs.

For a practical claim, identify a worthwhile invoice-only service and charge
its controller/preparation/readout calibration. The 8.1458% constructed query
gain and 89.7406% noisy-design example do not establish that practical event.
Ordinary implementations of those constructions tie.

## E12-R: reliability and selective verification, kept as a reserve

The proof-support, cycle-space/cut-space and selective-guard bounds are useful
derived reliability adapters. A positive contribution event would need both
rigorous sharpness reconstruction and a precise useful difference from the
existing reliability/PSAT, error-detecting-code and randomized-certificate
formulations. Marginal-error premises, selection and conditional acceptance
must be kept distinct. A workload would also need economical calibration,
useful acceptance and priced fallback.

The early economic negatives make a large new learner inappropriate now:
fresh joint calibration can dominate componentwise calibration, narrow clauses
can require nearly exhaustive generic audits, and integral certificate systems
can make randomized spot-checking no better than buying a complete certificate
at random. Retain the exact distinctions and return to broader generation.

## Forecast discipline

R11-M and I11-M retain their historical UNASSESSED status; s12 does not silently
promote or erase them. The October 14 F01/S28-B1/V1 range **20–45%, central 30%**
is a dated carry for its specified fresh complete-cost service event. No part
of s12 executed that event. It is not the probability that the broader program
will make a worthwhile contribution. No new 90% time-to-readiness interval is
inferred from the number of notes or from a four-hour floor.

The 80% minimum, 90% preferred, applies to the proposed useful contribution
actually being assessed. A high belief in an elementary lemma does not establish
that full event, and lacking a general mathematical-uncertainty learner is not
a reason to reject a modest contribution. A future forecast should state which
claim, prior comparison and resource/application obligations it includes.
