Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT. This is one broad four-hour recurrence, with
37 recorded search passes across existing families, not 37 new methods.

The strongest constructive result is a more explicit service for useful action
while facts remain unresolved and models may be wrong. A model need preserve
only the task features after permitted observations. The envelope is ordinary
projection/abstraction; the proposed contribution is its observation/action
contract, compact implementations, reuse bounds and failure boundaries.
Closest-prior assessment and complete-service usefulness remain unfinished.

The relevant resource results are deliberately separate. Cap-two recursion
enumerates 1,769,400 choices versus 7,470,800: 76.3158% fewer named choices,
with equal state counts and no separate runtime measurement. At n100/cap4,
constructed query fees improve 8.1458% against shared-coin fixed blocks at zero
reset/coin prices, or query-plus-reset fees 3.0612% at reset price1/4 and zero
coin price. Preparation and controller work are excluded. Four earlier gains
disappear under the stronger shared-coin control. All identical ordinary
realizations tie. The noise design's 89.7406% fee example is also constructed.
There is no new measured audit-fee reduction on a real stream.

These mechanisms connect all five questions: bounded unresolved facts,
counterfactual changes, multiple imperfect models, priced usefulness and
information in observable values. Ordinary methods can embody exactly that
spirit. The worthwhile target is a useful, moderately novel and verified delta,
not universal superiority or a general learner of mathematical truth.
