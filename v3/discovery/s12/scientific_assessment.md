# Contribution assessment against the original goal

Codex (GPT-6), October 19, 2026. Same-model DEVELOPMENT assessment.
This is a scientific assessment; clock closure and publication are separate.

## What has materially improved

This recurrence adds constructive structure in several directions. Its strongest
return is a better specified service for acting with unresolved facts and
fallible models, together with more exact acquisition-cost results. It has not
established a new end-to-end application speedup or a general uncertainty learner.
Neither is required to make the work worthwhile, but the absence matters when
interpreting the numerical examples and readiness.

The source/operation work has advanced from a collection of finite repair
optimizers to a reusable local interface. For a bounded-width target, the
[permission compiler](permission_compiler.md) characterizes every safe atomic
toggle using local source projections. A source of width two permits exact
updates from unit observations without solving those projection questions
again. [receipt_width.md](receipt_width.md) gives both the precise semantic
boundary and a counterexample beyond it. This is a stronger capability statement
than merely checking that a particular selected edit happened to work.

[Lineage](lineage.md) extends that reuse through copies, complements and
overwrites, with a bound on the work after preparation. The
[task-adequacy envelope](task_adequacy.md) then weakens the premise from global
model truth to preservation of the observable/acted-on projections. A model
can be false about an intermediate fact and still warrant every allowed task.
[scoped_service.md](scoped_service.md) combines these pieces, retains alternative
model identities, and gives one coverage event for an adaptive stream of releases.
It explicitly prices preparation and does not condition a marginal safety claim
on a convenient release branch.

The late [joint-adequacy strengthening](union_adequacy.md) weakens this further:
different models may witness different target scopes after the same permitted
observations, even when every full model and every individual adequacy envelope
fails. A finite four-bit example enables an edit under this weaker premise.
This is not a license to combine incompatible witnesses inside one target
scope. With only marginal task-error warrants, ordinary per-task model selection
already matches the simple risk bound and can permit more actions; that
negative comparator is part of the result, not a reason to hide the extension.

The late [ordered-source extension](ordered_sources.md) shows that this idea
is not intrinsically Boolean: median-closed sources and interval receipts have
the same exact pruning property. The [difference-constraint specialization](temporal_receipts.md)
uses prepared distances, O(n) work per unary bound and O(1) requested difference
queries. This supplies a plausible connection to schedule transformations,
while keeping actual scheduling/execution semantics and costs outside the
claim. Nonconvex receipts have an explicit failure example.

[Temporal adequacy](temporal_adequacy.md) supplies a concrete endpoint edit
despite false intermediate timing claims, with an explicit preparation and
observation break-even condition. [Observable adequacy](observable_adequacy.md)
unifies these source events with public invoice transcripts, while retaining
the crucial distinction between a private queried answer and public cost.
Neither semantic unification is by itself a novelty or fee claim.

These are useful mathematical and representation deltas over full-world
enumeration, repeated local inference, or a blanket demand that every model
detail be true. Their ingredients are established: 2-SAT, forgetting, logical
filtering/regression, abstract interpretation, majority polymorphisms and
temporal networks. An ordinary implementation of the same composition ties.
The remaining contribution question is whether the exact combined contract,
paid bounds and boundary results provide a sufficiently distinct and useful
formal adaptation. That question is more feasible than universal superiority,
but is not settled by renaming the existing primitives.

## A separate information/acquisition construction

The invoice branch now has exact hard-fee results for parity, conjunction,
coarse monotone readout and all tasks at cap two. It connects graphical source
relations to ordinary minimum cuts and binary sources to ordinary unequal-cost
search. The [transcript source theorem](transcript_sources.md) identifies
width-(H-1) CNF as the reachable source class under cap H. At cap three it gives
an ordinary 2-SAT representation; cap four already admits hard forced-fact
inference, even with a known compatible world. These statements expose the
cost of using information, not just its algebraic identifiability.

[Variable stopping](variable_invoices.md) and [priced randomness](randomness_price.md)
give lower declared expected bills in some settings. The unrestricted
five-bit screen now matches the prefix construction at 97/16 for parity and
full recovery. Expected optimality for arbitrary size is still open. Two
attempted cubical lower-bound arguments fail, including one restricted to
reachable sources; they remain failed arguments. The valid
[entropy lower bound](invoice_entropy.md) is weaker and applies with the
target's actual entropy, not an invented n-bit entropy for parity.

[Noisy observations](noisy_invoices.md) preserve population identification for
known independent additive noise on finite invoice laws, but stable recovery
costs can be large. Priced marker selection and repeated-instance decoding
make that gap explicit. These are adapters of coding, query complexity,
experimental design and concentration; a useful invoice-only measurement
setting remains to be supplied. Cost observability must not be presented as
a reason ordinary probability cannot represent the same information.

## Which numbers are relevant, and what they mean

- At n=100, cap four, zero reset and random-choice prices, the restricted
  selector's expected query fee is **8.1458% lower** than strong fixed blocks
  using one shared fair coin. At reset fee 1/4 and zero random-choice price,
  the declared query-plus-reset gain is **3.0612%**. These are exact constructed
  comparisons; controller/preparation work is excluded. Four earlier apparent
  gains disappear under the stronger shared-coin control. At random-choice
  price 1/8, the tested selector instead chooses that control.
- At cap two, the reduced recursion considers **1,769,400 versus 7,470,800**
  private choices, a **76.3158% reduction in that named search count**, over
  the same 632,040 nonterminal states. The combined probe has no separately
  timed arms, so this is not a runtime or complete-computation percentage.
  Ordinary oriented tests are the reduced method and tie it.
- A noisy-readout design example uses **3,690 versus 35,967** worst fee units
  for the same stated sufficient accuracy bound: **89.7406% lower** within
  those two designs. Noise-aware ordinary design ties. It is a constructed
  price example, not a measured application gain.
- A ten-cycle coherence guard with supplied marginal error bounds 0.01 has
  sharp bad-release bound **0.05 versus 0.09** for a spanning path: **44.44%**
  lower as a guarantee, at the cost of one additional justified relation
  prediction. This is neither an observed error reduction nor a fee saving.
  Direct joint calibration can be much stronger than newly calibrating every
  component; that generic application was rejected early.

The old s10 audit-fee/named-computation result and s11 repair/feedback metrics
remain relevant historical evidence, with their dates and comparators. They
were not rerun here. This session does not establish a new audit-fee percentage
for a real stream, and no new number should silently replace that historical
one merely because a recurrence row needs content.

## Breadth, rejected directions and relation to the five questions

Q1 gains a bounded way to leave facts unresolved while certifying useful
operations; it does not gain truth estimates for arbitrary mathematics. Q2
gains explicit operation and all-tied weighted-repair semantics, including
the difference between conditioning and choosing a new repair optimum.
Q3 gains task-scoped reliance and error-propagation constructions for fallible
models. Q4 gains priced observation, prediction and evidence-reuse interfaces.
Q5 gains observation-span, transcript-source and noisy/quantized readout
boundaries. These connections fit the original goal without making one learner
or one minor obstruction the entire research program.

The early screens matter. Broad random audits are often a poor way to validate
small-clause sources. Several graph certificate problems cannot benefit from
elaborate randomized audit selection beyond mixing in a cheapest exact
certificate. Repeated forecast confidence and independent-error intuition can
be wrong. Hidden random-choice fees erase some acquisition gains. Positive
repair slack and nonconvex ordinal observations invalidate attractive update
shortcuts. Keeping these failures changes where further work should go.

Conventional methods share the project's spirit when they treat models as
provisional, computations as priced and conclusions as task-relative. The
distinction is a proposed explicit synthesis or consequence, not a division
between philosophically enlightened Value Logic and supposedly infallible
ordinary reasoning. A tie with an identical realization is expected and does
not erase the delta relative to an earlier, narrower service.

## Readiness and next direction

No candidate is promoted. The component proofs and finite screens support
continued constructive work; they do not supply an independent reconstruction,
a finished closest-prior assessment of the combined service, or complete-cost
application evidence. Those are separate uncertainties. The proposed
[verification targets](verification_targets.md) therefore remain UNASSESSED
at the required contribution event. Mathematical confidence in a short lemma
must not be substituted for an 80% forecast of a useful moderately novel result.

The next recurrence should keep broad generation and early discard. A natural
configuration/schedule duty with paid source preparation and a task-adequacy
warrant is now a concrete option. A useful invoice-limited measurement setting
is a different option. The sharp marginal-risk and audit constructions are
another reserve, but should not acquire a large harness without an economic
reason. If close prior work subsumes a proposed delta, retain the implementation
or explanation honestly and seek a different contribution rather than testing
the same narrow candidate more rigorously by default.
