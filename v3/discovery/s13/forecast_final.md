construction, including reset-price regimes. Its binary experiment consequence
gives a decision-value stopping band without enumerating every partial
controller. Small exact screens cover decoding, local inequalities, reset

the exact closest-prior mathematical delta is not settled; and the usefulness
of this unusually restrictive information interface needs a persuasive account.
Searches failing to find the same formula do not establish originality.
An all-program rate proof is also more exposed to hidden information/termination
assumptions than its short recurrence may suggest. A useful theorem could
survive while a claim of practical computing advantage fails.

This estimate is a judgment about the combined event, not a multiplication of
invented independent subevent probabilities. There is no adequate resolved
forecast history to calibrate it quantitatively. In particular, the count of
passing probes and elapsed hours do not map to a promotion probability. A
closest-prior reduction showing the same theorem would lower it sharply;
independent reconstruction and a clear nonredundant use would raise it.
