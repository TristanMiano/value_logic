## Boundaries that changed the search

A biased parity-potential extension was explicitly refuted: its proposed
imbalance correction can vanish while the required charge still exceeds the
bill. A cap3/n2 oracle row needed a repeated marker outside the original
no-repeat interface; that row is isolated and its executed evidence retained.
Neither defect invalidates the n>=H uniform-source rate claim.

Other cheap screens redirected overbroad routes. Hard budgets do not inherit
the average-budget erasure frontier. Interval bill uncertainty can destroy
exact distinguishability. Low-order value receipts can miss a joint edit,
and tiny observable ports can be ill-conditioned. Exact setters cannot
change mathematical truth or establish the semantic correctness of a repair.
These are useful design constraints, not positive-contribution substitutes.

The final numerical bounds closely meet established multipoint interpolation.
Retain the explicit decision certificate and seek a distinct paid-refinement
duty; do not promote a familiar inequality by changing its vocabulary.
