## What changed scientifically

The earlier invoice work supplied finite patterns and useful policies. The
new proposed theorem covers every independent fair source size, private cap
and nonnegative fixed reset fee in its contract. Its charging argument bounds
all adaptive public controllers; a constructive decoder reaches the same
leading rate. The rate envelope explains when paying for a longer private
prelude is worthwhile. The binary-experiment consequence bounds what any
partial public evidence can do for any specified decision problem on the
parity parameter. It supplies a stopping band, rather than merely another
policy that works on a few enumerated instances.

The model/action advances are a collection of small mechanisms with different
duties. Closure compilation separates the worlds a model represents from
the worlds on which all its permitted actions are actually safe. Ordered
responses and minimum cuts make some action permissions and unsafe margins
computable. Conservative partial receipts support fixed-sample tolerance
calibration without assuming missing labels were seen. A monotone price ray
can retain only a chain of selected optimal actions, weakening the common
warrant required for an entire menu. Inspection followed by corrective writes
offers a separate robust fee/consistency tradeoff when exact setters exist.

These mechanisms do not yet constitute one economically validated service.
Common-menu safety can require far more evidence than one deployed policy.
The direct adaptive inspection oracle can substantially beat a fixed
certificate, even when the latter has a sound blind-write guarantee. Model
coverage, action semantics, snapshot stability, failed audits, solver work
and calibration purchases remain real costs. A useful next configuration
must justify the stronger duty it buys.

The numerical route distinguishes identification from decision sufficiency.
Full common-strength response curves have a finite exact-value reconstruction
under a known rank bound. Joint edits can evade all proper-subset observations;
near-zero ports can make low-order reconstruction poorly conditioned. With
independently justified spectral or sign constraints, one can instead bound
the requested value or omit a group of interventions with a controlled error.
The one-value worked decision improves its robust objective by an exact

example under a declared loss, not a measured application benefit.

The final numerical extension derives a simultaneous sharp band from two
positive-time receipts, using two endpoint-supported spectral laws and explicit
rational dual certificates. It can certify a threshold that separate

decision adapter. Its elementary formulas alone do not establish moderate
novelty. The numerical contribution route still needs a nonredundant paid-

This work follows Value Logic's organizing interest in what a bounded reasoner
may use, pay for, retain and revise. Conventional decision theory, abstract
interpretation, information theory and numerical analysis also address these
questions. They are overlapping tools and comparators, not a philosophical
opposition. An ordinary implementation of a proposed construction belongs to
the same useful synthesis. A tie therefore limits superiority claims; it does
not erase a new theorem or adapter. The novelty question is whether the
construction adds a nonredundant capability or guarantee to the closest prior
formulation. That question remains open for the strongest candidates.

The possible invoice novelty is specifically the interaction of acquisition
and communication. Ordinary cost-alphabet coding can begin with a sender who
already knows its message. Here the sender must first purchase hidden bits,
has bounded private access, and can reveal only the resulting bill. Conversely,
ordinary direct-query decision trees return purchased answers to the controller;
that is a richer interface. The proposed rate characterizes the gap between
these resources. This motivates a legitimate mathematical question, while
leaving open whether a close existing model already solves it. No practical
privacy or reasoning-system advantage follows merely from giving the interface
a new name.

The final numerical decision further warrants regret below0.0053 against the
best intervention for every compatible spectral model in its worked duty.
That is stronger than choosing a visually plausible interpolant, and weaker
than identifying the world or proving a net value of the second purchase.
The two extremizers used in its regret bound can differ, so the bound is valid
without a claim that it is sharp. This distinction is central to the broader
goal: useful action can be certified before deep model uncertainty is resolved.

For Q5, distinguish the probability objects. The invoice experiment has a
stipulated distribution over hidden Boolean worlds. The numerical construction
uses a positive spectral measure representing a quadratic response; its
weights are not automatically epistemic probabilities of mathematical claims.
Both show that values can retain useful information under a specified
representation. Neither establishes a representation-independent conversion
from arbitrary values to calibrated beliefs.
