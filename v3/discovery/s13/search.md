## Entry screen: obtaining useful reliance warrants

1. **A globally false model with task-correct outputs.** Preserve s12's exact
   envelope result, but reject the bare phrase as a novelty claim. Weakest
   assumption synthesis already supports task-specific validity and monitors
   with restricted observable interfaces. A warrant that requires buying all
   target labels can cost as much as answering the task. Seek a concrete
   interface consequence rather than a new name for assume-guarantee reasoning.
2. **Paid predictor subsets and selective labels.** Existing active multiple
   prediction-powered inference explicitly prices predictors and labels.
   Existing program S16/S23 already treats paid audits and censoring. Do not
   build another generic estimator or runtime harness without a distinct duty.
3. **Numerical residual certificates.** Primal/adjoint approximate-model
   composition and low-rank counterfactual reuse were already screened in s12.
   Goal-oriented error estimation is a strong antecedent. Freivalds-style
   checking likewise offers real bounded-computation value, but cheap checking
   alone is established; no new experiment commissioned here.
4. **Weighted premise failures in implication models.** Robust target truth
   under at most a priced set of false implications reduces to the previous
   min-cut repair/min-marginal construction. General 2-CNF penalties do not
   inherit a positive-implication cut representation. Keep as prior, not a new
   mechanism counted again.

## First constructive question: reliance after interface expansion

s12 defines H(F) as the largest source preserving chosen conditional target
projections for receipts on a fixed Q. Its fast service conditions F, which
is safe only while respecting that interface. However, the supplied event
x in H(F) is a full-world assertion about the *weaker source* H(F).
One can compile H(F) once and condition it directly on later truthful receipts,
even outside Q. This keeps the original warrant while admitting new questions
and targets conservatively. It cannot recover correlations that H erased.

Develop the exact price/representation change and a case where conditioning
the original false F is unsafe, but conditioning H both remains sound and
recovers useful information from the new observation. Compare ordinary
forgetting, abstract interpretation and weakest-assumption interfaces before
claiming a contribution. The old envelope algebra itself is not new.

## Second constructive question: a leading coefficient instead of exact DP

The earlier invoice branch's all-size expected-optimality question need not
be attacked by more exhaustive Bellman states. A cube-edge potential gives a
simple cap-three lower bound matching the prefix construction's leading
coefficient. [Derivation](invoice_edges.md) includes nonuniform-law and
bounded-error consequences. One small local screen passes. This is a useful
mathematical delta over finite n<=5 support, not a faster implementation,
new complete-cost gain or evidence that the invoice setting is practically
valuable. Higher-cap extension is a separate cheap-screen lead.

The [general rate derivation](invoice_rate.md) now includes every fixed cap
and reset fee using a private-tree charging recurrence. A parity-prelude
construction attains the proposed optimal asymptotic coefficient. At n100,
H4, reset1/2, the finite selector lowers declared fees by2.370985% against
the stronger prior adaptive prefix/fixed menu. Its option count increases;
an extra private-arithmetic tariff can erase the fee gain. These scope limits
are part of the result, not pending reasons to manufacture a larger benchmark.

## Third question: repair a false full model instead of learning its facts

If a scoped warrant is the full visible projection P_S(F), one may synthesize
hidden coordinates from visible ones to re-establish F, without first learning
the old hidden values. For the chain q=>z1=>...=>zk=>a, warrant q=>a permits
setting every zi:=q. This changes the state; it is not a discovery that old
zi values were true. Copy/read/write prices and loss from overwriting matter.
The old permission compiler already supports this example. Skolem/witness
synthesis is the direct conventional antecedent, so do not make this alone
a new candidate family or commission an artificial benchmark.

Two useful boundaries: a join of separate visible projections need not ensure
that their joint assignment extends to F (F:a=b, separate singleton projections
admit01). Also a single constant/copy/complement per hidden variable may not
realize a section of even a binary source. For hidden z with clauses
(z OR a),(z OR b),(not z OR c),(not z OR d), valid visible assignments can
require z=1 when either a or b is0 and z=0 when either c or d is0.
No one literal or constant handles all these cases. A Boolean formula such as
z=(not a OR not b) does, on the projected source. This marks a genuine action
language limitation, but the remedy is standard functional synthesis unless
a new priced or reusable consequence emerges.

## Fourth question: uncertain reasoning prices

[uncertain_prices.md](uncertain_prices.md) separates unknown individual
tariffs from loss of count observability. A sufficiently narrow common price

robust-interface consequence, not a new coding principle or practical result.

## Fifth pass: permissions, effects and changing objectives

[permission_closure.md](permission_closure.md) weakens preservation from all
local predictions to the fixed action permissions themselves. A two-pass
compiler gives a maximal warrant. [unary_actions.md](unary_actions.md) reuses
the old all-map first pass and adds a priced overwrite fragment. The old
first pass was initially rediscovered; the subsequent archive check corrected

[ordered_responses.md](ordered_responses.md) develops a related model with
unresolved monotone response thresholds. Its feasible interventions and dual
warrant both have implication representations, and arbitrary supplied unary
price tables reduce to ordinary minimum cut. [price_warrants.md](price_warrants.md)
retains only near-optimal actions and proves a bounded-repricing guarantee.
These are constructive leads, not new optimization primitives or established
application improvements. The finite probes are small development checks.

Early exclusions at this boundary:

- A mistake bound for learned clauses does not supply the missing labels or
  identify when an action is safe. Existing KWIK/mistake-bound distinctions
  and the program's paid audit mechanisms already address this problem.
- Unchecked mathematical analogy cannot be upgraded into a proof-transport
  receipt. Proof translation plus fallible search guidance is a conventional
  starting point, with no new learner supplied here.
- Generic monotone *interference* does not inherit the local-response min-cut
  theorem. If selecting an action activates a set of outputs and every output
  must activate, minimum-cost intervention already includes set cover, even
  with fully known deterministic effects. Do not generalize from local
  monotone response to arbitrary monotone causal models.
- Independent price intervals are not automatically solved by the fixed-price
  optimizer. Min-max regret and possible-optimizer sets are separate questions.
  Ordinary robust optimization literature has both tractable and hard variants;
  no new complexity classification is claimed for our restricted closure case.
- Small multiobjective action covers could weaken the required model warrant
  while preserving approximate values. Convex coverage was already developed
  in s05/frontier_sources.md with AOLS/CMOVE comparisons. The new connection is
  to the source preimages of retained actions, not a new coverage algorithm;
  no additional large harness is warranted for that elementary bridge alone.

## Eighth pass: learn useful decisions without over-demanding common model truth

[Warrant learning](warrant_learning.md) shows a sharp quantifier distinction.
In a two-threshold example, every empirically safe action can have small true
risk while the all-menu common warrant misses almost every new world. This
rejects a generic empirical-support learner before another benchmark. Compare
direct policy calibration when same-world adaptive menu protection is not the
actual deployment requirement; do not demand an unnecessarily strong source
event merely because it has an attractive compiler.

[Calibrated slack](calibrated_slack.md) retains a narrower constructive route:
compute a world's cheapest unsafe-action gap by constrained cuts, obtain
conservative gap bounds from paid partial threshold receipts, and calibrate
one repricing allowance using an ordinary order statistic. Adaptive refinement
of the fixed calibration cases is valid through pointwise lower bounds;
unobserved outcomes remain unresolved. A small exact screen passes. The
ordinary cut/tolerance realization ties, and direct deployed-policy calibration
may be cheaper when its weaker guarantee is sufficient.

Potential duties were considered before selecting an application. Solver
completion thresholds provide a natural monotone response, but actual consumed
steps differ from allocated-budget command prices, and dynamic portfolio
scheduling is a mandatory strong control. Dependency configuration with
overwrite/keep actions has clearer command semantics, but all-off/blind-reset
actions and task-directed checks can erase gains. Neither is being presented
as an executed application or a reason to run another arbitrary large sweep.

## Twelfth pass: a whole decision frontier from the invoice price

[experiment_frontier.md](experiment_frontier.md) strengthens the earlier
Fisher-information consequence using ordinary binary-channel common-part
decomposition. Every allowed experiment about the same parity parameter is
dominated by revealing complete parity with probability q=E0|posterior bias|.
The new price lemma gives C0>=beta*n*q, while the constructed exact decoder
costs K. The dominating experiment therefore costs at most K/(beta*n) times
the original fee, under the uniform reference source. For the existing
n100,H4,f1/2 construction that factor is approximately1.00203846.

With a zero-mean prior on the parameter, this yields upper/lower bounds on
the whole expected-budget decision-value frontier and a no-purchase condition
without enumerating partial controllers. It retains free-coin/unpriced-
controller limitations and does not apply to other world targets or hard
pathwise budgets. The small garbling screen checks standard finite identities;
the new all-protocol price premise remains the developing theorem. The
ordinary equivalent ties. This is a broader consequence of a promising branch,
not a reason to abandon the portfolio or declare promotion.

## Thirteenth pass: learn operational permissions instead of full predictions

[learn_permissions.md](learn_permissions.md) observes that a fixed implication
goal with keep/overwrite effects has only2n+g possible preimage clauses.
Ordinary positive-example conjunction learning can retain the exact empirical
action menu from partial worlds and give a common population-coverage event.
The same cases may guide acquisition and fitting because a finite-class
union bound covers every consistent output/refinement stage. No independent
censoring assumption is needed for truthful set-valued receipts.

This pays a model-selection complexity term, unlike fixed-source scalar
calibration. A bipartite construction shows that keeping every empirically
safe action can indeed need quadratically many examples; one safe action
can be much cheaper. The small preimage/closure screen passes. Valiant's
1984 bounded-CNF learner is a direct antecedent, including partial examples,
so the supported development is an operational learning adapter and its
scope/cost boundary. The conditional-risk and repeated-world limitations
remain explicit. No general mathematical-truth learner or empirical gain
is being claimed.

# Seventeenth pass: learning an optimal menu can still be expensive

optimal_menu_learning.md sharpens the earlier sampling obstruction to
strictly positive edit prices and minimum-cost repairs only. One chosen
repair can have a uniform individual-risk certificate with O(k/alpha)
samples, while a common warrant for all tied optimal repairs can require
Omega(k^2) distinct support examples. This rules out treating expensive
all-menu coverage as a prerequisite for a service that selects one action
before deployment observations. It also rules out silently converting that
cheaper single-action guarantee into arbitrary adaptive within-world reuse.
