## I13-R/v1: optimal rates and decision value for an acquisition channel

Object: invoice_rate.md and experiment_frontier.md, with the exact private-
reset, unit-query-count interface. For n independent fair Boolean coordinates,
cap2<=H<=n and reset fee f>=0, prove the displayed beta(H,f) lower bound for
every exact adaptive parity decoder and the constructive beta*n+O_(H,f)(1)
upper bound. The constant is explicit. For finite binary statistical outputs
under P_theta=2^-n*(1+theta*parity), prove the total-variation acquisition
bound and resulting near-optimal erasure/decision-value frontier under the
stated uniform predictive or robust-budget contract.

The positive verification event includes ALL of the following:

1. A fresh reconstruction validates the local mass relaxation, public adaptive
   telescoping, independent-coin handling, termination assumptions and upper
   construction. It must not import a private answer into public memory.
2. A separately reconstructed executable decoder and exact small oracle agree
   at their declared scopes. The proof, rather than finite enumeration, covers
   arbitrary n/H/f. Preserve the n2/cap3 repeated-marker correction and failed
   biased potential; neither belongs to the general theorem's support.
3. All resource claims distinguish queries/reset fees from computation,
   compilation, storage, coins, precision and calibration. The fixed n100/H4/
   f1/2 comparison includes the stronger prior adaptive menu, its494 versus592
   preparation options, and the identical ordinary implementation's tie.
4. Close comparison with cost-alphabet coding, adaptive side-channel synthesis,
   priced/value-dependent queries, edge-adversary methods and binary experiment
   comparison establishes a nonredundant, moderately novel mathematical delta.
   The proposed delta is the exact realizability-constrained acquisition rate
   and its decision frontier, not that costs can disclose information or that
   Bayes decisions can use partial evidence.
5. The result is useful as a mathematical construction: it identifies a genuine
   resource bottleneck, supplies a matching acquisition mechanism and rules out
   better partial experiments in its declared average-budget setting. Explain
   its connection to bounded reasoning through coarsened computational evidence.
   A hardware benchmark or general logical-uncertainty learner is not required
   for THIS event; neither is claimed. If the interface proves too artificial
   or the delta merely repackages an existing theorem, this event fails.

The exact finite-n optimum, known biased-prior optimum at general caps, priced
complete CPU execution, noisy timers and hard-budget partial-experiment optimum
are outside this event. They are genuine extensions, not silently assumed
facts. ambiguous_tariffs.md is a separately scoped robustness result and need
not be bundled into a first confirmation. Broad discovery remains available.

## N13-V: value-only intervention warrants

Object: numeric_edits.md, pairwise_edits.md, weak_ports.md and one_value_bounds.md.
The general idea is to certify the requested change in a scalar value without
reconstructing every latent model entry. Potential mathematical contributions
are the precise measurement interface, identifiability boundary, partial-value
certificate and economically justified intervention-scope reduction.

Woodbury/Schur reduction, principal-minor reconstruction, Stieltjes envelopes,
submodular variance reduction and certified reduced models are strong prior
methods. The cycle-sign and dormant-port counterexamples show that apparently
adequate low-order receipts can miss a joint edit. The sign-restricted omission
adapter and sharp one-value envelope repair particular cases. No new general
numerical algorithm or real measurement-saving rate is established. This full
event remains UNASSESSED; choose an independently warranted structural class
and useful threshold duty before widening the probe.

The final two_value_sharp/two_value_decision/interval_value_certificate notes
make a more focused numerical duty available: reuse partially computed scalar
receipts to certify a new intervention threshold or regret target. A plausible
interface exposes scalar responses and certified error bounds from a legacy
solver, while repeated future intervention requests make reuse relevant.
This is a proposed domain, not an observed application. Compare direct target
evaluation, residual-based bounds when available, and ordinary interval
Stieltjes/Pade dual certificates under identical access. If prior receipts
are reused, charge their original purchases once in a full-service comparison;
do not silently call their acquisition free. The sharp formulas are close to
established theory, so partial-computation stopping alone does not establish
moderate novelty. N13-V remains UNASSESSED despite this clearer duty. A small
economic feasibility argument and exact closest-prior match should precede
another large numerical screen.
