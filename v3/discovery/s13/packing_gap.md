# A real gap between fractional edges and shortest proof support

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT, October21,2026. A small classical binary-linear
configuration adapted to the invoice question. This repairs the selection
weakness of linear_invoice.md; it does not claim a new matroid phenomenon.

Let hidden z be three fair independent bits and S one more fair bit. For
each nonzero a in F_2^3, a query returns x_a=S xor (a dot z). The target is S.
There are seven queryable facts. No one or two of their coefficient vectors
span S: one has a nonzero z coefficient, and a sum of two has zero S
coefficient. Three facts x_a,x_b,x_(a+b), for distinct nonzero a,b, XOR to S.
Thus the shortest exact linear support is3. Ordinary zero-error adaptive
query evaluation also needs at least3 reads on every path: before then S
is independent of the observed linear values. Three reads suffice.

Target-changing latent directions are (u,1), u in F_2^3. A queried fact a
changes along this direction iff a dot u=0. Direction u0 changes all seven
facts; each nonzero u changes three. The fractional direction packing has
exact optimum7/3:

- Put weight1/3 on each of the seven nonzero u directions, and zero on u0.
  Each fact changes on exactly three of these, so its load is1.
- Give every fact dual weight1/3. Every target-changing direction contains
  at least three changed facts, so this is a feasible cover of total7/3.

These matching rational witnesses prove the gap without an LP solver. It
is the familiar Fano-line/affine-cube binary configuration. The classical
packing/covering gap is not claimed as an original result.
