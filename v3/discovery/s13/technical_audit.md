## Invoice rate and consequences

Rechecked the mass-tree recurrence from the branch alternatives. At a query
node the crossing pairs form a matching, bounded by the smaller child mass.
Two immediately stopped children cannot distinguish invoices. For a negative
child optimum, one continuing child with balanced mass is best; for a
nonnegative optimum, two continuing balanced children dominate. The child
optimum never falls below -rho, so the displayed cases exhaust the range
used at rho_star. Monotonicity with available depth justifies a single
transition between the two kinds of level. The explicit r,d shapes therefore
solve the *relaxation*, which is all the lower bound needs.

The upper construction reveals the parity of its removed prefix/prelude,
not the individual prelude bits. Its marker's answer is ignored, so a marker
read on a still unresolved coordinate does not condition that coordinate's
public distribution. Publicly known removed indices follow from the invoice.
A distinct marker exists because H<=n; no private answer cache is retained.
The bounded remainder is a convex-combination argument, not an unproved
renewal approximation. The f0 full-vector corollary uses the old r0 prefix,
not a parity-hiding prelude at positive reset price.

Rechecked the whole-vector bound separately from parity error. The cube
isoperimetric estimate and monotonicity of m log(m)/(m-1) bound remaining
edges by wrong-vector mass; a terminal cell has at most one correct vector.
For Hamming loss, each remaining edge has an endpoint wrong in its own
coordinate. For parity, both endpoints have opposite target signs. These
are different loss contracts and their coefficients cannot be interchanged.

The finite Bellman oracles discard only public no-op partitions with positive
fee. Their residual state W suffices because initial worlds are uniform,
private answers reset, and tariffs/program availability do not depend on
unrecorded history. At cap3 a final private answer cannot affect the invoice;
the enumerated count maps therefore cover all branches. The Fano panel
establishes a real weakness of the structured-edge packing but does not
refute the stronger shortest-support conjecture.

The binary experiment frontier uses ordinary common-part channel simulation.
Its price statement is initially under the uniform source, and its robust
ambiguity version needs the family to contain that source and free masking.
The multi-target counterexample and within-parity leakage caveat prevent
promoting it to arbitrary world decisions. Controller, coins and source
description are still outside the acquisition-fee theorem. Charging XOR
operations in one implementation is a sensitivity example, not a representation-
independent all-program computation tariff: another implementation may encode
the same aggregate in control state.

## Model and action interfaces

For inspect/edit, every selected sign has a direct write fallback, and the
query bill Q is at most the nominal expected fixed-plan price, itself at
most the blind certificate price B. Reoptimizing only after all queries
therefore retains Q+R*(h)<=2B without trusting predictions. The ordinary
adaptive oracle is stronger and already produces strict counterexamples.
Postponing writes uses the no-side-effect/no-intermediate-duty premise.

The calibration order statistic is taken from conservative lower scores.
Adaptive refinement of paid facts can increase these lower scores but never
above their full-world values. This pathwise ordering justifies the stated
fixed-sample coverage transfer; it does not permit changing the fitted action
family using the same calibration sample without another uniform argument.
The online clause-deletion screen correctly requires an informative violated
clause or a justified revision rule; a timeout is not a false-clause label.

For numerical edits, pairwise recovery is exact identifiability under
observable nonzero ports, while near-zero ports cause a precision obstruction.
Weak-port omission requires inverse M-matrix signs and a nonnegative task
vector. It omits interventions, not hidden nodes or their couplings. The new
one-point scalar envelope uses a positive spectral measure; cross responses
with different input/output vectors need not have that measure. The explicit
counterexamples remain part of these scopes.
