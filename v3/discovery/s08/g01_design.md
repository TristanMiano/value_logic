# G01: paid geometry, residual audits and threshold controls

**DEVELOPMENT — Codex (GPT-6), October 13, 2026 UTC.**
Prospective declaration before executing this family. S29 continuation,
not F01 confirmation or a newly claimed comparative advantage.

## Source and duty

Use n in {0,2,4} mandatory Boolean violation features, one exactly-one
decision pair, and an auxiliary triangle vertex-cover block with three unit
soft costs. Hard pair implications pass through a third variable; each
mandatory variable y is constrained by `(x3 or y)` and `(not x3 or y)`.
Auxiliary clauses are the three edges of a triangle. The public auxiliary
witness selects two vertices. Every source and policy is saved before calls.

Acquire actual source-bound resolution derivations for the mandatory units
and exactly-one pair; check every step independently. Also acquire a checked
auxiliary weighted-budget proof at budget 1 (exact lower 2), or budget 0
(initial lower 1) for the residual-proof variant. The ordinary branching
provider and raw checker from s05/s06 are reused with their admitted bytes.
Do not infer a negative proof from a native solver status.

Independent hidden costs: mandatory coordinates in {1,2}; one decision bit
chooses pair weights (0,4) or (4,0). Auxiliary weights stay 1. Each physical
coordinate audit returns one exact hidden cost with source/request identity.
For each n, execute every hidden configuration at prices
`2+n-1`, `2+3*n/2`, `2+2*n+1`, with tolerance 0 and 1. Repeated numeric prices
are deduplicated. Audits are new physical calls in each actual arm/request;
logical geometry and proof banks are retained across the declared horizon.
Enumerating hidden configurations is a development coverage design, not a
prior supplied to a controller. Controllers see only public supports and
their own purchased receipts. Exhaustive regret checks happen after output.

## Matched policies

1. Compiled quotient: use checked geometry and a lower/witness pair to compile
   integer stopping thresholds and quote all residual audit/proof outcomes.
2. Ordinary interval: keep the same scalar lower/upper interval and stop at
   the same replacement/repair inequalities; a practically usable threshold
   method is included even if it duplicates the candidate.
3. Ordinary compiled threshold: separately implemented threshold evaluation
   with the same cheap precomputation available to the candidate.
4. Full identification: purchase every cost coordinate, then supply the same
   warranted action. This is only a diagnostic weaker comparison.

All policies have the same source, witnesses, geometry, proof channels,
retained evidence, nonnegative weights, tolerated regret and audit caps.
No unimplemented optimal oracle is a primary comparator. Report outcomes
where replacement avoids even the decision audit. For an initial logical gap
above tolerance, retain a complete auxiliary proof/witness branch; never
assume more cost audits remove that logical gap.

## Prices, completeness and resource scope

External mandatory-audit price A and decision-audit price D are independently
symbolic positive amounts; display the supported interval A,D in [1,4] in
named-unit currency as a hypothetical tariff, not a market measurement.
Additional complete-provider fee P is nonnegative and symbolic. Named local
operations use the declared Meter categories; provider operations retain the
existing tariff. Report CPU/wall and serialized evidence bytes separately;
do not convert them to money or double count their price in software units.
Unknown host, byte-time and external source-preparation charges remain terms.
No complete economic claim is established if their differences are unbounded.

Save setup and per-request bills, failed prefixes, all acquired raw evidence,
all uniform output bounds, complete path maxima, source/code hashes and actual
calls. Compare full-horizon and incremental bills. A reuse threshold is
conditional on the complete per-request saving being positive; if the strongest
ordinary policy ties or wins, state that no repayment horizon is established.

This design's cheap screen is decisive: if the matched construction is the
ordinary threshold method with extra work and no complete price region,
preserve the result and redirect discovery instead of tuning this trace.
