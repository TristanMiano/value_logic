# An optimum-preserving quotient where global affine geometry is absent

**DEVELOPMENT — Codex (GPT-6), October 13, 2026 UTC.**
[Derivation and prospective family](improving_maps.md),
[source declaration](maps02/design.json), [executed results](maps02/results.json),
[executed source](maps02/impl.py).

The guarded-map service returns **84/84 zero-regret outputs**, one decision-row
audit per output, on the n=1,3,5 star sources and every declared hidden cost
configuration. An ordinary weighted-star controller gives the same outputs,
audits and named units. Both leave all theta rows unresolved; the actual
full-identification diagnostic makes **456 audits versus 84**. The combined
252 outputs pass post-output enumeration of their original source models and
all remaining compatible cost modes.

This tests a broader source premise than G01. Each original source's affine
hull is full-dimensional, and its reduced equations fail on respectively
1,7,31 feasible models for n=1,3,5. The checked map weakly reduces every
nonnegative objective and puts its image in the reduced set. The quotient
therefore preserves the optimum without asserting a false global equality.
Admission is a narrow explicit substitution checker: each mapped hard clause
must be tautological or already supplied, and the ordered feature signs and
nonnegative cost guard are checked. It is not a call to VeriPB or a general
entailment oracle. Additional admissible implications could require paid proof
work that this constructor does not supply.

The two invalid-input controls reject an added hard unit that makes the map
infeasible and a negative feature weight that invalidates its guard. A separate
signed-cost example has original optimum −3 and reduced optimum 0. Its actual
map error is 3; ignoring that error falsely certifies zero regret. For replacement
price 10, the correct bound is 13. A zero clamp justified only for nonnegative
costs would incorrectly reduce that bound to 10. This example is outside the
nonnegative acquisition service and checks the derivation's boundary.

The first invocation failed before source freeze or acquisition because the
module's `HERE` path binding was missing. Its [executed source](maps01/impl.py)
and [failure disposition](maps01/failure.json) are preserved. Version 2 corrects
that binding and adds durable per-audit prefix export; it uses a new directory.

## Resource consequence

Map/range admission totals 40,104,184 named units for the three sources.
The quotient and ordinary-star arms each consume **99,976 named units**;
full identification consumes **90,456**. Fewer audits leave larger support
sets for the uncompiled evaluator to process, so the local bill is higher.
This adverse result is retained. It prevents equating information reduction
with an economic saving.

At identical setup and decision-audit fees, the task-directed arm saves 372
theta audits but spends 9,520 more named units. Its conditional incremental
break-even is **A > 9,520/372 = 25.5914…** named-unit-equivalent currency per
theta audit, before any difference in unpriced resources. The prospectively
illustrated A,D interval [1,4] does not meet it. Even above that threshold,
the ordinary-star method ties the candidate. No complete-cost win or finite
strict-benefit reuse horizon against that control is established.

Actual host/CPU, serialization, source preparation, controller admission and
retained byte-time have not all been priced by this operation tariff. The
whole-development timing includes diagnostics and multiple arms, so it is
not a service-speed comparison. Reusing compiled signed supports could remove
repeated nuisance arithmetic, but the conventional control receives the same
capability. It would change the implementation bill, not the ordinary tie.

## What to develop next

The remaining constructive question is the cost guard itself. A useful map
may be only approximately improving, with its admissibility established by
partial cost observations. Correlations between those observations can make
the joint map-error bound much smaller than the sum of independent bounds.
Exact guard calculation can also be as hard as the original optimization;
the next derivation must expose that duty rather than silently supply an oracle.
S29 remains DEVELOPMENT, with no separate material-positive event ready for
promotion forecasting.
