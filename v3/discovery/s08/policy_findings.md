## A useful cheap specialization

For price A, anchor in{0,A}, A=10HW and tolerance HW, a particular weighted
cover exactly characterizes the full action duty. Either anchor observation
completes the request; without it, the least possible repair upper cost must
be at most HW. This becomes a capped sum of weighted maximum endpoint scores.
The observable, pointwise submodular utility and independent public source
put ordinary expected-gain-per-fee greedy under an established cover theorem.
ac01 checks12,000 partial states and98,840 diminishing-return comparisons.

ac02 then executes **2,048 outputs on new development streams**,512 per arm,
reusing and charging the exact original resolution evidence. It rebuilds the
four cheap policies and checks they choose the same queries as ac01. All16
acquisition units and16 all-compatible-world reconstructions finish first

The cover saves921 fee and1,535,068 named units relative to cheapest. Against

That is a sensitivity calculation, not an economic price recommendation or

The useful result is a cheap sufficient-observation policy for a specific
original-source duty. It does not improve F01's fixed-domain measurements or
establish an exclusive advantage over strong ordinary methods.
