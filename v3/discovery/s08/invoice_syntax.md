# A cheaper ordinary equivalence screen must precede whole diagrams

**Prospective DEVELOPMENT — Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026 UTC.**

ev02 binds the exposed cs01 fee vectors to two invoice classes at n=6 and
n=10, and five at n=16. Whole EVBDD bills are 40,521, 250,933 and 1,977,460
named units. These costs might be unnecessary to remove some redundancies.

Compile the same interval recurrence as a hash-consed arithmetic circuit.
Normalize sums by flattening their immediate sum terms, collecting identical
operands, sorting operands and adding constants. Normalize an indicator
choice by removing equal branches. Pull each branch's additive constant
into a root offset when the constants agree. Finally extract every common
summand from both branches: ITE(x,A+E,A+M)=A+ITE(x,E,M).
These are exact pointwise identities for arbitrary shared worlds.

They do not constitute a complete equivalence decision procedure. Distinct
circuits can denote equal functions; only a common canonical syntactic
record proves the claimed identity. Supply an independent recursive evaluator
and compare all small worlds, then keep the original recurrence as the
checker specification. Compare groups on the 22 ia01 vectors and three
cs01 fee vectors. Cap retained syntax nodes at 250,000. No physical audits;
do not selectively report only fee vectors with successful pruning.

This is ordinary common-subexpression elimination and algebraic
normalization, not an exclusive capability for a new controller. If it
matches the useful EVBDD policy groups cheaply, prefer it as the practical
comparison route and retain EVBDDs for contrasts that genuinely require
their stronger semantic operation.

## Local checking without semantic diagram expansion

The compiled invoice circuit can be checked more cheaply than converting it
back to an ADD. First validate the acyclic node references and compute every
node's variable support. Traverse the original policy from its full interval
through both indicator branches to derive the exact required interval/mode
table scope. This removes all unused root-map entries without assuming they
were unreachable from a sampled trace.

For each recurrence, independently flatten sums into a constant and a map
from atomic conditional nodes to integer coefficients. Cofactor the proposed
result at its prescribed queried variable. Every other atomic conditional
term must have support excluding that variable; the result of either
cofactor must equal the corresponding child sum plus the original audit
fee in this normalized arithmetic representation. Thus the checker only
accepts a restricted, syntactically decidable proof language. It may reject
a true identity requiring deeper algebraic reasoning, but cannot accept an
incorrect one if its local arithmetic checks are correct. The final source
identity follows by induction over interval length. Distinct normalized
roots are not evidence of inequivalence.

This checker is the next implementation; the original sy01 large-world
samples alone did not establish an all-world code result. Keep that timing
and evidence distinction explicit. Once checked, shared final circuit roots
are sufficient to merge fee-learning policies for every source law.
