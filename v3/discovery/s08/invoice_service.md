# Paid learning of a policy bank's invoices from one completed action

**Prospective DEVELOPMENT — Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026 UTC.**

Use all 12 ct01 fee vectors. For each, create two fresh stationary symmetric
Markov indicator streams with repeat probability .2 or .8, 256 requests per
stream, seeds 8141520 through 8141543 in design order. The controller is not
given the parameter or its family: it assumes only fresh stationary rows.
Ternary extreme labels are supplied by the same original-source provider as
earlier actual services. These are new DEVELOPMENT rows on exposed fee
vectors and an exposed mechanism, not fresh confirmation.

Generate the five model-menu policies plus left/right/midpoint policies as
proposals. Execute the ordinary all-world invoice screen, merging exact
fee-equivalent policies and removing pointwise dominated ones. Charge all
policy proposals, each policy/world execution and pairwise comparison.
Store this admission artifact and the source/fee binding. The two n=3 cases
may reduce to one policy; retain them and their zero-calibration outcome.
No policy is pruned using realized service rows or its hidden parameter.

Four arms receive the same remaining policy bank:

- **replay:** after the chosen action, sequentially replay each other policy
  using the same-row receipt cache and buy only missing coordinates. Reconstruct
  every policy's from-scratch invoice. This full feedback is gathered on
  requests 0 through 127, then stopped permanently. Choose the empirical
  lowest-mean policy before each calibration request and freeze the empirical
  winner for requests 128 through 255. Initial tie uses proposal order.
- **circuit:** identical controller and full-feedback schedule, but learn
  each other invoice by evaluating its checked contrast with the already
  known chosen invoice. Reuse all same-row receipts across comparisons and
  the compiled cofactor cache across requests. Charge circuit compilation,
  independent recurrence checks, cofactoring and every additional audit.
- **ordinary_circuit:** the same admitted direct-contrast capability and
  controller as circuit. Exact fee, output and named-operation equality is
  required; no exclusive value-logic advantage is assumed.
- **on_policy:** ordinary optimistic mean-fee learning from only the executed
  policy's own complete invoice, throughout all 256 requests. Use the ib01
  bounded confidence index, with the admitted exact global invoice limits.
  It buys no calibration observations. It does not pay for an unused circuit
  or a counterfactual replay route.

Every arm produces the same exact original-source action duty. Calibration
observations occur only after delivering that request's checked action.
Count those additional observations and fees in the arm's total service
bill. Counterfactual invoices always mean from-scratch policy fees; cached
receipts do not subtract from their statistical labels. They reduce the
physical price of acquiring those labels. Never feed a hidden row or a
diagnostic full-world invoice into a controller update.

For the full-feedback arms, 128 fresh complete invoice vectors support
ordinary simultaneous bounded-mean confidence intervals at alpha=.05.
Report their implied final best-policy regret bound; a small empirical
difference is not automatically a warranted optimality claim. The fixed
calibration schedule is a development choice, not an optimal metareasoning
policy. The own-policy arm has its existing all-time conditional guarantee.
All exact-action guarantees are separate from statistical fee guarantees.

Cap: 24 streams x 4 arms x 256 =24,576 completed action outputs, plus actual
recorded pilot observations. Freeze implementation and dependencies before
first acquisition. Preserve every interrupted prefix; use fresh processes
and authorized stochastic retries. Report realized fees, action/pilot counts,
named setup/evaluation costs, retained bytes and unknown costs separately.
Compute any symbolic price comparisons only from matching completed bills,
state the omitted costs, and include the direct ordinary mirror and on-policy
control. Do not infer an F01 benefit or promote a candidate from this screen.
