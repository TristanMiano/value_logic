The sharper interval reduces physical fees against the earlier Hoeffding
learner in n6/rho=.2 (25,517 versus 25,704), n6/rho=.4 (27,763 versus 27,948)
and n16/rho=.2 (59,094 versus 59,872). It stops earlier in several cases.
It still does not settle the low-rho n10 or n16 policy region within 1,024
requests. The ordinary plug-in comparator remains substantially cheaper
in named computation; its confidence duty is different. This ablation
establishes neither novelty nor a complete-cost advantage.

Several stochastic host failures are preserved in process logs, including
an invalid Fraction/dict operation, access violations and an inconsistent
region check in a process that then failed to exit. The latter process was
terminated only after its command and lack of an acquisition trace were
verified. It was rerun under the author's explicit retry authority. The
failed preparation-prefix bills remain unknown. Completed units were never
silently replaced. Fresh-process artifact reconstruction and mirror equality
supply additional checks, but timings from this unstable host are not a
reliable standalone performance result.
