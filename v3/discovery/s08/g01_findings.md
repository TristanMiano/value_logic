## Why the residual route is complete

Checked mandatory units imply a common cost T, and checked clause inclusion
gives an auxiliary lower L and witness upper U. At h high and l low observed
mandatory costs, T lies in [n+h,2n-l]. Replacement is safe if
$`L+n+h\geq p-\tau`$. A selected zero-cost decision repair is safe if both
$`U-L\leq\tau`$ and $`U+2n-l\leq p+\tau`$. Those are comparisons with
the same fixed price; the common mandatory cost is never cancelled against
replacement.

If the price interval becomes repair-ready but U-L exceeds tolerance, the
policy buys the declared auxiliary budget-one call. Initially L=1,U=2. A
checked SAT response would give an auxiliary witness of cost at most one;
the old lower forces exact cost one. A checked UNSAT response gives lower two,
matching the old witness. Both branches close the logical gap. The actual
triangle returns UNSAT, and that branch is saved. A hypothetical SAT outcome
is a quoted sound branch, not a fabricated actual purchase.

If neither output condition applies, buy another mandatory coordinate. After
all n coordinates the auxiliary call, if still needed, makes at least one
output safe. A repair also buys the independent decision bit. Thus the saved
complete reserve is n mandatory audits, at most one decision audit and at most
one additional complete proof call. The call's existing declared operation
cap is included; local checking, host and storage costs remain additional.
No timeout or interrupted process is represented as a successful leaf.

The candidate's integer thresholds are just ceilings of these ordinary
interval inequalities. The separate ordinary implementation uses the same
mathematics and reaches the same decision at every reachable state. The
equality is informative: paid logical integration and selective cost learning
are real capabilities, but this implementation is not a cheaper distinct
reasoning method.

## Reuse horizon and direction

Against the strongest matched compiled control, both acquisition savings and
named per-request savings are zero. Under equal unmeasured overhead there is
**no finite reuse horizon giving a strict benefit**. Without equal or bounded
remaining overhead, a complete price region is unestablished. Against direct
interval evaluation the measured quotient bill is worse. A large audit price
can repay geometry relative to full identification, but that weak comparison
does not establish a benefit over the ordinary task-directed controls.

Do not optimize quotation on this unchanged family. The next discovery branch
should make the acquisition decision itself more informative: choose which
source relation to prove for a stream of different contrasts, accounting for
failed generalization, proof scope and future reuse. Conventional backbone
extraction, reusable proof caching and cost-sensitive certificate acquisition
are required comparisons. First inspect their actual guarantees before
proposing a new mechanism. S29 remains DEVELOPMENT; no promotion follows.
