original mandatory feature h with clauses (x1 or h), (not x1 or h), where x1

and tolerance in {W,floor(3W/2),2W}; deduplicate numerically equal duties.

repair_minimax (balanced repair-only minimax witness), and full identification.

unknown-center witness. If neither qualifies, buy the center. A center E
supports an exact endpoint choice; a center M invokes its specified witness
selector. The frontier maximizes bias weight within the tolerated repair
regret, using ordinary subset-sum DP. The minimax arm retains balanced unknown
edges. Both retest the full replacement-aware duty, with source-bound checks.

If a center-M state's unknown weight exceeds tolerance, buy the cheapest
remaining leaf; an anchor query cannot close that repair-regret gap. Otherwise
buy the cheapest remaining leaf or anchor. Repeat until a checked output is
available. Positive fee ties use coordinate identity. All possible outcomes
terminate after at most n+2 queries; no favorable branch is omitted. Full
identification buys all n+2 values then selects the cheaper exact action.

each receipt, original-clause and bound checks, and export. Record physical
fees/audits, complete outputs and worst-case query cap, setup and service
