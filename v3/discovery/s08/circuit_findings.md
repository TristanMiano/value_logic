# Direct invoice evaluation survives some stronger-menu comparisons

**DEVELOPMENT — Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026 UTC.**

ct01 checks the declared 12 fee vectors and all ordered distinct-root policy
pairs. Smaller cases enumerate all worlds; n=8/10 use 128 fixed sampled
worlds each. Every reported terminal contrast is additionally checked on
**all completions of its final receipt pattern**, including in the larger
sampled cases. The original circuit recurrences are independently checked.
One n10/r0 process fails with Python's `Executing a cache` fatal error;
its prefix is preserved and a cold unchanged retry completes. No physical
observations are bought by this conditional screen.

The raw expanded-menu comparisons often favor circuit targets over complete
alternative-policy replay. But simple left/right policies are frequently
pointwise dominated or fee-equivalent. The executed post-screen ordinary
pruner computes each policy's full invoice function and removes exact
duplicates and policies that are never cheaper than another. It is an
exponential ordinary screen with a recorded bill, not a free optimal oracle.
After that pruning the n=3 cases have only one representative and no learning
comparison remains. Other small cases mix modest wins and losses.

The stronger-menu n6/r0 comparison totals 832 added fee units for direct
targets versus 1,720 for replay (112 wins, 24 losses, 632 ties). The two n10
comparisons total 5,009 versus 10,348 and 3,889 versus 8,790 respectively.
The first has 488 wins and 39 losses among 2,560 sampled world/pair cases;
the second 482 wins and 22 losses. Five representatives survive in each n10
case, including non-model heuristic policies. This is useful scope evidence,
not a prespecified population performance estimate or a complete-cost win.

These contrasts justify one bounded **paid** service development experiment.
It must retain the pruner's setup cost, the circuit proof and warm cache cost,
actual pilot purchases, output checking, failure prefixes and an ordinary
on-policy learner. The ordinary direct-circuit mirror gets identical
capabilities. No advantage over that mirror is predicted; the experiment
tests whether the integrated target is useful relative to replay and whether
its calibration can compete with simply learning one's own invoice.
