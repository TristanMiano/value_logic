# Source-free learning from completed acquisition invoices

**Prospective DEVELOPMENT declaration — Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026 UTC.**

The previous path controllers already observe the complete physical fee of
the policy they execute. An ordinary bounded-cost bandit is therefore a
required comparator. It need not infer a Markov parameter or prove a
polynomial identity. The following construction tests that omission in the
comparison menu; it does not claim a new bandit algorithm.

Fix a bank of deterministic valid path acquisition policies and fresh
independent rows from one fixed, otherwise arbitrary distribution. Fees,
source clauses and policies remain fixed. For each policy, use the exact
empty-receipt completion certificate to establish global invoice limits
$`l_i,u_i`$. Identical complete root maps are merged; equal expected-fee
polynomials are insufficient to justify merging outside the Markov family.

The normalized fee from each executed policy is in [0,1]. Predictable
selection before seeing the current row preserves the ordinary independent
sample law for that policy. At its nth execution use Hoeffding's inequality
with error allocation $`\alpha/[K n(n+1)]`$, where $`\alpha=1/20`$.
The inherited integer radius is conservative: replacing the logarithm of
the reciprocal tail budget by its ceiling binary logarithm only enlarges
it. A union bound over all policies and positive sample counts gives
simultaneous all-time mean-fee intervals. Zero-width policies need no samples.
Select the smallest lower confidence endpoint, breaking ties by execution
count and policy ID. Intersect successive valid intervals. A confidence
failure causing an empty intersection is a preserved failed run, not a
license to overwrite the interval. This precise index differs from UCB1;
the original paper's exact regret constants are not imported.

The action checker remains exact for every compatible row. The statistical
claim concerns expected acquisition fee and assumes stationarity and fresh
rows. It neither verifies that source premise nor supplies the earlier
continuum controller's particular epsilon-optimal stopping certificate.

Execute one ordinary arm on the nine **already exposed** cs01 environments,
with the same 1,024 rows and seeds. Construct the five old Markov proposals
as policy proposals only; charge their computation and all source-free
completion certificates/checks. Do not pass the hidden parameter to the
controller. Retain original-source receipts, exact actions, costs, policy
selection, confidence updates and all failures. Diagnostic expected regret
may use the old polynomial values outside the controller and is separately
labelled. Compare with carried cs01/mi02 arms on those same exposed rows;
this is an added-control development screen, not fresh confirmation.

Prospective cap: nine completed arms, at most 9,216 outputs. Stochastic
process failures may be retried under the author's explicit instruction,
with every prefix preserved and its unmeasured cost marked unknown.
Operation invoices remain named partial bills, not complete-cost evidence.
