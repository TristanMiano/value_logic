## Main result and its limit

The most favorable executed route joins a checked logical anchor to a cheap
budgeted observation policy. At a node, query only when its fee is below the
expected immediate saving from avoiding the anchor, with a remaining query
budget. Under the declared source law, its expected fee is at most the anchor
fee C; every realized fee is at most B+C. Correctness comes from the source
certificate and fallback, independently of that expected-fee model.

The actual bc02 run uses four fresh 128-row star streams, seeds 8141740–8141743,
four equal-duty arms and previously acquired resolution evidence. All 2,048
outputs reconstruct against the original source and compatible worlds.

## What larger source geometry changed

The graph construction admits source geometry once, exports original-variable
actions and checks resolution evidence and physical receipts. For general
graphs, lazy marginal maintenance executes the same choices as a full current
score scan. gc02 covers n=8/16/32 path, cycle, clique and sparse sources, 64
fresh rows each, four arms: 3,072 planned outputs, all reconstructed. Twelve
original resolution steps are actually acquired, zero complete SAT calls.

Lazy and ordinary lazy each spend 33,448 fee, 9,250 audits and 4,640,221 named
units; scanning makes identical purchases at 39,958,287 units. Anchor-only
spends 35,584 fee, 768 audits and 378,534 units. Thus query fees improve only
6.00% against anchor while computation increases. Even the two-component
aggregate proxy cannot yield a 10% gain at any nonnegative relative price.
All clique cases and n32 sparse have worse query fees than anchor.

Small cases check all compatible corners; large cases rely on the admitted
geometry proof plus actual-world checks, not exhaustive enumeration.

## Mathematical extensions with checked scope

The matroid construction uses m deterministic basis actions. Under independent
coordinate intervals, anchor improvement becomes weighted rank cover. Eighteen
uniform/graphic families check 604 source masks, 29,904 partial states, 19,102
upper actions, 5,760 weighted worlds and 1,135,791 submodularity comparisons.
Five failed Python attempts precede successful cold retries. A nonmatroid
family has increasing marginal gain, so this property does not extend to
arbitrary feasible families. Compact sequential-counter sources at n=4/8/16/32
check 165,216 clauses and 446,352 literal occurrences, with 12 negative cases.
These are generated known structures, not a general CNF matroid recognizer.

Shared query groups preserve rank-cover submodularity by composition with
group union. Their action regret is a different maximization problem: even
partition-matroid geometry can encode MaxCut after sharing. The mc03 checks
cover 19 cases, 118 regret corners and 1,845 submodularity comparisons.

For any binary feasible family and m-copy count vector a, Cartesian intervals
[l,u] yield a nonnegative scalar objective q with coordinates
$`q_i=a_i(u_i-l_i)+m l_i`$. Exact regret is
$`\sum_i a_i u_i-\min_v\sum_i q_i v_i`$.
An ordinary checked scalar lower therefore supplies a regret upper. br01
checks 33 small families, 4,288 interval states and 134,400 corners; a shared-cost
counterexample shows box regret 2 when true correlated regret is 0.
The actual br02 bridge acquires six complete negative proofs on a tiny original
triangle source, retains all proof bytes and checks 48 corners and 18 negative
controls. Provider/wrapper work is 1,896 named units, local work 905. A closed
ordinary formula is cheaper on this tiny source; the result establishes a
callable capability, not its economic advantage.

## Learning which computation to buy

The policy-learning sequence starts with exact weighted star and Markov path
policies, then accounts for compulsory calibration, model adequacy, partial
feedback and policy compilation. A favorable fee saving under a correctly
specified model does not warrant a free calibration source or a complete bill.

The P/Q adequacy counterexample has the same compulsory pair-observation law
but reversed policy ranking. Calibrated pair confidence therefore cannot alone
justify a policy choice. Exact invoice completion, paired decision diagrams
and normalized circuits target policy cost directly. Large ADD/EVBDD trials
at n32/n64 hit declared caps; normalized circuits represent n64 compactly,
but representation size alone does not imply cheaper acquisition.

Target queries can terminate before a replayed policy does. Conversely, at n10
the first restricted target rule used 1,852 queries against replay's 1,788,
with eight strict losses and no wins. Cofactoring/cancellation improves later
small cases. On 24 fresh is01 streams, however, direct on-policy learning has
lower physical fee than the circuit-pilot controller in all 20 nontrivial
duties. The four singleton n3 menus correctly avoid calibration. These
comparisons charge actual calibration/query traces rather than impute savings
by editing an old trace.

## Robust actions and stronger ordinary policies

Early source-quotient runs successfully reduce some information targets but
still pay more than simpler interval or full-cost controls. The graph/threshold
and replacement-frontier sequence derives residual regret directly in the
original source. Shared costs require two-copy bounded subset frontiers;
one-copy and nominal-only simplifications miss valid better actions.

fs01 delivers 6,528 outputs: frontier and ordinary frontier spend 13,399 fee,
against balanced 15,403 and full 44,160, with separately charged computation.
op01 exact minimax policies reduce physical fee to 6,625 against cheapest
7,847, but named preparation is enormous. op02 replaces an unnecessary global
optimality check with a selected-policy safety DAG: the same 6,625 fee remains,
at 28,932,145 named units, including 28,567,793 setup. This improvement does
not erase the earlier acquisition or diagnostic bill.

DIRECT preparation costs 193,035,927 named units and ties cheapest in 23 of
24 exact mean-fee cases, losing by 0.5 in the other; no paid service is run
after that negative screen. The budget/mean frontier distinguishes minimax
and expected fees instead of claiming one policy optimizes both. The subsequent
anchor-cover and budgeted constructions are cheaper sufficient-policy routes.
