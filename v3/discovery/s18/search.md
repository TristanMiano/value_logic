## Initial comparison and breadth screens

1. N16: eliminate the unretained second moment with conventional convex
   minimax. The exact matrix moment interval is classical. A rank-one moment
   completion reduces scalar cap witnesses to k+1 outputs and k inputs;
   two rational non-eigenvector examples passed after a preserved JSON-type
   repair. See [reduction](n16_reduction.md), [sources](literature.md) and
   [failures](failures.md). This is a useful structural refinement and a
   stricter novelty comparison, not a new full assessment or economic gain.

2. Asynchronous partial proofs: considered retaining every original random
   sample slot and lower-bounding its latent test wealth as proofs finish.
   Inspection shows this is already exactly covered by s16's randomized,
   joint and finite-catalogue proof constructions. Reject as a fresh candidate.
   Do not build a second implementation or count it as a new family.

3. Natural shared count proofs: a monotone ordered claim catalogue seems to
   offer a logarithmic shared boundary search instead of individual proofs.
   But a direct query at the population quantile settles its rate threshold
   deterministically when monotonicity is warranted. Likewise, a global
   degree/rank warrant that itself bounds all errors may make statistical
   sampling unnecessary. Reject these proposed examples as evidence of a
   statistical service advantage; keep the actual warrant acquisition bill.

4. Reusable Freivalds-style validation: a matrix-product stream is a possible
   natural setting for private checks/public repairs. The generic bounded-
   rejection argument is already s16. For a single declared bilinear payoff,
   direct matrix-vector evaluation is the strong control; verifying the full
   product first is excessive. A fixed low-dimensional affine error class
   also permits direct basis recovery. No new reusable-verification claim is
   made without a contract surviving these controls. Primary search leads
   were collected, not a full audit or an implemented economic comparison.

5. Value-only receipts for an unknown feasible action set: develop the exact
   future-value interval while retaining existential information from old
   partial optimization. This tests whether old scalar bounds can support
   a new decision without identifying any feasible action. The first concern
   is that an ordinary optimizer already returns a feasible incumbent; such
   vectors must be retained or honestly charged as discarded information.
   See [value receipts](value_receipts.md). This is an exploratory finite
   information construction, not yet a moderately novel contribution event.

6. Drift receipts: a fallible model can propose a nonnegative potential whose
   local expected decreases are checked directly. Such a certificate can bound
   remaining reasoning cost without identifying the entire transition kernel.
   The direct row-sampling interface, value range and drift margin are essential;
   this is not a size-only reset-sampling solution to unknown-MDP reachability.
   See [drift receipts](drift_receipts.md). Ordinary Bellman supersolutions and
   ranking supermartingales are the direct comparison.

7. Witness retention: pointwise-valid checked witnesses can be selected using
   a fallible forecast of future tasks. Ordinary maximum coverage accounts for
   diverse usefulness. Paying for independent inspections leads exactly to a
   Pandora reservation-value objective, retaining submodularity. Correlations
   or shared proof setup costs can destroy this reduction. See [retention](witness_retention.md).
   This is a useful cost boundary, not a new greedy or Pandora algorithm.

8. Complementary witness/proof retention: useful closure requires both sides
   of a gap certificate, so the set function need not be submodular. The
   [bounded archive probe](retention_findings.md) retains all55 tree closures
   with27 optional items and reduces warm bound-evaluation tariff from10655
   to9060. Ordinary sourcewise exact dynamic programming ties; acquisition,
   selection and future amortization are separate unpaid-for claims. No new
   full computation or audit-fee gain is inferred. Keep the complementarity
   boundary, not a tuned old-stream policy.

9. Affine calibration across batches: [inverse-gain lifting](calibration_batches.md)
   produces an ordinary linear feasible region, including interval reports.
   The exact rational probe gives a threshold certificate without recovering
   the full law. [Affine probability shape](calibration_shape.md) then shows
   how rank n-2 identifies every balanced preference, and one nondegenerate
   constant anchor resolves the remaining scale. These are deterministic
   common-law results; unrelated beliefs and unbounded measurement error do
   not inherit them. Novelty beyond calibration/linear algebra is unresolved.

10. A possible Bellman-reuse extension needed an affine rather than linear
    span when rewards are unknown. [The anchor note](affine_anchor.md) gives
    a two-state obstruction and the elementary repair. It is a hypothesis
    check on our proposed interface, not a claim of error in the literature
    or a replacement for the inherited immediate-charge construction.

11. Uncertain feasible witnesses: [convex cores](convex_core.md) and
    [translated regions](translated_core.md) specialize known guaranteed-hull
    support machinery. The common translated shape gives a Pontryagin erosion.
    An exact four-square example contradicts a specific inspected subset-hull
    identity in Lee/Abbas2024;512 rational corner configurations and a separate
    Farkas obstruction agree. The scope is that local identity, not all their
    consensus claims. [Retention](core_retention.md) shows why exact original
    receipt counts need not be dimension bounded, while margin permits ordinary
    approximate-hull compression.

12. Nonlinear reoptimization: [convex value envelopes](convex_values.md) reduce
    the worst scalar optimum over independent translated witnesses to a common
    translation, or more generally to common exposed-direction configurations.
    The ordinary four same-corner hulls match256 corner choices in the probe.
    A guaranteed value9/200 differs from the best universally feasible fixed
    action's9/100. The reduced family preserves marginal scalar envelopes,
    not joint value observations. This is an exploratory decision interpretation
    of prior geometry, not an established new optimization method.

13. A certified interior ball converts approximate witness location into an
    executable convex action. [Exact repair](interior_repair.md) characterizes
    the common core and separates its action from a stronger existential
    value bound. Slater interpolation and guaranteed-hull support methods are
    immediate ordinary controls. Retain the bounded formula and its scope;
    reject the basic interpolation step as a fresh principle.

14. Broad early screens after these constructions: a common unknown monotone
    distortion of reported values suggests ordinal decision identification,
    but P3-02 and s16 already distinguish preferences, values and calibration.
    Shared proof-state learning also risks measuring regret against a baseline
    with the wrong cache state; ordinary policy regret and investment/planning
    models are the necessary controls, not a new external-regret assertion.
    Inconsistent fallible-model reports with minimal repairs reduce directly
    to existing diagnosis/MaxSAT-style selection without new information.
    None is advanced as a new family or given another implementation here.

15. [Relative-error demonstrations](relative_demo.md): unknown linear reward
    and a scale-free approximate-performance warrant give an exact ordinary
    polyhedral test for excluding a proposed action. A1/d nontrivial-exclusion
    threshold initially looked distinctive, but classical Minkowski asymmetry
    supplies a stronger explicit center-ray rule. Preserve it as a possible
    useful inverse-optimization synthesis, not a new geometric bound. One
    small rational probe passed, including sharp inconsistency/no-information
    boundaries and affine covariance. Global value regret is an assumed
    warrant; KKT residuals or fallible self-reports do not supply it for free.

16. Geometric receipt acquisition: for a fixed planar target, already known
    uncertainty regions yield a circular-arc coverage problem. Hypothetical
    nested quality levels also reduce to ordinary weighted arc cover if their
    resulting regions are actually known in advance. This is usually an
    offline certification/retention problem. A fresh query may move a region's
    center or fail to validate a prediction, so the proposed reduction cannot
    silently be used as an optimal adaptive acquisition rule. Next screen:
    established uncertainty-query convex-hull algorithms and query certificates.

17. [Query scope](query_scope.md) rejects a dimension-only competitive query
    bound for fixed-target hull membership: ordinary OR search yields a
    linear gap from an omniscient certificate even in one dimension, with
    nondegenerate positive margin. Prior bounds for identifying a hull's
    full boundary answer a different output problem.

18. [Coupled search](total_search.md) combines extraction fees and eventual
    action loss. An exact deterministic reduction selects one contact receipt,
    one unqueried final action, and the ordinary Jackson order for the rest.
    A small rational DP agrees on507 cases. Separate worst-fee and loss bounds
    can be almost twice the coupled bound, but an ordinary minimax controller
    ties. Robust information-discovery optimization already covers the broad
    principle; retain the tractable specialization pending application and
    a closer novelty comparison, without spending the rest of the recurrence
    on it.

19. [Regret search](regret_search.md) separates finding a low-loss feasible
    action from certifying low regret. A6,142-case early probe found no gap
    for the larger first-positive policy class, but a two-action example
    immediately defeats the proposed one-contact extension. Preserve that
    scope boundary rather than treating the finite agreement as a theorem.

20. [Unknown proof runtimes](proof_scheduling.md) replace an exact fixed-fee
    truth oracle by positive proof procedures with possibly infinite runtimes.
    Offset dovetail gives a simple n-factor total-cost guarantee; shared primal
    and dual bounds can be combined across runs. Ordinary portfolio scheduling
    already supplies the core mechanism. Reject a generic new-principle claim
    and retain the explicit access/cost warning.

21. [Meet receipts](meet_receipts.md) adapt ordinary Horn closure membership
    to incomplete existential witnesses. A direct target test and monotone
    least-action construction can produce a concrete action without retaining
    full models. Khardon1995 supplies the fixed-generator test; ordinary
    characteristic-model reasoning is an immediate strong antecedent.

22. [Closure comparison](closure_receipts.md) gives an analogous majority
    criterion via unary/binary patterns and an ordinary2-SAT core. In contrast,
    affine-core membership is coNP-complete even for singleton/one-dimensional
    subcube reports by an explicit positive one-in-three adapter. A bounded
    exact probe passed467 core cases and15 reduction instances. This is a
    candidate information-contract synthesis, not new Boolean closure theory.
    One-action retention bounds, set-cover costs and a value/action separation
    make its scope explicit; closest incomplete-model literature is unresolved.

23. [Fallible contacts](fallible_contacts.md) replace each projection witness
    by r+1 distinct report identities under a warranted at-most-r error
    budget. For complete points under meet closure, m>(d+1)r guarantees a
    robust action and the threshold is sharp. Abstract convexity and resilient
    consensus are the strong ordinary controls; repeated model predictions
    do not supply the error-budget premise by themselves.

24. [Modular decisions](modular_decisions.md) considers a distinct mathematical
    value interface. For fixed residue codes over a full integer interval,
    worst-case binary threshold recovery needs the same error-correcting
    distance as full recovery. A one-sided modular exclusion can still stop
    early on individual large inputs, with a uniform randomized failure bound.
    Chinese remaindering/fingerprinting and direct count lower bounds are
    mandatory ordinary controls; no new integer-computation speedup is claimed.

25. [Separator interface](copoint_interface.md) puts incomplete and fallible
    witnesses into one finite closure contract. Positive target certificates
    cover separating closed sets; a surviving separator is an information
    countermodel, not proof of actual infeasibility. Ordinary closure duality,
    quantified Horn/2-SAT and resilient consensus are strong antecedents.
    The possible useful increment is a precise input/complexity distinction,
    not a new separation principle.

26. [Least-action values](least_values.md) shows how positive value receipts
    can identify one common optimum and support a small counterfactual edit.
    The key equalities are elementary; a normal solver's returned optimizer
    can erase the proposed scalar-query gain. Park the generic mechanism
    and retain the access-contract warning rather than implement another
    artificial oracle benchmark.

27. [Uniform edit certificates](uniform_edits.md) allow adaptive proof
    production and edit selection under one predeclared finite-family
    confidence event. A bounded witness-bank alternative handles continuous
    halfspace edits, but its restriction must survive later proof acquisition.
    An explicit planar shattering example rejects an edit-dimension-only
    guarantee. The quick primary comparison finds that multiple-instance
    learning already supplies stronger upper/lower complexity results.
    Retain this as a possible S08T application, not a new statistical theorem;
    do not build a full harness without a credible workload/cost advantage.

28. [Projection retention](projection_summary.md) gives an exact
    O(d^2 log(R+2))-bit summary for majority-closed contacts across changing
    hard fault budgets0..R, with a matching-order encoding lower bound.
    It permits action queries without retaining all report regions, but
    saturated counts cannot support arbitrary retraction. The implementation
    remains ordinary pattern counting and2-SAT; novelty is unassessed.

29. [Affine stream](affine_stream.md) produces a safe action and r+1 disjoint
    affine proof supports by report r(d+1)+1, despite the hard arbitrary-target
    problem. The immediate primary screen identifies the same threshold and
    stronger closure theorem in Patak's matroid Tverberg work. Retain this
    as a concrete ordinary adapter, not a new Tverberg result. A bounded
    [fault probe](fault_probe.md) checks the new error-budget adapters and
    summaries; it does not establish their moderate novelty.

30. [Backbone compression](backbone_receipts.md) preserves every guaranteed
    action under meet or majority closure, but can lose sharp value bounds
    and fails under affine closure. A [single-constraint countermodel](single_constraint.md)
    extends the explanation to conjunctive clausal
    source classes. This is a stronger information-sufficiency statement,
    accompanied by a simpler ordinary version-space explanation; it is
    not yet a nonredundant contribution claim.

31. [Conditioned hulls](conditioned_hulls.md) shows that an exact scalar-value
    surrogate can become unsound after a truthful value update. Exact
    conditioning on k linear optimum receipts admits at most n^k attainer
    branches, each restoring the independent-region envelope construction.
    Joint linear upper envelopes also have an ordinary LP formulation.
    This is a concrete repair to a tempting misuse of value-adequate models;
    disjunctive optimization and abstract-interpretation completeness are
    mandatory comparisons before any novelty claim.

32. [Attainer subset DP](attainer_dp.md) improves the exact conditional linear
    problem to O(n*3^k) elementary recurrence work after at most2*n*2^k face
    optimizations. It returns sharp endpoints and source witnesses, and
    jointly inconsistent receipts are detected. A direct SAT adapter explains
    hardness when k varies. The[small rational prototype](attainer_probe.md)
    agrees on201 segment cases and24 adapter instances. Inverse optimal-value
    inference is established prior work; the particular parameterized source
    structure and metadata tradeoff need a closer novelty/usefulness review.

33. [Surrogate scope](surrogate_scope.md) packages exact convex-value bounds
    into k+1 shared geometric sets while exhibiting2^n different joint linear
    observations. This is an explicit-family separation, not an exponential
    lower bound on symbolic storage. Certified attainer identities can avoid
    later subset ambiguity; full returned witnesses remain the strong
    conventional retention control.

34. [Tied ordinal feedback](tied_feedback.md) gives a finite mistake charge
    with unknown corruption count, initially using exponential exact ordering.
    [Strongest paths](beatpath_feedback.md) replace that computation by a
    conventional polynomial ranking kernel. A cycle must not automatically
    be treated as false when genuine utility ties exist. The exact probe
    and [current comparison](ordinal_comparison.md) delimit this new S36 route.

35. [Shared-report cuts](ordinal_cuts.md) give exact hard-budget preference
    entailment for winner-star reports, counting each bad report only once.
    Flow rankings and independent-argument interpretations are established;
    the scope is the report adapter. General grouped comparisons make the
    cut problem hard. These are separate from the main mistake guarantee.

36. [Matroid feedback](matroid_feedback.md) extends the charging argument
    through an ordered one-sided exchange matching. The [probe](matroid_probe.md)
    checks matchings and tied/corrupted streams. [Incremental paths](ordinal_incremental.md)
    reduce small updates; the raw-count monotonicity restriction matters.
    Winning-edge strengths cannot use that incremental formula unchanged.

37. [Feedback scope](ordinal_scope.md) handles merely improving item reports,
    but gives an explicit counterexample to transporting component order to
    arbitrary bundle families. [Selective querying](ordinal_queries.md)
    separates a known-budget finite query guarantee from an unknown-budget
    impossibility. An ordinary last-response cache is a strong small-context
    control. [Matroid warrants](matroid_warrants.md) remain hard even though
    the online mistake controller is cheap; exact inference is not required.

38. [Conditional receipt extensions](receipt_extensions.md) expose disconnected
    value sets and extend to piecewise-linear concave objectives with face LPs.
    The [covering sieve](attainer_sieve.md) improves the combinatorial stage,
    crediting standard inclusion-exclusion; it was not implemented. The
    scalar observation model itself is already established inverse optimization.

39. [Partial receipt proofs](partial_receipt_proofs.md) provide a concrete
    priced decision, a two-bit exact-attainer action consequence, arbitrary
    receipt complementarity and a sharp approximate-value tolerance. Ordinary
    covering and metadata-aware controls tie. These useful examples do not
    establish a practical storage workload or novelty by themselves.
