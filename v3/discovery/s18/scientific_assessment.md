## Progress toward the organizing goal

This recurrence broadened the available positive constructions. It did not
establish a general learner of unresolved mathematics or a complete economic
advantage. The strongest new work concerns how much information a reasoner
needs to retain, what can safely be inferred from fallible reports, and how
those inferences survive later decisions or changes to the problem.

The [ordinal construction](matroid_feedback.md) turns incomplete preference
feedback into a finite error guarantee even with ties and an unknown number
of corrupted rounds. Its [paid-query consequence](ordinal_queries.md) also
separates a warranted error ceiling, which permits finite acquisition cost,
from an unknown ceiling, which does not justify permanently stopping queries.
This directly joins fallibility, action choice and reasoning costs.

The [conditional-value construction](attainer_dp.md) and its
[ordinary sieve refinement](attainer_sieve.md) recover sharp future values
from a few retained scalar optima without pretending to know the hidden
feasible hull. [Attainer metadata](receipt_decisions.md) can change whether
buying a fresh solution is worthwhile. [Counterexamples](receipt_extensions.md)
show why an exact value envelope need not remain sufficient for nonlinear
use or new evidence. This connects unresolved model information with
counterfactual changes and deliberately limited retention.

The closure/contact developments give additional positive actions and
compact summaries under explicit structural premises. Several initially
attractive claims reduced quickly to characteristic-model reasoning,
matroid Tverberg theorems, multiple-instance learning or ordinary modular
computation. Those reductions saved further development effort. They are
valuable search outcomes, but are not counted as moderately novel results.

All these constructions use conventional mathematics. Their relevance to
value logic is the research question and explicit information/cost contract,
not an exclusion of probability, optimization or ordinary bounded reasoning.
An identical ordinary implementation necessarily ties. The contribution
test is whether the resulting scoped guarantee or synthesis adds something
useful beyond the closest existing statements.

is judged a useful, moderately novel contribution after the named prior
methods are credited:

For fixed unknown real utilities on d named elements, allowing ties,
arbitrary changing matroid base families, and at most C feasible but
nonoptimal reported bases, a learner not given C uses ordinary strongest

explicit. The [one-sided exchange proof](matroid_feedback.md) is part of
the claim; arbitrary M-convex sets, sharp lower bounds, paid-query savings

## Other candidates remain available

The conditional-optimum-receipt algorithm remains a strong alternative,
but has no numerical contribution-event forecast yet. Its model specializes
known inverse-optimal-value inference and its improved algorithm uses known
covering sieves. The next cheap discriminator is whether this exact sparse
receipt/independent-generator contract and its metadata tradeoff are already
standard in uncertain-data inference. A realistic retained-information duty
must justify scalar-only storage against retaining full optimizer vectors.

The late [partial-proof example](partial_receipt_proofs.md) supplies another
concrete consequence: three jointly retained coordinate optima determine a
new value1 although any two still permit value4. This can switch a priced
decision; two bits identifying a certified shared attainer can instead
recover an optimal action. These are exact worked information consequences,
with ordinary counterparts tied, not observed service-cost savings.
The same note extends the complementarity to arbitrarily many receipts and
gives a sharp approximate-receipt bound min(4,1+3*epsilon) in the small example.
The priced decision survives epsilon<1/6. This makes the example less reliant
on exact storage, but does not settle the missing natural retention duty or
the novelty of the general algorithm. It earns no automatic readiness rise.

The [incremental path refinement](ordinal_incremental.md) reduces named-item
updates to quadratic work and gives a rank/difference-sensitive matroid
implementation. The ordinary last-response cache has q+C mistakes on q
repeating contexts, so a fair future workload must test transfer across
contexts and charge retention. The new method is not uniformly preferable.

The majority-closure projection summary and exact shared-report cuts are
useful further candidates for a bounded-information synthesis. Their basic
counting,2-SAT and network-flow machinery is conventional. Preserve them
without turning every extension into a separate family or promotion event.

The paired-retention fixture improves its warm bound-work tariff by about
15% while preserving its closure count, but admission, setup and selection
remain payable. The ordinary sourcewise selector ties. It is not a new
audit-fee or complete-computation saving. Those distinctions belong in the
recurrence metrics, alongside the new theoretical and information metrics.
