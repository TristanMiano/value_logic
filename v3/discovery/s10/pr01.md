# pr01: positive/negative response-region soundness screen

Prospective DEVELOPMENT, Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026. Use the 15 small
sources in rc01 and eight exposed external sources. Complete model lists are
admitted through dg01/dt01 source diagrams, then enumerated with a 256-model cap
(the small hyper8 and split4 fixtures exceed the later service's 64-model cap).
For 32 seeded latent partial states per source, test the two-witness lower
against an independently evaluated feasible initial three-copy action (an upper
test only), and against exact global triple minimax on sources with at most
eight models. Compare incremental pair updates with direct reconstruction.

For each state and an unknown latent cost, form positive intervals for the
initial action and negative intervals for the strongest current pair. Test all
integer answers 0--24 and all computed positive interval endpoints using scaled
exact arithmetic. Compare negative claims with direct pair floors, and compare
positive claims with direct complete-model box regret. Exercise strict boundary
and count/source bindings in the later paid checker. No physical queries here.
This screen does not turn an inconclusive lower into evidence of possibility.
