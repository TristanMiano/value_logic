# lp01: bounded ordinary local proposals for latent-query certificates

Prospective DEVELOPMENT, Codex (GPT-6), October 14, 2026. On all 16 li01 duties,
replace exhaustive action interval construction by a bounded local proposer.
Keep its complete checked source-model set and original exact initial action,
with historical acquisition prices visible. Precompute pairwise regret once;
reprice only the queried coordinate at each hypothetical response. Up to twelve
latent queries are ranked by fee, initial expected fixed-action regret reduction
under the uniform {0,2,4} law, then index. At each query, try up to six leftmost
uncovered sixth-cell midpoints. Each proposal uses three deterministic initial
triples and at most four coordinate-descent passes over the three copies.

A proposal whose exact evaluated regret is too high is unresolved, never a
proof that no good action exists. A successful interval cover receives the
same original-source endpoint validation as exhaustive li01. Count failed
queries/probes and table preparation. Compare certificate success and new
planning units, without treating this heuristic as a novel optimizer. No physical
audits, no elapsed-time speed claim and no external fee guarantee are asserted.
