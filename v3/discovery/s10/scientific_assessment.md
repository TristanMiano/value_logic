# Contribution assessment and next discriminating measurements

Codex (GPT-6), DEVELOPMENT, 2026-10-14.

The new mechanisms make the representation more expressive and checkable:
signed original-source objectives, complete finite lower proofs, affine
response certificates, unit-propagated diagrams and hard-source conditioning.
The strongest mathematical extension is the three-score marginal bound with
an explicit extremal construction. All are finite or premise-dependent. No
general tractability of logical uncertainty or arbitrary counterfactual revision
is established. Retained-parent trust and source identity remain prerequisites.

The comparison question remains unresolved in the affirmative. Source-bound
proof reuse is useful relative to recomputing the same artifact, but ordinary
compilation and optimization already supply these mechanisms. Stronger exact
threshold and cheaper scalar threshold controls materially change which policy
is economical. The price interval in component_analysis.json describes the
observed menu, including its tied ordinary implementation; it cannot establish
advantage over an unrestricted best ordinary combination.

Paid feedback is more promising than an assumed free model, but its statistical
limits are now more explicit. Unique observations are not unbiased samples
under arbitrary within-request dependence. Stationarity, predictable selection
and independent requests are hypotheses, not consequences of correct source
proofs. Conservative tail bounds can prevent harmful exploration while also
preventing useful exploration. A fee reserve protects a separate resource
account and does not solve policy identification or full-cost pricing.

Three measurements would be informative next. First, test the cheap sharp
dependence bound and first-purchase calibration on a small fresh service panel,
including uf50-03's source scale but fresh formulas and response streams. Compare
exact threshold, empirical prefix, reserve wrappers and full fallback; do not
assume the one analytical surviving duty predicts broad success. Second, price
actual source-change workloads where more than one patch is requested, compare
retained checked conditioning to a persistent native solver and include cold
admission. Third, use actual application tariffs or explicit admissible ranges
for the currently missing costs before interpreting a component frontier as
economic value. These can define a new positive event if the results justify it.
