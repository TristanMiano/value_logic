## Most useful next direction

The generative aim is to choose between **buying cost observations** and
**buying logical proof work** for an actual repair decision. A common confidence
source W can represent uncertain penalty weights. Existing proof regions can
certify one feasible action uniformly over W. Further audits shrink W; new
proofs improve L/U or enlarge the certified region. A retained feasible
alternative can sometimes establish cheaply that stronger lower proofs alone
cannot rescue a particular fixed action over the unchanged W. This connects
the work back to learned costs, model usefulness and value of computation.
The combined service remains prospective: [calibrated repair](calibrated_repair.md).
