# Paid CNF evidence: narrow acquisition and proof preparation trade places

ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro), October 12, 2026 UTC. DEVELOPMENT.
[Adapter](cnf_receipts.py), [probe](cnf_probe.py), [source-bound result](cnf_results.json).

The new adapter makes the source premises concrete. Ninety-six CNFs are
generated without solving, at 8/10 variables and three specified clause
densities. Seven public clause revisions form a tree. The forecast that an
edge preserves satisfiability has **unknown accuracy**; no source probability
or independent-error model is supplied. Only the inherited checked CNF service
and valid paid witness/subset transport can reveal a label.

Each acquisition retains a source-bound vertex receipt. Repeated requests
reuse it, a checked SAT witness can validate another revision, and a proved
UNSAT formula transports to a checked clause superset. Failed proof attempts
remain unresolved and retain their spent-cost events. Provider caps are caps
on the checked solver service; adapter work is invoiced separately. The initial
root result is bought like any other vertex.

## An actual fixed source, not a supplied probability table

A uniform seeded sample selects 24 identities from the 96-row cohort. The
prospectively specified initial boundary pairs are `(0,3)`, `(2,6)` and `(3,6)`.
They collectively need four of seven vertex labels per row. Two later pair
requests, `(1,5)` and `(4,6)`, expand the required vertex set to all seven.
These requests were written in the probe before its first execution.

Each method starts with its own paid empty receipt cache. Full audit acquires
all seven vertices in public vertex order. Selected-boundary acquisition buys
only the endpoints requested so far. The direct policy bank compares two
policies that buy the same anchor and predict the target either equal or
opposite to it. Their loss difference is `-X_a*X_b`: common acquisition cost
cancels, and an ordinary direct audit can use the identical endpoint service.

Selected acquisition initially saves 7.4% under the declared combined tariff,
but costs 34.1% more after the two added requests. This is one source and one
ordering, not a general economic advantage. The direct bank uses exactly the
same provider evidence; its tiny extra bill is the explicitly counted scalar
loss-contrast operation. The boundary representation has no advantage over
this equally informed conventional kernel on this task.

All 120 sampled pair values per method agree, and all 168 final vertex answers
agree. Separate tiny semantic checks cover a failed retained witness, successful
SAT and UNSAT transport, a repeated cache lookup through later requests,
duplicate-endpoint cancellation, and two capped attempts that return unresolved
and never enter the label cache. All comparison requests complete; the smoke
checks exercise censoring, rather than claiming naturally censored cohort data.

The observations supply actual positive/negative counts. The reported cohort
intervals merely bound the unknown remaining mathematical counts; they do not
assume an accuracy rate or claim fitted statistical coverage. On this small
sample those deterministic intervals remain broad. A valid statistical bound
can be added to this source without changing where its labels come from.

## The useful mechanism revealed by the comparison

The largest ordering effect comes from **paying for a useful weaker claim**.
For sampled row 59, the base and the deletion revision are both UNSAT. Full
audit buys the deletion vertex 4 before vertices 5 and 6, then transfers its
UNSAT receipt to those stronger formulas. Its checked-provider bill is
597,610 units. Endpoint-only acquisition buys separate stronger UNSAT results
first and later still needs the deletion result, costing 1,178,834 units.
Row 53 shows the same pattern. Another row benefits from proving an intermediate
unit revision before a still stronger descendant. These are consequences of
checked implication and the actual acquired labels, not predictions given
to the controller in advance.

Thus the smallest boundary vertex set need not be the cheapest evidence plan.
A claim not directly requested may have positive computation value because
its receipt can settle several requests. This is a constructive next branch:
combine boundary-feature acquisition with a small menu of prospective
proof-producing ancestors. Evaluate their cost and transport value from paid
history, or use a simple fixed structural order as a strong baseline. Do not
select such extra queries using the diagnostic labels of the current row.

## Timing and invoice scope

Warm process and wall timers, provider operation breakdowns, adapter tariff
breakdowns, individual acquisitions and immutable source hashes are retained
in the result. The three complete arm wall times were approximately 1.23,
1.79 and 1.83 seconds in fixed order. These include instrumentation and are
not clean replicated timings. Source generation is timed separately; identical
public-source admission and retention are charged to each arm.

The operation totals are the explicit service tariff, not a complete count
of every Python instruction or allocation. The current adapter scans a sorted
global receipt cache when trying transport; a per-row index is a straightforward
future implementation improvement. This does not change the returned labels,
checked-provider invoices or the identified implication-ordering effect. It
does preclude treating this prototype's wall ratios as a mature implementation
comparison. No acquisition planner, deployment stream, calibrated positive
result or confirmation readiness is claimed by this probe.
