and matrix_lower.md for a retained0<C=W^T(I+M M^T)^-1 W<I, a target Wc

## R16-V/v1: a useful private-check/public-repair service

Establish the perfect-completeness ideal-path theorem, one rank or mistake-
potential adapter, and ONE credible paid application. The theorem permits a
private batch to survive rejections because repairs come from independent
public searches. It does not permit private counterexamples, input-dependent
invoices or arbitrary new error spaces to leak into the proposer interface.
Finite extra disclosure may use the stated transcript multiplier, with the
whole transcript and its acquisition cost included.

Strong controls include fresh lazy checks, known-rank stopping, direct common-
space acquisition, deterministic residual interpolation and ordinary reusable
validation. The univariate degree-four example is explicitly defeated by
five-query interpolation. The six-versus-eight conditional bound is therefore
not enough for this contribution event. Price truth, models, basis/learner
work, proof warrants, private evaluation and padding on the same workload.

Cheapest next screen: find a natural expensive truth interface satisfying
those assumptions, then check whether its public algebra or full-vector
return makes a stronger control cheaper. If so, return to broader discovery.
No empirical advantage or numerical readiness forecast is assigned here.
