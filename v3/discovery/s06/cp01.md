## 2. A common source binds cost observations and logical evidence

The joint development source has eight fixed cost rows and six public possible
integer cost modes. Each hidden row mode is committed before purchases. An
actual `audit(row, coordinate)` reveals that committed cell and narrows the
row's compatible modes. No distribution over hidden modes is supplied or
learned. The target is the mean of this declared cohort, not an unknown
population mean. Internal objectives are **cohort sums**, so price 48 and
tolerance 4 mean price 6 and tolerance 0.5 for the cohort mean.

If row i retains possible modes Q_i, the common uncertainty set is

```math
W=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{conv}\{m_k:k\in Q_i\}.
```

This Minkowski-sum representation includes the fixed cost sum. It uses the
available information without inventing independence or a probability law.
New observations refine existing rows; buying another row would be a different
source/acquisition contract. Logical purchases retain checked feasible repairs
and checked lower receipts bound to the same hard/soft source and objective
meaning. Exact diagnostic optima are computed only after the finished
controller runs and do not choose acquisition gates.

The supplied-weight gate remains `min(U,p)-min(L,p) <= tau`, with a checked
feasible U and independently warranted L. Under uncertain weights the service
checks a uniform action-regret bound over W. An unresolved certificate is a
reason to consider another evaluator or paid purchase; it is not evidence that
the action is bad. Unknown, interrupted or capped calls remain unresolved and
retain their incurred costs.

### Reusing scalar proof anchors after observations

Let `ell_k <= C*(N*m_k)` be independently admitted lower bounds for the same
logical source at full-cohort mode anchors, and set `d_k=ell_k/N`. Concavity of
the **true optimum** gives, for each compatible selection of row modes,

```math
C^*\!\left(\sum_i m_{h_i}\right)\geq\sum_i d_{h_i}.
```

The same argument covers fractional mixtures in each row. Different anchors
may use different checked proofs. This requires neither a probability law nor
a single proof that can be repriced everywhere. For one checked repair with
violation vector u, its certified regret is bounded by

```math
B_u=\max\left\{0,\ \sum_i\max_{k\in Q_i}u\cdot m_k-p,
\ \sum_i\max_{k\in Q_i}(u\cdot m_k-d_k)\right\}.
```

Replacement has bound `max(0,p-sum_i min_(k in Q_i) d_k)`. Each maximization is
rowwise. An actual audit can tighten these bounds without a new LP, proof call,
hull construction or Boolean-mask enumeration. The feasible source, full-cohort
scaling, witness identities and proof dependencies must remain bound to every
anchor. A cached numeric optimum or `checked` flag is insufficient admission.

This is a constructive specialization of ordinary robust regret reasoning and
concavity. A conventional controller may use the same kernel. No claim of
uniqueness or superiority over every ordinary composition is made.

### Choosing an audit or a complete logical purchase

The quotation interface considers all compatible outcomes. An audit permits
choosing a different checked action after its actual outcome, then certifies
that fixed action over the remaining source. For a query objective q and budget
b, a checked negative answer to `q dot z <= b` gives the scalar-anchor update

`ell'_k=max(ell_k,0,b+1-sum_j max(q_j-N*m_k[j],0))`.

The checked positive outcome supplies a new feasible witness; a warranted
coordinate enclosure bounds its cost uniformly over W. Both branches retain
the previous best certificate. A complete query's worst quotation takes the
larger of these two branch bounds. If a capped native call can return
unresolved, that outcome preserves the old bound and eliminates any guaranteed
strict singleton improvement. A `complete` flag is a conditional premise;
only a separately admitted finite provider contract supplies that capability.

[The source-anchor note](source_anchor.md) gives the exact positive bound and
symbolic price threshold for local improvement-per-cost comparisons. Such a
ratio is a heuristic, not a generally optimal policy. Quote construction,
checking, updates, retention and every possible failed branch are paid. Native
time and external audit price do not acquire an economic conversion from an
operation tariff. Complementary audit/proof bundles remain useful even when
individual guaranteed gains vanish.
