## Evidence limiting confidence

[Bousselmi--Hendrickx--Glineur operator interpolation](gram_sdp.md) already
provides a routine exact SDP formulation for the same information problem.
Young's inequality, robust least squares, inverse compression, matrix
completion and rational checking are established machinery. The proposed
increment is the particular analytic solution, its equality construction and
its usable information-limit interpretation. A close prior statement or a
short routine reduction could substantially reduce that increment.

The successful decision needs the valid upper inequality, not sharpness by
itself. Moreover, a [single well-chosen scalar witness](retention_tradeoff.md)
settles that decision. The full block is not a minimal acquisition for one
known target. The stronger old-solution control also reduces the historical
23.4% width headline to about 1.80% on that example. These findings do not
invalidate the theorem, but they limit what can count as its material benefit.

No empirical complete-cost, audit-fee or CPU advantage was measured here.
Such an advantage is not required for this bounded mathematical event, but
cannot be used to strengthen its forecast. The separate second-moment and
source-norm extensions are exploratory alternatives; their new decisions do
not silently enlarge or verify N16-C/v1.
