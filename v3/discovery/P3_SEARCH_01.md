## 1. Objective and scope

Find and develop mechanisms through which a bounded reasoner can make
materially better use of unresolved mathematics, fallible models, reasoning
costs or counterfactual changes. Search across the five author questions and
their connections. The intended output is a promising positive construction
with a credible path to rigorous verification and a useful, explicit delta.

This is one continuing task. Individual hypotheses are branches within it;
rejecting one does not complete the task. The existing finite representation,
CNF broker, mixture rules, certificate service and repair semantics are available
starting materials. They do not constrain the search to their parameter choices,
architecture or previously frozen environments. Representation and mechanism
changes are permitted within the author's objectives, with their reasons saved.

**P3-POS01** requires a material constructive advance, such as a useful new
mechanism, a nontrivial guarantee supporting that mechanism, or a reproducible
capability/resource improvement. A useful synthesis can qualify if its new
practical or mathematical consequence is explicit and substantial enough to
matter to the organizing question. An elementary reformulation, a contrived
success chosen for ease of proof, an isolated rejection, or the existing modest
P3-N01 support does not qualify by itself. Select the concrete positive claim
and explain its importance before making a readiness forecast; retain the
history of any later change to the target.

Compare against strong, specified conventional constructions with fair
information, actions and resource terms. The conventional combination may
contain an identical implementation of a newly derived kernel. Such a mirror
checks attribution and implementation, but makes strict superiority over every
member of the whole class an unsuitable target. State whether the positive
delta is a capability, guarantee, useful integration or measured advantage.
For performance claims, charge relevant acquisition, preparation, computation,
storage and failure costs and name the strongest relevant pre-existing controls.

## 3. Search directions, not frozen hypotheses

Use the following as starting questions. They are revisable families, with no
claim that a candidate has been discovered or any family has an 80% chance.
Develop materially different mechanisms rather than counting parameter variants
as independent ideas. No hypothesis-count quota applies.

| Search family | Constructive question | Cheap first screen and useful comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Learning where a model is useful | Can context, model disagreement or revision history support transferable usefulness estimates and better selection of fallible models? | Derive a small changing-context case; compare an equally informed contextual predictor/selector and include the cost of learning the context. |
| Decision-directed uncertainty | Can shared uncertainty be compressed or refined around decisions so that useful action guarantees arrive substantially before full truth resolution? | Work a coupled example by hand and estimate the complete representation/refinement bill; compare existing loss-retention, abstract-interpretation or interval methods. |
| Learning what to compute | Can a reasoner learn complementarities, diminishing returns or transfer in computation value, instead of using fixed mixtures or an assumed acquisition law? | Derive an acquisition example with affordable, observable feedback; compare a strong greedy or planning controller with the same access and costs. |
| Useful counterfactual revision | Can evidence about model adequacy guide counterfactual repair, dependency transport or choice among tied changes with a useful guarantee? | Identify what is learned rather than stipulated; try a minimal intervention/repair case and compare ordinary revision and structural-model constructions. |
| Information carried by values | Can noisy, partial or changing value observations support useful belief or action refinement without requiring complete probability recovery or known stakes everywhere? | Derive the identifiable object and query cost in a small case; compare established estimation/decision methods and reject hidden access to unobserved stakes. |
| Prediction beyond the current finite broker | Which resource-bounded structures could support useful anticipation or revision over richer mathematical streams? | Identify a computable mechanism and a nonvacuous finite consequence; reject an unpriced truth/consistency oracle before building a harness. |

Draw additional families by combining objectives, reversing dependencies,
changing representations, or importing a relevant primary-source construction.
Follow surprising promising ideas. These rows do not assign six mandatory
experiments or six separately timed tasks.

Existing [P3-10 results](../experiments/results.md) identify costs and limits
worth explaining. They can motivate changed mechanisms or applications; simply
retuning the exposed experiment and calling it fresh confirmation is not a new
positive result. Repeating a disproved route needs a changed assumption or
mechanism that could remove its specific obstruction.

## 4. Working cycle

**Generate and derive.** Expand the mechanism space, develop useful examples,
work through implications, combine partial ideas and build small constructive
prototypes. Spend the majority of research effort here. A promising derivation
may deserve long uninterrupted work; broad search does not mean abandoning it
to satisfy a candidate-count target.

**Screen cheaply.** Use the least expensive informative argument, source check
or probe to detect an elementary mistake, missing information, vacuity, a known
equivalent construction, unrealistic costs or a decisive unfavorable case.
Start with minutes, usually no more than roughly 5–20 minutes for an initial
screen; this is a soft upper budget, not a minimum. A longer screen needs a
concrete reason tied to the candidate's promise. Save a concise rejection and
the evidence needed to understand it, then return to generation or derive a
repair. Do not build a full experimental harness, a publication-length rejection
or an independent audit for every speculative idea.

**Deepen selectively.** Give the strongest branches progressively more
derivation, varied developmental examples and implementation. Keep alternative
families available. Where a cheap objection suggests a better construction,
derive it rather than preserving the original hypothesis for a formal defeat.

**Review the search.** At meaningful checkpoints, summarize what became more
promising, what was eliminated, what bottleneck remains and the next generative
move. After two cycles without constructive strengthening, reconsider the
representation, mechanism, application or allocation across families before
spending more on the same challenge. This is a replanning trigger, not a
termination rule. The 16/40/100-hour reviews assess the direction and returns
from further work; they neither mandate consuming those hours nor impose a cap.

The 75/20/5 purpose priorities are assessed approximately across two cycles.
D/L/E records what activity occurred; purpose records why it was done. For
example, coding a new construction may be generative E, and a proof may be
constructive D. Correctness checking is retained throughout. Extensive
adversarial reconstruction and final experimental challenge belong downstream.

## 6. Promotion to rigorous confirmation

Before promotion, specify the candidate version, claim, intended domain,
materiality threshold, comparison, complete relevant costs and proposed
verification procedure. The forecast event is:

> The specified candidate will survive the planned rigorous verification and
> establish this materially useful positive claim at the stated scope.

Include risks of incorrect derivation, implementation, unfair comparison,
costs, relevance and empirical transfer where applicable. Do not condition
those risks away or replace the claim with an easier minor one to reach 80%.

The readiness record needs a reconstructable mechanism; successful cheap sanity
checks and varied development where relevant; a credible material benefit;
an explicit comparison and attribution account; remaining uncertainties; and
a feasible rigorous verification plan. A short constructive review of the
mechanism and forecast is appropriate. Review by another same-model agent
does not create statistical independence or calibrated confidence.

Record an evidence-backed subjective probability or justified range. The
intended minimum is **80%**, with **90% preferred**; a range extending
below 80% is not evidence of meeting the minimum. Also record the basis,
sensitivity to key assumptions and whether any relevant forecasting track
record exists. **Unassessed** is valid and keeps the candidate in discovery.
Neither a checklist, favorable pilot nor elapsed time mechanically assigns a
percentage. Preserve prospective forecasts and resolved outcomes so later
confidence can be assessed against the actual record.

Once readiness is met, record the promotion and revise the **P3-11** work plan
and forecast before execution. Its existing 90-minute floor remains a minimum
for the downstream task; its original 60/30/0 D/L/E forecast must be re-estimated
for the actual confirmation needed. Freeze the candidate and its empirical
protocol before new confirmation exposure. Proof claims instead require exact
assumptions and rigorous reconstruction; they do not require an artificial
held-out dataset. Claims of empirical usefulness need their own relevant
evidence. All attempted confirmations, including adverse outcomes, stay visible.

A verified positive result can support P3-POS01 and an author gate decision.
A refuted or inconclusive result returns the unresolved objective to discovery.
Promotion of one branch does not automatically end the broader program or
declare all five questions solved. Author redirection or completion remains
an explicit disposition.
