## 3. The accepted composition contract

### 3.1 Received information and deterministic answers

P3-03 maintains a conservative cover of assignments compatible with the
received finite constraints. Splitting retains both covered children;
interrupted work retains its unexplored parent; pruning must preserve every
assignment satisfying the active premises. A true-answer guarantee additionally
requires those premises to hold of the intended deterministic answer vector.
Checker acceptance, consistency, and a nonempty outer cover are different
conditions. None alone supplies that soundness bridge.

Withdrawal can enlarge the source and invalidates dependent narrowed warrants.
Fixed-query resolution additionally needs the stated scheduling, progress
retention, capacity and soundness conditions. It does not establish economical
anticipation on arbitrary fresh mathematical questions. [P3-03][p303];
[principal reconstruction, sections 1–2][principal]

### 3.2 Sourcewise hypothetical selection

For every still-admitted source case, form that source's own feasible
hypothetical family and retain its minimum-ranked cases. Uncertainty about
which source is actual remains **outside** repair minimization. A branch with
no hypothetical requires an explicit undefined/infeasible result or a declared
fallback there. A repair in another source does not settle that branch.

The exact separator has source/rank/loss rows `(0,0,0)` and `(1,1,10)`.
Sourcewise evaluation retains losses `{0,10}`. Jointly minimizing rank across
the unknown sources retains only zero and gives an unjustified comparison
with a fallback costing four. Repair preferences have provided no observation
that excludes the second source. [CE04-6][selection]; [semantic review,
section 3.2][sem]

Counterpossible evaluation preserves the original quoted antecedent and names
its exceptional support rules, frame and normality policy. The contradiction
still has no ordinary Boolean valuation. Its paired support is a different,
explicitly hypothetical interpretation. Tied support identifiers acquire no
uniform probability law by default. Relevance, dependencies and exception
policies remain supplied rather than learned or uniquely identified.
[P3-04][p304]

### 3.3 Receiving evidence and fixed decisions

A transported comparison must bind the actual current domain, its feasible
incumbent or another valid nonemptiness warrant, the source map, rank coverage,
loss correction and units. Every current optimum must be covered, including
ties and cases that were not old optima. The old winner can disappear while
a valid larger old sublevel certificate remains useful.

Different leaves may use different valid old proofs of **one fixed receiving
action comparison**. This does not authorize selecting the action by an
unobserved case. In the counterpossible frame-withdrawal witness, an old
comparison bound of minus one becomes three after the checked correction;
retaining minus one would be false. [P3-05][p305]; [semantic review,
sections 3.3–3.4][sem]; [implementation review, section 3.1][impl]

### 3.4 Forecasts, feedback and later objectives

Issued forecasts remain fallible and immutable. Their feature weights,
numerical allowances and receipt chronology determine which score or regret
statement applies. A one-step forecast can satisfy the potential inequality
exactly while its estimated loss is two and the eventual loss is four.
Hard evidence travels through its own checked channel. Separate marginal
scalars do not become a coherent joint source automatically. [P3-06][p306];
[semantic review, section 4.1][sem]

Full feedback, delayed settled prefixes and purchased-only feedback are
different services. Scores on purchased answers do not describe all issued
questions without a justified selection argument. Changing loss prices on a
fixed tape, certifying a stored error vector and rerunning a changed learner
also have different information requirements. **No all-issued guarantee under
selectively purchased feedback is accepted at this gate.**
[Forecast transport][forecast-transport]; [P3-07 multi-action companion][multi]

### 3.5 Expected policy value and the comparison root

A P3-07 profile concerns a fixed bounded **complete policy** over a specified
request population or episode law, initial state and feature map. Deterministic
mathematical truth does not itself supply that law. A changing stateful learner
cannot inherit a profile that reset its state each query. One supported route
is to make the entire bounded adaptive episode the fixed sampling unit and
acquire evidence over fresh episodes from the stated initial condition.

The simultaneous feature event supports later selection within the retained
catalogue and constant-price family. New policies, unavailable feature
coordinates, changed generators, changed reset rules and outcome-selected
stopping require a new argument. Seeded development traces do not verify the
IID assumptions used by a confidence theorem. [P3-07][p307]; [implementation
review, section 4][impl]

A uniform bound over all valid cases can be integrated against a justified
law supported there. A positive expected gain does not imply a gain on each
query. The independent stipulated composition has mean saving `3/10`, setup
and assessment cost two, and horizon ten: its total expected gain is one,
although it loses `1/5` on the answer-one branch. That example proves a
nonvacuous compatible service under its premises, not an efficient new
application. An ordinary method has the same information and may use cheaper
shortcuts. [Semantic review, section 5][sem]

Profile procurement and assessment paid before a continuation decision remain
in the initial-root bill. Positive continuation value and positive prior value
of acquiring information are distinct. The known-prior acquisition planner
has its own supplied-model contract; it is not an oracle for the usefulness
of arbitrary reasoning. [P3-07 acquisition planning][acquisition];
[primary-source comparison][sources]

### 3.6 Actual information access, resources and implementation scope

The combined method must declare its scope versions, retained information,
admitted observations, source construction, precision, computation, proof,
acquisition, storage, checking and output costs. Local operation counters do
not already constitute common physical CPU or memory units. Tariffs and
cross-module conversions must be explicit and shared with controls.

P3-03's reviewed kernel has at most 12 atoms and 4,096 cells; the P3-05
portfolio receiver uses at most ten bits and one rank tier. These limits
must be respected at their actual interfaces. P3-07's complete policies
reserve cancellation and output work before starting optional computation.
The reconstructed maximum cancellation charge is 33 units, below the 65-unit
reserve. The current selector returns a fixed unprofiled fallback when scope
admission exhausts its budget and rejects a changed generator with sufficient
admission budget. [Implementation review, sections 2–3][impl]

These are trusted local executable contracts. They do not authenticate every
arbitrary Python object or create a malicious-code sandbox. Integration must
preserve the reviewed module closure and actual accessible evidence.
