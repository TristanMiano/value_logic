## What the hybrid is

1. **An abstract judgment core.** Performers, task instances, outcomes, judges
   and the two levels of evaluation are typed once, independent of any world.
   W1's definitions and results are stated at this level.
2. **The physics-task world**, kept as an instance of the core. It is the
   natural home of C3, C4 and C6 (Newton on a regime, local supersession,
   regime geometry).
3. **A multi-agent game-like world**, a second instance. Agents with goals act
   in a shared environment with scarce resources, repeated feedback, and
   inherited or transmitted evaluation rules. It is the home of W6 and W7, and
   a natural one for C7.

Further task families may be added as instances when a thesis needs them.
Each instance documents its correspondence to the core: what plays the role of
performer, task, outcome and judge, and what each party observes and when.

## Why, and what it changes

The fit assessment (Claude's judgment, not a result) was that the physics
world is strong for C3, C4 and C6, and weak as first written for C1/C2,
excellence centroids, C7, W6 and W7: with a few theories as performers, loss is
nearly fixed by regime, so judges have little to be good or bad at, and nothing
in the world has goals, resources or descendants. The first-pass module order
begins with exactly those weak fits.

The earlier plan asked for one world carried through every workstream, with the
connection itself as the phase's main contribution. Under the hybrid that
connection is no longer given by construction. It becomes a question for
P4-R8: which results transfer between instances through the core, under which
correspondence conditions, and which separating examples show where they do
not. Both outcomes are informative and both are to be sought.

The design of each instance (which performers, which game) is left to the
cycle that first needs it. No theorem target, module order or floor changes.

## End-of-task aim review (self-review)

**Aim.** Choose examples in which C1--C7 can each be seriously attempted. No
thesis moved; this is preparation. **Paths reviewed.** This record and the
changed status line, running-examples section and M0.2 entry of the plan.
**Both directions.** The hybrid adds settings where constructions for C1/C2,
W6 and W7 have room to succeed; the correspondence requirement and separating
examples keep limits visible. **Risk identified.** An abstract core invites
the drift the charter warns about, results that never mention judgment, value
or supersession; the plan therefore requires core results to be exhibited in
at least one instance. The reciprocal risk is announcing a result proved in
one instance as holding in all; transfer needs its own argument. The fit
ratings were produced in a few minutes and could be wrong in either direction;
they are a reason to diversify, not a finding about the physics world. This is
Claude's review of its own edit and does not guarantee impartiality.
