### Why the temporal repair preserves the intuition

F.4 already expresses the ideal log-loss improvement from a new report as its
conditional information about a held-out outcome, given nuisance context and
all earlier reports. A later independent noise bit provides no improvement.
A newly disclosed earlier measurement predictive of the held-out outcome can
provide an improvement. These elementary checks show why timestamp order alone
is not the mechanism. They do not refute temporal recurrence as a productive
source of informative feedback: W1 now asks how recurrence supplies that
information and changes the task representation.

Likewise, deterministic reprocessing of the same complete observation history
does not create Shannon information. It can still create a usable abstraction
for a computationally bounded judge. W1 must say which gain it is investigating,
then seek both its successful constructions and its limiting conditions. An
equal-information conventional implementation is a useful comparison; it does
not by itself dispose of the interpretive or organizational contribution.

### Why the geometric and logical repairs matter

A positive weighting can maximize over a whole face: on the triangle with
vertices (0,0), (1,0) and (0,1), weighting by (1,1) makes the entire edge between
(1,0) and (0,1) optimal. Its midpoint is an allowed maximizer without being an
extreme point of the triangle. The constructive target is therefore conditions
for recovering informative excellence faces or modes, with additional assumptions
when point concentration or low-rank structure is sought.

A section over today's domains can remain perfectly compatible while a better
theory becomes available tomorrow. A useful connection to finality needs a
specified future extension or persistence condition. The plan now seeks that
connection and examples of continued useful local supersession. It does not
retire the charter's sheaf proposal after noticing the distinction.

Similarly, a Löb-style argument needs the relevant language, internal predicate
and derivability/diagonalization properties. A finite external check and a
self-referential internal assertion are not interchangeable. W4 now gives a
positive cyclic construction the same standing as the applicable obstruction,
and makes the missing logical bridge an explicit research duty.

## Environmental convergence and meta-value: primary starting points

These sources support the new questions and supply comparison targets. They do
not establish the charter's truth thesis or the universality of any convergence.

| Source and read scope | What it supplies for the plan |
| --- | --- |
| Bostrom (2012), [The Superintelligent Will](https://nickbostrom.com/superintelligentwill.pdf), section 2, especially 2.1--2.3. | Shared instrumental incentives across differing final goals, with explicit qualifications; discussion of goal preservation and reasons to revise goal content. W6 can investigate the relation to the founding argument rather than assuming either immutable goals or their disappearance. |
| Turner et al., [Optimal Policies Tend to Seek Power](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01683v10), abstract and version-10 scope note. | A formal environmental-symmetry route to tendencies of optimal policies in MDPs. The authors distinguish these policies from real-world learned policies. W6 must reconstruct relevant assumptions before importing a theorem or making a temporal-learning claim. |
| Dekel, Ely and Yilankaya (2007), [Evolution of Preferences](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/74.3.685), abstract/introduction; [author-hosted text](https://cet.econ.northwestern.edu/dekel/pdf/evolution%20of-preferences.pdf). | A direct starting point for W7: subjective preferences affect behavior while external payoffs determine fitness; observability and strategic interaction change which outcomes are stable. This gives a concrete model of environmental constraints on preferences, not a claim that fitness alone determines a unique value system. |
| Allman, Matias and Rhodes (2009), [Identifiability of parameters in latent structure models with many observed variables](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.5032v2), abstract. | A constructive identifiability route using conditional independence and algebraic structure, including distinctions between generic and universal identification. W1 should investigate applicability and exceptions, not substitute the title for a proof of its own model. |

The user's environmental emphasis is central to both new modules. W6 asks why
different purposes can favor shared intermediate values. W7 asks how an
environment selects or changes the purposes and evaluative rules themselves.
The distinction between selection, instrumental justification and normative
justification is part of the constructive research question: seek the premises
that connect them, and characterize where a proposed connection fails and how
it might be repaired. It is not a reason to exclude meta-value from the program.
