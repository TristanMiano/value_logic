# P3-10 supplemental interpretation: what the complete paid transcript reveals

Contributor: **ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro), principal /root**, 2026-10-11.
Classification: **D/X; post-freeze mathematical interpretation and observer design**.
The construction was specified before the principal read final private outcomes.
It is not a frozen endpoint, a replacement report, a new paid execution, or a
claim of an economic improvement. Its purpose is to distinguish limited
information in a transcript from conservatism in a particular reporting rule.

## 1. Preserve the object being evaluated

Fix one complete successful episode in one unchanged semantic/source epoch.
For each position t, retain the issued forecast q_t, actual terminal action a_t,
and actual selected/unselected mask. Neither a later purchase nor this observer
can change those historical outputs. Let k(t) be the complete public claim key,
including semantics version, source version, variable count, and all clauses.
Equality of an opaque query identifier or a digest alone is insufficient.

The three frozen targets, expressed as sums over the scheduled horizon T, are

```math
F=\sum_t(q_t-y_{k(t)})^2,
\qquad
V=\sum_{t\text{ unselected}}\left[q_t+(1-2q_t)y_{k(t)}\right],
\qquad
Z=\sum_t\mathbf1\{a_t\ne y_{k(t)}\}.
```

F is issued Brier loss. Z is the error count of the actual terminal actions.
V is the frozen Bernoulli-error functional on unselected positions; its
conditional-expectation interpretation retains the frozen fair-action-bit
premise. The algebra below needs no random sampling premise and creates none.

Construct a map K of labels from all successful, checked, current paid receipts
in this episode. Check the provider/version, full key, source epoch, and label
binding. Reject any conflict. A failed or unchecked receipt supplies no label.
Private evaluator truths must not be used to construct K or any bound below.

## 2. Exact bounds in the relaxed key-label cube

Binary labels make each target affine in the unknown label:

| Target contribution | Constant A_t | Coefficient B_t |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| F, every position | q_t squared | 1 - 2q_t |
| V, unselected positions | q_t | 1 - 2q_t |
| Z, every position | a_t | 1 - 2a_t |

For one target, sum its A_t and B_t within each full key k to form A_k and B_k.
For known keys, insert the paid label. For every other key, allow one common
binary label for all its occurrences. The resulting marginal interval is

```math
L=\sum_{k\in K}(A_k+B_k\widehat y_k)
 +\sum_{k\notin K}\left[A_k+\min(0,B_k)\right],
```

```math
U=\sum_{k\in K}(A_k+B_k\widehat y_k)
 +\sum_{k\notin K}\left[A_k+\max(0,B_k)\right].
```

For a known key the contribution is fixed. For an unknown key its only two
alternatives are A_k and A_k+B_k. The relaxed domain is a product over keys,
so choosing the smaller or larger alternative independently attains each
endpoint. The actual sound labels are a member of that domain. This proves
containment and sharpness over this relaxation using exact finite arithmetic.

The width has a particularly useful form:

```math
U-L=\sum_{k\notin K}|B_k|.
```

Sharpness is not a claim about all logically realizable CNF worlds. Distinct
full keys can be equivalent, contradictory, or otherwise logically related;
their label coordinates have deliberately been relaxed. Nor is the product
of the three marginal intervals necessarily jointly attainable: minimizing
F can require a different key label from minimizing Z. No optimality of an
ordinary deployed reporting implementation follows from this identity.

## 3. Two separate sources of deterministic tightening

For F and V, the frozen chronology envelope treats a position as exact when
the reporter's accepted prior/current receipt rules identify its contribution.
Each other position contributes an independent width |B_t|. For Z the frozen
envelope is [0,n_live]; selected or hard-resolved terminal actions are exact
and each remaining binary error contributes width one.

Insert an intermediate envelope: apply the complete end-of-episode paid map,
but still allow a different binary label for each remaining occurrence.
Its width is

```math
W_{end,position}=\sum_{t:k(t)\notin K}|B_t|.
```

The difference between the original chronology width and this intermediate
width is **retrospective receipt gain**. It removes uncertainty for earlier
occurrences subsequently purchased, and for repeated labels a hard-disabled
reporting history did not use. It does not imply that those earlier forecasts
or actions knew the answer when issued.

The remaining gain from enforcing one label per unknown full key is

```math
G_{key}=\sum_{k\notin K}\left(\sum_{t:k(t)=k}|B_t|
 -\left|\sum_{t:k(t)=k}B_t\right|\right).
```

Let P_k be the sum of positive coefficients for that key and N_k the absolute
sum of its negative coefficients. Then

```math
G_{key}=2\sum_{k\notin K}\min(P_k,N_k)\geq0.
```

This exact cancellation identity separates repeated-key disagreement from
information purchased later. Repetition alone gives no cancellation gain if
all coefficients have the same sign. For F and V, cancellation requires
forecasts on both sides of one half for an unresolved repeated key. For Z it
requires opposite actual terminal actions on that key. An exact half forecast
has zero F/V coefficient and therefore reveals its own loss functional without
revealing the label.

These two gains add to the original deterministic width minus the grouped
end-of-episode width. Both are nonnegative on admitted complete transcripts.
The corresponding marginal interval is contained in the frozen deterministic
chronology envelope. It need not be contained in the frozen delivered sampling
interval: the latter can exclude label assignments that remain in the relaxed
cube. Compare both widths descriptively and retain the original A5 reference.

## 4. A hypothetical diagnostic example

Take two unselected occurrences of one unresolved key, with q values 1/4 and
3/4 and opposite terminal actions. Their total F is 5/8 for either label,
although treating the labels as unrelated gives [1/8,9/8]. Their total V is
exactly 1 instead of the positionwise [1/2,3/2]. Their total Z is exactly 1
instead of [0,2]. This example establishes a possible mechanism, not a final
cohort outcome, and adds no generated experiment case to the freeze.

Conversely, repeated forecasts all below one half have no coefficient
cancellation; they can still benefit from a later paid receipt. A transcript
with no repetition and no later purchase for an earlier key has neither gain.
These boundary cases prevent interpreting any width change as learning.

## 5. Observer and reporting requirements

The supplemental observer must read the sealed public records only after the
four unchanged frozen stages complete. It must use its own exact rational
arithmetic and full-key grouping, without calling a frozen reporter, truth
evaluator, source service, generator, or paid worker. A separate private check
may subsequently ask whether the actual targets lie inside the already fixed
public-only bounds; the private labels must not feed back into their creation.

For every one of the 126 scheduled broker/mirror reports, retain the status,
scheduled horizon, original deterministic and delivered widths, end-position
and end-key widths, two nonnegative gains, and the number of paid-known and
unresolved distinct full keys. Preserve failed or incomplete records explicitly.
Normalize all width differences by the same scheduled T. Do not substitute the
number of purchases, unresolved keys, or successful records as denominator.
Aggregate in the frozen equal-seed-then-equal-population hierarchy, separately
by configuration, and include identity mirrors.

Also retain an observer source digest, input/seal digests, command, UTC/runtime
observations, output digest, and failures. An observer defect calls for a
versioned observer correction; it is not permission to replay a frozen stage.

This audit has unbilled map, arithmetic, storage, and output work. It supplies
no economic denominator for comparison with the paid report. A priced service
would require a declared input contract, sound applicability checks, resource
accounting, all-path completion analysis, and a future prospective comparison.
P3-10 does not authorize that future execution or open P3-11.
