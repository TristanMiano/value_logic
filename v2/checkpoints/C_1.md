## 3. Why the inference and application criteria are substantive

### Scientific replacement

The [F13 scientific derivation](../derivations/06_case_studies.md#29-a-complete-scientific-decision-and-what-would-reverse-it)
constructs the cheaper-rule comparison from a joint coefficient strip, bounded
node discrepancies and errors, and a sample price. Its concrete bound is

```text
L_S - L_Q <= 1/64 + (3/200)(28633/46200) - 1/32
          = -4873/770000.
```

The common target-error coordinate may remain unbounded: it cancels from the
relative comparison. The separate error and price premises then establish a
negative replacement margin. Removing the joint strip or asking for absolute
accuracy changes what is justified. This is composition with a consequential
information dependency, even though ordinary support-function arithmetic can
derive the same bound.

### Bounded self-assessment

In [F13's actual later-reasoning example](../derivations/06_case_studies.md#4-actual-bounded-native-procedures-and-later-reasoning),
short proof procedures and a complete fallback are executed, and their checked
outcomes supply prefix moments. The metalevel derivation is

```text
C = 5 + 5 m_1 + 5 m_12 + 20 m_123,
m_1 = 1/2, m_12 = 1/4, 0 <= m_123 <= q,
C - 9 <= -1/4 + 20q.
```

Joint information therefore determines whether a cascade can beat the
fallback under the declared audit-work objective. The even-parity population
chooses cost 35/4; the odd population chooses fallback cost 9. The ordinary
source-visible strategy does better still, at five/six expected emitted nodes.
That negative control limits efficiency claims while preserving the actual
staged self-assessment. Node count is an audit-work proxy, not total runtime.

## 5. Neural disposition and its relevance to this gate

The original [F15 report](../experiments/results.md) remains **0/5 complete
identity intervention support**, with **5/5 task-ready ordinary-trained
models**. That is a negative result for the frozen conjunction, not successful
causal interpretation. The complete endpoint is demanding: it includes
absolute accuracy, controls and alternative-scale discrimination. Decodability
alone, or useful individual edits, cannot replace it.

**Ordinary prediction training gives a network no task-specific reason to
organize each expected cost into one clean eight-neuron block.** Shared,
mixed or distributed features are plausible explanations for difficult
extraction. [ND01](../experiments/F15_ND01_results.md) also found that known
structure could meet accuracy criteria while failing the complete
control-superiority endpoint, and that wider search/fractional interventions
improved development accuracy. This clarifies the failure mechanisms; it
does not establish technical superposition, a joint or unique utility
representation, or a revised confirmatory success.

Gate C requires an honestly completed bounded investigation, not a positive
neural result. The contribution case in §4 rests on other supported results.
The neural null therefore neither supplies novelty automatically nor blocks
the present contribution. **F15-ND02 remains optional, deferred and unstarted**
in [OPP-02](../opportunities.md#opp-02--is-the-lossvalue-structure-actually-learned-priority-2).
