## 3. The justified shortlist

### Preferred development route: checked, source-aware loss comparisons

Investigate a small language of finite loss expressions, shared source identifiers,
scopes, affine comparisons, and explicit certificates/enclosures. Its attractions
are a modest exact checking kernel, transparent premise use, finite proof traces,
and a concrete connection between min/plus/ReLU computations and certificate
selection. Signed *differences* and unbounded common baselines must not be confused
with an arbitrary convention for adding positive and negative infinity.

This is a preference for F05 to investigate, not a syntax or a permanent carrier
already frozen by Gate A. F04's restricted finite piecewise-affine route needs
explicit approximation or richer operations for nonlinear closure. Full Rational
Lawvere Logic has multiplication: the CPWA obstruction is not a negative result
about the complete published RLL language.

### Retained alternative: source-preserving continuation/value semantics

Keep a finite-state or finite-outcome continuation-transformer formulation that
retains the common uncertain source through sequential use. It naturally describes
how later costs become present values, including report-dependent behavior.
Nonlinear results such as theta² arise directly. Quantifying out shared uncertainty
at each step can change the problem; rectangularity, independence and admissible
policy information must be explicit whenever invoked.

The alternative must remain operationally finite/described, not an oracle that
is simply given the answer for every continuation. Its potential costs include
larger retained functions, more demanding optimization and evidence-update rules.
No general efficiency advantage over the first route has been established.

### Why this is not a manufactured choice

On nonempty finite polyhedral sources and a fixed affine query, the two routes
give the same tight numerical bound, by ordinary linear consequence/duality.
That equality is a sanity check, not a discriminator. Their *declared native
operation and representation contracts* differ on stochastic composition and
finite CPWA exactness. F04 supplies an explicit mismatch and a useful conservative
repair, so neither candidate has to be dismissed merely for losing exact function
recovery. Evaluated scalars remain a useful output baseline; achievable guarantee
sets remain useful for feasibility/action witnesses. Neither is declared universally
inferior or removed from later research.

## 7. Threats, nonblocking limits and invalidation conditions

**Shared-source validity is the leading assumption.** A numerical certificate
can be arithmetically correct while its empirical source is wrong or stale.
F05 must expose source identity, interpretation, scope and evidence mode. A later
valid counterexample within the *declared* source invalidates the corresponding
claim; changing the declared source creates a new obligation rather than proving
an old theorem false.

**Proxy improvement is not value improvement by fiat.** The paired discrepancy
premise is necessary evidence in the worked case. Treat learning or testing this
premise as a separate future question. Neither model confidence nor a low training
loss can silently supply it.

**Proof choice is not policy choice.** The deployed report/policy must be fixed
at the information available to the agent. Per-case proof witnesses do not grant
knowledge of the hidden case. A formulation that lets the policy see that case
would fail the intended semantics and trigger a repair.

**Do not hide all inference in the source oracle.** The resulting calculus must
eventually derive a useful composite bound from component/source premises. A
language that merely displays a supplied final score would not fulfill F06/B.

**Representability is not learned structure.** The existing ordinary-MLP probe
must retain independent labels, negative controls and meaningful interventions.
The current algebra provides a hypothesis and a validity check, not proof of
learning, causal realization or interpretability.

**Richer closure can change the shortlist.** If the preferred language needs
unwieldy approximations for the selected use, F05 can choose the continuation
alternative with a recorded reason. If both lose an essential distinction and
no scoped repair preserves DIR01, reopen Gate A through a named repair. There
is no hidden commitment to ReLU, a truth-degree interpretation, one value range,
or unqualified proof reflection.

No currently required readiness premise was refuted by this review. These are
explicit development obligations and falsifiers, not unresolved contradictions
inside the premises used for PASS.
