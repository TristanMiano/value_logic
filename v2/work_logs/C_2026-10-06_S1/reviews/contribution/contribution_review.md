There would be insufficient contribution support if the project's claim were
that attaching losses, uncertain sources, proof certificates, retained data,
revision checks and consumer requirements creates a new general reasoning
capability. The established ordinary combination described in
[F16's baseline](../../../../work_logs/F16_2026-10-05_S1/reviews/contribution/ordinary_baseline.md)

The present review directly rechecked the optimal-recovery and relative-center
formulations rather than treating new terminology as a mathematical difference.

Nor does F15 establish empirical superiority. Its 960 nonredundant
equal-information comparisons agree exactly with the ordinary control. The
small-edit numeric and regret tolerances are already met by elementary bounds
`|epsilon|/2` and `|epsilon|`. Full-law arithmetic can be cheaper, and the
0/5 complete neural endpoint supplies no positive causal-interpretation claim
or automatic novelty. These are consequential negative controls, not details
to discount when judging contribution.

The mathematical application also assumes unusually rich information: exact
old population means whose minimal linear summary already has dimension
`2^k-k`. A repair saving or requirement of `k-1` coordinates is modest relative
to that input. The sharper error formula does not retain a large uniform
advantage over the elementary bound as `k` grows. The review therefore rejects
a pass argument based on broad architecture, superior arithmetic, a practical
compression breakthrough, or a successful neural interpretation.

This is the strongest substantive objection because it removes nearly every
expansive reading of the original ambition. It would also defeat a purely
verbal synthesis claim. It does not remove the specific application result

## 2. What remains different, and why it matters

### Exact retention and revision

The generic row-space criterion gives an algorithm for asking what linear
information must be retained. It does not itself evaluate this reset-price
matrix. [C4-T1/T2](../../../../derivations/09_c4_price_revision.md) evaluates it:
two nonproportional positive price profiles determine every proper moment;
with a positive terminal penalty their numeric means determine the full law.
After one nonzero positive-preserving price edit, a minimal old numeric summary
can be repaired by exactly `k-1` actual new order means, with a constructive
choice and a matching lower bound.

The useful consequence is a precise contrast between consumers. Arbitrarily
small nonuniform edits can destroy exact numerical sufficiency while an
unchanged old optimal action still has regret at most `|epsilon|`. A request
for exact future numbers therefore has a different information cost from a
request for a sufficiently good action. This is an explicit result for a
declared application, beyond the generic observation that discarded evidence
can matter. Known general principles remain credited.

### The compatible-decoder result

The strongest surviving mathematical addition is
[F16-C1](../../../../derivations/10_f16_coherent_recovery.md). On every full
exact equal-old-price fiber, for `k>=2` and `M>=0`, one compatible law attains
the unrestricted common minimax error for each separately applied admissible
single-price edit. The endpoint/adjacent-level mixture constructs that law,
and singleton width determines the common radius. Residue offsets allow
nonexchangeable original laws; exchangeability is a description of the
remaining uncertainty, not an unannounced assumption on the whole population.

Here is the precise gap between the established method and this consequence.
For a source polytope `P` and target rows `C_i`, compute
`L_i=min_P C_i p`, `U_i=max_P C_i p`. The unrestricted radius is

`r_free = max_i (U_i-L_i)/2`.

The ordinary compatible-output problem adds `q in P` and requires
`U_i-C_i q <= r` and `C_i q-L_i <= r` for each coordinate. It can solve for
the best compatible law. Its general formulation does not prove
`r_compatible=r_free`. The normalized triangle with vertices `(1,1,0)`,
`(1,0,1)`, `(0,1,1)` has respective radii `2/3` and `1/2`, as realized inside
the reset model by F16's additional-source example. Thus generic convexity or
LP computability cannot supply the equality without an additional argument.

F16 supplies that argument for its entire specified fiber family and gives
an attaining law. This changes what can be constructed and understood: the
answers may share one source-compatible representation without an error
premium in that family. It is stronger than a list of isolated interval
midpoints. It does not authorize using the decoded estimate as exact source
truth for arbitrary later queries. The proof's exact source and consumer
restrictions, including exclusion of extra source constraints, are part of
the contribution.

The ordinary comparator is entitled to use this same construction. A
specialized explicit solution remains an eligible formal adaptation/application
even when standard optimization could recover its answers numerically. It
would be displaced as a new consequence if an antecedent already supplied
the same theorem under an equivalent contract; no such displacement was
established by the inspected comparison.
