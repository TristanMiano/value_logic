## Retention interpretation contract

The declared quality services are separate: six numerical answers, a coherent
all-alternative decision, and a native proof for the current requested comparison.
The native proof concerns improvement against fallback; it does not replace the
ordinary same-law regret certificate against all orders.

| Disposition | Exact rule / required qualification |
|---|---|
| Exact numerical answer | Interval endpoints agree. |
| Approximate numerical answer | Positive interval width at most `2 tau = 1/10`; use the midpoint, with error at most `tau = 1/20`. |
| Refused numerical answer | Width exceeds `1/10`; no admitted estimate. |
| Certified decision | Coherent worst-case regret at most `epsilon = 1/20`; fallback can itself be certified. |
| Refusal to fallback | Coherent regret exceeds `1/20`; execute fallback and report its actual regret without useful-choice credit. |
| Native receipt | Current source, request, units and producer-budget checks pass; separately report full-law truth and retained-fiber validity. |

A useful application derivation is a direct price/program revision with an
executed nonfallback order, a received current native bound at most `-1/20`,
at least two distinct nonzero source premises and admitted coherent regret.
The frozen application criterion requires at least four distinct seed/variant
episodes across two seeds, including price and program edits. It additionally
requires a useful no-reacquisition `tailored` or `exact_intervals` row with more
than one feasible-law vertex **and an approximate selected-order cost**. A
multiple-vertex fiber with an exact selected scalar mean is insufficient.

Equal-information groups are `fresh`/`cached_proof`/`full_joint` and
`tailored`/`exact_intervals`. The analysis requires equality of numerical
intervals/dispositions/errors, deterministic decisions and coherent/actual
regret, native semantic targets and acquisition choices within those groups.
Proof cost, cache behavior and budget-limited receipt status can differ. The
hidden full-law scoring oracle is a different-information reference for
restricted rows. A literal equality of two refusals is not admitted service.

Summarize source withdrawal/drift, stale-cache rejections, budget unavailability,
true-full-law but unsupported-fiber comparisons, approximate numbers accompanying
certified orders, and refused decisions that still lose value. Correct admissions
are necessary but do not alone satisfy the application criterion.

Resource reporting must include retention payload, old source/proof, current
source/proof, public metadata, complete source archive and initial exposure.
Charge two-mean repair's two scalar fields **and sixteen source path executions**;
full-law repair returns eight fields by wire convention (information rank seven).
Include common eight-field initial inputs and known-marginal charges at sensitivity
prices `0`, `1/20`, `1/2`. CPU time and bytes have no implicit conversion to loss.
Keep arithmetic-only and native-inclusive time separate; do not add diagnostic
substage timers twice. The `I + h U` results at horizons 1, 4, 16 and 64 and strict
eventual crossing are algebraic projections of one observed update, not repeated
update performance or distributional speed evidence.

## Neural interpretation contract

Each full identity model requires ordinary task readiness, conditional base
readiness in all ten role/stratum cells, intervention/decision adequacy, each
matched-control contrast, each same-subset scale contrast and numerical validity.
At least four of five models must meet all identity conditions, and all five
must have valid numerical controls, for pilot support.

| Requirement | Frozen simultaneous-bound test |
|---|---|
| Ordinary probability MAE | Upper bound at most `.05`. |
| Ordinary decision regret | Upper normalized bound multiplied by `11/8` at most `.05`. |
| Conditional base and intervention MAE | Separate upper bounds at most `.05` for every role/stratum. |
| Adjusted-effect MAE | Derived upper bound from base plus intervention bounds at most `.10`; not RMSE or a per-example guarantee. |
| Mixed near/far decision disagreement | Upper bound at most `.35` / `.10`. |
| Matched-control specificity | Per-role, equal-stratum pooled lower MAE improvement at least `.01` against **each** random, permuted-concept, shuffled-donor and untrained arm. |
| Same-subset scale specificity | Identity-selected intervention's lower MAE advantage at least `.01` against every rival on separating rows, both roles. |
| Separating coverage | Every planned separating row retained; conditional-frequency lower bound at least `.90`. |
| Numerical validity | All four transported gauges, factor-two scale and unused-duplicate checks pass `1e-10`, with zero refits. |

The Hoeffding family uses `K=560`, `alpha=.05`. At 8,192 samples the `[0,1]`
radius is about `.0247261`; the `[-1,1]` radius is about `.0494521`. Pooled
40,960-sample radii are about `.0110578` and `.0221157`. Thus, for example,
observed intervention MAE just below `.05` does not pass the `.05` upper-bound
criterion. The conservative design can produce deliberately inconclusive tests.

Interpretation must retain these distinctions:

- If an upper-bound adequacy condition fails, support is absent; this alone
  does not establish falsification. A stratum's **lower MAE bound above `.05`**
  specifically falsifies that selected subset's stated tolerance claim on that
  conditional population. Report which cells satisfy this stronger condition.
- Ordinary task underlearning is failure at this frozen training budget. It
  limits inference about the intended internal representation question and does
  not establish absence of all expected-cost representations.
- Adequate intervention fit without matched-control or scale separation supports
  no distinctive extraction method or unique absolute-cost interpretation. A
  successful random subset search can be consistent with an intervention relation.
- Rival scales preserve ordinary predictions but can change one-role
  interventions. Same identity-selected subset comparisons and separately
  searched rival subsets answer different questions. All rival searches remain
  visible, including their threefold aggregate budget relative to identity.
- Decodability is auxiliary. High decoder quality cannot compensate for failed
  causal interventions. Positive per-role partial correspondence is not ordinary
  circuit necessity, a joint constructive abstraction or unique utility semantics.
- Overlapping selected subsets and order-dependent composition remain secondary
  diagnostics. Positive gauge checks cover the specified positive scales and
  permutations; they do not establish invariance under every functional equivalence.
- Negative fixed-coordinate results do not exclude distributed alignments,
  larger subsets, other architectures/tasks, or more training. Those would be
  subsequent work with a fresh protocol, never rescue search on these final data.
