## Derivations and worked adapters

The new note treats q↦D(q||p) as a Bayesian belief-functional subcase, not all
imprecise belief. It proves finite conditional-KL, geometric-pooling,
conditioning-offset and soft-value identities with their support assumptions.
It distinguishes candidate fit from belief strength, fixed target entropy from
policy entropy, and controlled outcome laws from uncertain external models.

A proper nonnegative lsc penalty on a finite simplex yields a monotone,
shift-preserving concave loss transformer on finite signed continuations.
The KL case has a closed exponential form; no single global value bound is
required. The notes give a triangle counterexample for KL, a residual-closure
counterexample for general lsc beliefs, and a finite belief/cost reallocation
that preserves choices but changes the decomposition. These do not refute the
previously proved replacement-loss triangle or select a new core.
