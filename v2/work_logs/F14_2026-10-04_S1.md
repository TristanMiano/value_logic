## D: what the protocol fixes and why

The normative [protocol](../experiments/protocol.md) and
[configuration](../experiments/config.v1.json) fix two bounded questions.
The [revision design](../experiments/retention_design.md) uses three fixed reset
procedures and eight rational outcome worlds, ten revision variants, six methods
and two acquisition regimes. Fresh solving, cached proof/replacement, full joint
retention, tailored means, exact uncertainty intervals and a marginal diagnostic
have declared information contracts. Development seeds are 14101 and 14102;
F15 seeds 15101–15116 are specified but unused.

Native validity, actual source truth and present-request reception are distinct
fields. Exact recovery is separated from an approximate answer with radius at
most 0.05, refusal, certified fallback and a useful selected action. A useful
application requires actual nonfallback selection and receipt with a margin of
at least 0.05 against fallback, source premises and a same-law regret certificate.
The selective-retention case must have an approximate **selected cost**; uncertainty
elsewhere in the law is insufficient. Complete quality vectors distinguish
literal equality (which can include shared refusals) from admitted useful service.
Equal-information methods must agree on exact intervals, deterministic decisions,
regret, source-based native targets and acquisition; proof receipt/bytes are
reported separately. No proof-reconstruction speed advantage is required.

All methods pay source/context and retained-summary storage, initial production,
checking, updates, archival availability and measured acquisition/fallback work.
The source is not inferred for free from public generator seeds or enumerating
the finite development generator support. Two actual new order means can repair
the stable one-price family; other repairs pay for the full source. Horizons
1/4/16/64 report the declared algebraic initial-plus-update-cost model, not a
claim to have executed long stateful trajectories. Linear measurement ranks
and serialized bytes are not lower bounds on arbitrary nonlinear encodings or
shortest programs. Fixed reset outcomes, declared consumers and no free source
reacquisition remain material limitations.

The [neural design](../experiments/neural_design.md) freezes an ordinary 4→32→1
ReLU/sigmoid network with 193 parameters. Bernoulli outcome labels and weighted
binary cross-entropy train the task; internal expected-cost targets are excluded.
Adam uses 3,000 batches of 256, and post-hoc searches use 128 candidates with
exactly eight coordinates per role. Five matched search arms (aligned plus four controls), four high-level cost
scales, two roles and five donor strata include random, permuted-concept,
wrong-donor and untrained comparisons. Positive rescaling and permutation
transport the subset/decoder rather than refitting it.

The contract fixes 8,192 evaluation pairs per role/stratum/model, five final
models, familywise alpha 0.05 over 560 interval rows, base/intervention tolerances,
control advantages and cost-scale separation. All final models must be trained,
saved, hashed and validated before **any** final data are generated. Final
preparation/evaluation are explicit F15 actions and have not run. A development
sample of the same count is still development. CPython 3.12 and NumPy 2.3.5 are
pinned separately from the legacy neural runtime, with one-thread numerical
settings. Deterministic PCG64/SeedSequence streams, search budgets, rejection
limits, file hashes and a bounded one-retry policy make execution reviewable.

[Design notes](../experiments/F14_design_notes.md) retain the argument that
intervention sensitivity is feasible while broader mechanistic claims are not
identified. A cancellation example passes isolated cost-style swaps without
