## 2. The strongest ordinary construction is capable of the same service

The construction in [ordinary_baseline.md](contribution/ordinary_baseline.md)
uses the same available information, rational source sets, expressions, units,
source authority, query budgets, and acquisition rights as the native route.
For a retained finite law it stores either the full law or any basis of the
same old-measurement row space. It solves revised numeric queries and pairwise
regret over the **same feasible law set**, using exact arithmetic. For the
larger signed piecewise-affine fragment it splits the same expression cases
and supplies exact branch certificates. If native availability is being
tested, its premise access is restricted to the same unit-reachable reduct;
full-source validity is a separately labeled service.

The ordinary producer may emit the same native proof language. The receiver
compares the proof root and complete context against the actual current
request. A source version, permitted evidence path, expression, unit or budget
change triggers the same eligibility decision. A proof of an old true claim
does not discharge a changed request. An old stored law also loses authority
when the protocol withdraws it. None of those restrictions creates an
exclusive native capability.

For decision action `a`, the ordinary method calculates

`R(a) = max_b sup_{p in P} (C_a(p) - C_b(p))`.

This equals coherent worst regret over the common law set `P`. It keeps the
same dependence that separate scalar intervals may lose. It may refuse an
absolute cost estimate while certifying an action, or certify a tolerance
regret claim without certifying a tighter comparison with fallback. These
are changes of requested guarantee, not failures of ordinary decision theory.

All five source/solver/checker/retention/decision components can be ordinary
and still jointly implement the full useful service. Empirical applicability
and calibration remain additional assumptions for both routes. This
reconstruction is an explanation of reproducibility, not a proposal that
the native method receives information its comparator must discard.

## 3. Decisive prior-method comparisons

Full bibliographic information, exact reading scope, source URLs, web locators
and failures are preserved in [source_record.md](contribution/source_record.md).

**Incremental abstraction-carrying code is the closest new interface challenge.**
Albert, Arenas and Puebla's 2006 construction retains a program abstraction
and dependencies, propagates changes from untrusted program updates, and
tests the updated certificate against the consumer's policy. Its discussion
also recognizes that certificate reduction can remove information needed
for later incremental checking. This makes source change, retained proof
information, consumer-specific acceptance and storage/checking tradeoffs
established parts of a single operational approach, rather than isolated
ingredients that Value Logic first assembles. The specialization here concerns
the loss consumer and its explicit price-generated information problem.

**PCC already requires a consumer-defined interface and validation of its
conditions.** It further grounds why a proof of some proposition is insufficient
for an actual consumer. Current-request identity in this repository is useful
engineering, and formalizing its exact treatment is useful application work;
the general need is not a new logical insight.

**Optimal recovery already provides the entire sharp-answer solver.** Substituting
the probability simplex for the model set, retained old means for the
observation operator, and revised means for the quantity of interest in
Ettehad–Foucart's polytope result yields the ordinary midpoint/Chebyshev-center
and dual-certificate construction. Bounded old-observation errors also fit
that model directly. The potential addition is the evaluated reset-price
matrix and its explicit constants/witnesses, not the general recovery method.

These readings weaken a standalone architecture claim substantially. They do
not show that the specific price-family classifications or specialized radius
were previously stated. Conversely, not seeing those formulas in a bounded
reading is not evidence that they are globally new. The positive assessment
rests on an eligible worked application with explicit additional consequences
relative to the actual compared formulations.

## 5. Fresh ordinary stability control limits the small-edit evidence

For one unchanged reset procedure family, changing only attempt price `j` by
`epsilon` gives a pathwise cost change `epsilon * 1{j is reached}`. Therefore
every revised mean belongs to

`C_old + [min(0,epsilon), max(0,epsilon)]`.

Exact old means alone permit a midpoint estimate with maximum error
`|epsilon|/2`. Keeping an old minimizer over the same action family, including
an unchanged fixed fallback, gives worst regret at most `|epsilon|`. Both
results are uniform over all laws in the old-summary fiber; neither requires
reconstructing that law or optimizing the fiber.

F15 uses `|epsilon|=1/40` and tolerance `1/20`. Thus the coarse numeric error
bound `1/80` and regret bound `1/40` already satisfy the corresponding
acceptance criteria in the two small-price arms. Their favorable numeric and
action outcomes cannot specifically establish the utility of C4-A1's sharper
`|epsilon|/4` radius at `M=4`.

This argument **does not claim to reproduce all 28 useful small-edit episodes**.
The native `1/20` fallback margin, actual selected order and nonzero-premise
requirements are additional conditions. Their satisfaction can depend on
sharper information. F15 also contains 15 useful program-edit episodes and
large-price outcomes. Its saved common-fiber example at seed 15105 certifies
an order with regret zero while every scalar answer is too imprecise and the
rectangular relaxation fails. That is a worthwhile application illustration,
with the exact ordinary fiber method correctly obtaining the same answer.

The distinction matters for future ambition: the sharp radius has a concrete
operational threshold interpretation, but F15 is an application test, not an
experimental confirmation of that closed-form theorem. For example, at
`epsilon=1/10, M=4`, a numeric tolerance between `1/40` and `1/20` distinguishes
the specialized radius from the coarse stability bound. This is a possible
future discriminator, not a retroactive F15 endpoint or new scientific run.

## 6. Costs and favorable-case selection

The reported accounting is appropriately hostile to speed claims. At equal
admitted service, cached proof is slower than fresh in every 98 no-reacquisition
and 126 adaptive comparison. Tailored versus ordinary intervals has positive
mean paired complete-service differences in both access regimes. Tiny median
resident savings do not establish a general memory improvement: an ordinary
method is allowed the same five-dimensional basis and encoding, while the
larger uncertain contexts can dominate the resident payload.

All arms initially see the eight source fields. A five-field retained summary
therefore does not demonstrate lower **initial observation cost**. Adaptive
source archives and the actual two-mean source work remain charged. F15's
quality-first policy buys numeric answers as well as decision quality, so its
38 zero-action-improvement repairs per selective method are an intended
limit on economic interpretation, not a concealed improvement. Its horizon
tables repeat one measured update algebraically; they are not a repeated
revision experiment with retained acquired state.

There is no evidence here of changing the frozen generator or dropping
unfavorable episodes after seeing the outcome. Nevertheless, the known
three-procedure generator, exact old information, fixed tolerance, and positive
initial weight support strongly bound external relevance. General-`k` rank
derivations supply breadth of mathematical scope; they do not by themselves
measure scaling or real-world applicability.
