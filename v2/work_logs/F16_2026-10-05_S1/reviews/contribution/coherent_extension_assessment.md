## 2. Why this is more than generic LP computability

Let `P` be the current law fiber and `Q(P)` its vector of demanded means.
Generic optimal recovery solves for a center `z` in the ambient output
space. Requiring `z=Q(p_c)` for some `p_c in P` is an additional feasibility
constraint. An ordinary LP can enforce it, but general convexity does not
guarantee that it leaves the optimum unchanged.

The new proof establishes that equality for this entire, precisely specified
family, and constructs a witness. It consequently answers a question that
the generic LP formulation merely poses. The singleton-dominance conclusion
also identifies which uncertainty controls the shared tolerance. These are
concrete mathematical consequences capable of supporting the author's
eligible modest synthesis/application criterion.

The distinction from the old `k=4` example is essential: the theorem supplies
one center at the largest required coordinate tolerance, not a simultaneous
realization of every coordinate's individual interval midpoint. Narrower
coordinates may move away from their own midpoints while remaining within
the common optimal tolerance. Both statements can therefore remain true.

## 3. The ordinary baseline remains fully capable

The ordinary competitor may use exactly this level-mixture formula, or solve
the coherent-center LP over the same law set. It receives no additional
observations and needs no new native inference primitive. The extension thus
adds an explicit specialized solution and understanding; it does not defeat
the ordinary equal-information construction in the original F16 review.

The global-radius formula uses `O(k)` arithmetic evaluation, and its maximizer
can use the stated exact adjacent comparisons. This is a genuine reduction
of that mathematical subproblem. It is not an end-to-end runtime or memory
result: reading an arbitrary old summary, obtaining canonical residues,
materializing an exponential-size law, and constructing/checking certificates
remain separate costs. The ordinary implementation may exploit the same
simplification. No F15 timing or acquisition outcome is changed.

## 4. Source and consumer boundaries are load-bearing

The source must be the **full** compatible fiber for the complete exact old
profile. In the inspected §1, the phrase "No additional nonconvex restrictions"
should be tightened to "No additional source restrictions": excluding only
nonconvex ones could invite the invalid convex-extension reading exposed by
§10. This wording issue was sent to the principal; this reviewer did not edit
the derivation.

The proposed §10 triangle has ambient midpoint radius `delta/2` and a
compatible-center radius `2delta/3`. Once its reset-law realization receives
the separate acceptance check, it supplies a sharp local boundary: even extra
convex information can create a **relative gap** between the two decoder
contracts. This does not mean that adding information increases absolute
uncertainty compared with the original larger fiber.

The positive theorem does not establish the corresponding equality under
noisy old summaries, new confidence-set intersections, unequal old prices,
simultaneous price edits, changed terminal penalty, or arbitrary downstream
loss functionals. A bounded family of **separately applied** single-price
edits has the stated supremum-magnitude scaling. Broader consumers require
their own error analysis.

A compatible decoded law is still an estimate, not newly established source
truth. Its common error guarantee applies to the declared consumer family.
It may be reused as a coherent representation with that guarantee, but a
receiver must not insert it as exact authority for arbitrary future queries.
Current source/version binding and a certificate over the actual full source
remain necessary.
