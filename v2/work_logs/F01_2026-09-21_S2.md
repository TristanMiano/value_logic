The main result for task design is that exact recovery and useful inference
are different success conditions. For uniform ternary marginal payoffs,
covariance zero leaves an interval of possible expected bottlenecks, but its
sharp interval [-1/2,-1/3] can certify a tolerance that the marginal-only
interval [-2/3,0] cannot. A second pointwise proof weakens the premise to
bounded support and specified moments. An explicit family shows that dropping
bounded support invalidates both endpoints; a weaker moment-only sharp range
survives instead. Thus a failed exact representation may still be a useful
approximate representation under declared consumer requirements.

strict sign judgments; preservation of bounded regret under a restricted cost

unbounded evaluations. Every result states its scope and a constructive positive
