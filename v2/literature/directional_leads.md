# Primary-source orientation for DIR01

Accessed September 24, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles; September 25 UTC).
This supports a **direction decision**, not another completed F03 research
session. The F03 numbered source manifest, import register and clock credit
remain unchanged. The D-identifiers below are local to this note. Most checks
are of author abstracts: they justify relevance, not importing full theorems.
No full papers or images are redistributed, and no new priority claim is made.

## D01 — Utility-oriented logic

Enrique H. Ruspini, *Truth as Utility: A Conceptual Synthesis*, UAI 1991,
pp. 316–322; archival arXiv:1303.5744v1, submitted 2013.
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.5744v1 .
Inspected: abstract, publication comment and report number.

The author relates constraint-dependent solution quality to logical operations
and aggregates goal-specific desirability into utility; the abstract also names
ignorance about utility. This supports the author's conceptual starting point.
It does not establish a modern ML surrogate-loss theorem. A future exact
comparison must read the relevant definitions and operations, not rely on this
orientation or call the 2013 archival upload the original publication date.

## D02 — Arithmetic and consequence as a mechanistic comparison

Giorgio Bacci, Radu Mardare, Prakash Panangaden, Gordon Plotkin,
*Rational Lawvere Logic*, CSL 2026, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3.
Primary full HTML: https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol363-csl2026/html/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3.html .
Inspected: sections 2–4, particularly residual definition, semantic consequence,
rule descriptions and the deduction-theorem discussion; see F03 S12 for the
previously checked interfaces and limits.

The additive residual on finite inputs is truncated subtraction. Identifying
that finite operation with ReLU is elementary algebra, not neural interpretation.
The paper distinguishes numerical residuals from Booleanized formulas. Its
deduction theorem must not be confused with a language of self-referential proof
predicates or an unrestricted reflection theorem. The project's value/loss
interpretation and any changed domain or reflective extension need their own
semantics and proof. Endpoint conventions and source guards remain relevant.

## D03 — Quantities need not be degrees of truth

Kevin Batz, Benjamin Lucien Kaminski, Joost-Pieter Katoen, Christoph Matheja,
Thomas Noll, *Quantitative Separation Logic — A Logic for Reasoning about
Probabilistic Programs*, arXiv:1802.10467.
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.10467 . Inspected: author abstract.

Its quantities and lifted connectives conservatively extend predicates and
support quantitative program reasoning. This is an antecedent for placing
Boolean assertions inside a broader quantitative language. It is not a claim
that the current Value Logic fragment inherits its soundness or frame rule.

## D04 — Uncertainty about one's own reasoning

Scott Garrabrant, Tsvi Benson-Tilsen, Andrew Critch, Nate Soares, Jessica Taylor,
*Logical Induction*, arXiv:1609.03543.
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03543 . Inspected: author abstract.

The construction assigns changing probabilities to formal statements, including
statements about its own probabilities, with stated self-reference properties.
It is a concrete lead for logical non-omniscience and introspection. Its formal
language and assumptions must be audited before adaptation. This abstract check
supplies no efficient implementation, arbitrary metalogical uncertainty theorem,
or result about the proposed loss-based calculus.

## D05 — Feedback-aware self-reference

Benja Fallenstein, Jessica Taylor, Paul F. Christiano, *Reflective Oracles:
A Foundation for Classical Game Theory*, arXiv:1508.04145.
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04145 . Inspected: author abstract.

The oracle answers questions about machines that can query that same oracle,
using randomization on some queries to avoid diagonalization. This warrants
examining probabilistic self-reference rather than banning cycles in advance.
It does not supply a readily executable ML evaluator, uniqueness of all fixed
points, or a theorem that every uncertain reflective calculus is coherent.

## D06 — The proxy-to-task bridge is a theorem question

P. L. Bartlett, M. I. Jordan, J. D. McAuliffe, *Convexity, Classification,
and Risk Bounds*, Berkeley Statistics Technical Report 638 (2003).
Primary author-institution record:
https://live-statistics.pantheon.berkeley.edu/tech-reports/638 .
Inspected: report identity and abstract, not the full proof.

The report describes relationships between nonnegative surrogate risks and
classification excess risk under consistency conditions. This supplies a
specific comparison for why a low proxy loss requires a bridge to task value.
The classification setup cannot simply be relabeled as arbitrary utility.
Before claiming a novel bound, compare the exact assumptions and constants.

## D07 — ReLU realization is a baseline

Raman Arora, Amitabh Basu, Poorya Mianjy, Anirbit Mukherjee,
*Understanding Deep Neural Networks with Rectified Linear Units*,
arXiv:1611.01491. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01491 .
Inspected: author abstract; existing phase-one realization proofs remain the
project's detailed reference for the finite continuous piecewise-affine fragment.

The source studies representable functions and complexity. It motivates small
MLP examples without implying that their neurons encode cost or that ordinary
training discovers the desired interpretation. Check actual learned structure
separately from representation capacity.

## D08 — Interventions as evidence for an internal interpretation

Atticus Geiger, Hanson Lu, Thomas Icard, Christopher Potts,
*Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks*, arXiv:2106.02997 (NeurIPS 2021).
Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997 . Inspected: author abstract.

The method aligns neural representations with variables of an interpretable
causal model and tests that alignment by interchange interventions. This is a
methodological precedent for evaluating the proposed loss/value interpretation.
Its reported natural-language model experiment is not evidence about this
project's untrained or newly trained MLP. Exact intervention assumptions and
controls must be checked when the concrete probe is designed.

## What has not been established

No claim that these intersections are absent from the literature, no new
calibration theorem, no reflective-model existence theorem, and no learned
neural mechanism is established by this note. The value of the orientation is
its concrete next questions. The opportunity register and F04 must turn a
promising relationship into a discriminating test rather than extending the
source catalogue indefinitely.
