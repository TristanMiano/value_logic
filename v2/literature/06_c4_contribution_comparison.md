## 1. Utility, quantitative logic and assurance are established ingredients

**Enrique H. Ruspini, _Truth as Utility: A Conceptual Synthesis_**, UAI 1991,
316–322; [archival manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.5744), uploaded 2013.
Fresh inspection: introduction and sections 3.1–3.4. Goal-relative adequacy,
utility-based connectives and conditional comparisons are precedents. The
paper does not simply identify factual truth with personal convenience; its
utility construction has an explicitly relative scope. Value Logic should
claim neither the first utility-oriented logic nor the first response to
uncertain useful knowledge. Its signed loss objects and revision interface
are a different realization to compare, not a new motivation.

**Giorgio Bacci, Radu Mardare, Prakash Panangaden and Gordon Plotkin,
_Rational Lawvere Logic_**, CSL 2026,
[primary PDF](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol363-csl2026/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3.pdf).
C4 inspected the semantic-consequence discussion around theorem 17; the
broader theorem/guard audit remains the earlier F10 record. Likewise reuse
F10's Riesz-proof comparison rather than claiming a fresh complete reread.
Quantitative arithmetic, logical consequence and polyhedral proof machinery
are antecedents. The project-specific directed unit reduct and current-request
interface need their own scoped claims; no general complexity result is
transferred to this implementation.

**Benjamin Herd, Jessica Kelly, Jan Sabsch and Lydia Gauerhof,
_Towards a compositional semantics for quantitative confidence assessment
in assurance arguments_**, [arXiv:2605.22213v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22213v1),
May 21, 2026. Fresh inspection: sections II, III-B and IV-C. The proposed
Subjective Logic semantics carries contextual assumptions and makes edge
warrants explicit, including second-order confidence. Conditional and
unconditional readings differ; assumption confidence must not be discounted
twice. Thus compositional assumptions, confidence and warrants are not new
by themselves. Their opinion calculus and our worst-case signed loss bounds
answer different questions; this does not prove either cannot encode the
other. No calibration or native completeness theorem is imported.

**Benjamin Herd, Jessica Kelly, Clarissa Heinemann and João-Vitor Zacchi,
_A Subjective Logic-based method for runtime confidence updates in safety
arguments_**, [arXiv:2605.22530v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22530v1), May 21, 2026.
Fresh inspection: sections 2.2–2.3.5 and the limitations in section 5.
Operational indicators revise confidence, including deliberately non-Bayesian
refuting penalties. Dependence and overlapping evidence remain explicit
limitations. Runtime evidence changing an assurance judgment is established
methodology. Value Logic's receipt/source-version checks cannot themselves
supply a calibrated probabilistic interpretation of such updates.

**Robin Bloomfield and John Rushby, _Quantifying Confidence in Assurance 2.0
Arguments_**, [arXiv:2604.00034v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00034v1), 2026;
the author's bibliography also identifies the underlying December 2025
technical report. Fresh inspection: introduction and sections 3.2.3–3.2.4.
The argument separates logical, dialectical and probabilistic assessments,
and treats dependence through probability bounds. Distinguishing internal
system probabilities from confidence in an argument is a close precedent.
Our four-way account of modeled validity, native warrant, current reception
and deployment applicability is a project-specific operational decomposition,
not a first discovery that proof and confidence differ.

## 2. Proof grounding and learned monitoring already combine several layers

**Junyu Ren, _Evidence-Grounded Verified Agentic Reasoning: A Path Toward
Eliminating LLM Hallucination in Empirical Inference via Tool-Attested Kernel
Proofs_**, [arXiv:2607.12650v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12650v1), July 14, 2026.
Fresh inspection: sections 3.1 and 3.6, theorems 3.1–3.2, section 5.1,
appendices M.1 and P.1. The architecture already combines source tools,
interpretive lifts, kernel proofs, dependency restrictions and abstention.
Its guarantees remain relative to attestation and audited interpretation;
semantic faithfulness is a separate boundary. This is a strong counterexample
to a generic "first proof-grounded evidence system" claim. Our Python checker
is not their Lean kernel or attestation service. The checked material does
not give our weighted loss-revision ranks or revision-specific approximation
radius; its software and empirical claims were not reproduced here.

**Francesca Cairoli, Nicola Paoletti and Luca Bortolussi, _Conformal
Quantitative Predictive Monitoring of STL Requirements for Stochastic
Processes_**, [arXiv:2211.02375v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.02375v2), April 6, 2023.
Fresh inspection: quantitative semantics, problem 1, section 3 definition 0
and coverage equation (1), compositional monitoring and experimental setup.
Quantitative logical semantics, learned predictions, uncertainty and runtime
decisions are already combined. Coverage probabilities require their stated
sampling interpretation; a marginal calibration result is not automatically
a conditional guarantee at every chosen input. C4 does not adjudicate every
coverage claim in that paper or import it as a calibration theorem for the
planned neural probe. Value Logic has not yet demonstrated a learned monitor.

## 3. Exact retention: the generic principles and the specific matrix

**Oleksandra Gasanova and Lisa Nicklasson, _Chain algebras of finite
distributive lattices_**, J. Algebraic Combinatorics 59 (2024), 473–494,
[open primary text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-023-01294-8).
Freshly rechecked theorem 3.4 and its maximal-chain span argument. F13's
equal-price rank has this direct antecedent. C4's extra calculation intersects
two unequal-price kernels and identifies their surviving information; the
checked theorem does not state that calculation, its minimal actual-mean
repair or its known-marginal variant. That is a comparison with this theorem,
not proof that no equivalent result exists elsewhere.

**Felix Happach, Lisa Hellerstein and Thomas Lidbetter, _A General Framework
for Approximating Min Sum Ordering Problems_**,
[arXiv:2004.05954v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.05954v2), 2020 manuscript,
published 2022. Reuse F13's checked section 2, equations (1)–(2), and section
2.4; C4 renewed the primary-source search but claims no second complete read.
Weighted first-success chain costs fit an existing optimization formulation.
The new candidate is a specific observation matrix's revision sensitivity,
not a new scheduling objective or approximation algorithm.

**Rayen Tan and Viswanath Nagarajan, _Distributionally Robust k-of-n
Sequential Testing_**, [arXiv:2603.23705v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.23705),
March 2026. Fresh inspection: abstract and section 1.1, including the prefix
cost formula and uncertainty model. Robust expected-cost sequential testing
is an existing problem. Their independent Bernoulli probability intervals
and algorithmic approximation objective differ from our arbitrary correlated
joint-law simplex and exact-retention consumer. We do not claim a new robust
testing problem or transfer their algorithmic guarantees.

**Satinder Singh, Michael R. James and Matthew R. Rudary, _Predictive State
Representations: A New Theory for Modeling Dynamical Systems_**, UAI 2004,
[primary manuscript](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~isbell/reading/papers/uai2004psr.pdf).
Fresh inspection: system-dynamics matrix, section 4/theorems 4–6 and section 5.
Minimal linear prediction representations and core-test bases are established.
Nonlinear representation classes can change compression possibilities.
C4 restricts linear retention of a finite probability law but permits arbitrary
decoding; it does not identify those assumptions with every PSR model class.
Its explicit weighted finite query ranks are the candidate delta.

**Alex Kulesza, Nan Jiang and Satinder Singh, _Spectral Learning of Predictive
State Representations with Insufficient Statistics_**, AAAI 2015,
[primary article](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/9635).
Abstract-level inspection only. Approximation quality under reduced predictive
statistics is an established concern; exact rank is not a sample or stability
bound. No theorem body from this paper is used in a C4 proof.

**Rianne de Heide, _Statistical reduction before the target is known: two
boundary results_**, [arXiv:2609.05286v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05286v1),
September 4, 2026. Fresh inspection: section 2, theorem 1, proof and corollary 2;
the Gaussian-stream result only at abstract/introduction level. Its finite
Blackwell–Bahadur specialization already connects preservation of all later
decision problems to sufficiency. C4 instead fixes a restricted family of
numeric price queries and computes its exact ranks and repair count. It must
not claim to discover the generic obstruction to unknown future uses.

**László Györfi, Tamás Linder and Harro Walk, _Lossless Transformations and
Excess Risk Bounds in Statistical Inference_**, Entropy 25(10), 1394 (2023),
[open primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10606681/).
Fresh inspection: theorem 1 and its sufficiency interpretation, theorem 3's
conditional sub-Gaussian hypotheses, corollary 1 and section 4 applications.
Universal and approximate preservation across losses are already explicit.
Our sharp radius concerns worst-case recovery of a specified finite vector
of means from an exact old vector, not excess Bayes decision risk or an
information-theoretic learning guarantee. A generic "loss-aware compression"
claim would therefore be much too broad.

## 4. Approximation: use optimal recovery rather than reinvent its principle

**Mahmood Ettehad and Simon Foucart, _Instances of Computational Optimal
Recovery: Dealing with Observation Errors_**, SIAM/ASA J. Uncertainty
Quantification 9(4), 1438–1456 (2021),
[author manuscript](https://foucart.github.io/publi/OR_Uncertainty_v2.pdf),
[publication record](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/20M1328476).
Fresh inspection: equations (1)–(9), section 2.1/theorem 1 and its dual-LP
proof. Their framework already supplies worst-case recovery of linear
quantities, consistency sets, Chebyshev centers and polytope algorithms.
C4-A1 is a small explicit evaluation for the reset-price query matrix, not
a new general minimax or LP method. Its sharp constant and attaining laws
are the candidate technical addition. No execution of their code is claimed.

interaction index domain by means of sparse modeling_**, Expert Systems with

The indexed primary preview supplied the abstract and introduction; a direct

studies identifiability using augmented decision-matrix rank and sparse
representations. This is a close precedent, not evidence of a newly discovered
generic identification question. F16 should inspect its full identifiability
section if obtainable before making a stronger priority claim. No absence
of the precise C4 formulas from that unread section is asserted.
An additional exact-title/author search and institutional-copy search at
close still did not obtain that section. A same-author 2017 SBrT precursor's
indexed abstract discusses sparse interaction-representation fitting, but
its full PDF timed out and is not substituted for the 2022 identifiability
analysis. This limitation remains visible in the contribution assessment.

**Ezgi Türkarslan and Vicenç Torra, _Measure Identification for the Choquet
Integral: A Python Module_**, International Journal of Computational
Intelligence Systems 15, 89 (2022),
[primary open text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44196-022-00146-w).
Fresh inspection: section 2 definitions 1–4 and the identification setup.
The paper supplies the finite Choquet formula, normalized fuzzy-measure
constraints and established fitting machinery, including restricted measure
classes. The new notebook maps reset costs explicitly into a Choquet sum.
Our coverage function can be nonnormalized because all procedures can fail;
its feasibility also requires a Boolean joint law. These distinctions prevent
blind import of normalized-capacity claims. No execution of its package or
general advantage over its identification methods is claimed.

## 6. Search scope and consequences

Searches covered utility logic, quantitative assurance, runtime confidence,
proof-grounded empirical inference, weighted maximal chains, sequential
testing/search, joint-distribution identification from mean costs, predictive
state rank, sufficient reductions and optimal recovery. Searches used the
alternate terms capacity identification, weighted permutation paths and
set-function reconstruction as well. Searches used the
ordinary problem terminology as well as the project's vocabulary. Some hits
concerned unrelated weighted-poset enumeration or sequential hypothesis
testing. A guessed AAAI URL led to a different article and was rejected;
the correct PSR record is linked above. Several HTML/PDF endpoints failed;
alternate primary PDFs supplied the readings explicitly reported here.
No external code was executed, no exhaustive citation-graph search was made,
and no failed retrieval is counted as an inspected source.

The strongest ordinary construction combines: a joint source model, a
polyhedral/optimal-recovery solver, explicit inference certificates, source
and query version checks, and conventional decision or metareasoning rules.
It can reproduce the useful behavior under equal assumptions. That defeats
claims of exclusive capability or generic solver superiority. It does **not**
automatically defeat a modest integration/application contribution; its
defensible content must be the precise combined contract and worked new
consequences, rather than possession of these established components.

The review supports a bounded comparison: the inspected works establish the
ingredients and general methods, while C4 supplies an explicit two-price
classification, minimal actual-mean repair and a sharp specialized recovery
radius in this project. The optional equal-price extension also reduces exact
conditional recovery to a residual moment problem and supplies a joint-law
coherence counterexample. Those use established moment/optimal-recovery
methods. Worldwide firstness remains unestablished. The
project-level synthesis is best presented together with these concrete
consequences; a broad assertion that value-oriented or evidence-grounded
reasoning itself is new is displaced.

## 7. Sampling extension: established uniform empirical control

**Henry W. J. Reeve, _A short proof of the Dvoretzky–Kiefer–Wolfowitz–Massart
inequality_**, arXiv:2403.16651v1 (March 25, 2024),
[primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.16651v1).
Fresh inspection: independent common-distribution setup, theorem 1 and
corollaries 1–2 on pages 1–3; the introductory comparison with Massart (1990).
Corollary 1's global one-sided bound, applied also to reflected integer
observations, yields the familiar two-sided `2 exp(-2 n eta^2)` bound used
in C4-A3. This concentration principle is established. C4 only supplies the
nested-prefix observation reduction under exact old-summary information.
The Massart DOI endpoint and Dudley exposition endpoint failed to load;
neither is counted as fully inspected. A guessed Reeve v2 HTML was unavailable;
the PDF explicitly identifies v1. No external package was run.
