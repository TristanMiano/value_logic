# Decision: Build the Value-Based Calculus First

Date: September 19, 2026.
Status: authorized roadmap change; not a completed research result.

## Decision and authority

The author selected the calculus-first alternative over model substitution,
inquiry/self-revision, and continuation of the contract-and-inverse roadmap,
and explicitly requested publication of these changes through GitHub.

The author also required recurrent task design, evidence of a sufficiently
well-founded calculus before advancing, prospective and measured effort,
minimum-duration tasks, separate derivation/literature/empirical work, and a
balance between reliable and difficult/uncertain gains.

## Consequences

The active control document is `../../TODO_v2.md`. Phase-one calculus choices
remain available results, not mandatory foundations. The former contract
carrier, price oracle, task quotient, and active-identification experiment are
deferred alternatives rather than the next phase's fixed theorem targets.

The new roadmap compares foundations through separating examples, develops
rules and proofs, checks them against prior literature and executable models,
and reaches publication only through explicit evidence gates. Failed gates
create targeted repair loops, with a strategy review after repeated failure.

The 60/15/25 D/L/E and 60/40 reliable/exploratory allocations, individual
forecasts, and recurrence reserve are provisional scheduling decisions. They
are not claims about what the author explicitly prescribed. Protected task
minimums and their measurement rules are explicit, not implied by those shares.
