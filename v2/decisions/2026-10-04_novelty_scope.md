# Author clarification: novelty type, magnitude and scope

October 4, 2026. Recorded by **Codex (GPT-6)** during C4.
Status: adopted at the author's explicit direction.

Novelty may occur in the project's framing, in a useful synthesis or application
of established techniques, in a methodology, or in an individual technical
result. A modest original combination may qualify without a new primitive
method, an efficiency advantage, or a major theorem. Evaluate both the whole
project and its parts; no one level is mandatory.

Every positive assessment must say what is added, relative to which checked
antecedents, with what usefulness and magnitude, and what remains unverified.
Separate supported comparative distinctiveness from a global claim to be first.
An independently implemented or locally derived known result is technical
progress, not automatically externally novel. An exact combination's absence
from a bounded search is evidence with limits, never proof of worldwide absence.

Gates C/D retain the requirement for an honestly supported contribution, but
must apply this wider range of eligible types and modest magnitudes. Do not
require performance superiority for a conceptual, methodological or synthesis
claim. If the eligible claim still lacks support, assign the precise further
comparison or work; if it is supported, record its narrow scope and any stronger
claims that remain open. Historical assessments remain dated evidence under
their original comparison and do not disappear by administrative relabeling.

This clarification authorizes C4 under its existing research60 floor and
forecasts. It does not execute F14, alter the cumulative package clock, or pass
a readiness gate. Current selection and outcomes belong in the TODO and
contribution plan.
