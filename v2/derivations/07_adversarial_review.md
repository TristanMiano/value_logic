## 3. Load-bearing mathematical reconstruction

### 3.1 What is proved, and relative to what?

The semantic object is a typed, signed piecewise-affine loss expression on
explicit source assignments. Contexts contain finitely many nonempty rational
polyhedral live cases. A judgment bounds the difference of one literal pair
over its stated domain. The scalar budget is part of that judgment, not an
assertion that the source premises or the chosen loss function are empirically
true. A collection of cases denotes a union, not their intersection.

Three levels must remain distinct. Full-source modeled validity can use every
source constraint. Native warrant can use only premise units admitted by the
directed unit graph. Current receipt additionally binds the checked root to
the caller's actual context, pair, scope, unit and requested budget. A
successfully checked proof of one statement cannot certify another caller's
request. None of these levels independently validates external source honesty,
the semantics of an action in deployment, or probability calibration.

The sixteen native rule families admit a pointwise induction. Shared source
variables are evaluated at one assignment, so addition and cancellation do
not require independence. Min/max congruence preserves signed budgets through
translation by a common constant. Residual congruence instead needs clipping
at zero: the unchanged zero branch prevents an unrestricted negative result.
Unit conversions use their actual positive factors; cycles need not multiply
to one. Lexical normalization keeps captured environments distinct and checks
typing before erasing zero coefficients.

Live-case nonemptiness blocks vacuous contextual claims. Infeasible temporary
sign branches in a derivation are a different object: they need a strict
infeasibility certificate before discharge. All-case aggregation needs the
same pair in every live case; the semantics of a minimum of terms do not
automatically supply a deployed policy that can observe which case holds.

### 3.2 Completeness without circular macro use

The correct native characterization concerns the target unit's reduct, not
arbitrary full-source entailment. A foreign-unit row can constrain the full
model while remaining unavailable to a root with no incoming path. A simple
one-way factor-two conversion gives a concrete separation. Graph-wide
full-source completeness needs every relevant source unit to reach the target;
particular contexts can have weaker sufficient conditions.

The completeness construction proceeds through finite piecewise-affine
normal forms, affine branch consequences and exact linear duality. The
positive-min macro is not justified by the theorem it is used to prove. Its
dependency chain can be reconstructed from native disjoint-hinge identities,
generic affine splitting, typed substitution and distributivity before using
the characterization theorem. A 192-node native positive-min proof with no
source rows and a fourteen-node converted-min proof provide concrete checks
of these construction dependencies. They are demonstrations of those macros,
not complexity bounds for the complete producer.

The relevant unit-dependence lemma is **denotational**. An unused lexical
binding can contain a syntactic foreign-unit source while the result denotes
zero in the target unit. The separate reviewer initially stated the stronger
syntactic claim, then corrected it after checking the existing proof, which
already used the right condition. This is a review-process correction, not
a newly found defect in the accepted theorem.

### 3.3 Revision and retained proofs

For a fixed constraint matrix and query, dual feasible vertices supply a
finite portfolio of candidate certificates whose best value is the exact
bound across admissible right-hand sides. A particular retained witness can
remain safe while becoming less sharp than a fresh optimum. Withdrawal of a
source row restricts eligible dual multipliers to its zero-coordinate face;
it does not authorize reusing its old contribution through changed metadata.
Existing source-transport results already record the corresponding portfolio
condition. This review supplies a fresh reconstruction and examples, not a
new claim of discovering certificate maintenance.

Changing evidence right-hand sides, changing a query's prices, and changing
program meanings are different operations. The first can preserve a fixed
certificate template. The second changes the root expression and request.
The third needs an operational transport argument. Current receipt protects
the declared numerical contract; a stale content hash is neither a transport
proof nor an empirical warranty.

Source-distance penalties are also conditional on geometry. The dual feasible
set can be unbounded even when its relevant finite vertices give a finite
Hoffman coefficient. Taking the supremum over all dual multipliers would be
wrong. For rows `x<=0` and `-x+e*y<=0`, the sharp coefficient is `1+2/e`;
with e=1/100, row relaxations of 1/100 permit an x+y increase of 201/100.
Small row violations alone therefore give no scale-free practical stability.
Weighted query sensitivities can sharpen a particular transfer, but zero
weights give a seminorm that no longer characterizes the whole source set.

### 3.4 Applicability, self-assessment and dependence

A simultaneous source-coverage event supports a query selected after seeing
the data: accepted-and-false is contained in source-coverage failure. This
does not imply the same error rate conditional on acceptance, or under
arbitrary stopping from merely fixed-time coverage. It also does not turn a
high-probability signed loss bound into an expectation bound without control
of exceptional losses. The fresh notes include exact Bernoulli examples and
the needed acceptance-event and integrability qualifications.

The bounded report/self-assessment calculation keeps the report, its
consequences and the branch-conditioned probabilities separate. A chosen
policy's expected loss is an affine function of its fixed randomization
parameter and source probabilities. Treating both that parameter and those
probabilities as free object-language variables would introduce products
outside the signed piecewise-affine fragment. Minimax robustness does not
by itself mean every underlying case improves, and an internally asserted
confidence report cannot authenticate its own applicability.

Several proofs sharing one source are not independent evidence. If at most
k arguments are inapplicable, the (k+1)-st smallest bound is safe under that
specific assumption. If instead at most k **sources** are faulty, the correct
envelope is the maximum, over allowed fault sets, of the best surviving proof
whose dependency set avoids the fault. Replicating one proof does not improve
that guarantee. The root notes derive the associated hitting-set condition
and distinguish its construction cost from checking one received witness.

### 3.5 Retention, price revision and the new coherence result

The reset-family mean is obtained by charging each attempt on its preceding
failure event plus the terminal penalty on complete failure. Adjacent swaps
give the proper-moment kernel; connected subset-exchange graphs and nonzero
price products establish its coefficients. Two nonproportional price profiles
remove every proper-moment direction. Whether the remaining terminal direction
is visible depends on the penalties and on whether the consumer demands
numeric means, within-profile differences or cross-profile differences.

The lower bound on linear retention permits arbitrary decoding but needs an
open set of source directions. A finite law grid, arbitrary real-number
encoding or an uncharged original-law archive changes that information
contract. After one nonzero price edit, k-1 actual new order means determine
a chain of reach moments and repair the lost rank. Following a deterministic
adaptive acquisition transcript at an interior law does not remove the
linear-nullspace lower bound. This is an exact-information statement;
dividing noisy differences by a small edit may be poorly conditioned.

For k=3, the old fiber is a compact convex planar set in the common singleton
and pair shifts. Every such planar set contains its coordinate bounding-box
midpoint. Therefore one compatible law supplies all the proper moment
midpoints and all one-price revised-order mean midpoints. Its terminal moment
need not be at that moment's own interval midpoint. This proves F16-R01
without depending on the larger optional theorem.

For arbitrary k, the canonical retained contrasts reduce the remaining law
to a distribution on Hamming levels with one fixed expected level cost.
F16-C1 averages the singleton-minimizing endpoint distribution with the
singleton-maximizing adjacent-level distribution. The proof bounds every
proper-moment interval around that candidate using a one-turn polygonal
envelope argument and an explicit lower affine bound. Separate audits checked
both halves, including k=2, M=0, ties and nonexchangeable residue offsets.
This gives the common minimax radius from the singleton formula. The original
k=4 incompatible-midpoints example remains valid; common optimality and
coordinate-by-coordinate midpoint optimality are distinct demands.

The optional extension is a result about selecting a compatible prediction
law under uncertainty. It does not reconstruct the true population law and
does not identify a network's internal utility representation. Additional
source constraints change the fiber; the exact convex-source counterexample
in the extension exhibits a genuine coherence penalty in that larger class.

## 6. The strongest ordinary combined baseline

For a nonempty finite-law polytope `P` formed from the same authorized source,
let `C_i p` denote the demanded mean coordinates. An ordinary exact method
computes `L_i=min_P C_i p` and `U_i=max_P C_i p`. Without an output-law
constraint, the sup-norm radius is `max_i(U_i-L_i)/2`, attained by coordinate
midpoints. This does not assume independent costs: maximization over the
finite coordinate set commutes with the supremum over the common law.

The law-valued ordinary comparator solves the further linear program

    minimize r over q in P and r>=0,
    subject to U_i-C_i q<=r and C_i q-L_i<=r for every i.

It can therefore reproduce the coherent answer with exactly the same source.
Generic convexity only bounds its radius between the unrestricted radius and
twice that radius. F16-C1 proves equality of the two radii for the particular
full old-summary family and supplies an explicit attaining law. Its extra
convex-source triangle has a factor **4/3** gap. The theorem thus answers a
specific question posed by the ordinary LP; it does not introduce minimax
recovery as a new method or defeat the ordinary solver.

The ordinary method can also retain the same five-dimensional old information
in F15, use the same row-space repairs, calculate exact same-law regret as
`max_b sup_P(C_a-C_b)`, and compile native proofs for the same receiver. For
the general finite signed piecewise-affine fragment it uses the same source
and expression cells, with the same directed-unit eligibility. Full-source
reasoning is a separately labeled service for either method. Setup, source
storage, proof construction, current checks, failed reuse and acquisition must
all be charged. Neither a small certificate checker nor a smaller selected
payload proves a complete-service advantage.

An elementary control further limits the frozen small-price evidence. A
single price edit changes each path cost by `epsilon*1{edited procedure is
reached}`. Exact old means therefore give numeric error at most
`|epsilon|/2`; retaining the old optimizer over the same actions, including an
unchanged fallback, gives regret at most `|epsilon|`. With the frozen edit
`1/40`, these bounds are **1/80 and 1/40**, already below the **1/20**
tolerances. The specialized radius **1/160** at k3,M4 is sharper, but these
arms do not distinguish it by a new tolerance pass. The stronger native
fallback-margin/premise criterion is additional, so this argument does not
purport to reproduce all useful-native counts by itself.
