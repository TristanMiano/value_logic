## R1. What the Boolean entailment theorem actually translates

Begin with a classical valuation v and assign source x_i=1−v(p_i). In each
constructor the translated loss is the complemented classical value. The
conjunction of premises fails with loss P=max_i L(A_i); the conclusion fails
with loss L(B). The inequality L(B)<=P excludes exactly a valuation that makes
all premises true and the conclusion false. This proves classical entailment
without treating source assumptions as an arbitrary set of logical formulas.

The source interface uses affine rows, so Booleanity is supplied by finite
point cases. Every point is feasible, and a single literal comparison is proved
on every case. An inconsistent logical premise family merely makes P=1 on all
Boolean points; it does not require an empty or contradictory source context.
Thus the translation preserves explosion in its stated conditional form while
respecting the native nonempty-source requirement.

The bounded producer additionally proves every formula's value from those point
rows, composes the resulting native inequalities, and passes the unchanged
request receiver. It never asks the receiver to trust its Boolean interpreter.
The independent truth loop can reject an invalid input early; it is not a
trusted proof rule. Its six-atom development limit is an execution bound, not
a limitation of B2's mathematical statement.

## R2. The phase-one bridge has three separate interfaces

First, the meet-algebra equation codes already supplied statuses. Second,
the interval/rectangle adapter computes the same inclusive support and strict
refutation conditions as the actual old numerical consumers. Third, the full
old assessment also depends on well-formedness, atom roles, payloads and
provenance. None of the first two removes the third.

The exact point certificate [0,0] is a useful hostile test: at tolerance zero
the atom is supported, not refuted or open. At negative tolerance it is refuted.
The discontinuity of this external assessment map is compatible with the
continuity of its numerical margins; confusing those two maps would falsely
claim an internal discrete evaluator. The source's empirical validity is still
conditional on the certificate mode and trace, on both sides of the comparison.

Joint conjunction introduces the quantifier exchange

    for every x, some required i fails
    versus
    some required i fails for every x.

The first can hold without the second. The exact failure condition is nonempty
individual accepted sets with empty intersection. Cartesian independent blocks
repair the exchange by combining accepted witnesses. It is an information
assumption; stochastic independence is neither stated nor needed.

The optional conflict antichain adds exactly the missing answers for a fixed
profile family. It is a declared stronger consumer, not evidence that the old
phase-one evaluator violated its own specification.

## R3. Faithful Boolean substitution depends on joint coverage

Here is a reconstruction of B2/B6 using F08's image principle. Suppose closed
terms t_1,...,t_n are each certified Boolean in a new context D. Their joint
image Z on the target reduct is a nonempty subset of {0,1}^n. Every classical
validity remains native after substituting these terms, because it holds on
the whole cube and hence on Z. No independence assumption is needed for this
preservation direction.

Reflection of **all** classical entailments holds exactly when Z is the entire
Boolean cube. If a vector e is missing, let A_e be the conjunction of literals
describing exactly that valuation (p_i when e_i=0, not p_i when e_i=1). Its
negation is true on Z but false at e. This supplies an explicit extra validity
after substitution, so reflection fails. If all vectors occur, any classical
countervaluation has a preimage and still refutes the translated query.

Since each Boolean term is constant on every connected component of the
accessible reduct, realizing all 2^n vectors requires at least 2^n components.
Conversely, with at least that many components, label them surjectively by the
vectors and use B6's separators to construct each coordinate bit. This proves
the exact component requirement for a faithful free n-atom Boolean fragment.
It is not an ambient dimension bound: one real source restricted to 2^n
distinct rational points can encode those components. The case/term complexity
and explicit source contract remain; no efficient free compression is claimed.

This also distinguishes a Boolean term from access to a hidden policy label:
two overlapping source cases cannot receive different Boolean values at their
common point, and a chain of overlapping cases must receive one value throughout.

## R4. Presentation equality is not unchanged trace syntax

Reconstruct a unit conversion from the commuting square: old x in v has value
k*x in u, so the new factor must send a_v*x to a_u*k*x, namely k'=a_u*k/a_v.
The affine offset must likewise be c_u−k'*c_v. These equations fix the terms;
they are not chosen by resemblance of two sample numbers.

For a common positive scale, the actual normalizer preserves recognized
equalities, including residual expansion, constant folding and lexical lets.
For heterogeneous unit scales or a nonzero offset, a native lattice identity
can intervene. The two counterexamples in the scaling note keep denotational
equality while the blind constant-rule instruction fails. The accepted
consequence theorem follows through matching reducts and U1; the checked
direct compiler has the narrower common-positive-scale contract.

Negative affine coefficients were an omission in the first presentation draft.
The reconstruction adds the pair-reversing law and distinguishes it from a
positive unit conversion. A zero coefficient and a noninjective monotone map
both lose order reflection. Fixed rational parameters are separated from
unknown source-dependent multiplication.

## R5. Reconstructing the normalizer and weakening a scale hypothesis

For the common-positive-scale compiler, describe a collected form as a rational
linear combination of source/min/max atoms plus a constant. Define Phi_a on
these forms recursively: source atoms stay source atoms, offsets multiply by a,
and a min/max atom receives Phi_a on each child form. Rational coefficients
remain fixed. Phi_a commutes with collected addition, scalar multiplication,
unchanged named conversion factors and residual expansion max(b−a,0). Constant
min/max folding commutes because a>0. Lexical instantiation uses the same map
on its environment. Phi_(1/a) is its inverse, so no new accidental atom collapse
invalidates the argument. An old recognized difference equality therefore maps
to a new one, and a constant difference b maps to a*b. Together with the
positive-homogeneous budget constructors, this reconstructs the direct native
trace result rather than inferring it from successful examples.

The general budget-rigidity proof initially assumed a continuous increasing h.
The assumption audit found it unnecessary. Exact rational-budget comparison at
b=0 already forces strict order and g(0)=0. Partitioning a fixed finite interval
into increments of length 2/n bounds g(1/n) by a quantity tending to zero.
This forces uniform continuity on the whole line. The equality of rational
increments then forces affinity by rational approximation. S3 now states this
stronger conclusion with its full elementary proof. It still requires one map
to preserve **all** rational budget tests over arbitrary baselines; a single
fixed threshold or a restricted source family is a different contract.

## R6. Reconstructing the geometric hypotheses

The component separator needs a *uniform* positive gap on the other components,
even when they are unbounded. Mere disjointness of arbitrary closed sets would
not establish that gap. Here V_A is finite CPWA and the other domain is a
finite closed polyhedral union. Each common affine cell has a closed
one-dimensional polyhedral image, so its finite nonnegative minimum is attained.
Zero would contradict disjointness; the finite minimum over all cells is
therefore positive and rational. This checks the precise extra hypothesis
behind B6's explicit bit term.

The finite-grid theorem also needs a particular geometry: the full independent
cube, divided by comparisons of literals x_i, 1−x_i and constants. Active
equalities form a signed coordinate graph; an unanchored component without a
reversing cycle has a two-sided feasible perturbation and cannot be a vertex.
The only new unanchored fixed value is 1/2. General affine source rows break
this argument, as the min(x,y) example on x+y<=1/2 demonstrates.

For the phase-one conflict bound, the source must be convex for one d+1 bound.
The sparse rational infeasibility certificate uses at most d+1 row columns in
R^(d+1), and a feasible source guarantees at least one is a requirement row.
Parameterizing the source's affine hull improves d to its actual dimension.
The sum of the case dimensions plus one per case controls a finite union; the
parabola point family attains the resulting k-point bound. A distinct component
bound applies to native Boolean-valued requirements. These are compatible:
threshold assessments can vary over a connected source even when native
Boolean functions there are constant.

Finally, same full-source geometry does not imply same native typed theory.
Discarding an infeasible child using foreign-unit evidence can leave an
accessible sign guard in the remaining cases. The saved refinement witness
shows a new U-bound despite an unchanged full set of models. A source
presentation must preserve the relevant reduct, not merely its full denotation.

## R7. Checking that phase one has not been reduced to its scalar example

The earlier formalism permits general ordered risk spaces and acceptable
regions. The reconstruction therefore adds an exact finite-polyhedral region
adapter and a componentwise hard-constraint representation, while retaining
the common target-unit evidence view. A signed sum can cancel a violated hard
condition; maximum or a sum of positive violations cannot do so at zero allowance.

The limits are explicit: incomparable vectors cannot be faithfully collapsed
to one ordinary scalar order; a curved quarter-disk region is not a finite
CPWA sublevel set; an open certificate region can be disjoint from acceptance
without a uniform positive separation. These are obstructions to particular
imports, not proofs that every richer encoding of the old calculus is impossible.

Two empty-family conventions must also stay separate. Classical entailment
with no premises is meaningful and uses zero antecedent loss. A phase-one
profile with no required slot is outside that core's well-formedness contract.
Likewise report-only diagnostics do not enter the required meet, but their
records still belong to the total diagnostic/provenance contract. The scalar
aggregate alone cannot recover safety alarms or the identities of failed atoms.

## R8. Checking that bounded vectors do not evade the stated obstruction

For an unbounded finite-polyhedral source and any finite CPWA vector encoding,
one common affine cell is unbounded. Its recession ray is either mapped to an
unbounded output ray or collapsed. Hence a bounded image rules out injectivity
even with several encoded coordinates. Conversely a finite CPWA decoder on a
bounded relevant source range cannot produce an unbounded old coordinate image.
The extended S5 states both arguments, including the target-reduct qualification.

These results do not say that every query needs absolute unbounded information.
A paired comparison can discard a common offset; a finite tolerance can justify
an approximate encoding; and a nonlinear decoder outside finite CPWA may use
an open bounded code domain with singular behavior near its boundary. Those
are different contracts. Keeping them separate prevents an artificial demand
that all useful representations be unbounded, while also preventing an unjustified
claim that ordinary bounded native arithmetic is an exact global replacement.

## R9. Audit of the additional protected-time derivations

The affine certificate construction in [05h](05h_affine_certificate_transport.md)
has two logically separate inductions. First, a canonical root-unit form
preserves the normalizer's recognized **difference** equalities. Second, native
lattice proofs connect that form to the actual presentation macros. Replacing
the second induction with a constant-rule assertion would revive the already
refuted blind relabeling algorithm. The rule audit explicitly elaborates rows
and residual congruence, retains proof-minimum parents, and fixes source paths
and canonical syntax across every case. Helpers retain their local/global
scope. Its inverse macro composition recovers the original collected forms
and reconstructs against the original context fingerprint. These are paper
proof obligations discharged by the stated construction, not tests of an
implemented general compiler. The one-way, offset examples separately check
positive and negative allowances and attain the asserted bounds.

The nonlinear extension in 05e uses a different argument. It changes cases
and transports operations, then invokes U1 on a target that sees **all** rows
and guards. The sufficient reachability condition prevents the inaccessible
foreign-row pruning used in its counterexample. Its exact query includes an
inverse decoder. It therefore makes neither a claim about arbitrary directed
graphs nor an exact base-independent nonlinear budget replacement.

The strengthened rigidity corollary in 05b uses just one nonzero threshold
when h is finite CPWA on R. Continuity identifies the increment at the threshold;
repeating that increment reaches an affine tail and forces the same line
everywhere. The infinite-piece, strictly increasing periodic-increment example
checks why the finite-global-piece hypothesis matters. At threshold zero the
nonlinear increasing finite-CPWA example remains a counterexample to rigidity.

Finally, the convex conflict family in 05f has sharp margin
`max(0,1-(m-1)/r)` for an r-slot profile. Its continuous margins, despite a
connected source, are not Boolean-valued terms. A Boolean realization instead
needs a separate component for every maximal accepted (m-1)-subset. The
binomial-size antichain alone does not establish a representation lower bound,
since this particular family has a short cardinality description. The separate
counting argument varies every central-level antichain independently and fixes
the decoder with no external source access. Those qualifications are essential
to the worst-case information conclusion.
