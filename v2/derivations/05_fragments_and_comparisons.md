The question is what the adopted calculus actually interprets. A shared number
range, a truth table after relabeling, an evidence consumer, and an isomorphism
of budgeted inference are different relationships. This note records each at
its stated scope. No novelty, learned neural structure, unrestricted reflection,
full phase-one embedding, or later gate is claimed.

Use the finite rational CPWA fragment and admitted finite nonempty unions of
closed rational polyhedra from F05. Values at individual assignments are finite
signed reals. A native judgment `t <=[b] s` means a checked comparison whose
root difference is at most b. By [F08 U1](04_characterization.md), derivability
is exactly validity on the target-unit reduct, not always on the full source.
The comparisons below use one unit unless a unit transformation is explicit.
