https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol363-csl2026/html/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.3.html

Checked the arithmetic/sequent interface in sections 3–4. Antecedent quantities
add, whereas hypotheses are collections of sequents; these are different uses
of multiplicity. The residual supplies the max(b-a,0) mechanism. F06 uses a
separate signed, source-scoped language and does not import the paper's full
multiplication/division language or finite-theory completeness theorem. Row
reuse in F06 is reuse of a conditional premise, not independent empirical data.
