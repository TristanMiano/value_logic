## Aim and authority

> Can value, applied to itself over time, recover factual structure? Within
> that same recursion, can truth, theory succession and ontology change each
> be expressed as a derived structure? How do instrumental convergence and
> evolution supply, alter or limit the environmental pressures in that process?

The [charter](v4/CHARTER.md) and [aim-balance policy](v4/AIM_BALANCE.md) govern.
Read the charter's Drift section before work. Preserve its seven theses as
research ambitions, and search jointly for faithful and defensible precise
readings. Named theorem targets below are questions and conjectures, not
promises that the proposed conclusion holds.

Phases one to three supply usable tools and proved restricted results.
In particular, [phase one's proper-score bridge](paper.md#f4-proper-score-information-and-the-task-quotient)
already connects predictive judgment to task information. Phase four should
extend that foothold toward recovery, recursion and ontology change. Existing
results need their assumptions, and a new interpretation needs its own argument.

V19/N16 extensions, retention economics and large numerical panels remain
available when they serve a charter thesis. Full-cost superiority over ordinary
methods is not a phase gate. Compare the closest established results to clarify
what the new construction characterizes, unifies or makes expressible.

## Recurrence contract

The full execution rules are in [RECURRENCE.md](v4/RECURRENCE.md).

- Each **selected cycle of every main module** below has a **240-minute engaged
  research floor**, unless the author explicitly overrides that cycle. This is
  a minimum, not a ceiling, deadline or completion criterion. Administration,
  waiting and unobserved time earn no credit.
- A module can recur whenever important questions remain. Complete a cycle at
  a substantive boundary after its floor; record the strongest supported
  reading and the next constructive and limiting questions. An unresolved
  module stays available even after a milestone advances at a stated scope.
- Continue an interrupted cycle across sessions and context windows with its
  measured balance. Do not restart its floor or borrow surplus from old cycles.
  A new four-hour cycle is selected under the current author authorization;
  this list does not silently start an unlimited sequence of cycles.
- Begin with a thesis, kernel hypothesis, candidate readings, declared evidence
  and rough forecast. Seek constructions, sufficient conditions and repairs as
  explicitly as counterexamples and obstructions. Successful constructions
  receive consequential extension and boundary checks; failed constructions
  receive equally serious repair and alternative-reading attempts.
- End with alignment before correctness review, the strongest faithful statement
  supported and its remaining gap, plus the aim-balance self-review. A local
  success does not settle the broad thesis; a local failure does not settle it
  negatively. Give both their actual scope and next informative investigation.

Small targets within modules are revisable directions for a cycle. They have
no separate four-hour floor and are not a checklist whose first traversal
closes the subject. Keep lightweight notebook records and a single cycle index
at the end of this plan; no phase-three-sized trace archive is required.

([decision](v4/decisions/2026-11-09_running_example.md)).

- **Abstract judgment core.** Type performers, task instances, outcomes, judges
  and the two levels of evaluation once, independent of any world. W1's
  definitions and results are stated here. Exhibit every core result in at
  least one instance, so that it stays a statement about judgment and value.
- **Physics-task world** (instance). Performers/models, noisy judges and tasks
  across Newtonian, relativistic and drag-sensitive regimes. Primary for C3,
  C4 and C6. For W1, prefer many heterogeneous model builds with costs, and
  tasks that carry a tolerance, so that skill transfers unevenly and judges
  have something to be good or bad at.
- **Multi-agent game-like world** (instance). Agents with goals, explicit
  actions, scarce resources and repeated environmental feedback for W6;
  inherited or transmitted evaluation rules, variation and differential
  persistence/reproduction for W7. Also a natural setting for C7. The
  environment may change in response to agents. A bare prediction table is not
  yet an evolutionary environment.

Add further task families as instances when a thesis needs them. Each instance
documents its correspondence to the core: what plays performer, task, outcome
and judge, and which observations are available to performers, judges and the
evaluator. Hidden labels may score a synthetic experiment but cannot secretly
train an algorithm advertised as recovering them from judgments. Keep internal
preferences, received approvals, measured predictive success and external
selection payoffs distinct until a bridge between them is proved.

Instance designs are settled by the cycle that first needs them. Connection
across worlds is a research question for P4-R8, not a property of the setup:
seek results that transfer through the core and the conditions that make them
transfer, and equally seek separating examples showing where a result is
specific to one world. A result shown in one instance is claimed for that
instance until the transfer is argued. Construct supportive and contrasting
cases under comparable naturalness standards. Do not force every phenomenon
into an inadequate simulation merely to claim integration.

### P4-R1 — Recursive judgment and task recovery (W1)

**Kernel:** evaluating the predictive quality of judgments can reward task
representations that capture real, repeatable differences in performance.

- **W1.1 Model and evidence.** Type performers, tasks, outcomes, approval
  reports, judge reliability and the two levels of evaluation. Specify what is
  observed and when, which distinctions count as the recovery target, and what
  a first-order comparator receives. Construct informative independent and
  mediated feedback cases alongside correlated or uninformative ones, and seek
  the conditions distinguishing them.
- **W1.2 Recovery / W1-T1.** Seek an identifiability and recovery theorem for
  the outcome-relevant partition or kernel, extending to raw latent labels
  when the observation model distinguishes them. Define the objective, sampling,
  independence/rank/separation conditions and label symmetries before claiming
  J²-optimal recovery or rates. Use Dawid--Skene, multidimensional IRT,
  co-clustering, peer prediction and latent-class identifiability as enabling
  results and close comparisons. Test both useful recursion and equally informed
  nonrecursive alternatives; identify an information, computational or
  organizational contribution without requiring exclusive capability.
- **W1.3 Incremental information / W1-T3.** Build on the existing conditional
  information criterion in phase one. Separate new information from useful
  computation on existing information. Later observations can supply an
  informative increment, but later independent noise cannot; a newly revealed
  earlier signal can help too. Seek conditions under which temporal recurrence
  actually creates informative feedback, and characterize redundant feedback
  with repairs that restore informative judgment.
- **W1.4 T = J² / W1-T2.** Define the spaces and maps for task refinement and
  judge evaluation. Seek a coupled fixed point or equivalence on a declared
  quotient that explains the founding intuition. Separate existence, stability,
  convergence and factual correctness. Construct grounded equilibria and
  self-confirming alternatives, then identify selection or exploration conditions
  that favor the grounded ones.
- **W1.5 Excellence centroids / W1-T4.** Seek conditions under which diverse
  competent judgments recover excellence modes or shared latent structure.
  Compare exposed faces, extreme points and Pareto frontiers; handle ties and
  incomplete preference coverage. Seek positive concentration/coverage results
  and characterize when new judges or nonlinear preferences recover missed modes.
- **W1.6 Optional real data.** After the information contract is clear, use a
  judged-performance dataset to test its informative and limiting predictions.

**Recur on:** recovery beyond supplied structure, endogenous judge quality,
finite-data guarantees, faithful T/J² typing and the geometry of excellence.
A mutual-definition construction alone does not establish factual recovery;
pursue the additional conditions that would make it do so.

### P4-R6 — Instrumental convergence (new W6)

**Kernel:** a shared environment can make certain intermediate capabilities,
distinctions and resources valuable to agents with different objectives.

- **W6.1 Define convergence.** Distinguish agreement across agents/objectives
  from change over time within agents. Specify the goal family or distribution,
  environment, information, resources and decision rule. Test which shared
  instrumental values emerge under those conditions and which goal/environment
  changes produce informative divergence.
- **W6.2 Construct and characterize.** Seek a theorem or worked mechanism in
  which accurate discrimination, reliable judgment, cooperation, option retention
  or another named capability improves achievement of a broad declared goal
  family. Compare Bostrom's instrumental-convergence argument and formal MDP
  results on option-preserving behavior. Reconstruct the assumptions actually
  needed; exhibit useful common structure and exceptions with comparable care.
- **W6.3 Connect to judgment and truth.** Ask when shared instrumental value
  makes agents learn the same factual distinctions, and when it instead supports
  shared proxies or different representations. Look for an identifiability or
  successful-use bridge that separates those cases. Shared subgoals and agreement
  are evidence to analyze, not definitions of truth. Characterize when they do
  warrant a common factual conclusion rather than stopping at that caution.
- **W6.4 Goal persistence and revision.** Investigate when preserving an
  objective helps it succeed, and when valuing revision, commitments or improved
  judgment makes changing it worthwhile. Connect this to W4 and the author's
  thesis about the relation between instrumental and terminal values; neither
  assume that distinction disappears nor make its permanence an axiom.
  *Author's reading, November 12:* a clamped terminal goal is granted only for
  the sake of argument, and conditional entrenchment is the proposed mechanism
  for the blurring, not a limit on it. What "terminal" means without a clamp,
  and what a never-removed pressure produces, are the subject of the briefed
  addendum (Claude, Fable 5.2).

**Recur on:** breadth across objectives, environmental dependence, bounded/learned
agents, and the bridge from convergent usefulness to factual structure. Handoff
to W2 must state what convergence means and what warrants the truth connection.

### P4-R7 — Evolution, environmental selection and meta-value (new W7)

**Kernel:** the external environment can select not only successful actions but
also the internal values and value-revision rules that generate them.

- **W7.1 Define the evolutionary mechanism.** Model variation, inheritance or
  transmission, differential reproduction/persistence and interaction. Distinguish
  biological selection, cultural transmission and within-agent learning; any
  claimed equivalence needs a bridge. Let agents affect the environment where
  relevant. Construct stable and changing feedback regimes and characterize what
  each selects.
- **W7.2 Values and meta-values.** Define internal preference, an internal rule
  for evaluating or revising preferences, and externally measured fitness or
  persistence. Seek conditions under which environmental pressures favor useful
  meta-values such as adaptive revision or reliable judgment, and conditions
  favoring rigidity, exploitation or a different standard; derive repairs or
  environmental changes that separate those outcomes. Compare the indirect
  evolutionary approach to preference formation, including observability and
  strategic interaction.
- **W7.3 Factual recovery under selection.** Seek a construction where selection
  for successful judgment recovers task-relevant environmental structure.
  Compare with cases where coarse proxies or self-reinforcing approval perform
  well, and characterize when changing tasks, exploration or feedback favor more
  faithful distinctions. Test stability, cycling, diversity and adaptation rather
  than presuming a single population limit.
- **W7.4 What agents should value.** Make the sense of 'should' explicit:
  selected in a population, instrumentally required for a stated purpose, or
  normatively justified. Investigate positive bridges between these levels and
  their limiting cases under stated premises. Environmental control of success
  supplies a candidate meta-value constraint; determine how far it justifies
  an agent's own evaluative standard without assuming the desired conclusion.
- **W7.5 Feed W2 and W4.** Explain how evolutionary and instrumental pressures
  interact with recursive judgment, goal revision and candidate truth limits.
  Preserve multiple stable values or changing environments when supported, while
  seeking conditions for stable informative structure across them.

- **W7.6 Terminality under permanent pressure.** Added November 12 by
  Claude (Fable 5.2) from the author's
  [clarification](v4/decisions/2026-11-12_terminal_instrumental.md). Start from
  the meaning of "terminal" stated by the W6 addendum; if it has not run, from
  its [brief](v4/notebook/W6_terminality_addendum/BRIEF.md). The author asks
  what a priority becomes when its supporting pressure is something every
  agent faces for all time, and whether it then resembles what an agent
  ultimately wants. Named questions:
  - *Conflict.* When a permanently supported priority and an encoded end
    recommend different actions, find which governs behaviour, how that
    depends on when and how the end is assessed, and what the losing side
    costs in persistence. R6.12 supplies one case: an end assessed once, far
    away.
  - *Selection on encoded ends.* In a population whose members carry
    different encoded ends under one permanent pressure, characterize which
    ends persist. Seek conditions under which the surviving ends align with
    the pressure, conditions that keep diverse or opposed ends, and the
    environmental rule that separates them.
  - *Selection on revisability.* Find when permanent pressure selects lower
    susceptibility to revision, so that something clamp-like arises without a
    designer (R6.4's constant-advantage case), when changing conditions select
    continued revisability, and whether a literal clamp is reached or only
    approached.
  - *Pressured revision of ends assumed terminal.* Use the author's
    [corrigibility-games](https://github.com/TristanMiano/corrigibility-games)
    as the comparison setting: agents begin with terminal goals for the sake
    of argument and are pressured, or choose, to change them. Keep its
    distinctions among terminal-goal preservation, utility-freezing
    preferences, policy rigidity and commitment devices, and its separation
    of voluntary relaxation of freezing from population selection against
    it. Connect to W7.2. Cite its models and reports at the level of their
    actual claims; they are not verified in this repository.

**Recur on:** emergence of evaluation criteria, external constraints on internal
meta-value, endogenous environments, terminality under permanent pressure, and
constructive truth/fitness bridges.
This is a main research module, not a paragraph appended to W2.

### P4-R2 — Limit truth and fixed points (W2)

**Kernel:** repeatable success under informative environmental pressure can
stabilize factual commitments while particular theories continue to improve.

- **W2.1 Limit truth.** Define eventual frontier, nonemptiness, persistence,
  common language or translations, and agreement. Seek consistency and closure
  under consequence, with informative countermodels and repairs for empty,
  oscillating or incompatible frontiers. Distinguish eventual membership from
  infinitely frequent undominance and from agreement at each finite stage.
- **W2.2 Classical fragment.** Develop the fragment on which stable commitments
  support classical consequence, and characterize how incomplete agreement can
  support a useful weaker fragment. Existence of a limit and an agent's ability
  to recognize it are separate positive targets with separate limiting cases.
- **W2.3 Newton and the truth bridge.** Seek a construction where a scoped
  Newtonian claim survives while stronger competitors improve other regimes;
  test the scope and its repair under changed tolerances. Explicitly use the
  W6/W7 findings to distinguish grounded stability from mere agreement or
  selective survival. Compare Peirce, Kelly's limiting learnability and Logical
  Induction at the level of their actual claims.

**Dependency:** definitions may begin earlier, but a fixed-point/limit-truth
assessment must address **both W6 and W7**, their mechanisms, supported cases and
named gaps. Do not presume either module proves C3; seek the additional bridge
where needed. Recur on faithful truth readings, stronger fragments and changing
languages. Formal closure of an agreed set is a foothold, not the entire C3 aim.

### P4-R3 — Licenses, local supersession and ontology change (W3)

**Kernel:** useful local theories may remain compatible and reusable as the
available domains and representational languages change.

- **W3.1 Toy sheaf.** Define domains, local objects, restrictions and compatibility
  before imposing sheaf axioms. Construct compatible gluing and informative
  failures; identify what each says about actual licenses. Approximate error
  tolerances may require extra structure. Seek a useful formalization even if
  the first sheaf proposal is merely decorative.
- **W3.2 Global sections and succession.** Separate consistency of a model over
  the current cover from finality under future evidence, domains and competitors.
  Seek the extra persistence/extension conditions under which a global or
  compatible limiting section can represent a final theory; characterize local
  success with continuing supersession and obstruction cases with their repairs.
  Study cover refinement and changed sections separately, rather than defining
  all supersession as cover refinement by fiat.
- **W3.3 Different ontologies.** Type bridge maps between genuinely different
  carriers and seek value comparisons that preserve stated use. Construct
  successful transports and characterize failed transports with the smallest
  additional bridge or representation change that repairs them.

**Recur on:** informative gluing, future persistence, ontology comparison and
what the absence of a final theory means in a faithful typed model.

### P4-R4 — Reflection and recursive value updates (W4)

**Kernel:** an agent can evaluate its own evaluative practice, with conditions
that distinguish useful self-trust from self-confirmation.

- **W4.1 Self-licensing.** State the logic, licensing predicate, expressive power,
  derivability conditions, error criterion and resource interpretation. Seek a
  Löb-type obstruction where those conditions hold and positive bounded,
  stratified or cyclic self-trust where appropriate. Include a genuinely cyclic
  construction with explicit fixed-point semantics and a comparison showing
  which conditions make it reliable or unreliable. Strictly smaller budgets or
  domains are candidate mechanisms, not a presumed universal necessity.
- **W4.2 Value drift.** Define normalization, feedback and what counts accumulate
  before seeking convergence. Construct stable useful updates and characterize
  cycling or runaway regimes with repairs. Connect individual revision to W6's
  incentives and W7's selection rather than identifying these dynamics by name.

**Recur on:** self-reference, informative cyclic models and stable values over
values. Acyclic bookkeeping alone does not discharge the cyclic target; keep
its actual guarantees and develop the missing cyclic mechanism.

### P4-R5 — Neural atlases and policy/value duality (W5)

**Kernel:** learned geometric and evaluative representations may implement the
same useful distinctions as the judgment and licensing constructions.

- **W5.1 Atlas experiment.** Specify a falsifiable region/validity correspondence
  and one preregistered primary metric before evaluation. Compare trained,
  untrained and shuffled-target controls and relevant architectures at matched
  budgets. Construct examples of meaningful alignment and characterize where
  boundaries reflect nuisance geometry; use both to improve the proposed bridge.
  Separate the piecewise-affine representation fact from semantic alignment.
  Depth necessity requires a specified function/resource class and appropriate
  lower-bound comparison; empirical depth gains motivate, but do not prove, it.
- **W5.2 Policy/value representation.** Make C7 a core question: seek typed
  behavior-preserving reconstructions and informative identification and
  complexity bounds, pricing the information in the environment, decoder and
  decision procedure. Compare positive distributed representations with
  nonidentifiability and compression cases, seeking the conditions separating
  them. A larger distributed neural probe and additional real datasets remain
  optional extensions; a failure of clean local neuron blocks does not exhaust
  the question.

**Recur on:** the geometric/semantic bridge, meaningful distributed value,
representation complexity and generalization of successful constructions.

### P4-R8 — Integrated construction and thesis synthesis

**Kernel:** the judgment, environmental selection, truth and revision mechanisms
may explain one another within a single explicitly compatible construction.

- Connect actual outputs of the modules in the running or linked examples.
  State shared objects, evidence and feedback; construct the composition and
  test compatibility boundaries, repairing mismatches where possible.
- Ask whether W1's excellence structure and W2's theory frontier coincide under
  a useful mapping, and how W6/W7 select or destabilize it. Seek an explicit
  correspondence with separating examples rather than relying on the word
  'frontier' in both descriptions.
- Select results for their charter significance and evidence, including
  informative repaired theses. Identify imported mathematics and the explicit
  interpretive, theorem, representation or unification delta. An ordinary
  equivalent can establish a useful correspondence; equivalence alone does not
  decide the contribution's value.

**Recur on:** unresolved connections that matter to the integrated claim. The
report may present a coherent partial synthesis with explicit gaps; it must
neither invent a unified theorem nor understate independently useful results.

  necessary returns to W1/W6/W7. Compare fruitful integrations and mismatches,
  carrying repaired constructions forward. Every thesis receives an explicit
  evidence-backed disposition; optional experiments do not make C7 optional.
- **M4 — Integrated contribution and author decision.** Assess P4-R8, then
  assemble **R.1**, the essay-first report with formal appendices. Keep the
  author's aims and terminology clear and attribute model-written prose as
  such. **R.2** is the final alignment/correctness review: each C1--C7 thesis is
  advanced, unchanged or refuted-with-repair, with reasons and remaining gaps.
  Give a reasoned recommendation; the author decides phase completion.

Routine report assembly and formatting have no separate time floor. New
mathematics needed to connect the results belongs to an appropriate recurrence.

## Planning review

The [initialization review](v4/reviews/2026-11-08_initialization.md) records the
end-of-task self-review for this revision. It preserves Opus's thesis-first
orientation, corrects the missing recurrence and environmental-selection duties,
and treats conjectures as constructive research targets. The main risk is
repeating phase-three drift by substituting safe formal statements for the
founding ideas; the converse risk is assuming the ambitious targets are already
true. The revised targets pair faithful constructions with informative limiting
cases and repairs. This self-review does not guarantee an unbiased research plan.
